# Evidence on prepaid and virtual card spend

## The 30,000 KRW line still applies
A purchase on a prepaid card or a single-use virtual number is evidenced exactly like a corporate card charge. **A prepaid card is not pre-approved spend that skips the receipt** — it only pre-approves the amount, not the paperwork.

| Spend | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Up to 30,000 KRW | A simple receipt is fine |
| Over 30,000 KRW | Qualifying evidence required |

## Where this catches people
Staff sometimes treat a virtual number issued for one vendor as self-documenting, since finance already knows which vendor and how much was loaded. That knowledge covers the transaction, not the invoice. Without qualifying evidence above 30,000 KRW, the 2% penalty applies exactly as it would on a corporate card charge.

## Foreign vendors
If the vendor is overseas, the foreign-currency conversion rule applies unchanged — the card statement's transaction-date rate sets the KRW figure that decides which row of the table above applies.

Once the spend itself is settled, what's left of the loaded balance is a separate question, covered under unused balances.
