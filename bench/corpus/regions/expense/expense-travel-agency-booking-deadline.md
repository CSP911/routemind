# Booking lead time for the corporate travel agency

## The window
Submit a domestic booking request at least 2 working days before departure, and an international booking request at least 5 working days before departure, to guarantee the fare the agency first quotes.

## What happens after the window
A request inside the window is still accepted, but the agency quotes whatever fare is available at the time, which is frequently above the cap. **A late request doesn't extend the fare cap — if nothing under the cap is available, the traveler needs a draft approval for the gap, exactly as with any other out-of-policy booking**, rather than the agency simply absorbing the difference.

## Why it matters
The 2 and 5 day windows are also what the agency uses to decide whether a held fare is still guaranteed if approval is delayed; a request filed inside the window but approved late can still lose its quoted fare when it expires.
