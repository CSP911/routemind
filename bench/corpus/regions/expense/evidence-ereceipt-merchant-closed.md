# When the e-receipt merchant is gone

## Reissue assumes the merchant still exists
The pending-reissue queue works when the seller can resend a copy — that route runs 3–5 working days once you've filed the statement of circumstances as a draft. It depends on there being a business on the other end to ask.

## When there isn't one
A platform that has shut down, a pop-up vendor that has closed, or an account that no longer exists means reissue is not coming. **Waiting for a reissue that will never arrive burns the same clock as a genuine pending case — file the statement of circumstances immediately instead of leaving the claim open.**

## What the statement needs
Note what was purchased, the amount, the date, and why the receipt cannot be recovered — that the merchant has ceased trading — with whatever secondary proof exists: a bank statement line, an email confirmation, a screenshot of the now-dead account. The division head reviews it the same way as any other statement.

This sits next to the ordinary reissue queue — the difference is entirely in whether asking again will ever produce anything.
