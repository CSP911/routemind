# How to top up a prepaid card

A prepaid card's balance doesn't refill itself. When it runs low against ongoing spend, the holder or the approver who issued it requests a top-up through the same portal as the original issuance.

## Steps
1. Submit a top-up request naming the amount and the remaining purpose of the card.
2. The original approver signs off — the same person who approved issuance, not a new chain.
3. Finance loads the balance within 2 working days of approval.
4. The balance updates; the reconciliation end date does not reset unless requested separately.

## The trap
**Requesting a top-up without also extending the reconciliation end date leaves the balance due back on the original date**, even though money just landed on the card — so a top-up two days before the deadline still has to be reconciled on schedule.

## Limits
Top-ups are capped at 500,000 KRW per request, and a card can be topped up at most twice before finance requires it be closed out and reissued instead.

Once the purpose the card was issued for ends, whatever's left on it follows the unused-balance rule, not the top-up one.
