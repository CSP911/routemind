# Reporting a lost prepaid card or virtual number

## Report it immediately
Call the card desk the moment a physical prepaid card goes missing or you suspect a virtual number has been compromised. Do this before reporting anything else about the trip or the purchase — the card desk suspends the card first and asks questions after.

## What suspension does and doesn't do
Suspension stops new charges from clearing. **It does not reverse anything already authorised** — a charge that cleared in the minutes before you called is yours to dispute separately, through the same route as a corporate card charge you don't recognise.

## Balance after suspension
| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Card suspended before further spend | Remaining balance is frozen, then returned or reissued |
| Charge clears during the call | Treated as a disputed charge, not a lost-card matter |

Report within 24 hours of noticing the card or number is gone. Past that window the desk still acts, but any intervening charge is harder to separate from your own spending.

The card itself is only half the story — what happens to money left on it once the trip or the purchase is done is covered separately.
