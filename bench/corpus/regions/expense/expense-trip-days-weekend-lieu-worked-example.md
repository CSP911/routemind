# A Saturday spent travelling, and what it actually paid

## The trip
A team member flew out on a Friday for a Monday client meeting and flew back the following Saturday, so the itinerary covered eight calendar days including two weekends. The claim listed all eight days as allowance days, meals and local transport included.

## What was assumed
The traveller expected the first Saturday — a full day lost entirely to flights — to convert into a day off during the following week, a straight swap of a lost weekend for a lieu day at the office.

## What was paid
**Every travelling day, weekend or not, drew the standard daily allowance — but no lieu day was granted.** The allowance compensates for being away; it does not carry a second form of compensation on top of it. The team lead's calendar showed the following Monday as an ordinary working day, and the claim was settled on that basis.

Where a trip's days and its allowance days genuinely diverge for reasons other than a weekend, that's a mismatch the discrepancy desk still has to confirm before it's paid.
