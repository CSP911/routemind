# Quote requirement bands

A purchase order and a quote are not the same trigger. The quote threshold is flat and applies to every purchase over a fixed amount, regardless of who is buying or what their card's per-transaction cap allows.

## The bands

| Purchase amount | Quote requirement |
|---|---|
| Up to 300,000 KRW | None — proceed as usual |
| 300,000–1,000,000 KRW | One written quote on file before purchase |
| Over 1,000,000 KRW | Two written quotes, compared before purchase |

**A team lead's card allows a single transaction up to 1,000,000 KRW — that does not mean a 500,000 KRW purchase skips the quote.** The card cap and the quote threshold are checked independently; clearing one says nothing about the other.

## Where the quote goes
Attach the quote to the purchase order request, or to the card claim if the supplier still takes a card despite the amount. Either way, procurement checks for it before release.

This connects to who signs off once the quote is in hand — that approval band sits alongside this one.
