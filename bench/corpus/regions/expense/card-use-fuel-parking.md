# Fuel and parking

## Company vehicle fuel
Fuel for a company-registered vehicle is charged on the card against the actual receipt, capped only by the per-transaction limit for your grade.

## Personal car and parking

| Situation | How it's paid |
|---|---|
| Company vehicle fuel | Actual cost, receipt required |
| Personal car instead of fuel | 300 KRW per km — record start, end and distance |
| Parking, either vehicle | Actual cost, receipt above 30,000 KRW |

**Fuel receipts and the per-km rate cannot both be claimed for the same trip** — pick one method per trip and stay on it. Mixing them for the same journey is what gets a mileage claim rejected outright, not the amount itself.

Parking under 30,000 KRW follows the same simple-receipt rule as any other small spend.

The daily allowance on a domestic trip already assumes local transport is covered separately from this.
