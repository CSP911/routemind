# Seasonal gifts to clients

A gift sent around Chuseok or the New Year to a client contact counts as entertainment, not a general purchase, even though no meal is involved.

## The cap

| Item | Cap |
|---|---|
| Seasonal or occasion gift | 100,000 KRW each |

The cap is per recipient. A basket of gifts for five client contacts is 500,000 KRW of eligible spend, not a single 100,000 KRW line with the rest disallowed.

## Evidence

Above 30,000 KRW the purchase needs qualifying evidence, same as any other spend. Above that, the recipient list — name and company — has to accompany the claim, the same way an attendee list backs a meal.

**Buying gifts in one transaction and filing one receipt without a recipient breakdown gets the whole purchase capped at 100,000 KRW total, not 100,000 KRW per person.** The receipt has to be split, or attached with a written breakdown, before it will be read as five gifts rather than one.

This sits next to the meal cap: both are entertainment, both need the same evidence line, and both are checked per head rather than per invoice.
