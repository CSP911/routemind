# Who resolves a day-count discrepancy

## The desk
The travelling employee's approver — team lead or above, matching the usual trip sign-off chain — is the first check on whether a claimed allowance day matches an actual trip day. They confirm the itinerary, the attendance record, and any lieu arrangement before the claim moves to finance.

## Where it escalates
Finance's audit desk reviews day-count discrepancies flagged during the monthly sample, the same review that catches other recurring findings. If a discrepancy involves an amount that pushes a team past its budget line for the month, it also goes through the same variance-reporting step as any other overspend.

## What the approver does not decide
**The approver can confirm which days were actually worked, but cannot waive the evidence requirement for a day added after the fact.** A late addition still needs its own supporting record — a sign-in log, a boarding pass, a meeting invite — before it's accepted, no matter how confident the approver is that the day happened.

Escalation beyond the approver follows the same finding-response path used for any other audit query on a trip claim.
