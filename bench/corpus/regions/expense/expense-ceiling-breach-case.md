# A team over its ceiling mid-year

A ten-person team, sitting in the 50,000,000 KRW band, had used 78,000,000 KRW by the end of September against an approved reforecast — already past 100% before the month even closed, following an unplanned overseas client trip that hadn't been in the original plan.

## What happened

1. The October monthly report flagged the team well past 100%, not just at the 80% mark.
2. The team lead filed a variance narrative explaining the cause — the unplanned trip — rather than waiting for the standard 80% check-in, since that stage had already been skipped.
3. The division head reviewed the narrative and granted a one-time exception covering the specific overage, rather than raising the team's ceiling.
4. From that point through December, every new discretionary claim needed the division head's pre-approval before filing, per the 100% rule. Already-booked hotel and flight charges for a prior trip still processed without it.
5. On 1 January, the team's ceiling reset to the standard 50,000,000 KRW for its band. **The October overage was not carried forward as a debit against the new year** — the reset applied in full, exactly as it would have for a team that finished the year exactly on budget.

The narrative filed in step 2 follows the same three-part format required for any monthly variance explanation.
