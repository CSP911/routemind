# A stopover that earned its own night

## The itinerary
A Seoul–Nairobi trip routes through Dubai. The connecting flight lands at 11pm and the onward leg departs at 9am the next day — under 12 hours, but no realistic transfer without a hotel room. The traveller books one night in Dubai and continues the next morning.

## What was claimed
The Dubai night was claimed as its own lodging line at the UAE's band, plus one day's allowance at that band. The Nairobi nights that followed were claimed separately at their own band. Nothing was blended into a single average.

## Why it held up
A stopover earns its own band and lodging line whenever the connection genuinely requires an overnight stay — a layover long enough that no same-day onward flight exists. A two-hour layover with a same-day connection, by contrast, is folded into the connecting leg and pays no separate lodging or allowance.

## The trap
**A long daytime layover with a same-day onward flight does not qualify just because it runs eight or nine hours.** The test is whether an overnight stay was unavoidable, not how many hours passed in the terminal.

The same logic decides whether a mid-trip band change starts from the stopover date or the next arrival.
