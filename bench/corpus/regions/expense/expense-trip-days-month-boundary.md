# Trips that cross a month boundary

## Why the boundary matters
A trip that starts in one month and ends in the next doesn't produce one claim — it produces allowance days that fall on either side of the boundary, and each side is reported against the month it actually occurred in, not the month the trip started or the month it was filed.

## What this affects
- The team's monthly budget line absorbs each day's allowance in the month it fell on, not as a lump sum in the departure month.
- The monthly close calendar applies separately to each side: days before the cutoff close with that month, days after roll into the next month's batch.

## The trap
**Filing the whole trip as a single claim dated to the departure month understates one month and overstates the other.** The claim has to be split at the boundary, with each portion's allowance and lodging dated to the night it actually covers.

Where a claim lands once split follows the same close calendar that governs every other expense line that month.
