# Requesting an out-of-policy booking through the agency

## When this applies
Use this when the fare, class or hotel tier needed doesn't fit inside the standard caps — an upgraded cabin, a carrier outside the negotiated panel, or a hotel above the lodging band for the city.

## Steps
1. Submit the standard booking request to the agency first; let it return the in-policy fare and cap comparison.
2. Attach the reason the in-policy option doesn't work — schedule, accessibility, client requirement — to a draft approval request.
3. Route the draft to the approver for the amount above cap.
4. Once signed, send the approved draft back to the agency, which rebooks or upgrades against it.
5. File the draft number on the trip claim alongside the fare.

## The trap
**Asking the agency to ticket the upgraded fare before the draft is signed is the same as booking it yourself — the agency will quote and hold it, but won't ticket an out-of-policy fare without an approved draft attached**, and a held fare that expires while approval is pending has to be re-quoted from scratch.
