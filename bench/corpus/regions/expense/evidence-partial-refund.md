# Partial refunds versus full refunds

## Both get reported, not just one
A refund landing after a claim has already been settled has to be reported within 5 working days of it hitting the card or account, whether it covers the whole amount or only part of it.

## What differs
A full refund cancels the original spend — the claim is reversed and the amount is repaid in full. A partial refund — a late discount, a partially returned order — only adjusts the claim down by the refunded amount; the rest of the original evidence still stands and doesn't need to be re-filed.

## The trap
**A partial refund is easy to miss because the claim already looks settled and the remaining amount looks unremarkable — but the 5-working-day clock runs from the refund landing, not from when someone notices it.** Left unreported past that window, the full refunded amount is treated as undisclosed income against the claimant, not just the difference.

## What to file
Note the original claim reference, the refund amount and the reason, and route it through the same channel as a full refund.

This is the smaller sibling of the full-refund case already covered elsewhere — the reporting clock is identical either way.
