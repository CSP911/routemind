# Filing the monthly variance report

## The cut-off
Each team lead files the actual-vs-budget report by the 5th working day of the month following close. Finance's consolidation run reads whatever sits in the system on that date; anything filed after it rolls into the next cycle instead of being backdated.

## What counts as filed
Submitting the actuals alone doesn't close the report. Any line that falls into a variance band requiring a written explanation has to carry that explanation before the report leaves draft status — numbers with no narrative attached don't count as filed.

**A report submitted on time but missing a required explanation is treated as not filed at all.** The deadline sits on the complete report, not on the act of clicking submit, so a team that files the figures on the 5th and adds the explanation on the 7th has still missed the cut-off.

## After the cut-off
A report that crosses into the next cycle unresolved copies the division head automatically — no separate escalation step is needed for that alone.

The reforecast that follows a quarter's report runs on a different clock; see the reforecast deadline for how long that window stays open.
