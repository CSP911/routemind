# Trip emissions entry

Each leg of a trip claim needs its own emissions entry before the claim closes. The trip claim system pre-fills what it can from an approved itinerary; anything booked outside that itinerary needs the fields entered by hand.

## What has to be filled in
- Mode (rail, domestic air, international air, own car, taxi)
- Distance in kilometres for that leg
- Whether a rail option existed and was passed over, and if so the mode-choice approval reference it was booked under

## The trap
**Leaving the distance field blank does not stop the claim from closing — the system defaults it to the rail factor.** That understates the entry for any leg actually flown: a flight logged with no distance entered will look, in the annual figure, exactly like a much cleaner train ride.

## Filing
The entry travels with the trip claim through the normal close calendar; there's no separate submission. Fill it in at the same time as the fare and lodging lines, using the itinerary the mode-choice approval was granted against.
