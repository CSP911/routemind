# A month's underspend that didn't carry over

A team spent 4,000,000 KRW under its March travel budget after a client visit was postponed. The team lead's April variance report assumed the extra room would still be there and booked two trips against it.

## What actually happened

The March underspend was never entered as a reforecast line — nobody filed one in the 3-day window after the March report went in. April's budget was untouched by March's result, so both April trips landed as an overrun against the original April figure, crossing the 2,000,000 KRW threshold and requiring an explanation.

## What the explanation had to say

The note couldn't just point back to March. It named the two trips, the dates, and confirmed they were within total year-to-date budget even though April on its own was over. The division head signed off on that basis, since the year total was intact.

**Being under budget for the year doesn't excuse a single month running over it — each month is checked on its own line first.** The fix, going forward, was a reforecast entry the next quarter rather than a hopeful assumption in the report itself.
