# How a finding is answered

A finding is not a rejection of the claim — it is a request to either produce what was missing or accept the consequence the rule attaches to it.

## The steps

1. The audit desk issues the finding to the claimant and their approver together, citing the specific rule.
2. The claimant has until the response window closes to reply.
3. **Missing evidence** is answered by supplying the qualifying document, or by filing a statement of circumstances if it genuinely cannot be produced.
4. **A threshold breach** — a cash spend over 30,000 KRW with only a simple receipt — is answered by accepting the 2% penalty; no evidence supplied afterward reverses it.
5. Once answered, the desk marks the finding closed or escalated. An unanswered finding at window close escalates automatically to the team lead.

**Answering a finding late does not reopen the 2% penalty question — the penalty is fixed by the missing evidence itself, not by how quickly you respond.** Speed only affects whether the finding escalates, not what it costs.

Escalated findings move to the desk covered next.
