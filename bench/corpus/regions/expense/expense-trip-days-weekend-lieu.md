# Weekend days worked in lieu

## What happens to a weekend spent travelling
If a trip requires working through a Saturday or Sunday, that day still draws the daily allowance like any other trip day — it was travel, and the allowance covers meals and local transport regardless of which day of the week it falls on. What it does not do is generate a second form of compensation.

## Lieu time replaces the day off, not the allowance
Compensation for losing the weekend itself is a day off in lieu, arranged with your team lead separately from the expense claim. The allowance and the lieu day sit in two different systems — payroll and HR for the lieu day, the expense claim for the allowance — and approving one does not approve the other.

## The trap
**Claiming a weekend allowance and then also expecting it to count toward lieu time automatically is a mismatch that has to be untangled by hand.** Neither system checks the other; the lieu day has to be requested through your team lead regardless of what the expense claim shows.

The same separation applies to a trip that overruns into an unplanned overnight — the extra night's allowance and any time-off adjustment are filed on their own tracks.
