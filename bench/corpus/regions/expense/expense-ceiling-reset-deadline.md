# The ceiling reset date

The annual ceiling resets on 1 January regardless of when a team crossed 80% or 100% the year before.

## The cutoff that matters

Spend incurred in December has to be filed and closed by that month's close date — the same date used for the monthly close calendar — to be counted against that calendar year's ceiling.

**A December claim filed after the close date is tested against the new year's ceiling, not the year the spend actually happened in.** For a team already close to its limit, that means a late-filed December claim can push the new year's ceiling toward 100% within its first month, before any new spending has even happened.

## What to do

File any outstanding December claim before the close date shown in the close calendar. If a claim genuinely can't be filed in time — a late-arriving invoice, a pending reissue — flag it to the team lead so the projection used for the 80% check-in accounts for it rather than being surprised by it in January.

The mechanics of what the close date locks and what still needs a manual entry are covered in the close system document.
