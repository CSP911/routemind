# Who approves travel to a restricted country

## Level 3 — non-essential travel only
Two sign-offs are required before the travel desk will issue a booking:
- The **division head**, confirming the trip's business purpose can't be handled remotely or deferred.
- The **risk desk**, confirming enhanced insurance has actually been arranged for the destination — the standard automatic policy alone is not sufficient here.

Both have to be on file before ticketing; the travel desk will not book provisionally and sort approval afterward.

## Level 4 — do not travel
**No desk holds the authority to approve a Level 4 destination.** This isn't a higher escalation tier than Level 3 — it's the absence of one. The insurer will not underwrite the trip, so even a signed exception from a division head carries no cover behind it and the travel desk will decline to book regardless of signature.
