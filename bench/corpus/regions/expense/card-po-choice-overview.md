# Choosing between the card and a purchase order

Most spend goes straight on the corporate card. A purchase order only comes into play when the supplier won't invoice a card transaction, or when the purchase calls for a paper trail the card alone doesn't give you.

## When the card is enough
Anything within your per-transaction cap, evidenced the usual way, stays on the card. Staff, team leads and division heads carry different per-transaction ceilings, and that doesn't change just because a PO exists as an option.

## When a supplier insists on a PO
Some suppliers — construction, equipment rental, anything billed against a formal contract — won't accept a card at all. They want a purchase order number before they'll even confirm a price, and they invoice the company directly rather than running a card terminal.

**Being under your card's per-transaction cap does not exempt a purchase from the quote requirement.** The two thresholds are separate: one caps what the card will authorise, the other decides when a competitive quote has to sit on file before you spend anything, card or PO.

## What decides which document you file
If the supplier takes a card, evidence rules apply as normal. If they need a PO, the request form and its approval band take over from there.
