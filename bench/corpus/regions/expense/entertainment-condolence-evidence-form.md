# What the invitation or notice copy must show

A condolence or congratulation claim doesn't run on a receipt, but the substitute document still has to carry enough to identify the occasion.

## Required fields

| Field | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Name of the deceased or the couple | Confirms the occasion the payment was for |
| Date of the funeral or wedding | Ties the payment to a specific event, not a recurring relationship |
| Relationship to the company | Shows why the company is paying rather than the individual |
| Amount paid | Checked against the 200,000 KRW cap |

A photograph of the physical invitation or notice is accepted; it does not need to be the original paper copy.

## The trap

**A text message or verbal notice with no document behind it does not qualify, even when the occasion is genuine and well known within the team.** Ask the family or the couple's side for the printed notice or invitation before the claim is filed — asking for it afterward, once the event has passed, is much harder.

Where no such document exists at all — an informal notice passed around a chat group — treat it the same as any other undocumented small spend and route it through a disbursement form instead, at the division head's discretion.
