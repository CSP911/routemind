# A team that crossed the sign-off threshold

## The setup
A team's monthly actuals came in 22% over its budgeted line for the month — past the 20% mark that requires a division head's sign-off, not just a written explanation.

## What the report showed
The team lead filed the narrative required at the lower band: a cause (a vendor invoice landed a month early) and a corrective note (the following month would show an equivalent underspend). That explanation is exactly what a 10–20% variance calls for.

**Filing the narrative does not clear a variance once it crosses 20% — that band needs the division head's sign-off as well, and the two aren't interchangeable.** The report sat in the queue for four days before anyone noticed the sign-off line was still blank.

## How it resolved
The division head reviewed the same narrative, added the sign-off, and the report closed in that state — no extra explanation was required beyond what the team lead had already written.

## The lesson
The band a variance falls into decides which step is missing, not how much text is attached to the report. Check the variance threshold table before assuming a narrative alone is enough.
