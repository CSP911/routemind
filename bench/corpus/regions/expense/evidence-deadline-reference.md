# Deadlines across evidence exceptions

## Quick reference
| Situation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| E-receipt reissue via statement of circumstances | 3–5 working days to complete |
| Refund landing after a claim was already settled | Report within 5 working days |
| Unrecognised charge or lost card | Raise within 7 days of the statement |
| Disbursement form for a small, undocumented spend | Submit within 14 days of the spend |

## Why these differ
The 5- and 7-day windows run from when the money moved or the statement issued, not from when you noticed. The 3–5 day figure is a processing estimate on the merchant's side, not a deadline you control directly.

## The trap
**None of these clocks pause for each other.** A refund on a trip that also needed an e-receipt reissue still has to be reported within 5 working days even if the reissue is still pending — the two run in parallel, not one after the other. Treating a slow reissue as an excuse for a late refund report is exactly the mistake this table exists to prevent.

For the awkward cases themselves, rather than just their deadlines, the overview page is the place to start.
