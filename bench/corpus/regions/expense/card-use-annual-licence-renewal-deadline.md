# The notice window before an annual licence renews

An annual software licence or professional membership renews on its own unless someone tells the vendor to stop it — and that notice has to go in well before the anniversary date, not on it.

## The window

Request non-renewal or a downgrade to IT or procurement **at least 15 working days before the licence's renewal date.** Vendors' own cutoffs are often similar or tighter; missing the internal window usually means missing theirs too.

## What happens if it's missed

**A renewal that lands because the notice went in late is still a valid company charge, not an error to dispute** — it goes through the same evidence and approval steps as the original purchase, and the prior year's invoice does not carry over to cover it.

Multi-seat or multi-year licences add one more step on top of this — division head sign-off before the term even starts.
