# Who signs off a reforecast

## The chain
A team lead submits a reforecast inside the window that follows each quarter's report. That submission alone doesn't change the figure the system measures variance against — a division head has to approve it first, and finance applies the approved number to the system.

## Where each desk's part ends
- The team lead's job stops at submitting the revised figure with a reason for the change.
- The division head's job is the approval itself — checking the reason is sound, not re-deriving the number.
- Finance's job is applying the approved figure to the system; finance doesn't approve or query the reforecast on its own authority.

**An unapproved reforecast sitting in draft doesn't pause the variance calculation — the system keeps measuring actuals against the old figure until approval lands.** A team that submits a reforecast expecting immediate relief can still show a large variance the following month if the division head hasn't signed off yet.

## Why it matters
The reforecast deadline sets how long the submission window stays open; this is about what has to happen inside that window before the number actually moves.
