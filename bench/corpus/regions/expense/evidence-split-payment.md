# Splitting a bill across payments

## What it looks like
Two card taps for one 45,000 KRW dinner, each under 30,000 KRW, so neither seems to need qualifying evidence — or two claims filed separately for what was really one merchant visit.

## Why it doesn't work
**Payments at the same merchant, on the same day, that add up to over 30,000 KRW are treated as a single spend for evidence purposes, however many receipts they produced.** Accounting reconciles by merchant and date, not by the individual slip, so a split that looks clean on paper is still caught.

## What to do instead
If a genuine business reason produced two separate transactions — different attendees each paying their own share, for instance — note that on the claim rather than letting it read as the same spend twice.

Filed the claim already and the money came back before it closed? That refund needs its own paperwork.
