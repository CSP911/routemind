# Countries the company will not send staff to

Destinations are banded by the government travel advisory level in effect at booking time, not by internal judgement of risk.

| Advisory level | What it means for a booking |
|---|---|
| Level 1 — normal | Books as any other trip |
| Level 2 — exercise caution | Books normally; travel desk logs it |
| Level 3 — non-essential travel only | Needs division head and risk desk sign-off before booking, with enhanced insurance arranged in advance |
| Level 4 — do not travel | **Blocked outright** — no booking is issued regardless of business justification |

## The trap
**A Level 4 destination is not a case of the company declining to insure the trip — the insurer will not issue a policy for it at all, and the travel desk will not book it.** A traveller volunteering to go without cover, or a client insisting the visit is essential, does not change this; there is no waiver that reopens a Level 4 destination.
