# Month-end close and expense audit

Every claim filed during the month feeds into a close that finance runs on a fixed schedule, independent of when your own team's budget report is due. Close is not a formality — a sample of that month's claims gets pulled and checked against the evidence rules before the books lock.

## The four stages

1. **Filing cutoff** — claims must be submitted before the close calendar's cutoff to land in that month.
2. **Sampling** — the desk pulls a sample across cost centres, weighted toward high-value and cash-heavy lines.
3. **Findings** — anything short of qualifying evidence, a missing attendee list, or a mismatched name gets flagged.
4. **Response** — the claimant or approver answers the finding inside a fixed window, or it escalates automatically.

**A claim that clears initial approval is not the same as a claim that clears the audit — approval only checks that spend looks reasonable, not that the evidence underneath it qualifies.** The two checks run at different times, against different rules.
