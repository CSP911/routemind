# Who arranges cover and who handles a claim

## Before the trip
The travel desk arranges the policy automatically the moment an overseas itinerary is confirmed in the booking system. No request, form or premium approval is needed on the traveller's side — if a trip is booked and approved, it is covered.

## During an incident
The insurer's own 24-hour assistance line is the first call, not an internal desk. It coordinates emergency treatment and can arrange direct billing that no internal team is positioned to do.

## After the trip
The risk desk takes over from there: it receives the internal 24-hour notice, tracks the formal claim against the 30-day deadline, and is the point of escalation if the insurer disputes a line item. **The travel desk that issued the policy is not the desk that handles the claim** — routing a claim question back to them after an incident just adds a step, since they hold no visibility into claim status once a trip is underway.

For a Level 3 destination, the risk desk also signs off before departure, which is where cover and approval meet before anything goes wrong.
