# A trip with three evidence problems at once

## The trip
A staff member on an overseas trip paid for a client dinner in two taps of the same card — 25,000 and 20,000 KRW — at one restaurant, kept a receipt printed in the local script, and had a hotel refund a no-show fee back onto the card three weeks after the claim settled.

## How each was handled
- The two dinner payments were treated as a single 45,000 KRW spend, since they hit the same merchant the same day — qualifying evidence was required, and the attendee list came with it as entertainment.
- The foreign-language receipt was accepted once the merchant, date and amount were confirmed and the total converted at the transaction date's exchange rate.
- The hotel refund was reported within 5 working days of landing on the card, well inside the window.

## The trap
**The claimant nearly filed the dinner as two receipts under 30,000 KRW each, which would have skipped qualifying evidence entirely.** It was only caught because both slips showed the same merchant and timestamp minutes apart.

The exchange-rate mechanics behind the foreign receipt are covered on the overseas rates page.
