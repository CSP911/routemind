# A trip that ran from July into August

## The itinerary
A five-day domestic trip ran from 30 July to 3 August: two days in July, three in August, with lodging and the daily allowance claimed for each night and day in between.

## How the days landed
The system split the claim automatically at the calendar boundary, generating one line dated 30–31 July and a second dated 1–3 August, each carrying its own share of the daily allowance and lodging.

## What tripped the claim up
**The two lines close on different schedules.** The July days fell inside a cycle that hadn't yet run its monthly report when the claim was filed, so they carried into that cycle's close without incident — but only because the claim went in before that cycle's own deadline. Filed even a few days later, the July portion would have needed a separate late-correction entry rather than riding through with the rest of the trip.

File a trip that crosses a month boundary as soon as it ends, not once it's convenient — the earlier line has less room to slip a close date than the later one does.
