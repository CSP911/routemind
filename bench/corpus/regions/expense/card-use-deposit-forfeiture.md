# A forfeited venue deposit

A team booked a venue for an offsite and put down a 500,000 KRW deposit on the corporate card. The event was cancelled inside the venue's no-refund window, and the deposit was kept in full — nothing came back.

Because no money returned, the standard deposit-refund procedure never applied; there was no refund to report against. Instead the charge was written off as a cancellation cost against the trip budget, with the division head noting the reason directly on the original claim.

## The trap

**A forfeited deposit is not "pending refund" left open indefinitely — it's closed out as spent the moment forfeiture is confirmed.** Leaving it flagged as awaiting a refund that was never coming just delays the write-off and confuses anyone reconciling the trip's budget later.

Where a deposit does come back, in full or in part, a different clock starts running, and that one does need active reporting.
