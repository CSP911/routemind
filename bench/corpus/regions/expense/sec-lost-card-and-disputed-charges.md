# Card loss and disputed charges

This area covers a card going missing and a charge turning out to be wrong, which are related but not the same problem and are reported through different steps.

## What it covers

A card lost or stolen abroad, a subscription that kept billing after a cancellation request went in, a charge posted under a merchant's trading name rather than the name on the receipt, and a charge disputed after the standard window has already closed.

## The common case

A subscription still charging the corporate card a cycle after the cancellation confirmation email, discovered only when the statement lands.

**Reporting a card as lost does not itself reverse any charge on it** — freezing the card and disputing a specific transaction are separate actions, and the second one still has its own deadline running whether or not the card has already been cancelled.

The same charges often resurface at month-end close, where the audit desk checks whether a dispute was logged before its window shut.
