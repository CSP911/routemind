# When a prepaid card replaces the corporate card

A prepaid card gets issued instead of adding someone to the corporate card programme when the spend is short-term, one-off, or sits outside the approval chain a corporate card assumes.

## When it applies
- Contractors and interns who won't hold the role long enough to justify a corporate card application
- A single event or project with a fixed budget and a named owner
- Overseas local hires who aren't on Korean payroll and can't be added to the corporate card's billing entity

## What it doesn't cover
A prepaid card isn't a smaller corporate card. It has no monthly cycle, no automatic reload, and no ongoing line to a cost centre until someone loads it. **Treating it like a corporate card with a ceiling is the trap** — the balance is fixed at issuance, and anything past it comes out of the holder's pocket until a top-up clears.

## Requesting one
Finance issues the card against the same approval chain as any other expense of that size; the request names an amount, a purpose, and an end date the balance has to be reconciled by.

Loading more onto that balance later is its own request, not a repeat of issuance.
