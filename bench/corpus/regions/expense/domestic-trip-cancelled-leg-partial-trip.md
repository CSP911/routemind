# One leg cancelled, the rest of the trip goes ahead

## The trip
An outbound KTX leg is cancelled by the operator for weather; the traveller rebooks the next available train an hour later and the rest of the itinerary — hotel, meetings, return leg — runs unchanged.

## How it settles
The operator refunds the cancelled ticket directly to the card. The rebooked ticket is claimed as the actual fare for that leg. The trip as a whole is filed as normal, with the refund and the new fare both visible against the same leg.

## The trap
**Claiming both the original ticket and the rebooked ticket as two separate fare lines double-counts the leg.** Once the refund lands, only the rebooked fare stands; the original is a wash, not an additional expense, and it should not appear on the claim as a cost at all.

## Reporting the refund
Because the refund can land after the claim is already drafted, report it within 5 working days of the refund posting to the card — the same window that applies to any post-settlement refund, not from the day of travel.
