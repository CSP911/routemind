# What the corporate travel agency will and won't book

## Booking scope
| Category | Booked through the agency | Handled elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic and international flights | Yes | — |
| Rail tickets, KTX and equivalent | Yes | — |
| Hotel nights on an approved trip | Yes | — |
| Taxis, ride-hailing, local transport | No | Corporate card |
| Visa applications and processing | No | Personal card, reimbursed |
| Travel insurance | No | Issued automatically on approval |
| Personal travel added before or after a business trip | No | Traveler's own arrangement and cost |

## The trap
**Asking the agency to add a personal leg "while they're at it" gets refused, not quietly split — it only quotes and invoices the business itinerary**, so a personal extension has to be booked and paid for separately from the start, or the whole itinerary risks being priced as one non-business fare.
