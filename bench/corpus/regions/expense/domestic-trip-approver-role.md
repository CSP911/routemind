# Who signs off a domestic trip case exception

Most domestic trip claims settle against the standard caps without anyone's separate sign-off beyond the usual claim approval. A few situations need an explicit decision first:

- **Same-day-to-overnight reclassification** — the traveller's team lead confirms the change before the claim is filed, based on the notice given inside the reporting window.
- **Mode change to air on a marginal leg** — team lead confirms the business reason was genuine, not a convenience choice, before the fare is claimed at the air rate.
- **Anything settled through a disbursement form rather than a receipt** — this stays at the division head's discretion, and only up to roughly 100,000 KRW; above that, qualifying evidence is mandatory regardless of who is willing to sign.

**A team lead's verbal "that's fine, go ahead" is not the sign-off — the confirmation has to be on the claim itself, because a claim queried later is checked against what was recorded, not what was said in the corridor.** Division head discretion, in particular, only ever protects amounts under the disbursement-form ceiling.

Above that ceiling, the case is no longer a matter of discretion — it needs the evidence itself.
