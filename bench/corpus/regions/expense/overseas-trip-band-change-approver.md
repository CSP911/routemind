# Who approves a mid-trip band change

## Who signs off

When an itinerary change moves a traveller into a different region band partway through a trip, the same approver who signed the original trip request confirms the change — not the traveller, and not the hotel or booking platform. The confirmation has to reference the date the new leg starts, since that date is what the band change hinges on.

## What happens without it

If lodging or transport is booked into the new band before the approver has confirmed the change, the claim is held at filing rather than paid automatically. **A booking receipt from the new location is not itself proof the band changed — only the approver's confirmation is.** The system will still show the traveller in the original band until that confirmation is logged, whatever the receipts say.

Once the change is confirmed, everything from that date forward follows the new band; the window for telling the approver in the first place is the part that decides whether it applies from that date or retroactively.
