# Petty cash caps and what it covers

Petty cash is for genuinely small, immediate office spend — not a substitute for the card or a way to skip an approval routing.

## Caps

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Per withdrawal | 50,000 KRW |
| Monthly total, per team | 300,000 KRW |
| Custodian | Team lead or delegate, holds the box and the log |

## What it excludes

Petty cash does not cover entertainment, subscriptions, travel fares, or anything with its own dedicated route on the card. It is for stationery, small courier fees, and similar one-off costs under the withdrawal cap.

## The trap

**Petty cash spend above 30,000 KRW still needs qualifying evidence, exactly like a card charge.** Because the box is informal, receipts are often not asked for at all — and a log entry with no receipt above the line is treated as no evidence, not as a simple receipt.

A float held for a specific trip or project, rather than a standing office box, is reconciled differently — see the worked case.
