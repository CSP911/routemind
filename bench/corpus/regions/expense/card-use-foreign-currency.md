# A charge in a foreign currency

## Converting the charge
Use the exchange rate the card statement shows for the transaction date — not a rate looked up separately, and not the rate on the day the claim happens to be filed.

| Step | What to use |
|---|---|
| Amount | The card statement's converted KRW figure |
| Rate | Transaction date, as shown on the statement |
| Threshold | 30,000 KRW, applied after conversion |

**Filing against the rate on the submission day instead of the transaction date can push a spend across the 30,000 KRW line in either direction** — a charge the merchant billed clearly under the line can land just over it, or the reverse, and the wrong version gets sent back.

## What the receipt needs
A foreign receipt qualifies without a Korean business registration number; it still needs the merchant, date and amount legible enough to check against the statement.

Receipts in another script carry one more requirement on top of conversion.
