# Trip-day scenarios at a glance

## The comparison
| Scenario | What the claim should show | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Trip confirmed late | Allowance from actual departure date, added manually if the system defaults to the approval date | **Backdated approval doesn't backdate the day count automatically** |
| Day attended, not claimed | Missing day added before filing, with attendance evidence | Being on site isn't the same as being on the claim |
| Weekend day worked | Allowance paid as normal; lieu day requested separately through HR/payroll | Lieu time and the allowance don't offset each other |
| Trip crosses month boundary | Split into two dated portions, each against its own month | A single claim dated to the departure month understates the other month |

## Reading the table
Every row pays the same 20,000 KRW domestic daily allowance once a day is properly listed; what differs is which record has to exist before that day is accepted, and by when.

The five-working-day correction window applies across every row in this table, not just the ones that feel urgent.
