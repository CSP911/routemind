# Starting or stopping a payroll allowance

## The steps
1. File the eligibility form with HR, stating the condition and its effective date.
2. HR checks the date against that month's payroll cutoff.
3. If the form clears before cutoff, the change applies to that month's run; if not, it applies to the next one.
4. HR confirms the new amount, or the stop, on the following payslip.

## Where this differs from the cutoff itself
The cutoff date decides which run a change lands in; this procedure is what gets a change to HR in the first place. A late-filed stop is still a valid stop — it just lands a month later than expected.

## The trap
**Filing the stop request is not the same as the allowance actually stopping that month.** Until HR confirms it against the cutoff, the prior amount keeps paying, and any month paid past the real end date has to be recovered rather than simply corrected on the next payslip.

Meal, car and remote-work stipends all move through this same procedure, whichever one is changing.
