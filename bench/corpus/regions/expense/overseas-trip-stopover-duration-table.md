# Stopover length and when it earns its own band

## Duration decides the treatment

Not every layover generates a separate claim line. How long it lasts decides whether it folds into the connecting leg or stands on its own.

| Layover length | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Under 8 hours | Folded into the connecting leg — no separate band, no lodging line |
| 8–24 hours, no overnight hotel booked | Folded into the destination country's band |
| Over 24 hours, or an overnight hotel is booked | Its own band and its own lodging night |

## The trap

**Booking a hotel during a short layover does not, by itself, create a new band.** A hotel booked for a six-hour layover to rest between flights is still folded into the connecting leg — the lodging receipt is evidence of a spend, not proof the layover crossed into its own band. The duration is what matters, not whether a room was taken.

The same logic applies however many stopovers a single itinerary carries, which is where a multi-stop routing starts to look more like the multi-country case than a single detour.
