# Conference fee windows and their evidence

Registration fees don't all arrive with the same paperwork. What the organiser issues depends on the booking channel, and that decides whether a simple receipt is enough.

## Fee type and what it produces

| Registration type | Typical evidence | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Early-bird, booked by card | Tax invoice or itemised card statement | Paid before the trip is approved — see the early-registration rule |
| Standard registration | Tax invoice | Above 30,000 KRW, qualifying evidence is required as usual |
| Group or team rate | Invoice plus attendee list | Mirrors the entertainment rule, not a coincidence |

## The currency trap

**A registration fee billed in USD or EUR converts using the transaction date's rate shown on the card statement, exactly like any other foreign charge — it does not get a separate conference exchange rate.** Treating it as a special case just produces a KRW figure that won't reconcile.

Once the invoice is in hand, filing follows the same evidence rules as any other spend over the threshold.
