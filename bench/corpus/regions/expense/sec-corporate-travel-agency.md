# Corporate travel agency — where to start

The corporate travel agency is the default channel for flights, rail, and hotels booked ahead of a trip. This section holds eight pages covering how a booking is placed, what the agency is and isn't allowed to book, how its service fee is charged, and what happens when a trip has to go outside the agency's catalogue.

## What this part covers
Booking through the agency, the boundary of its mandate across domestic and overseas travel, the service fee that rides on top of each booking, and the approval path for an out-of-policy booking when the agency can't fulfil what the trip needs.

## The common case
A traveller submits a trip request, the agency quotes and books against it, and the service fee lands on the same invoice as the fare. No separate reimbursement claim is needed for the fare itself.

## Where it breaks
**The one thing that catches people out is assuming the agency can book anything the travel policy allows** — its catalogue is narrower than the policy, and a route or vendor outside it has to go through the out-of-policy path with its own sign-off, arranged before the booking, not after.

The pages under this section sit upstream of domestic and overseas trip cases, which pick up once the booking itself is settled.
