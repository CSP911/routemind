# The seven-day window on a disputed charge

## The rule
Raise a charge you don't recognise **within 7 days of the statement showing it.** After that it is treated as your own spending, whatever the outcome would have been if you'd raised it on time.

| Days since the statement | Status |
|---|---|
| Within 7 days | Reviewed as a disputed charge |
| After 7 days | Treated as your own spending — no review |

## Why the line is firm
The issuer's own chargeback window with the merchant is short, and it starts running from the same statement date. A dispute raised on day 9 internally has often already missed the point where the company could recover anything from the merchant side, so there is nothing left to review.

## What to do inside the window
File it the same day you notice, through whichever desk handles card disputes — don't wait for the next reconciliation cycle to raise it, since that alone can push you past the line.

**The seven days count from the statement, not from the trip.** A charge from an overseas leg that only shows up on next month's statement still gets seven days from that posting, not seven days from when you were there.
