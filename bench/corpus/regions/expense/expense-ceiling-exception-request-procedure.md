# Requesting an exception once the ceiling is frozen

Once a team crosses 100% of its annual ceiling, new claims stop clearing automatically. The freeze lifts only for the amount an exception covers — not for the rest of the year.

## Who files it

The team lead requests the exception, not the individual whose claim is stuck. Resubmitting the same claim just bounces it again.

## What has to be in the request

- The expected overage for the rest of the year
- A reason tied to a specific event, not general workload
- Confirmation the spend can't be deferred into next year

**A request without an expected overage figure is bounced, not queued** — the approving desk won't estimate it for you, and the freeze stays on until it's refiled.

## Who signs off

| Size of exception | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000,000 KRW over | Division head |
| 1,000,000–5,000,000 KRW over | Division head and finance jointly |
| Above 5,000,000 KRW over | Finance director |

Approval unfreezes the account for that amount only. A later overage needs a fresh request.

What actually triggers the freeze at 100% is covered separately.
