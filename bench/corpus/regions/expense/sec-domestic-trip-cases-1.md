# Domestic trip cases, sorted by city

This section holds fifteen pages of domestic trip cases organised by destination: which cities carry the higher lodging cap, what a client-site visit in a given city pays when no hotel is booked, and how a trip touching more than one city is worked end to end.

## What this part covers
City-level variation in lodging and allowance, and the trips that string several cities together in one itinerary.

## The common case
A single-city trip books against that city's cap without needing anything from this section beyond the lookup itself.

## Where it breaks
**The one thing that goes wrong here is applying the departure city's cap to the whole trip** — a multi-city itinerary is priced night by night against the city the traveller actually slept in, not against wherever the trip started.

The sister section, sorted by mode, covers the same trips from the transport side rather than the destination side.
