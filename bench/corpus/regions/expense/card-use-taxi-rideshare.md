# Taxi and ride-hailing

## When it's covered
Taxi and ride-hailing fares are covered for travel between sites, late-night work past normal transport hours, and client-facing trips — not the daily commute, which gets rejected if flagged as a repeating commute pattern.

## What the receipt has to show
The app or in-taxi receipt needs the date, time, fare and pickup/drop-off points. Below 30,000 KRW a simple receipt is enough; above it, the app's tax invoice or equivalent is required like any other spend.

## The name on the booking
**A ride booked and paid for under a colleague's ride-hailing account, even for your own trip, produces a receipt in their name — that receipt does not count as your evidence.** Book under your own account, or if that isn't possible, have the fare added to a shared receipt at settlement rather than claiming someone else's.

Fuel and mileage for a personal car run on a completely different basis.
