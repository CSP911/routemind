# Accountability on a shared prepaid card

## One card, several spenders
A prepaid card issued for a short project or a one-off event sometimes gets shared across a small team rather than reissued per person. The corporate card programme tracks spend to a named holder by default; a shared prepaid card does not.

## Who answers for a charge
The person who requested the card from finance is the one who answers for every charge on it, regardless of who actually spent it. **This is the trap** — a team member's undocumented purchase becomes the requester's problem to explain, not the spender's, because the card has only one name attached to it at finance's end.

## What keeps this workable
| Practice | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Log the spender's name against each receipt | Lets the requester reconstruct who spent what |
| One purchase per checkout | Avoids a mixed receipt nobody can attribute |

Without a log, an unattributed charge above 30,000 KRW without qualifying evidence sits against the requester's name for the 2% penalty as well as the missing paperwork.

A card issued to one name for a team's use is a different animal from a multi-user software licence charged to the corporate card — that one stays with one owner throughout, this one doesn't.
