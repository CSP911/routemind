# Finding response deadline

Once the audit desk issues a finding, the clock does not wait for the next close cycle — it runs inside the current one, against the same calendar that governs everything else that month.

## The window

- Findings are issued on the 5th working day of close.
- The claimant and approver have until the **10th working day** to respond.
- An advance still open past its own settlement deadline is flagged automatically and follows this same 10th-working-day window.

**The 10th working day is a hard stop, not a target — a finding still open when close locks on the 11th working day escalates to the team lead whether or not a reply is in progress.** Being in the middle of gathering evidence does not pause the window; file a statement of circumstances to hold the line instead of missing it outright.
