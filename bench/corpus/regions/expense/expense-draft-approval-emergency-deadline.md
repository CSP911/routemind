# How soon an emergency spend has to be regularised

## The window

| Spend type | Deadline to file the emergency draft |
|---|---|
| Card transaction | 5 working days from the charge |
| Cash or personal-card spend | 5 working days from the spend |
| Purchase order issued without a prior quote | 3 working days from issue |

The clock starts on the spend itself, not on when someone notices it was never approved.

**Missing the window doesn't just mean a late draft — past it, the spend is no longer eligible for emergency regularisation at all, and it converts to unauthorised spending regardless of how good the original reason was.** There is no discretion to extend it after the fact; the approver who might have granted the exception in advance has nothing left to approve.

File as soon as the situation allows rather than waiting for the deadline to become a factor — an approver reading a draft two days after a typhoon shut down transport reads it differently to one filed four days in. Filing early also keeps the spend inside the month it was actually incurred in, ahead of the fixed dates that decide which claims land in that close.
