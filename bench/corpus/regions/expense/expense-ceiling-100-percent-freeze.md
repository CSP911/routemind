# Reaching 100% of the ceiling

Once a team's cumulative spend reaches its annual ceiling, new discretionary spend needs an extra layer of sign-off before it can be filed.

## What changes

- New claims for travel, entertainment or discretionary purchases require the division head's pre-approval, filed before the spend happens, in addition to the normal per-claim approval chain.
- Charges already committed before the ceiling was reached — a running subscription, a booked and non-refundable trip, a signed contract — continue to process without additional sign-off.
- The card itself is not blocked. Nothing about reaching 100% touches the per-transaction or monthly card limits; those caps apply regardless of where the team sits against its ceiling.

**Reaching 100% is not the same as the card being suspended, and the two get confused often enough that teams stop swiping when they shouldn't.** Ordinary, already-approved recurring charges keep landing on the card exactly as before — it is only new discretionary spend that needs the extra sign-off.

The forfeiture that follows a ceiling breach at year end is covered under the no-carryover rule.
