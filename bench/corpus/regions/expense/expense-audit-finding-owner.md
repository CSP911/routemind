# Who owns a finding

Three different people can touch a finding, and each has a narrower job than the others.

## The split

- **The audit desk** raises the finding and states the rule it traces back to. It does not decide the outcome — only that a check failed.
- **The claimant's approver** is copied at issuance and can supply missing evidence or authorise a statement of circumstances on the claimant's behalf.
- **The team lead** only enters once a finding escalates past the response window; from there they can accept the 2% penalty on the team's behalf or push the claim back for one more attempt at evidence.

**The audit desk has no discretion to waive a finding — that authority sits with the division head, the same one who signs off a disbursement form or a statement of circumstances.** Asking the audit desk for an exception simply routes the request to someone with no power to grant it.

The disbursement form's own ceiling, separate from anything a finding can touch, is covered on its own page.
