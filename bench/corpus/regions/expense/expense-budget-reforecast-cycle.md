# The reforecast cycle

The annual budget is revisited four times a year, alongside the variance report for March, June, September and December. This is the reforecast: a chance to move budget between months and lines based on what the year has actually looked like so far.

## What carries automatically

The system rolls the unrevised months of the year forward at their original figures. It does not move an underspend from one month into the next — that has to be entered by hand, as a reforecast line, or it simply disappears from the year's plan.

## What needs a manual entry

- Moving budget between line items within a cost centre
- Moving budget between months
- Adjusting the year's total up or down against actuals so far

**An underspend that isn't reforecast is not "saved" for later use — the following month's budget stays exactly as originally filed.** Teams that assume a quiet month buys room in the next one find the room was never actually granted.

The window to submit these entries once a quarter's report is filed is short, and covered separately.
