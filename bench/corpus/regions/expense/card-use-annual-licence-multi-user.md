# Shared licences and the cardholder of record

A licence used across a whole team — a shared design tool, a shared analytics seat — still charges to one person's card, and that person remains the cardholder of record even after the original requester moves teams or leaves the company.

## Tracking usage

Seat-level usage lives in the vendor's own dashboard, not on the card statement. The statement only ever shows one line a year, so reconciling who actually uses the licence has to happen against the vendor's records, not against the expense claim itself.

## The trap

**When the team roster changes mid-year, the charge does not move with it.** The card that paid for the licence stays liable for the full renewal even if half the original team has since left — nothing reassigns it automatically. Someone has to actively request a handover to a new cardholder before the next renewal runs.

That handover request still has to clear inside the same non-renewal notice window that governs any other annual licence, not on its own separate schedule.
