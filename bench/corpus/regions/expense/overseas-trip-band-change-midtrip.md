# A band change mid-trip

A traveller already overseas gets approval to extend into a further country — for example a Tokyo trip (Band A) that picks up an unplanned London leg (Band B) after a client asks for a face-to-face meeting.

## Steps

1. File the itinerary change with your approver as soon as the extra leg is confirmed, before booking the new lodging.
2. Nights already claimed under the original band stand as filed — they are not restated.
3. From the date the new leg starts, lodging and allowance switch to the new band's cap.
4. Attach both approvals (original trip, extension) to the same claim; a second trip number is not raised.

## The trap

**The switch applies from the date of change forward only, not retroactively.** Re-filing the Tokyo nights at the London band because the overall trip "became" a Europe trip is not accepted — those nights were genuinely spent in Band A and are capped accordingly.

A short stop between two such legs raises a separate question — whether it counts as a destination at all.
