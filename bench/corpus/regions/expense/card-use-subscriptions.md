# Subscriptions and renewal

## Putting a subscription on the card
Software, cloud services and paid memberships that recur monthly or yearly may go on the corporate card once the team lead approves the subscription itself, not just the first charge. That approval covers the service, not a fixed number of billing cycles — you don't re-approve every month.

## What renewal actually bills
Most platforms auto-charge the same day each cycle, and the card statement shows only a line item, not an invoice. **A renewal charge above 30,000 KRW with only the statement line as proof is not qualifying evidence** — the tax invoice or receipt has to be pulled from the vendor's billing portal at the time of charge, since most portals only keep it retrievable for a limited window after that.

## Cancelling before it renews
Cancel a subscription before the renewal date rather than after. A charge that lands and is then refunded still has to be reported on the same 5-working-day clock as any other post-settlement refund.

Foreign-billed subscriptions carry one more step: converting the charge and matching the receipt's script.
