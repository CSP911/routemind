# Reimbursement without a card — where to start

The lost-receipt case is the one everyone remembers, but most reimbursement-without-a-card claims have nothing to do with a missing receipt. They're an advance that needs settling, a float that needs replenishing, or an employee who paid for something on a personal card because no company card was in hand.

## What sits under this section

| Situation | What it needs |
|---|---|
| Cash paid up front for a trip or purchase | An advance or a float, settled afterward |
| Small, recurring low-value spend | Petty cash, not a claim per transaction |
| A personal card used for a company expense | The disbursement form |

## The common case

An employee fronts a modest cost on a personal card — a client taxi, a small supply run — and files a disbursement claim with the receipt attached. No card, no advance, just a repayment.

## The trap

**An advance that isn't fully spent still has to be settled to zero — returning the unspent balance is a separate step from filing the claim, and skipping it leaves the advance open indefinitely.**
