# Three days at a client site with no hotel claim

## The trip
A staff member spends three consecutive days at a client's plant two hours away, staying with a relative each night rather than booking a hotel. No lodging line is ever filed.

## What still gets paid
Each day still carries the 20,000 KRW daily allowance and the round-trip rail fare for the days actually travelled. With three days on site, that's three allowance lines and two fare legs — one out, one back — not three of each.

## The trap
**Because there's no lodging claim, the trip claim system doesn't auto-populate anything for these days — the allowance has to be entered manually for each date.** Leaving a middle day blank because "nothing happened" is the most common way a client-site trip comes back short, since the system has no lodging line to infer the missing allowance from.

## Filing it
List every date on site individually, even the ones with no separate transport cost, and attach the itinerary showing why no hotel was booked. That itinerary doubles as the evidence an approver needs before signing off a trip with no lodging receipt attached at all.
