# Conference and seminar fees

## Registering on the card
Registration fees for a conference, seminar or external training run through the card like any supplier invoice, provided the event is approved as part of a trip or the training budget beforehand. Fees above 30,000 KRW need qualifying evidence — normally the registration confirmation with the organiser's tax invoice or an equivalent document attached.

## Bundled with travel, not with entertainment
A conference fee is a training cost, not a client-facing spend, even when the seminar includes a dinner. **Do not fold the registration fee into an entertainment claim to raise the per-head cap** — the two run on separate lines with separate evidence, and mixing them sends the whole claim back.

## Overseas organisers
Many conference organisers are based overseas and issue a receipt with no Korean business registration number at all — expected, and it doesn't block the claim. The receipt still needs the event, the date and the amount clear enough to convert and check.

Where the confirmation is in another script, the usual foreign-language evidence rule applies on top.
