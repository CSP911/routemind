# Which days on an overseas trip pay the daily allowance

## The general rule
The daily allowance is paid for every day the traveller is on the approved itinerary and away from their home base, whether or not there is a meeting scheduled that day. It is not conditional on attendance at an event.

## Table
| Day type | Allowance paid |
|---|---|
| Travel day (departure or return) | Yes |
| Working day with meetings | Yes |
| Rest day between two working legs, still abroad | Yes |
| Stopover night required by the connection | Yes |
| Personal leave attached to the trip | No |
| Days after an early return, ahead of the approved itinerary | No |

## The trap
**A day with nothing on the calendar is not the same as a day off the clock.** A rest day inserted between two client visits still pays the allowance because the traveller remains on the approved itinerary abroad; it is personal leave taken during the trip, not an empty schedule, that stops payment.

Where a trip changes band partway through, this rule decides whether the allowance is paid at all, and the band decides how much.
