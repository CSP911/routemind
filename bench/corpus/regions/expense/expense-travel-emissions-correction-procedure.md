# Correcting a travel emissions entry

## When a correction is needed
A trip's emissions line is built from the mode and distance on the approved itinerary. If that itinerary changes afterward — a flight swapped for rail, a leg added, a distance mis-keyed at booking — the emissions entry does not update itself. Someone has to go in and fix it.

## The steps
1. Confirm the correct mode and distance against what actually happened, not the original booking.
2. Open the trip's claim record and edit the emissions line directly. The fare and allowance lines are untouched by this — it is a separate field, not a recalculation of the claim.
3. Note the reason for the change in the comment field. That note is what gets checked later, not the number by itself.
4. Submit the correction before the cutoff that closes the year's reported figure. After that cutoff, the entry stands for the year even if the trip is reclassified later.

**A correction filed after the itinerary is settled but before the year's figure locks still counts as on time — it is the annual cutoff that matters, not the claim's own monthly close.** Holding a correction back to bundle it with the next monthly close is the usual way it misses the window.

A multi-leg trip is corrected leg by leg, the same way a two-stop domestic trip keeps its lodging caps separate by city rather than blended into one figure.
