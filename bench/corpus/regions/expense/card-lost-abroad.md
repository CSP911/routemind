# Lost or stolen card while travelling

## Call the international line, not the domestic one
Block the card through the issuer's international hotline. The domestic Korean card desk number printed on the back often will not connect once you are outside the country, and every hour it stays open is an hour someone else can spend on it. Note the time of the call and the reference number the operator gives you — you'll need both later.

## A replacement will not catch up with the trip
Couriering a new card to a hotel two time zones away rarely works before you're due home. File a statement of the circumstances as a draft rather than waiting for a card that isn't coming, and keep travelling on whatever backup you have — a personal card, or cash.

## Claiming the gap back
Spend on a personal card or in cash while the corporate one is blocked is claimed exactly like any other cost on the trip: a simple receipt under 30,000 KRW, qualifying evidence above it. **The block itself is not evidence of anything you bought after it** — each receipt still has to stand on its own, the same as if the card had never been lost.

A card frozen for suspected fraud rather than lost outright follows a different path — see the next section.
