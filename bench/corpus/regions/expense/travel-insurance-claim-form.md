# What a travel insurance claim needs to include

Filing the claim with the insurer is a separate step from reporting the incident to the company. The incident procedure gets help moving and gets the company informed; the claim form is what actually gets money back, and it wants its own paperwork.

## What every claim needs
- The approved trip itinerary showing the dates and destination covered by the policy
- A copy of the boarding pass or ticket for the leg affected
- Original receipts for anything being claimed back

## Category-specific documents

| Claim type | Extra document required |
|---|---|
| Medical treatment | Hospital or clinic invoice, itemised, plus the diagnosis |
| Evacuation | The evacuation provider's own report, not a self-written summary |
| Baggage | A police report or airline loss report, filed locally |
| Cancellation | Written proof of the reason — airline notice, medical certificate, or equivalent |

**A stolen or damaged bag needs a local police report, not the incident report you filed with the company.** The company report gets you support and records the event internally; it does not satisfy the insurer, which will decline a baggage claim filed without one. File the police report before leaving the country if at all possible — it is far harder to obtain once home.

Once the paperwork above is assembled, how long you have left to submit it is covered separately.
