# Tagging window for a mid-trip exchange-rate move

## The window

When the won moves partway through a trip, each charge still converts at its own transaction date's rate — but the traveller has to tag which charges fall before the move and which fall after when the claim is filed. **Raise the tagging within 5 working days of returning.** After that, the system reconciles the whole claim against the settlement-date rate instead of each charge's own date.

## Why it matters

A rate move of even a few percent, applied to the wrong side of a multi-day trip, changes the KRW value of every lodging night and allowance day after it. Left untagged, the claim doesn't fail — it just settles at a rate that may be less favourable than the one that actually applied on the day of each charge, and there's no second pass once the 5 days have run.

Tagging by charge date is what lets a single trip legitimately show two different rates for the same currency, which is the same mechanic a multi-country itinerary relies on when it crosses back into a country it already left.
