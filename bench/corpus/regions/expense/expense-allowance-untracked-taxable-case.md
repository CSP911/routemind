# A worked case: an allowance that outlived its condition

## What happened
An employee received the own-car allowance for regular business use of a personal vehicle. The car was sold in March. No change form was filed. The allowance kept paying at the same flat monthly rate through June, when a routine payroll audit cross-checked allowance recipients against vehicle registration records.

## What the audit found
Four months of payments had gone out after the underlying condition — owning and using the car — no longer existed.

## What it cost
**The correction did not stop at the amount over the 200,000 KRW tax-free ceiling; it reclassified the entire four months of payments as taxable income**, because none of it qualified as a car allowance once the car was gone. The employee owed the shortfall in withholding plus the usual year-end interest treatment, not just tax on the excess.

The same exposure applies to a remote-work stipend paid past the date an arrangement actually ended.
