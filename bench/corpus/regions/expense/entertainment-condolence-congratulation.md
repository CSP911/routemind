# Condolence and congratulation payments

A payment made on a client's or partner's bereavement, wedding, or similar occasion sits outside the meal and gift caps entirely.

## The cap

Condolence and congratulation payments up to 200,000 KRW are evidenced by a copy of the invitation or the notice — a funeral notice, a wedding invitation — rather than a receipt, because there usually isn't one to produce.

## What counts as one payment

The 200,000 KRW line is per occasion, not per item. A cash condolence payment plus a wreath sent for the same funeral are one occasion and share the single 200,000 KRW ceiling between them.

**Sending both a cash gift and a physical item for the same event and filing them as two separate claims does not double the cap.** The second claim gets rejected once it is matched against the first, and the person who filed second carries the correction.

Where the total genuinely needs to exceed 200,000 KRW — a senior client relationship, for instance — that needs approval before payment, the same way any over-cap entertainment spend does.

This is the narrowest of the entertainment caps and the one most often confused with the gift cap sitting next to it.
