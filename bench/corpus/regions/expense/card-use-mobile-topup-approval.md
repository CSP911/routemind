# Who approves a shared-device mobile top-up

An individual's own phone top-up is a simple case — it's either your registered company line or it's blocked. A company-owned SIM used in a shared device (a field tablet, a pooled hotspot) works differently, because no single person's name sits on the plan.

## Approval routing

- Up to 50,000 KRW a month: team lead approves, tied to the device inventory tag rather than a person.
- Above that: division head approval, with a note on which project the device supports.

## Who's accountable

**Approval sits with whoever requested the device, not whoever happens to be holding it when the top-up posts.** If the device changes hands mid-month without the inventory record being updated, the charge is queried against the original requester first, and it's on them to redirect it.

A personal-line top-up is a much shorter conversation — no shared device, no routing question, just a name check.
