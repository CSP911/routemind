# The monthly own-car allowance

Staff who use a personal car for routine work purposes — visiting sites in the ordinary course of a role, not a specific approved trip — draw a 200,000 KRW own-car allowance through payroll. It is set once, by eligibility, and paid every month without a form.

## The tax-free ceiling

Like the meal allowance, this is non-taxable up to 200,000 KRW a month. As long as no additional car-related payment is added on top through payroll, the full amount stays untaxed.

## What it does not replace

**Receiving the monthly car allowance does not exempt a domestic trip from the separate 300 KRW per km mileage claim.** The allowance covers the general cost of having a car available for work; the per-km rate covers a specific, approved business trip and is claimed on the mileage form with start point, end point and distance, exactly as it would be for someone with no car allowance at all.

The two payments track different things and both apply when both conditions are met — treating the monthly allowance as if it already covers a trip's mileage just means the mileage claim goes unfiled, and unfiled money doesn't get paid retroactively.

The remote-work stipend looks similar on the payslip but carries none of this ceiling.
