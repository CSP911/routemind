# Same-day return that overran

A trip booked and approved as a same-day return sometimes does not come back same day — the last train is missed, a meeting runs long, the return leg is cancelled by weather. The traveller ends up needing a hotel that was never part of the approved plan.

## How it is handled

1. Book the hotel and keep the receipt as normal.
2. Note the reason for the overrun in the expense report — a same-day return that becomes overnight is not self-explanatory from a receipt alone.
3. The lodging cap still applies: 80,000 KRW a night, or 100,000 KRW in Seoul or Busan.
4. If the amount is small enough that a disbursement form would normally apply, the division head's roughly 100,000 KRW discretion still holds.

**An overrun does not need approval requested in advance, because there was no advance — but it does need the reason recorded at claim time, not assumed from context.** A hotel receipt with no explanation on a trip booked as same-day return looks like a booking error rather than a change in circumstances, and gets queried accordingly.

The daily allowance is unaffected either way; it is paid per day travelled regardless of how the day ended.
