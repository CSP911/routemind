# A client meal that turned out internal

A meal was booked as client entertainment: venue reserved, client invited, attendee list drafted in advance. The client cancelled an hour before, and the team went ahead with the reservation on its own.

## What changed

Once no client attendee is present, the meal is no longer entertainment. It gets reclassified as an internal team meal, and the entertainment cap of 50,000 KRW per head no longer applies — internal meals of this kind carry their own guideline of 15,000 KRW per head, well below the entertainment line.

## What that means for the claim

The attendee list requirement that applies above 30,000 KRW for entertainment drops away, since there is no client to list. But the tighter internal cap now applies instead, and a bill that comfortably cleared the entertainment cap can fail the internal one.

**Reclassifying the meal is not just a label change on the claim form — it moves the ceiling down, and a bill that was fine as entertainment can come back over cap as an internal meal.** The approver has to see both figures: what the bill was, and which cap it now sits under.

Where the client only partially cancelled — one attendee still came — the meal stays entertainment and keeps its original cap.
