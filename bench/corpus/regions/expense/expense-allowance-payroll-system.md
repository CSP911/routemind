# What the payroll system does with a standing allowance

## What runs automatically
Once a car allowance, meal allowance, or remote-work stipend is set up, the system carries the same flat amount into every payroll run without anyone re-entering it. It does not check a mileage log, a vehicle registration, or an attendance record — it simply repeats the last confirmed instruction.

## What needs a manual entry
Starting, stopping, or changing the amount all need someone in HR to enter the change before that month's cutoff. Nothing about the employee's actual circumstances feeds the system on its own.

## The trap
**The system has no way to detect that eligibility has lapsed — it keeps paying a stopped condition until a human tells it to stop.** A car sold, a remote arrangement ended, or a role that no longer qualifies will all keep drawing the allowance indefinitely if the stop request is never filed.

What that unfiled stop costs once it is caught is a recovery problem, not a system one.
