# Receipt format and reissue cases

This set covers evidence that exists but arrived in an unusable form: a receipt printed in a language the desk can't read, or an e-receipt that was never sent, was sent to the wrong address, or needs a formal reissue from the merchant.

## What it covers

Translation requirements for foreign-language receipts, the reissue request path when an e-receipt merchant's portal has already dropped the record, and what happens when the original merchant itself has since closed.

## The common case

A booking confirmation that generated no automatic receipt, chased weeks later through a merchant support inbox that may or may not still monitor it.

**A reissued e-receipt does not reset the submission deadline** — the clock runs from the date of purchase, not the date the reissue finally arrives, so a slow merchant is the claimant's problem to escalate early, not a reason to file late.

Where the merchant can't produce any version of the document at all, the case moves to the merchant-status track rather than staying here.
