# Splitting one purchase across two cards

## Not the same as two receipts
Paying for one purchase partly on the corporate card and partly on a personal card — because the limit was close, or the card was declined mid-transaction — is different from two separate taps at the same merchant. It's one purchase, two payment methods, and one piece of evidence covering the full amount.

## What has to reconcile
The receipt shows the total price, but only the corporate-card portion is being claimed. **The claim has to show the split clearly — the receipt total, the corporate-card amount, and the personal-card amount paid separately — otherwise the claimed amount won't match the evidence attached, and it gets kicked back for clarification.**

## Where the threshold applies
The 30,000 KRW line is judged on the receipt total, not the corporate-card share alone — a 40,000 KRW meal split 20,000 / 20,000 across two cards still needs qualifying evidence for the full amount, exactly as it would on one card.

This is the mixed-payment cousin of two taps at the same merchant — the aggregation logic is the same, only the card changes partway through.
