# Rail preference for the emissions entry

Where a domestic leg has a rail option, the emissions entry always logs what that leg would have cost in emissions by rail, alongside whatever mode was actually booked. This applies whether or not a mode-choice approval let the leg fly.

## Why the two never merge
The mode-choice approval decides whether the fare is reimbursed at the air rate or the rail rate. It has no bearing on the emissions entry, which always carries the rail comparison figure as a baseline regardless of the approval.

## The trap
**A valid mode-choice approval that lets a leg fly does not remove the requirement to file the rail comparison line.** Skipping it doesn't block the claim from closing, but it surfaces as a finding the next time the claim sits in the audit sample — the fare was properly approved, the emissions entry was simply never filled in.

## Where it doesn't apply
Jeju and other island destinations, where no rail option exists at all, carry only the actual mode's figure — there's nothing to compare against.
