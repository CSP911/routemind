# Annual emissions figure lockdown

The company's annual travel emissions figure is compiled from every trip claim that closed within the calendar year, using whichever mode and distance sat on the claim at the point it closed.

## The cutoff
- Trip-level entries can be corrected up to **10 working days after the trip claim closes**.
- The annual figure itself locks on **31 January**, covering the prior calendar year.
- **A correction filed after the figure locks does not retroactively adjust the reported number** — it is logged as a note against the following year's figure instead, not folded back into the one already published.

## Why the gap matters
A claim that closes in December has very little runway before the January lock. A mode-choice change confirmed late can miss the 10-day window entirely if the correction sits unfiled.
