# Domestic trip cases at a glance

Most domestic trips are a straightforward fare, a lodging receipt under the city cap, and the daily allowance. The cases below are where that straightforward pattern breaks down, and each is handled differently.

## Where to look

| Situation | See |
|---|---|
| Choosing rail or air on a short leg | Rail vs air on marginal domestic legs |
| A trip touching more than one lodging tier | A domestic trip with two overnight stops |
| No hotel spent at all | A client site visit with no hotel booked |
| A same-day return that didn't come back same day | Same-day return that overran |
| A booked fare that fell through | A cancelled leg |
| Driving your own car | Own-car trips and tolls |

**None of these change the underlying caps** — 80,000/100,000 KRW lodging, 20,000 KRW daily allowance, 300 KRW per km. What changes is which figure actually applies to a trip that didn't go the way it was planned, and what has to be recorded to show why.

The same 30,000 KRW evidence line that governs every other expense still governs the fare and lodging receipts on these trips; nothing about a domestic case relaxes it.
