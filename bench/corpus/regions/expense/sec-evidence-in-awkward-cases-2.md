# Merchant status and VAT-exemption cases

This set covers merchants that are structurally unable to produce the document a normal claim relies on: sole traders below the registration threshold, VAT-exempt categories, and private individuals paid directly for a service.

## What it covers

Which seller categories are recognised as VAT-exempt, what a VAT-exempt merchant issues instead of a tax invoice, and the minimum fields a claim still needs when the counterparty is a private individual rather than a business.

## The common case

A small local vendor at an overseas site visit who simply has no invoicing system, paid in cash against a handwritten receipt.

**VAT-exempt does not mean evidence-exempt** — a receipt from an exempt or private seller still has to carry the payer, the amount, the date and a description, or it's treated as unevidenced regardless of why the merchant couldn't do better.

These cases turn up often enough in sampling that the audit desk treats a bare cash slip as the first thing to check at close.
