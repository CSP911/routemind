# A float that wasn't fully spent

A team drew a 200,000 KRW float ahead of a three-day site visit, covering small vendor payments the card couldn't reach. The trip ran under budget: 140,000 KRW was spent and evidenced, 60,000 KRW came back.

## What happened

1. Actual spend was filed with receipts, qualifying evidence attached above 30,000 KRW as usual.
2. The unspent 60,000 KRW was returned to the custodian within the 7-working-day settlement window.
3. Finance logged the return against the original float, closing it at the actual spend figure.

## The trap

**The leftover cash was returned on time, but only because someone remembered the float existed.** A float left open past the settlement window, even with the cash sitting untouched in a drawer, is treated the same as an unsettled personal advance — deducted at the next payroll run rather than simply requested back.

The fastest way to avoid this is to settle the float the same day spend stops, not the day before the deadline.
