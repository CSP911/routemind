# Cancelling a subscription before it renews

A recurring subscription on the corporate card does not stop on its own. Cancelling the vendor account and stopping the card charge are two separate actions, and only one of them is guaranteed to happen the moment you ask.

## Steps

1. Find the renewal date on the original invoice or in the card statement's recurring-charge history.
2. Submit the cancellation to the vendor with enough notice for their own cutoff — most require several business days before the billing date.
3. Get the cancellation confirmed in writing before the renewal date, not after.

## If the charge lands anyway

**Cancelling the vendor account does not remove the card from file, so a renewal can still be charged after you've cancelled.** That charge is treated as a new spend needing its own evidence, not as an error to be waived outright. If the vendor later refunds it, report the refund the same way as any other post-settlement refund, within 5 working days.

A licence renewed once a year follows the same logic, just on a longer clock.
