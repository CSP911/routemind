# The 2% penalty on unevidenced spend

## When it applies
| Spend | Evidence held | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 30,000 KRW | Simple receipt | Accepted, no penalty |
| Over 30,000 KRW | Qualifying evidence | Accepted, no penalty |
| Over 30,000 KRW | Simple receipt only | Accepted, 2% penalty on the amount |

## Who carries it
The penalty is charged against the amount of the individual spend line, not the whole claim it sits in — one uncovered dinner in an otherwise clean trip claim only costs 2% of that dinner. It is deducted from the claimant, not the department budget.

## The trap
**Getting qualifying evidence after the penalty has already been applied does not reverse it.** The 2% is triggered at settlement against what was attached at the time; a tax invoice that turns up the following week reduces nothing retroactively. This is why filing a statement of circumstances before settlement, not after, is what actually avoids the charge.

The threshold behind all of this — 30,000 KRW — is the same one that governs entertainment evidence and the ceiling on a simple receipt elsewhere in this section.
