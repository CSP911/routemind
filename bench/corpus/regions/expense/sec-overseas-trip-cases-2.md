# Overseas trip cases: sorting the case set

Thirteen cases sit under this section, grouped by what makes the claim non-standard rather than by destination. Two trips to the same country can land in different cases; two trips to different countries can land in the same one.

## The families

| Family | What triggers it |
|---|---|
| Routing | A second country, a stopover, a leg that drives instead of flies |
| Timing | A band change, a trip that starts before a rate move |
| Money | An exchange-rate shift landing mid-trip, currency mismatches across legs |

## The common case

Most claims never need this list at all — one country, one band, no timing complication. These cases exist for the ones that do.

## The trap

**Picking a case because it matches the itinerary rather than what actually changed: crossing a border on its own doesn't put a claim in the "band change" case — only a border crossing that moves the band does.**

## Closing

Read the case whose trigger matches your trip, then check the band-and-country grid above if you're unsure which band a leg falls under. Trips that also carried a prepaid hotel deposit will need the prepaid-card pages as well.
