# Replenishing the petty cash float

## When to top up
Request a replenishment once the box is down to receipts rather than cash — don't wait for it to hit zero, since a stalled float leaves the team fronting spend out of pocket in the meantime.

## Steps
1. Count what's left and total the receipts collected since the last top-up.
2. File a replenishment request for exactly that spent amount, not a round-up to a convenient figure.
3. Attach every receipt the float paid out, in the order they were disbursed.
4. Submit to finance for the cash drawdown.
5. Log the new balance against the float's running total before any of it goes back out.

**Topping up to a round number instead of the amount actually spent is what breaks the float.** The difference sits unexplained in the log until an audit surfaces it, and by then nobody remembers which receipt it belongs to.

The per-withdrawal and monthly ceiling on what the float can pay for in the first place live on the petty cash limit page, not here — this is only about keeping the box's balance honest once it's running.
