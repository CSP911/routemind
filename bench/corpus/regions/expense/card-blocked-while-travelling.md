# Card blocked mid-trip by fraud monitoring

## It is not the same as a lost card
Foreign spending, especially several charges in one day in a country you don't usually visit, sometimes trips the issuer's own fraud monitoring. The card is not lost or stolen — it simply stops working, often without warning, and you find out at the point of sale.

## Confirm and unblock
Call the international hotline and confirm your identity; if the block is a false positive the issuer can usually lift it on that call. **Do not report this as a lost card** — reporting it that way cancels the card outright and starts a replacement you don't need, instead of a five-minute unblock.

## If it can't be lifted immediately
Some blocks require a manual review that outlasts the call. Treat the gap the same as a lost card: spend on a backup, keep receipts, and file a statement of the circumstances if the review runs past your return date.

Whether the card was blocked by the bank or genuinely lost, what you did with money in the gap still needs its own evidence.
