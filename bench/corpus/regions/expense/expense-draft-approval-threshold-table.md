# Draft threshold by spend type

## When a draft is required

| Spend type | Draft required above |
|---|---|
| Single card transaction | 1,000,000 KRW |
| Purchase order raised without a supplier quote on file | 500,000 KRW |
| Cash or personal-card spend awaiting reimbursement | 300,000 KRW |

These are separate from the card's own per-transaction cap and from the flat quote threshold that decides whether a PO needs a quote at all. **Staying under the card's per-transaction limit does not mean a draft is unnecessary — the draft threshold and the card cap are two different lines, and a purchase can clear one while still tripping the other.**

## Who signs it

The draft goes to the same approver who would sign a cash advance of the same size — staff-level spend up to a team lead, anything larger up to a division head. There is no separate approval chain just for drafts.

## What it doesn't cover

A draft is about getting ahead of a spend, not about excusing missing evidence afterward. A purchase that later can't produce a receipt still needs a disbursement form or a statement of circumstances, regardless of whether it started with a draft.
