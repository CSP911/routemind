# The monthly meal allowance

Every staff member draws a 200,000 KRW meal allowance through payroll each month, whether they eat at their desk, at home, or on the road on an ordinary working day. It is not tied to any receipt and does not require a claim.

## The tax-free ceiling

The allowance is non-taxable up to 200,000 KRW a month. Since the paid amount and the ceiling are the same figure, the allowance stays entirely tax-free as long as it isn't topped up separately — which is exactly where the trouble starts.

## Meals claimed on top

**A lunch receipt expensed on an ordinary office day is not a separate, stackable benefit — the cost it covers is already paid through the allowance riding on your pay.** Submitting it anyway either gets reversed as a duplicate, or if it slips through, pushes that month's meal-related pay over the tax-free ceiling and the excess is taxed as ordinary income.

This does not apply to the 20,000 KRW daily allowance paid on a travel day — that covers a different day type and stacks with the monthly meal allowance without conflict. The distinction is the day, not the amount: travelling days draw both; an ordinary day at your usual desk draws only the one that's already in your pay.

The own-car allowance runs on the same non-taxable structure — and the same trap, in a different shape.
