# What happens to an unused prepaid card balance

A prepaid card is issued against a specific purpose and end date. Whatever's left on it once that date passes doesn't roll over to the next project or sit there for someone to spend informally.

## What has to happen
- The holder reports the remaining balance to finance within 5 working days of the reconciliation end date
- Finance nets the balance against the amount originally loaded, and the difference is what the department actually spent
- Physical prepaid cards with a returnable balance are handed back; balances are drawn down electronically where the provider supports it

## The trap
**An unused balance that goes unreported past the 5-day window is treated as undisclosed spending against the holder, not a rounding error finance chases up on its own.** The card doesn't expire the balance automatically, so silence reads as spent, not forgotten.

## What doesn't count as unused
A balance already earmarked against an approved top-up request that hasn't cleared yet isn't unused — it's pending, and follows the top-up timeline instead.

Where the card was tied to a single online vendor rather than general spend, check the single-use number split before assuming there is a balance to report at all.
