# Who sets and monitors the ceiling

Three different people touch the ceiling, and they are not interchangeable.

## The split

- **Budget desk (finance)** sets each team's annual ceiling during the Q4 planning cycle, using the headcount band as of that date. This is a once-a-year action, not something finance revisits when a team's spending pattern changes mid-year.
- **Team lead** monitors usage monthly, using the same actual-versus-budget report filed for the variance overview, and is the one who has to notice the 80% mark before the system flags it.
- **Division head** approves any exception once a team reaches 100% — this is a separate sign-off from the one that approves the underlying trip, purchase or entertainment claim.

**A team lead cannot approve their own team's exception past 100%, even at team lead grade** — the ceiling sits above the normal per-claim approval band, so it always escalates to the division head regardless of the amount of the individual claim.

Setting the ceiling and reforecasting the quarterly budget are separate processes; the reforecast cycle covers how the latter moves during the year.
