# Taxi fares outside standard hours

A taxi receipt on its own only proves a ride happened, not why. For fares between 22:00 and 06:00, that gap gets checked more closely, because this is also when personal errands most often slip onto the corporate card.

## What has to accompany the receipt

For a ride in this window, add one line stating the business reason — a late finish, an early flight, a client drop-off — alongside the usual booking under your own name.

## The pattern that gets flagged

**A late-night fare that repeats to the same home address, week after week, is read as a commuting subsidy, not business travel, and is rejected even with a valid receipt attached.** One or two such rides after a genuinely late finish are fine; a standing pattern is not.

Fuel and parking follow a different logic again — actual receipts during the day, mileage only when you're driving your own car.
