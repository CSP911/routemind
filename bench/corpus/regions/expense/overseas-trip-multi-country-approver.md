# Who signs off the band split on a multi-country trip

## The check
Before a multi-country claim is filed, the travel desk confirms the night-by-night band allocation against the approved itinerary — which nights fall in which country, and which band each one carries. This is a check on the split, not a re-approval of the trip itself.

## What the desk does and does not do
The travel desk verifies that claimed nights match the boarding passes and hotel folios attached, and that no night is counted twice or left off. It does not decide which band a country belongs to — that table is fixed — and it does not adjust the cap for a traveller who books above it.

## Escalation
Where the itinerary changed after departure — an added country, a dropped leg — the travel desk's sign-off follows the mid-trip band-change rules, not the original approval, and a corrected claim goes back through the same desk before filing.

## The trap
**The travel desk's confirmation is not the same approval that authorised the trip budget.** A claim that clears the band-split check can still be rejected by the approver if the total exceeds the budget the trip was originally approved for; the two checks run separately and both have to pass.

The same desk fields questions on stopover nights, since those often sit at the boundary between two bands.
