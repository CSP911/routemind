# A finding worked through close

A team member paid for a 45,000 KRW client dinner in two taps on the corporate card, each under 30,000 KRW, at the same restaurant on the same night.

## What happened

- The card feed reconciled both charges on the 1st working day of the following month.
- The sample pulled the claim on the 3rd working day, flagged as a possible split payment.
- The finding was issued on the 5th working day: two receipts at one merchant, same day, treated as a single 45,000 KRW spend requiring qualifying evidence.
- The claimant had only simple receipts for both taps; no tax invoice had been requested at the time.
- The approver filed a statement of circumstances on the 7th working day rather than let the window close unanswered.
- Reissue through that route took 4 working days, landing just inside the 10th-working-day deadline.

**The finding closed because the statement was filed before the window ran out, not because the underlying receipts improved — a statement filed after the 10th working day would have escalated regardless of what it eventually produced.** Timing decided the outcome as much as the paperwork did.
