# Reforecast submission window

Once the variance report for a reforecast month — March, June, September or December — is filed, there is a **3 working day window** to submit a revised forecast for the remaining months of the year.

## Why the window is short

The consolidated group budget is rebuilt from every team's reforecast right after that quarter closes, and it has to be built once, not patched line by line as teams trickle their entries in over the following weeks.

## What happens if it's missed

**Missing the window does not mean the reforecast is late — it means there isn't one.** The original figures for the remaining months carry forward unchanged for the rest of the year, and the next chance to revise them is the following quarter's report.

This is separate from the monthly variance report's own deadline, which runs every month regardless of whether that month carries a reforecast.
