# Who writes the variance explanation

The team lead who owns the budget line writes the explanation, not the person who happened to submit the expense claim that caused it. A trip claimed by a junior staff member still gets explained by the team lead whose budget absorbed it.

## Where finance sits

Finance closes the ledger, consolidates every team's report, and checks that each line over the threshold carries a note. **Finance does not judge whether the cause given is a good one — that's the division head's job at sign-off, not part of consolidation.** A note that says "vendor invoiced early" is accepted at consolidation even if it later turns out incomplete; it comes back at sign-off instead.

## Where the division head sits

Above the escalation threshold, the division head has to confirm the explanation before the report goes in. This is a check on the reason given, not a re-approval of the spend itself — the spend was already approved when the claim was filed.
