# Preparing the monthly variance report

The report is filed by the team lead, not finance — finance closes the ledger and consolidates what every team submits, but the line-by-line read of what happened belongs to whoever owns the budget.

## Steps

1. Pull the actuals once the month closes; they are available from the general ledger by the second working day of the following month.
2. Match each actual line against its budget line, not just the cost-centre total.
3. For any line that crosses the threshold, draft a short explanation naming the cause — an invoice, a headcount change, a trip that overran.
4. Submit the report, with explanations attached, by the **5th working day** of the following month.
5. Division head sign-off is required before submission for any line beyond the escalation threshold.

**A report filed on time with the threshold lines left blank is treated as not filed.** Finance returns it rather than consolidating a gap, which pushes the whole team's submission past the deadline it was trying to meet.

The bands that decide which lines need a note, and which need sign-off, are set out separately.
