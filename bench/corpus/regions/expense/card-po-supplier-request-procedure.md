# Requesting a purchase order for a card-averse supplier

Some suppliers won't run a card transaction at all and want a PO number before they'll confirm a price. Here's how to get one issued without stalling the purchase.

## Steps
1. Confirm with the supplier that a PO is genuinely required — not just their default preference — and get their business registration number.
2. Collect whatever quote the amount requires before requesting the PO.
3. File the purchase order request form with the supplier details, quote reference, amount and cost centre attached.
4. Route it to the approver for your amount band. A PO above your own approval limit needs sign-off one level up, same as the card.
5. Once approved, the PO number goes to the supplier. Only then do they invoice — the company pays by bank transfer, not the card.
6. Match the supplier's invoice against the PO and the quote when it lands, before it's passed for payment.

**Ordering from the supplier before the PO is approved leaves the purchase unauthorised, even if the amount would have cleared easily on its own.** The PO has to exist before the order does, not after.

This is the same discipline the quote threshold enforces on the card side — the paperwork comes first, the spend second.
