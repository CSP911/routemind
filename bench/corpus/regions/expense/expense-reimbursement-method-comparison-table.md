# Comparing the ways to get paid back without a card

## Side by side
| Route | Ceiling | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Disbursement form | Roughly 100,000 KRW, division head's discretion | Same claim cycle |
| Cash advance | Per-request band by rank, up to 3,000,000 KRW | Released before the spend, settled after |
| Standing float | Set at issue, no per-use ceiling below it | Reconciled and topped up monthly |
| Personal card reimbursement | Same 30,000 KRW evidence line as any claim | Next payroll cycle |

## The trap
**An advance is not free money against a ceiling — it still has to be settled in full against receipts within the settlement window, exactly like the float.** Treating the per-request band as a spending allowance rather than a draw against a later reconciliation is what leaves an advance sitting as outstanding long after the trip that justified it is over.

Which route fits a given spend usually turns on timing rather than size: a disbursement form fits a receipt you'll never get, an advance fits a cost you already know is coming.
