# Own-car mileage claim fields

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Date | Date of the leg driven |
| Route | Start point and end point, city to city |
| Start / end odometer | Both readings, not just the total |
| Distance (km) | End minus start — this is what the rate multiplies |
| Rate | 300 KRW per km, fixed |
| Tolls | Actual cost, receipt attached |
| Total | Mileage (distance × 300) plus tolls |

Tolls are claimed separately on top of the per-km rate — the rate covers fuel and general wear, not road charges, so the two lines are never merged into one figure.

**A toll receipt under 30,000 KRW is fine as a simple receipt, but several tolls on one trip that add up past 30,000 KRW at the same operator are treated as a single spend for evidence purposes**, the same as any other same-merchant total — qualifying evidence is then required for the toll line, even though the mileage line itself never needs a receipt.

Fuel itself is never claimed on top of the per-km rate; that choice between fuel-on-card and mileage is made once for the trip, not leg by leg.
