# How long a quote stays valid before purchase

A quote collected to clear the threshold doesn't stay good indefinitely. **A quote older than 60 days at the time of purchase is treated as expired, even if the supplier never withdrew it** — prices move, and procurement won't release a PO or accept a card claim against a stale quote.

## The window
- Quote obtained and purchase made within 60 days: accepted as-is.
- Quote older than 60 days: request a refreshed quote before proceeding, even if the price hasn't changed on paper.

## Why it matters at approval
The approver checking your PO or claim looks at the quote's date, not just its presence. A quote attached but past the window sends the request back rather than through.

## What to do if the purchase slips
If approval or supplier lead time pushes the purchase past 60 days from the original quote, get the supplier to reconfirm the price in writing rather than resubmitting the same document. That reconfirmation is what keeps the file current.

This window sits alongside the approval band — a quote can be valid and still need a fresh signature if the amount crossed a level in the meantime.
