# Crossing 80% of the ceiling

The monthly report system tracks cumulative spend against the annual ceiling automatically and flags the month a team's total crosses 80%.

## Steps

1. The system attaches an 80% flag to that month's actual-versus-budget report when it's generated.
2. The team lead reviews remaining months against remaining balance and estimates whether the team will land under, at, or over the ceiling by year end.
3. If the projection shows the team landing over, the team lead sends a short note to the division head before the next month's claims are filed — projected overage, cause, and whether spend can be deferred.
4. If the projection shows the team landing under or at the ceiling, no note is required; the flag is informational only at this stage.

**The 80% flag does not pause or restrict spending by itself** — claims continue to be approved normally at this stage. The only hard change comes at 100%, not at 80%; treating the flag as a spending freeze delays claims that don't need to be delayed.

What actually changes once the ceiling is reached is covered separately.
