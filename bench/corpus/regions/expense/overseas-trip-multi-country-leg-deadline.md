# Reporting window for an added country leg

## The window

Adding a country to an itinerary after a trip has already been approved is not automatically picked up by the claim system. **Report the added leg to your approver within 3 working days of the change**, so the night-by-night band allocation can be updated before the trip is filed.

## What happens if it's missed

Past that window, the claim defaults to the band split from the original approved itinerary — the added country's nights are folded into whichever band was already on file, rather than claimed at their own rate. Fixing this after filing means resubmitting the whole claim for reallocation, not just adding a line.

## The trap

**A confirmed flight or hotel booking in the new country is not the same as reporting the change.** The booking sits in the traveller's own records; the approver only sees it once it's logged against the trip, and the 3-day window runs from the change itself, not from when the claim is eventually filed.

Once the added leg is confirmed, each night is allocated its own band exactly as it would be on any other multi-country itinerary.
