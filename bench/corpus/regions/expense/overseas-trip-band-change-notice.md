# Reporting an itinerary change that moves a band

## What triggers it
An overseas trip that adds an unplanned country, extends into a higher-band region, or drops a leg entirely changes the band for every night from the date of the change forward. Nights already spent under the original band are not reopened.

## The deadline
Tell your approver **within 3 working days** of the change becoming known, before you file the claim. Reported inside that window, the system re-splits the claim at the correct date automatically. Reported after filing, the whole claim goes back for manual correction, and allowance already paid at the old band is offset against the new one rather than topped up.

## The trap
**Silence is read as the original itinerary standing.** If the reporting window passes and the claim is filed as though the trip never changed band, the excess or shortfall is caught at audit rather than at approval, and the traveller carries the correction personally regardless of who booked the change.

Where the change overlaps two bands rather than replacing one with another, the nightly split follows the same rule as any multi-country trip.
