# Monthly parking passes versus per-visit parking

Metered, per-visit parking is straightforward — pay on the card, keep the receipt, done. A monthly pass is not, because it covers days you may not have used the car for company business at all.

## The two cases

- **Per-visit parking**: charge it directly, same as fuel. No apportionment needed.
- **Monthly pass**: only claimable in full if the vehicle is used exclusively for company travel that month. Otherwise the business-use share must be declared in advance and only that share is charged.

## Where mileage still applies

**Buying fuel on the card and claiming the 300 KRW-per-km mileage rate for the same trip are mutually exclusive — the rate only applies when you use your own car and no fuel receipt is submitted.** Mixing the two on one trip gets the mileage claim rejected, not just the duplicate portion.

Mobile costs sit outside this entirely — no mileage equivalent, no monthly proration.
