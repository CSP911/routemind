# Lost card and misuse

## If it is lost
1. **Call the card issuer and stop the card first.** That comes before telling the company.
2. Tell the office & purchasing desk (ext. 2210).
3. File a statement of the circumstances as a draft. Reissue takes 3–5 working days.
4. Fraudulent charges made before the stop go through **the issuer's liability process**. The company
   does not absorb them up front.

## If a charge is not yours
- Raise it **within 7 days** of the statement. After that it is treated as your own spending.
- A charge that was yours but was never explained is not fraud — it is **unexplained**, and it is
  deducted from pay.

Unavoidable spending while the card is stopped goes on a personal card and is claimed on a
disbursement form.
