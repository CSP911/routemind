# What the travel agency booking portal carries forward automatically

## Auto-filled from the trip request
Once a trip request is approved, the portal pulls the traveler's name, cost center, destination and dates straight into the booking form, along with the fare or lodging cap that applies for that route and city.

## What still needs manual entry
The portal doesn't know about a mid-trip itinerary change, a draft approval for an out-of-policy fare, or a group booking that should be priced under the flat group fee — each of those has to be entered by hand before the booking reflects it.

## The trap
**A group of travelers booked one by one through the portal each get charged the standard per-ticket fee, because the system has no way to notice they're travelling together unless the booking is flagged as a group request up front.** Flagging it after the fact doesn't refund the difference.

## Next
That fee difference is exactly why the group rate has to be requested at the time of booking, not reconciled afterward.
