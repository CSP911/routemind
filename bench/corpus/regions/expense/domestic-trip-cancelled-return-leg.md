# A cancelled return leg on an overnight trip

The outbound leg of a two-day trip runs as planned. The return train is cancelled by weather the following evening, and the traveller books a replacement for the next morning, adding an unplanned third night.

## How it's claimed

This isn't a same-day overrun — the trip was already overnight — so there's no resubmission step for turning a day trip into a stay. The extra night's lodging is claimed under the ordinary cap for that city, on top of the two nights already planned. The daily allowance simply extends by one more day at the flat 20,000 KRW rate.

The cancelled return fare goes through the refund procedure once the airline or rail operator processes it, and the replacement fare is claimed as its own line.

**Claiming the cancelled fare and the rebooked fare as two full, unrelated fares is flagged as duplicate spend** — the claim needs to show the refund confirmation for the cancelled ticket alongside the new one, not just the new ticket on its own.

The refund procedure for cancelled fares covers how that confirmation gets attached; the same-day overrun documents cover the equivalent problem when the trip wasn't overnight to begin with.
