# Team annual spending ceilings

Every team has a single annual figure that its card charges, travel claims, entertainment and reimbursements all draw against. It is not a per-category budget — it is one ceiling covering all of it, set once a year by headcount band.

## The bands

| Team size | Annual ceiling |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | 20,000,000 KRW |
| 6–15 | 50,000,000 KRW |
| 16 or more | 100,000,000 KRW |

## What happens as it fills up

Crossing 80% triggers a mandatory check-in with the division head before the next month's claims go through. Crossing 100% means new discretionary spend needs division head pre-approval on top of the normal claim approval — already-committed charges like subscriptions still process.

## No carryover

**The ceiling resets to the band figure every 1 January. Whatever is unused on 31 December is forfeited, not added to the following year's ceiling** — a team that underspends this year does not get a larger allowance next year, and one that overspends does not start the new year in debt.

The line between this ceiling and the monthly actual-versus-budget report is worth keeping straight; see the variance overview for how the two interact.
