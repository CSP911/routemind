# A two-city trip, worked

A three-night trip: Daejeon, then Busan, then Daejeon again before returning to Seoul.

| Night | City | Lodging cap | Claimed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daejeon | 80,000 | 76,000 |
| 2 | Busan | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| 3 | Daejeon | 80,000 | 82,000 → capped at 80,000 |

Each night is checked against the cap for the city the traveller actually slept in that night, not an average across the trip and not the highest city's cap applied throughout. The daily allowance of 20,000 KRW a day runs separately and covers all four days of the trip regardless of which cities were visited.

**A common mistake is claiming one blended lodging figure for the whole trip, hoping the Busan night's higher cap covers the overrun on night three.** It does not — each night is checked on its own receipt, and the excess on night three is simply not paid.

Fares between the two cities are claimed as separate legs, following the same rail-versus-air rule that applies to any domestic leg.
