# Card use by situation — the common cases

## What this covers

The specific situations grouped under this section: how a subscription's billing cycle and renewal window work, when a conference or seminar fee needs pre-approval versus a straight card charge, what taxi and ride-hailing evidence looks like by fare size, how fuel and parking are told apart from a mileage claim, and how a mobile top-up gets approved on a shared device. Thirteen pages below this one walk through each.

## The common case

Each of these has one dominant, unremarkable path: a subscription renews on schedule, a conference fee is paid once and reconciled against the registration, a taxi fare under the low-value threshold needs only the card receipt.

## Where it goes wrong

**Fuel and parking on the same trip are easy to double-claim — once against a mileage claim and once against the card itself — because both look like ordinary road spend and neither system flags the overlap.** The pages below cover this and the equivalent traps for subscriptions and conference fees.

The quick map one level up sorts these situations by name only; this page is where each one's actual case detail starts.
