# Gift, meal and condolence caps compared

These three lines get confused with each other because they are all entertainment and all sit near the same evidence threshold. They are not interchangeable.

## The caps

| Occasion | Cap | Evidence above 30,000 KRW |
|---|---|---|
| Meal | 50,000 KRW per head | Qualifying evidence plus attendee list |
| Seasonal or occasion gift | 100,000 KRW each | Qualifying evidence plus recipient list |
| Condolence or congratulation | 200,000 KRW | Invitation or notice copy accepted in place of a receipt |

## What doesn't change

Every line still uses the same 30,000 KRW evidence threshold underneath it. A gift bought for 25,000 KRW is fine on a simple receipt; one bought for 45,000 KRW needs qualifying evidence regardless of which of the three caps it sits under.

**The condolence and congratulation line is the only one where a simple invitation substitutes for a receipt — it does not extend to gifts or meals just because they happened at the same event.** A funeral wreath and a meal served afterward for the same visit are two different lines with two different evidence rules, not one combined claim.

Where the occasion is genuinely ambiguous, treat it as a meal or a gift, not as condolence, since that cap is the narrowest of the three.
