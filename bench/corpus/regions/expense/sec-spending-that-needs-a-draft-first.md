# Spending that needs a draft first — where to start

Most spending is either within cap and needs no draft, or already covered by a purchase order. This section holds the two situations in between: a spend over the normal cap that gets approved before it happens, and an emergency spend that happens first and gets regularised after.

## The two situations

| Situation | Order of events |
|---|---|
| Over-cap spend | Draft raised and approved, then the money moves |
| Emergency spend | The money moves, then a draft documents and regularises it |

## The common case

An over-cap purchase — travel booked above the standard fare, a vendor priced above the usual threshold — gets a draft raised for sign-off before anyone commits to it.

## The trap

**An emergency spend regularised late is still judged against the same approval chain as if it had been drafted first — "it was urgent" shortens the deadline to regularise, it doesn't remove the need for sign-off.**
