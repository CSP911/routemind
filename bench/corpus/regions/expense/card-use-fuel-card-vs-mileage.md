# Fuel card charges versus mileage claims

A company-registered vehicle's fuel goes straight on the corporate card. A personal vehicle used for business does not — it claims the per-km mileage rate instead, which already covers fuel, wear and insurance together.

| Vehicle | Fuel on card | Mileage claim |
|---|---|---|
| Company-registered vehicle | Yes | No |
| Personal vehicle | No | Yes — 300 KRW per km |

Record the start point, the end point and the distance for any mileage claim; without those three the claim has nothing to check the rate against.

## The trap

**Putting fuel on the card and also claiming mileage for the same trip is not adjusted down — it's rejected outright.** The mileage claim for that trip is voided in full, not trimmed by the overlapping fuel amount, because the two are meant to be mutually exclusive ways of paying for the same drive.

Parking follows a similar split by vehicle and trip type, though the rule for a monthly pass works differently again.
