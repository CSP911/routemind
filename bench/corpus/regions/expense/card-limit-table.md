# Card limits

| Grade | Per transaction | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | 300,000 KRW | 2,000,000 KRW |
| Team lead | 1,000,000 KRW | 5,000,000 KRW |
| Division head and above | 3,000,000 KRW | 10,000,000 KRW |

- **Do not split a payment** that is over the per-transaction cap. Raise a purchase request instead.
- Blocked categories: entertainment venues, duty free, gift vouchers, jewellery, online games. The
  authorisation itself is declined.
- Weekend and late-night charges (22:00–06:00) **need a reason recorded with them.** Anything left
  unexplained is recovered at month end.
- Spending on clients follows its own cap → Entertainment.
