# Who approves a cash advance or float

## Approval bands
The approver for a cash advance or standing float follows the same rank bands used to set the request ceiling — not the desk that clears card charges.

| Rank | Per-request ceiling | Monthly ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | 300,000 KRW | 2,000,000 KRW |
| Team lead | 1,000,000 KRW | 5,000,000 KRW |
| Division head and above | 3,000,000 KRW | 10,000,000 KRW |

## Who signs off
A request inside your own band goes to your direct manager. Anything above it — a staff member drawing more than 300,000 KRW for one trip, say — needs the division head's sign-off regardless of who submitted it, and finance countersigns once a request pushes the monthly ceiling into view.

**The approver on an advance is not the desk that approves corporate card spend** — routing a float request to whoever clears card charges just delays it, since that desk has no visibility into advance balances at all.

Approval only releases the cash. The advance still has to be settled against receipts by its own deadline once you're back.
