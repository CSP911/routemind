# Reclassifying a same-day trip that ran long

A trip booked and approved as same-day sometimes runs past the last realistic return leg, forcing an overnight stay that was never budgeted or approved as such.

- **Notify your approver within 3 working days of returning**, before the claim is filed — not after it's queried.
- The claim itself then includes a lodging line at the normal city cap (80,000, or 100,000 in Seoul and Busan), on top of the fare and allowance that were already expected.
- If the overrun happened because of a missed connection or a meeting that ran long for business reasons, note that on the claim; it's what separates an overrun from a trip that was simply planned wrong.

**Filing the claim as if it were still same-day — omitting the lodging line to avoid the conversation — is worse than flagging it late, because an unclaimed hotel receipt discovered later reads as evidence the trip ran outside its approved shape.** Flag the change, then let the lodging cap do its normal job.

The cap itself works exactly as it does for any planned overnight stay, city by city.
