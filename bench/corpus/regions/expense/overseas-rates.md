# Overseas travel caps

## Region bands
| Band | Region | Daily allowance | Lodging |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | US, Western Europe, Japan | USD 80 | USD 250 |
| B | China, South-East Asia, Eastern Europe | USD 60 | USD 180 |
| C | Elsewhere | USD 50 | USD 150 |

## Exchange rate
- Use **the first posted mid-market rate on the day the trip departed**, not the rate on the day you
  settle.
- A local card payment is taken at the amount the issuer billed (it is not re-converted).
- State the rate date on the claim. Without it the claim is sent back.

## Daily allowance
- The departure day and the arrival day each count as a day.
- The allowance is paid without receipts, but **there is a separate cap on how much of it rides on pay
  tax free** → Tax-free allowances.
- For a trip whose receipts were lost, see Case: receipts lost on an overseas trip.
