# Who owns a disputed charge

## Two different desks
A genuinely unrecognised charge goes to the card issuer's dispute team through the international hotline — the company cannot resolve it internally, since the money moved between the merchant and the bank, not between you and Finance. A lost, stolen or fraud-blocked card is reported to the same hotline but handled as a card action, not a dispute, and runs on its own timeline.

## Where Finance comes in
Finance does not adjudicate whether a charge is legitimate. Its role starts once the bank has resolved it: reconciling the statement of circumstances filed in the gap, checking the seven-day window was met, and matching any refund back to the original claim.

**Sending a dispute straight to Finance instead of the card issuer wastes the window** — Finance has no channel to reverse a charge with the merchant, and by the time it's redirected to the right hotline, days of the seven can already be gone.
