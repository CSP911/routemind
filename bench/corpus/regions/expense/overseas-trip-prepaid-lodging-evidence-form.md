# Evidence fields for a home-billed overseas prepayment

## What the form needs

A hotel prepaid on the corporate card in KRW, before departure, is checked against the band's lodging cap once the trip's actual nights are known. To make that check possible, the prepayment record needs:

| Field | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Hotel invoice, itemised by night | Confirms the nightly rate, not just the total |
| Dates matching the approved itinerary | Confirms the nights claimed were nights actually stayed |
| KRW amount as charged | This is the figure checked against the band cap, not a converted local-currency estimate |
| Booking confirmation number | Ties the prepayment to a specific stay if the invoice is reissued later |

## The trap

**A card statement line showing the hotel's name and a KRW figure is not, on its own, qualifying evidence for a prepayment.** The itemised invoice is what lets the settlement step confirm the nightly rate stayed under the band cap — without it, the whole prepayment can be treated as a single undocumented lodging charge rather than five nights at a rate within cap.

Once the invoice is on file, the settlement step runs the same reconciliation as any other lodging claim against the trip's confirmed nights.
