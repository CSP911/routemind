# Annual licences

## The first charge
An annual software licence or membership is approved once and billed once a year, evidenced like any spend over 30,000 KRW — a tax invoice or equivalent from the vendor at the time of purchase.

## What happens twelve months later
**A licence that renews automatically after a year still needs a fresh receipt and fresh qualifying evidence — last year's invoice does not carry forward**, even though the approval that first allowed the licence is still technically in force. Treat each annual renewal as a new spend to evidence, not a continuation of the old claim.

## Cancelling an annual licence
Cancel well before the renewal date. Most vendors give little or no refund once the new year has billed, so a late cancellation risks a charge with no offsetting refund to report.

Monthly subscriptions carry the same renewal-evidence problem on a much shorter cycle.
