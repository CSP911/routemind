# Domestic travel caps

| Item | Cap | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Fares | Actual cost | Standard-class rail. Air only on legs over 2 hours |
| Lodging | 80,000 KRW a night (100,000 in Seoul and Busan) | Actual cost, receipt required |
| Daily allowance | 20,000 KRW a day | Covers meals and local transport, paid without receipts |

- A same-day trip with no overnight stay pays the allowance only.
- Using your own car pays **300 KRW per km** instead of fuel. Record the start, the end and the distance.
- Spending above a cap is accepted only where it was approved in advance. There is no approval after
  the fact.
