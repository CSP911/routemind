# Insurance claim filing deadline

File the formal insurance claim within **30 days** of the incident date, or of your return if treatment continued past the end of the trip. This is the insurer's deadline, not an internal one, and it is not extended for being busy after a trip or for waiting on a hospital's final invoice — submit what you have and add documents afterward if the insurer allows it.

## Where this gets confused with other deadlines
- The 24-hour notice to your approver and the travel desk is an internal administrative step. **It does not itself count as filing the insurance claim.** Sending the notice and assuming the claim is "in progress" is the most common way this deadline is missed.
- This is unrelated to the 7-day window for disputing a card charge you don't recognise — an incident abroad and a suspicious charge are different problems with different clocks, even when they happen on the same trip.

Once the deadline passes, the claim is refused outright rather than reduced, so if documentation is incomplete near day 30, file anyway and follow up. Whether the destination itself was even approved for travel is the next question.
