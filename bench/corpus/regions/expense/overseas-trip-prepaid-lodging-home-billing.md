# Prepaid lodging billed at home

Many overseas hotels and booking platforms charge the corporate card in KRW at the time of booking, weeks before departure, rather than in local currency at checkout.

## How it's treated

The charge is evidenced by the booking confirmation and the KRW amount shown on the statement on the booking date — not by a local-currency figure or the exchange rate on the travel dates themselves. That KRW figure is compared directly against the night's lodging cap for the destination's band.

## The trap

**A prepaid KRW charge does not get reconverted or adjusted for the exchange rate on the actual travel dates**, even if the won has moved significantly by the time the trip happens. If the booking is later cancelled, the refund is evidenced against that same original KRW charge, not against a new conversion done at cancellation.

Days on the itinerary with no meetings scheduled raise a separate, allowance-specific question.
