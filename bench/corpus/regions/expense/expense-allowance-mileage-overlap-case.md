# Car allowance and mileage overlap, worked

A field engineer with a standing own-car allowance also filed a mileage claim most weeks, using the same car for both. The claims were rejected on review for one leg but accepted for another, and the difference is the useful part.

## What was rejected

Several of the filed trips were the engineer's ordinary commute to a fixed client site they were assigned to full-time for the quarter. That is the routine use the 200,000 KRW monthly allowance already covers — it was rejected as double payment for the same driving.

## What was accepted

A separate trip that quarter — a one-off visit to a different client's site, approved in advance as a domestic trip — was accepted at 300 KRW per km on the standard mileage form, start point, end point and distance recorded as usual.

## The distinction that mattered

The allowance pays for having a car available for work in general. The mileage rate pays for a specific, approved trip that isn't part of the routine. **The same driver, the same car, and even the same road can sit on either side of that line depending on whether the driving was a standing assignment or an approved one-off trip** — the allowance doesn't retire the mileage claim, and the mileage claim doesn't retire the allowance; each has to be judged on its own terms.
