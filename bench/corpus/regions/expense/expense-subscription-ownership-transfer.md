# Subscription ownership transfer

The registry entry for a subscription names one budget owner, and that person is who the system notifies before each renewal. When they change teams or leave, the entry does not update itself.

## What has to happen
- The outgoing owner's manager names a replacement before the owner's last day, not after.
- The replacement confirms the seat count and cost centre are still correct — a handover is also the moment to catch a licence nobody uses anymore.
- Finance updates the registry entry; the old owner is dropped from renewal notices only once this is done.

**A renewal notice sent to an address that no longer exists still renews the subscription** — the vendor doesn't check whether anyone read it, and the charge lands regardless.

If no replacement is named, the subscription defaults to the requesting team's team lead until reassigned, which is why an unclaimed licence often surfaces first in a team lead's monthly statement rather than in the registry itself.

This is the ownership gap that tends to produce the seat drift covered next.
