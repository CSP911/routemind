# A team that finished the year under ceiling

A twelve-person team's annual ceiling for the year was set at 40,000,000 KRW. By December it had spent 32,000,000 KRW — client travel had been lighter than planned, and two conferences were cancelled.

## What the team lead expected

The team lead asked finance whether the unused 8,000,000 KRW could be added to next year's ceiling, reasoning that the team had "saved" it. Finance's answer was no.

**The unused balance is forfeited at year end, not banked** — it does not raise next year's ceiling, reduce the headcount-based calculation, or offset an overage run up by any other team. The ceiling resets to the standard band figure for the new year regardless of what happened the year before.

## What actually carried over

Nothing did. Headcount for the new year, not last year's spend, is what sets the new ceiling. A team that grew from twelve to fifteen people over the winter had its ceiling recalculated on the new headcount, same as any other team — the prior underspend played no part in it.

Reforecasting a monthly budget line works differently, and is covered on its own.
