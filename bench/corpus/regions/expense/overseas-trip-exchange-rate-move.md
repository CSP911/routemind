# A trip that started before a rate move

A traveller departs for a nine-day Band B trip. Four days in, the won weakens sharply against the local currency. Card charges from before and after the move sit on the same claim.

## How it was settled

Each card charge converts at the exchange rate shown on the statement for its own transaction date — the hotel charge from day 2 uses day 2's rate, the restaurant charge from day 7 uses day 7's rate. Nothing is reconverted at a single "trip average" rate.

The daily allowance itself is untouched by any of this: it is fixed in KRW per the band table and paid at face value regardless of what the won did that week.

## The trap

**A weaker won partway through the trip does not retroactively raise earlier charges to the new rate**, and it does not lift the daily allowance either. Only the card charges that post after the move convert at the new rate; everything else stands as originally converted.

Lodging paid before departure, rather than on the road, follows a different pattern again.
