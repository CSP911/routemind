# A trip through three bands

An engineer travels Seoul → Tokyo (2 nights) → Frankfurt (3 nights) → Nairobi (1 night) for a joint client rollout. The claim is not filed as six nights at one rate.

## How it was claimed

| Leg | Band | Nights | Lodging cap used | Allowance used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tokyo | A | 2 | 120,000 KRW | 40,000 KRW |
| Frankfurt | B | 3 | 180,000 KRW | 50,000 KRW |
| Nairobi | C | 1 | 150,000 KRW | 45,000 KRW |

## The trap

**The trip is not averaged into a single band for simplicity.** Filing all six nights at the Frankfurt rate, on the reasoning that it was the longest leg, overpays five of them and gets flagged at reconciliation. Each night's cap and each day's allowance is set strictly by the city the traveller slept in that night.

Where the itinerary itself changes mid-trip rather than being planned this way from the start, see the band-change procedure next.
