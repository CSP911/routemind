# Conference fee deadlines and early registration

Conference and seminar organisers often price registration in bands: a discounted early-bird rate before a cutoff, then a higher standard rate after it. The card can be charged for either, but the timing carries a specific risk.

## Registration windows

| Window | What applies |
|---|---|
| Early-bird | Discounted fee, charged before the organiser's cutoff |
| Standard | Full fee, charged any time before the event |
| Late or on-site | Highest fee, sometimes cash-only at the venue |

**Paying the early-bird fee before your travel request is actually approved leaves you exposed if the trip is later cancelled** — the fee is rarely refundable by the organiser, and an unapproved trip is not a reason the company will cover it after the fact.
