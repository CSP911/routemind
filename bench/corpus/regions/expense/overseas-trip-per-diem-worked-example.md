# Per-diem day with no attendance — worked example

## The itinerary

A five-day trip: two days of client meetings, a free day with no scheduled business activity, a public holiday at the destination with an optional site visit, and a return flight delayed a day by the airline.

## What each day draws

The two meeting days draw the daily allowance without question. The free day is where it goes wrong — nothing on the itinerary evidences business activity that day, so it does not draw the allowance on its own. The public holiday does, because the traveller attended the optional site visit and the approver logged it as work. The delayed return day also draws the allowance, since the delay was outside the traveller's control and the itinerary — not the traveller's choice — kept them there.

**A day the itinerary shows as "abroad" is not the same as a day it shows as "attended."** The approver has to confirm actual attendance before a borderline day pays out; a free day inserted for personal reasons doesn't default into the allowance just because the flight home hadn't left yet.

This is the same distinction that decides whether a stopover earns its own lodging night — duration and attendance, not the traveller's calendar, drive the outcome.
