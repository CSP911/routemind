# Deposits and prepayments

## Paying a deposit
1. Pay the deposit on the card and keep the deposit receipt, even though it isn't the final invoice.
2. Note the booking reference somewhere retrievable — venues and vendors often issue the final invoice only after the event, under that same reference.
3. Claim the deposit now if it's non-refundable; if it's refundable, hold the claim until the balance is paid so both go through together.

## When the deposit comes back
**A deposit receipt is not final evidence on its own — a refunded deposit still has to be reported within 5 working days of landing back on the card**, on the same clock as any other post-settlement refund, even if no claim was filed for it yet.

## Prepayments for the full amount
A full prepayment, an annual venue booking for example, is evidenced at the time of payment; there's no second document to chase later unless the booking itself changes.

Refund reporting more generally follows the same rule regardless of what triggered it.
