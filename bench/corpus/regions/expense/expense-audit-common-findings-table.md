# Findings that recur

Most findings are not novel — the same handful account for the large majority of what the sample turns up month after month.

## The recurring five

| Finding | What triggered it |
|---|---|
| Missing qualifying evidence | A spend over 30,000 KRW backed only by a simple receipt |
| No attendee list | An entertainment claim over 30,000 KRW with no names attached |
| Split payment | Two receipts at one merchant, same day, each under 30,000 KRW |
| Third-party name | Evidence issued to a colleague, not the claimant |
| Late refund unreported | Money back after settlement, not reported within 5 working days |

**A finding against last month's claim does not touch this month's budget line — it is booked against the month the spend originally landed in, which is why a variance report can look clean the same month a finding is raised.** The two run on separate tracks and reconcile on different schedules.

How each finding gets answered, and by when, is covered on the next page.
