# Budget against actual — where to start

## What this covers

This is where a team's spending gets compared against its plan: how the monthly variance report gets built, what counts as an explained variance versus one that just gets waved through, and the reforecast cycle that resets the baseline partway through the year. Fourteen pages beneath this one carry the thresholds, the templates, and who signs off what.

## The common case

Most months, a team's actual spend sits close enough to plan that the variance report is a formality — a short narrative, a few line items reconciled, submitted before the close calendar's cutoff.

## Where it goes wrong

**A variance narrative that just restates the number — "we spent more than planned" — gets rejected, because a variance has to explain a cause, not the fact of the gap itself.** The pages below set out exactly what a narrative has to say and who has to write it.

This sits next to the team ceiling pages: a ceiling is the annual limit a team is not meant to cross, while a variance report is the monthly account of how it got there.
