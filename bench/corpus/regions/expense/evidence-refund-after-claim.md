# A refund after the claim was filed

## When this happens
A hotel cancels a booking after you've already claimed it, or a merchant refunds an overcharge once the expense has gone through settlement.

## What to do
1. Notify accounting as soon as the refund posts — don't wait for the next claim cycle to mention it.
2. **Report it within 5 working days of the refund landing on the card or account.** Left unreported, it is treated as undisclosed income against the company card, not as an oversight.
3. Accounting offsets the refunded amount against your next settlement rather than asking for a separate repayment.

## What not to do
Don't resubmit the original receipt as if the trip still happened at full cost — attach the refund confirmation alongside the original claim so the file shows both sides.

Refunds are the exception; most awkward evidence questions come up before the claim is filed, not after.
