# Cancelled, but it charged again

## The case
A team lead cancelled a monthly analytics subscription through the vendor's portal eleven days before the renewal date, well inside the notice period, and received a confirmation email. The card was charged in full on the renewal date regardless.

## What this is, and isn't
This is not the same as forgetting to cancel — the confirmation email is the evidence that notice was given on time. It is treated as a vendor-side billing failure, not as an unrecognised charge, so it does not go through the seven-day dispute path.

## How it was resolved
The confirmation email was attached to a statement of circumstances, the charge was claimed as normal for that month, and the vendor was chased separately for a refund on the following statement. **A cancellation confirmation is not itself proof the charge didn't land — the statement still shows what it shows, and the claim has to match the statement, with the confirmation attached to explain it.**

Whether that refund, once it arrives, is reported before or after settlement depends on the timing — covered under evidence for a late refund.
