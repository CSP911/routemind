# What a finding response has to show before it closes

## Required fields
- The finding reference number the audit desk issued — not the original claim number
- A one-line explanation of what happened
- The corrected evidence, or a note that none exists and why
- The claimant's own confirmation, unless the claimant has since left the team, in which case the team lead confirms instead

## What each outcome does

| Response outcome | What happens |
|---|---|
| Evidence supplied and accepted | Finding closes, no penalty |
| Evidence supplied but insufficient | Returned once for revision |
| No response before the deadline | Escalates automatically past the claimant |
| Response confirms an error | Finding closes with the 2% penalty applied |

## The trap
**A response filed without the finding reference number doesn't attach to the open finding at all — it lands as a fresh, unlinked note, and the original finding still shows overdue until someone matches the two by hand.** Always quote the reference the audit desk gave, not the claim number it was raised against.

Who actually receives that response, and where their authority to close it stops, is covered separately.
