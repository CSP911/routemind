# Declaring eligibility for a payroll allowance

## What the declaration covers
Starting a car allowance, a remote-work stipend, or restoring a meal allowance after a pause all begin with the same form to HR, not a memo to a manager. The form asks for the condition that makes the payment valid — vehicle registration and a valid licence for the car allowance, an approved remote-work schedule for the stipend — and the date that condition became true.

## What counts as a change
Selling the car, letting the registration lapse, or a remote-work arrangement ending are all changes that have to be reported the same way the original eligibility was declared. A change of address or team does not, unless it touches the underlying condition itself.

## The trap
**An allowance that started correctly does not stay correct on its own.** HR pays it every month until a stop request comes in — nobody checks whether the car is still owned or the remote days are still approved. The declaration only proves eligibility on day one; keeping it accurate afterwards is the employee's job, not payroll's.

Once eligibility actually changes, what happens to the months already paid is a separate question.
