# Booking through the corporate travel agency

## What the agency does
The travel agency is the required channel for domestic and international flights, rail tickets, and hotel nights on an approved trip. It quotes against the fare and lodging caps set in the domestic and overseas rate tables, so a booking that clears the agency has already cleared the cap check.

## How a booking works
Submit the approved trip request number, dates and destination through the desk or the booking portal. The agency returns a domestic fare the same working day, an overseas fare within two working days. The itinerary and invoice attach straight to the trip claim; no separate receipt upload is needed for the fare itself.

## The trap
**Booking directly with an airline or hotel because the agency is slower does not exempt the spend from the fare or lodging cap — it just moves the cap check to after the fact, once the difference has already been paid.** A direct booking is compared against the same caps used for reimbursement, and a mismatch gets queried, not waved through because it was booked in a hurry.
