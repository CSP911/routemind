# What the purchase order request form needs

## Fields
- Supplier name and business registration number
- Item or service description
- Quote reference, where the amount requires one
- Total amount and cost centre
- Requester and approver for the amount band
- Delivery or service date expected

## What trips people up
**Leaving the quote reference blank because the supplier already gave a verbal price does not pass review.** The quote has to be a written document on file, not a number someone remembers from a phone call — procurement checks for the attachment, not for confidence in the price.

## After submission
Once the approver above signs off, procurement issues the PO number and sends it to the supplier. The form itself stays attached to the eventual invoice for matching.

This form only starts once the quote threshold has already been cleared — check that first.
