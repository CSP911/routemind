# Team spending ceilings — where to start

Every team draws against a single annual ceiling for discretionary spend — cards, purchase orders, entertainment, the lot. This part covers how that ceiling is set, what happens as a team approaches it, and what happens to whatever is left when the year closes. The pages beneath this one carry the actual bands and sign-off names; this page only lays out the shape.

## What this section covers

- How the ceiling is sized against team headcount
- The two checkpoints — 80% consumed, then 100% consumed — and what each one changes about approval
- Why a team that finishes the year under ceiling gets nothing carried forward

## The common case

Most teams track quietly under 80% all year and never see an escalation. The checkpoints exist for the minority that run hot mid-year or back-load spend into Q4.

## Where it goes wrong

**Crossing 100% does not stop spending — it changes who has to approve it.** Teams that read the ceiling as a hard cap keep committing spend after the checkpoint, assuming the system will simply reject it, and are surprised when an exception request lands on their desk instead. By the time that request is approved, the spend has often already happened.

None of this resets early: whatever a team saves against ceiling in November does not soften the ceiling in December, and it does not follow the team into January either — see why nothing carries over, next.
