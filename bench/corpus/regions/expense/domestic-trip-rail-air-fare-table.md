# Rail vs air fares on marginal legs

Rail is the default for any domestic leg. Air is allowed only when the direct rail journey would take noticeably longer, or when the air fare is not materially higher than rail on that specific route. The comparison is made per leg, not per trip.

| Route | Rail (KTX) | Air | Rail time | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seoul–Busan | 59,800 | 89,000 | ~2h40 | Rail — time difference too small to justify air |
| Seoul–Jeju | N/A (ferry only) | 78,000 | N/A | Air — no rail link exists |
| Seoul–Yeosu | 47,500 | 95,000 | ~3h10 | Rail — fare gap too wide |
| Seoul–Pohang | 46,900 | 72,000 | ~2h50 | Rail, unless the KTX schedule adds over 2 hours of layover to the traveller's itinerary that day |

**Booking air "for convenience" on a route where rail is clearly faster or cheaper gets the air fare rejected outright — the claim is settled at the rail price and the difference falls on the traveller.** If a schedule genuinely forces air (last rail departure missed for business reasons beyond the traveller's control), note the reason on the claim at filing, not after query.

Lodging once you arrive follows the city cap regardless of how you got there.
