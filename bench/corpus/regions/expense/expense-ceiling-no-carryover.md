# Why nothing carries over

The annual ceiling resets on 1 January to the flat band figure for the team's current headcount. Nothing from the prior year moves with it.

## Both directions are flat

- A team that finishes the year at 60% of its ceiling does not get a larger ceiling next year — the unused balance is simply forfeited.
- A team that finishes the year over its ceiling, having spent under an approved exception, does not start the new year owing anything against the new ceiling — the overage is not deducted from January's balance.

**The reset is a clean slate in both directions, which surprises teams used to budgets that bank an underspend.** There is no request process to carry an unused balance forward; asking finance to apply last December's surplus to January's ceiling will be declined, because the figure is set by headcount band alone, not by history.
