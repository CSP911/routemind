# Ceiling by team size

The annual ceiling is fixed by headcount, counted at the start of the fiscal year, not by the team's spending history or its manager's grade.

## Bands

| Team size | Annual ceiling | Recount trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | 20,000,000 KRW | Headcount change of any size |
| 6–15 | 50,000,000 KRW | Headcount crosses a band boundary |
| 16 or more | 100,000,000 KRW | Headcount crosses a band boundary |

A mid-year headcount change only moves a team into a new band if it crosses one of the boundaries above — going from 8 to 10 people, for instance, changes nothing. Going from 15 to 16 does, and the ceiling is recalculated from the date the change is confirmed, not backdated to January.

**A team that shrinks below a boundary does not have its ceiling reduced mid-year** — the higher band holds until the next annual reset, so a team dropping from 16 to 14 in June still spends against the 100,000,000 KRW figure through December.

The band table sets the number this document's siblings enforce — what happens at 80%, at 100%, and at the January reset.
