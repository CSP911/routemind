# Conference fee paid on a personal card

Registration for a conference often opens before a trip is approved, and early pricing can close before sign-off comes through. Paying personally and reclaiming later is allowed — but it runs through reimbursement, not the card.

## How to reclaim it

1. Keep the registration receipt or invoice — it needs the event, the date and the amount, same as a card charge would.
2. File the claim once trip approval exists, attaching the receipt as qualifying evidence if it's over 30,000 KRW.
3. Settlement runs through the normal expense claim. There is no card statement to reconcile against, because the card was never used.

## The trap

**A personally paid registration reimbursed after the fact does not carry the early-registration protection that card-booked fees get.** If the fee would have qualified for a lower tier at the time of booking, that saving is gone once approval lands late — the claim reimburses what was actually paid, at whatever tier that was.

Where the fee ends up categorised — as a conference charge or folded into entertainment — depends on who attends and why, which is decided separately from how it was paid.
