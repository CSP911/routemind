# Who confirms attendance on an ambiguous per-diem day

## Who decides

The itinerary alone doesn't settle whether a weekend or a travel day on an overseas trip draws the daily allowance — the traveller's approver confirms it, based on whether business activity actually happened that day, not on whether the traveller was physically abroad.

## Why it isn't automatic

**The claim system defaults every calendar day inside the itinerary's start and end dates to "attended" unless someone corrects it.** A Saturday with no meetings, sitting between two working days, will draw the allowance automatically unless the approver flags it as a non-working day with no attendance — at which point it's removed rather than paid and later queried.

## What the approver checks

The approver looks for a business reason tied to that specific date — a meeting, a site visit, or travel itself — the same test used to sort the itinerary's non-working days into the ones that still pay and the ones that don't.

Getting this flagged before filing is what keeps the claim from being bounced back for a correction that could have been made in one line at the time.
