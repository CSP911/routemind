# Card or purchase order — where to start

## What this covers

The choice between putting a spend on the corporate card and raising a purchase order instead: a spend that sits under the card cap but still needs a quote, a supplier who will only invoice against a PO, and a repeat purchase that has quietly crossed into a higher approval band. Ten pages below this one carry the quote thresholds and the PO form itself.

## The common case

Most one-off purchases under the card cap just go on the card, no PO involved, and that is correct for the large majority of spend.

## Where it goes wrong

**A spend under the card cap still needs a PO if the supplier requires one or a quote threshold applies — the card cap and the PO trigger are two separate tests, and clearing one does not exempt a purchase from the other.** The pages below set out which test fires first and when both do.

The purchasing desk page next door covers who has authority to approve either path; this page only covers which path applies.
