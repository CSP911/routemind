# What the audit desk samples

The monthly sample is not a random slice of claims — it is weighted toward the lines most likely to hide a problem. A single claim can be pulled for more than one reason at once.

## What gets pulled

- Every claim over 500,000 KRW, regardless of category.
- Every disbursement form, since it substitutes for evidence rather than attaching it.
- Every cash advance settlement still open at close.
- A random 10% slice of everything else, rotated by cost centre each month.
- Any claim already flagged once this year for the same finding type.

**Being sampled is not an accusation — most sampled claims clear with no finding at all.** The trap is treating a sample request as optional because the claim was already approved; approval and audit check different things, and a claim can clear one and fail the other without contradiction.
