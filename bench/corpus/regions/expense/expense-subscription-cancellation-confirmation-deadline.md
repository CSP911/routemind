# Confirming a cancellation actually landed

Filing a cancellation request is not the same as the vendor having processed it. Some vendors confirm within minutes; others queue the request and process it only just before the next billing date, sometimes after it.

## What to check, and when
Once a cancellation is filed, check for the vendor's own confirmation — an email, a status change on their billing page, or a support ticket close. Do not treat "request submitted" in your own registry as proof of anything on the vendor's side.

## If the charge lands anyway
**A renewal that lands despite a cancellation on file is not automatically reversed — it is treated as an unrecognised charge, and the same 7-day window from the statement date applies before it counts as your own spending.** Missing that window because you assumed the cancellation had taken effect is the single most common way this goes wrong.

Check the statement the day it posts, not the day you remember the cancellation was filed. The steps for raising the dispute itself, and what a renewal that lands anyway counts as, sit in the cancellation procedure this deadline supports.
