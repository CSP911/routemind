# Allowance payroll cutoff

Starting, stopping or changing eligibility for the meal allowance, the own-car allowance or the remote-work stipend has to reach HR by the 20th of the month to take effect in that month's payroll run.

## What happens either side of the cutoff

A request filed on or before the 20th applies from that same month. A request filed on the 21st or later applies from the following month — the current month runs on whatever was already in place, whether that means the allowance stops one month later than expected or starts one month later than expected.

**There is no retroactive adjustment for a cutoff missed.** A remote-work stipend that should have stopped when someone returned to the office full-time in March, but whose stop request landed on the 22nd, still pays out in March in full — and finance does not claw it back after the fact, because payroll has already run.

This is a fixed calendar date, independent of the monthly expense close covered elsewhere in this area — missing one doesn't affect the other, but confusing them is a common way to file an allowance change too late for both.
