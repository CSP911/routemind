# Who signs off a subscription's renewal

## Two separate approvals
Registering a recurring service needs one sign-off: the budget owner who agrees the first charge can land on the card. That approval covers the first cycle only. It does not carry forward automatically to the second year, the second seat block, or the next multi-year term. Each renewal needs its own reconfirmation from whoever currently owns that line in the budget.

## Where this breaks down
**A subscription keeps renewing even after the person who approved it has left the team, because the card doesn't check for a live approval before billing — it only checks that the service is registered.** The registry shows an owner field, but an owner leaving doesn't pause the charge; it just means nobody is left to say yes or no before the next renewal fires.

## What the reconfirming desk actually checks
The current budget owner has to confirm three things before a renewal is allowed to proceed unchallenged: the service is still in use, the seat count still matches who's on it, and the line still sits inside this year's forecast. If any of the three fails, the renewal should be flagged for cancellation before the billing date, not after.

This sits next to what happens when the registered owner actually leaves — the handoff itself follows a separate procedure.
