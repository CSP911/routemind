# Allowance owner

HR payroll sets and administers the meal allowance and the own-car allowance — both are standard entitlements tied to employment terms and headcount, not to individual approval each month. A new hire is added automatically; someone leaving the qualifying role is removed at the next cutoff.

## Where the remote-work stipend differs

The remote-work stipend is not automatic. **A division head has to confirm remote-work eligibility before HR payroll will add the stipend, and that confirmation has to be renewed if the arrangement changes** — moving from full remote to a hybrid schedule, for instance, is a new eligibility decision, not a continuation of the old one.

## Where this doesn't reach

None of these three desks has any say over the per-km mileage claimed on a specific domestic trip, or the fuel and parking charges that go on the corporate card directly — those run through the travel approver named on the trip claim itself, not through payroll.

Getting the eligibility change filed on time still depends on hitting the monthly cutoff already covered — the desk that owns the decision isn't the desk that owns the calendar.
