# A repeat purchase that crossed a band

A team ordered printer supplies from the same vendor four times in one month — 120,000 KRW, 150,000 KRW, 90,000 KRW and 100,000 KRW. Each order individually sat well under the 300,000 KRW quote threshold, so nobody attached one.

## What actually happened
Procurement's monthly review adds up purchases from the same supplier across the month, not just per order. The four orders totalled 460,000 KRW — inside the 300,000–1,000,000 KRW band, which needs a written quote on file before purchase.

**A string of small orders from the same supplier is checked cumulatively over the month, the same way same-day payments at one merchant are checked for evidence purposes.** Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under the threshold doesn't avoid it — it just gets caught later.

## How it was resolved
The team retroactively obtained a quote covering the vendor relationship and filed it against the fourth order, with a note explaining the pattern. Going forward, recurring orders from that supplier get a standing quote reviewed each quarter rather than one per order.

## The lesson
Treat a recurring supplier relationship as a running total, not four separate small purchases, before deciding whether a quote is needed.
