# Country to band mapping for overseas trips

Overseas lodging caps and the daily allowance are set by region band, not by a flat overseas rate. The band is assigned per destination country on the approved itinerary.

## Bands at a glance

| Band | Typical countries | Lodging cap | Daily allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | China, Japan, Southeast Asia | 120,000 KRW/night | 40,000 KRW/day |
| B | Europe, the Americas, Oceania | 180,000 KRW/night | 50,000 KRW/day |
| C | Middle East, Africa, Central Asia | 150,000 KRW/night | 45,000 KRW/day |

## The trap

**The band follows where you actually are, not the airline's home country or the currency the ticket was billed in.** A Seoul–Dubai–Johannesburg routing on a Gulf-based carrier still bands as C once you land and stay in South Africa — Dubai only matters if you overnight there too.

A trip that touches more than one band doesn't average out — see how each leg is claimed separately in the multi-country case that follows.
