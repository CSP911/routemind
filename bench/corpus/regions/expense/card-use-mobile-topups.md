# Mobile top-ups

## What qualifies
A top-up for a company-issued SIM or a work data plan may go on the card, evidenced by the carrier's receipt or app confirmation like any other spend under 30,000 KRW.

## What doesn't
**A top-up for a personal phone number, even one used for work calls, is not a business expense and sits among the categories the card blocks outright** — the fix is getting a company SIM issued, not routing a personal top-up through the card and hoping it clears.

## Overseas SIMs and roaming
A local SIM or roaming pack bought for an overseas trip is treated as a travel cost, not a top-up. Convert it at the transaction date's rate and keep the purchase receipt regardless of amount, since carrier kiosks abroad rarely issue anything above a basic slip.

Blocked merchant categories in general are covered under the card's caps and limits.
