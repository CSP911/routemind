# Lodging and allowance across two overnight stops

A two-city trip doesn't get one blended lodging cap. Each night is priced against the cap for the city the traveller actually slept in that night.

| Night | City | Lodging cap | Daily allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Busan | 100,000 KRW | 20,000 KRW |
| 2 | Daejeon | 80,000 KRW | 20,000 KRW |

The allowance line doesn't move with the city — it's a flat 20,000 KRW a day everywhere, covering meals and local transport, and it's paid without receipts regardless of which night it falls on.

**The two lodging caps are not averaged or applied at the higher rate for both nights.** A claim that puts 100,000 KRW against the Daejeon night because that's what Busan allowed will be corrected back to 80,000 KRW, not waved through because the trip total happens to land under some combined ceiling — there is no combined ceiling, only two separate ones.

File each night's hotel receipt against its own city in the claim system rather than a single lump lodging line; the worked example for two-stop trips shows how the system splits this automatically once both cities are entered.
