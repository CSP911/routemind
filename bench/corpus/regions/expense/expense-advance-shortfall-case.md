# When an advance doesn't cover the full spend

## The case
A staff member drew a 300,000 KRW cash advance for a two-day client trip. Actual costs came to 340,000 KRW; the 40,000 KRW gap was paid on a personal card at the airport once the advance ran out.

## How it settled
The 300,000 KRW advance was settled first, against receipts totalling that amount, closing it out inside the advance settlement window. The 40,000 KRW shortfall was filed separately as a personal card reimbursement, evidenced the same way as any other spend over 30,000 KRW.

**Filing the full 340,000 KRW as a single personal-card reimbursement double-counts the part already drawn as an advance.** Finance sees 300,000 KRW settled twice — once against the advance, once against the reimbursement — and the second claim gets kicked back.

Keep the two claims apart from the start: one settles the draw, the other reclaims only what the draw didn't cover.
