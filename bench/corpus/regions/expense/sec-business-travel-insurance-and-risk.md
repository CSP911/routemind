# Business travel insurance and risk — where to start

## What this covers

Cover for a trip abroad, what actually happens after something goes wrong on the ground, and the short list of countries the company will not send anyone to regardless of business case. Ten pages beneath this one carry the coverage limits, the claim filing deadline, and the sign-off chain for a restricted-country exception.

## The common case

Most trips are covered automatically once a trip request is approved and booked through the agency; nobody thinks about the insurance until something actually happens.

## Where it goes wrong

**A trip booked before a restricted-country sign-off is issued is not automatically covered once the sign-off later arrives** — the sign-off has to precede the booking, not just exist somewhere in the chain by the time of travel. The pages below work through this and the reverse case, a trip discovered to touch a restricted country only after departure.

This connects forward into the settlement pages: an incident abroad changes what a claim needs to include, but it does not excuse the traveller from also filing the ordinary trip settlement.
