# When a flight was booked directly instead of through the agency

## What happened
A traveler needed a same-day fare change and booked directly with the airline on a personal card rather than wait for the agency desk to reopen. The fare cleared, and the traveler filed for reimbursement expecting the direct booking to stand in for the agency's invoice.

## What actually happened
The claim was checked against the same fare cap the agency would have quoted for that route and day. The direct fare came in higher, so only the amount up to the cap was reimbursed; the difference was left with the traveler. The agency's per-ticket fee didn't apply, since the agency never touched the booking — but neither did its negotiated rate.

## The trap
**Booking directly doesn't waive the fare cap and doesn't automatically justify the higher price paid; a same-day change on its own isn't treated as sufficient reason, and the traveler still has to show why the agency wasn't used.**
