# Card use by situation: subscriptions through mobile top-ups

This part of card use by situation walks through the everyday spend that shows up on a corporate card without a business trip attached to it: subscription renewals, conference and seminar fees, taxis and ride-hailing, fuel and parking, and mobile top-ups. Thirteen pages underneath work through each situation as it actually turns up in an expense queue — a renewal that fires before anyone reviews the seat count, a taxi receipt issued under a driver's personal name, a fuel charge sitting next to an unrelated mileage claim.

## What this part covers
Recurring and one-off charges that are card-first: something a cardholder buys or pays for directly, rather than something claimed back after the fact. Each page below settles the same two questions for its situation — which card to use, and what evidence has to travel with the charge.

## The common case
Most of these charges clear without incident: a subscription renews on schedule, a taxi receipt names the company, a fuel purchase matches the vehicle log. The pages below exist for the charges that don't.

## Where it breaks
**The one thing that trips people up across all thirteen pages is treating the merchant's receipt as sufficient on its own** — a taxi fare, a top-up, a seminar fee can each look complete and still be missing the one field that ties it to a business purpose.

Card use by situation sits next to the corporate travel agency, which covers the same ground for anything booked ahead rather than paid on the spot.
