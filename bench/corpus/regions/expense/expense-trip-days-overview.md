# Trip days versus allowance days

## Two different counts
A trip's calendar span and the number of days it draws a daily allowance are not the same number. The calendar span is set by the approved itinerary — departure to return. The allowance days are set by what the claim actually lists, day by day, and each listed day has to justify itself: travel, attendance, or an approved non-working day that still counts.

## Where the two drift apart
- A trip **confirmed after it started** often has an itinerary that no longer matches what was actually approved, so the system's day count and the claim's day count diverge from day one.
- A day you were physically at a client site can still be **missing from the claim** if nobody entered it, and a missing day does not draw an allowance by default.
- A weekend day worked in place of a scheduled day off is compensated as **lieu time, not an extra allowance day** — the two don't stack.
- A trip that crosses a month boundary splits its allowance across two months' claims, not one.

## The trap
**Assuming the number of nights booked equals the number of days paid.** The claim only pays for days it lists; an unlisted day is not paid retroactively without a correction.

Each of these situations is worked through on its own in the pages that follow this one.
