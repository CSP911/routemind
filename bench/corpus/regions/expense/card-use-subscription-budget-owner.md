# Subscription budget ownership

Every new subscription charged to the corporate card needs a named budget owner before the first charge lands, not after the trial converts. The owner is whoever's cost centre absorbs the recurring line — usually the team lead who asked for the tool, not whichever cardholder happened to check out.

## Who signs off

| Annual cost | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead, recorded in the subscription request |
| 500,000–3,000,000 KRW | Division head, before the trial converts to paid |
| Above 3,000,000 KRW | Routed through procurement as a purchase, not a card subscription |

## What happens without sign-off

The card doesn't check for an owner — it just charges. A subscription with no recorded owner renews exactly like one that was approved properly.

**The renewal itself is never blocked by a missing sign-off; only the next request to add a new tool is.** That's why an unapproved subscription can sit on the statement for months before anyone traces it back to a person.

Once a subscription is flagged, it still follows the same notice window as any other renewal before it can be stopped — check how much lead time a cancellation actually needs before assuming today's request stops tomorrow's charge.
