# Reclassifying a same-day trip as an overnight

## When this applies
A same-day trip that turns into an unplanned overnight stay has to be reclassified before the claim is filed — not patched afterwards by adding a hotel receipt to a same-day form.

## Steps
1. Notify your approver as soon as the overrun is clear, within the window set for same-day-to-overnight cases.
2. Ask the approver to reclassify the trip in the case system from same-day to overnight.
3. Attach the hotel receipt for the unplanned night once reclassification is confirmed.
4. File the claim against the overnight template, which carries its own lodging and allowance lines rather than the flat same-day figure.

## The trap
**Filing the claim on the original same-day form and simply attaching a hotel receipt doesn't work — the same-day template has no lodging field to attach it to,** so the receipt sits orphaned and the claim comes back for correction. Reclassification has to happen first.

## After reclassification
Once the trip carries the overnight template, the lodging cap for the city involved applies exactly as it would for any planned overnight stay — check the city's band before assuming the standard 80,000 KRW figure applies everywhere.
