# Subscription lifecycle overview

A recurring subscription passes through four stages that rarely get equal attention: registration, renewal ownership, cancellation, and the seat count sitting underneath the bill. Most disputes trace back to one stage being skipped rather than done wrong.

## Registration
A new recurring charge needs a named budget owner and a cost centre before the first charge lands, not after — see the registration form for what's required at that point.

## Renewal
Each renewal carries an owner of record who has to either reconfirm the subscription or request cancellation before that cycle's cutoff. Ownership follows the registry entry, not the person, until someone actively reassigns it.

## Cancellation
A request only stops the next charge if it lands before the notice window closes. After that, the charge is a renewal already committed, not a mistake to be refunded.

## Seat counts
Team licences bill against the last confirmed seat count, not the current headcount. **A shrinking team does not lower the bill on its own — someone has to file the adjustment.**

This overview sits above the registration form, the ownership rule and the seat audit that follow it.
