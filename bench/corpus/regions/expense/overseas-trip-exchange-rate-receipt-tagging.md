# Tagging receipts across a rate move

## The rule
Every card charge converts at the exchange rate shown on the statement for its own transaction date. A trip that opens before a rate move and closes after it does not get one rate for the whole claim — each night's lodging, each meal, each taxi receipt carries its own date's conversion.

## The procedure
1. Pull the statement covering the full trip dates, not just the settlement date.
2. Match each receipt to its transaction date line on the statement, not the date it was issued or submitted.
3. Enter the converted KRW figure per line; the claim system does not re-convert a blended total.
4. Where a charge posted a day after the actual purchase — common with hotel folios — use the transaction date printed on the receipt, not the posting date on the statement.

## The trap
**A refund that lands after the rate has moved again converts at the refund's own date, not the date of the original charge.** A hotel deposit taken before the move and refunded after it will not net to zero in KRW even though the foreign-currency amount is unchanged — the gap is real and is not an error to chase down.

Where the trip also crosses a country line, this per-transaction rule combines with the band split rather than replacing it.
