# Reimbursing a personal card charge

This covers any business spend that lands on a personal card by necessity, not because approval came late for a specific booking — that case is handled separately for conference fees.

## Steps

1. File the claim with the original receipt attached, same as a corporate-card spend.
2. Apply the standard evidence line: **above 30,000 KRW you still need qualifying evidence, not just the personal card's own statement.** A screenshot of the card app is not a substitute.
3. Route the claim by amount for approval.

| Approver | Per-transaction limit | Monthly limit |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | 300,000 KRW | 2,000,000 KRW |
| Team lead | 1,000,000 KRW | 5,000,000 KRW |
| Division head and above | 3,000,000 KRW | 10,000,000 KRW |

4. Finance pays the reimbursement into the employee's registered account on the next settlement run.

## The trap

Claimants often assume that because the money is already out of their own pocket, the evidence bar drops. It doesn't — a personal-card claim without qualifying evidence above 30,000 KRW carries the same 2% penalty as a corporate-card one, just charged against the reimbursement instead of the card statement.

What counts as qualifying evidence here is the same list used everywhere else in this area.
