# Who approves an out-of-policy travel booking

## The split
The agency will hold an out-of-policy fare or room rate, but won't ticket it until a draft approval is attached. Who signs that draft is set by the size of the gap above the in-policy fare, not the ticket's full price.

## Bands
| Amount above the in-policy fare or cap | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 200,000 KRW | Team lead |
| 200,000–800,000 KRW | Division head |
| Above 800,000 KRW | Division head and above |

## The trap
**The approver signs off the difference above the in-policy fare, not the full ticket price** — a 1,200,000 KRW business fare against a 900,000 KRW economy cap needs sign-off on the 300,000 KRW gap, and routing the full fare to the wrong approver just sends it back down a level.

## Next
Once the draft is signed, the agency's own booking record carries the approval forward automatically.
