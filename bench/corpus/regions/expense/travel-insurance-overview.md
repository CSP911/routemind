# Business travel insurance and risk

## What triggers cover
Every overseas trip booked and approved through the travel system carries a corporate travel insurance policy automatically. Cover attaches the moment the itinerary is confirmed — there is no separate application and nothing to expense. Domestic trips are not covered under this policy; national health insurance and the standard daily allowance are treated as sufficient for travel within Korea.

## What it covers
The policy covers medical treatment abroad, emergency evacuation, baggage loss and trip cancellation caused by circumstances outside your control. See the coverage table for the limit on each.

## Where cover stops
**Cover follows the approved itinerary, not the traveller.** A personal leg tacked onto a business trip — a weekend extension, a side visit booked after departure — falls outside the policy the moment it diverges from what was approved. If a personal extension needs cover, arrange it separately before you leave; the company's policy does not stretch to it retroactively, even if the incident happens on the same physical trip.
