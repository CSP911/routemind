# Budget against actual

Every cost centre has a monthly budget, and every month the actual spend against it is checked, line by line, against that number. This is the routine that catches a subscription that renewed twice, a trip that ran long, or a hire that landed a month early — before it turns into a bigger surprise at quarter end.

## What gets compared

Actuals come from the general ledger once the month closes; budget comes from the plan filed at the start of the year, adjusted by any reforecast since. The two are matched line by line, not just at the cost-centre total — a total that nets out because one line ran high and another ran low still has to show both lines.

## When it needs explaining

Not every gap needs a written note. Small drift within a line is normal. What crosses the line into "explain this" is set out on its own page, along with what happens above that.

**A variance explained only by restating the number — "spend was higher than budget" — is sent back.** The note has to name the cause: which invoice, which hire, which trip, which renewal.

The report itself, and the reforecast that follows it each quarter, are covered next.
