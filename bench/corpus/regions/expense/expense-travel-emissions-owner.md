# Who owns the emissions figure

Two desks touch a trip's emissions data, and neither one can do the other's job.

## Compiling the figure
Finance's sustainability desk pulls the year's trip-level entries each January and compiles the annual figure. It does not review individual bookings — it takes whatever mode and distance sat on each claim at close.

## Flagging a gap
The expense audit desk, the same one that samples claims each month, is the one that catches a missing rail comparison line or a blank distance field during its normal review. **The audit desk can raise a finding against a missing emissions entry, but it has no authority to change what mode a leg was booked under** — that decision belongs to whoever approved the mode-choice exception in the first place, and it stands regardless of what the emissions entry says.

## Where an exception goes
A dispute over which factor applies to an unusual leg — a chartered vehicle, say, or a mixed-mode segment — goes to the sustainability desk, not the audit desk, since it owns the factor table itself.
