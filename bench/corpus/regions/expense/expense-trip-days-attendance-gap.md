# A day attended but not claimed

## What this covers
Sometimes a traveller is on site for a day that never makes it onto the expense claim — the itinerary listed a shorter trip, or a day was added at the last minute and nobody updated the form. Attendance and the claim are two separate records, and only the claim pays.

## How to fix it before filing
Add the missing day to the claim before submission, with whatever confirms attendance — a sign-in log, a meeting invite, a client email. The daily allowance for a domestic trip is 20,000 KRW; that figure doesn't change because the day was added late, only the total claimed does.

## After filing
Once a claim has been submitted and closed, an attended day that was never claimed cannot be added back through a simple correction. It has to go through the same statement-of-circumstances route used for missing evidence elsewhere, and it is not guaranteed to be accepted.

## The trap
**Being on site is not the same as being on the claim.** The allowance is paid against what the form lists, not against what actually happened, so an unlisted day is, as far as settlement is concerned, a day that didn't happen.

File the correction before the trip's claim goes to close — the calendar for that is fixed and doesn't wait for a missing day to surface.
