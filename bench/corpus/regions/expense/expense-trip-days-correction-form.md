# The form to add a missing allowance day

## What it's for
Use this when a day you were actually travelling or at a client site never made it onto the trip claim — most often because it was left off the original itinerary, or because approval landed after departure and the system never auto-populated it.

## Fields required

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Trip reference | The original claim number the day belongs to |
| Missing date | The calendar date being added |
| Reason | Why the date wasn't on the original claim |
| Confirming approver | The person who can confirm you were actually travelling that day |

**The approver's confirmation is not optional — your own note that you were there does not stand in for it.** Without a name in that field, the desk reviewing the correction treats the day as unconfirmed and drops it rather than paying it provisionally.

File it before the correction window closes; past that point the day is treated as though it never happened, evidence or no evidence.
