# A client who brings extra guests

The 50,000 KRW per-head cap applies to everyone at the table, not just the people your side invited. If a client brings two colleagues who were not on the original invitation, the total bill is still divided by the full head count present.

## What the attendee list needs

Above 30,000 KRW, the claim needs the attendee list as well as qualifying evidence. That list has to include the client's guests by name and affiliation even though they are not company staff and were not invited by you.

## The trap

**Extra guests are checked against the average, not against the invited count.** A dinner for four invited people that comes in under cap can go over once two extra guests arrive, because the same bill is now divided by six. Filing the claim against the original guest list of four, ignoring the two who actually sat down, is what gets a claim rejected on review rather than merely queried.

If the overage is real, note it on the claim rather than trimming the attendee list to fit the cap — an honest overage with an explanation is a smaller problem than a list that doesn't match the receipt.

This is the same head-count arithmetic that governs a fixed-headcount event, just running in the other direction.
