# Requesting a cash advance before a trip

An advance is drawn against anticipated spend — a site visit, a small purchasing trip, a project with several vendors who won't take a card. It is not a way around the corporate card for spend the card would otherwise cover.

## Fields on the form

- Purpose and linked trip or project ID
- Estimated amount, broken down by category (fares, lodging, materials)
- Cost centre to be charged
- Requested disbursement date
- Approver signature, routed by the same amount bands used for reimbursement claims

## The trap

**The estimated amount should track anticipated spend, not round up for safety.** An advance padded well above what the trip needs draws scrutiny at settlement, and a pattern of over-requesting is what triggers a division head review of the whole float, not just the one claim.

Once the advance is issued, the clock starts on settling it — that window is covered next.
