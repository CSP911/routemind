# Allowances that ride on pay — where to start

## What this covers

These are the recurring, payroll-attached allowances rather than one-off travel or entertainment claims: the monthly meal allowance, the monthly own-car allowance, the remote-work stipend, and the tax-free ceilings that decide how much of each survives untaxed. Fourteen pages below this one work through rate tables, eligibility declarations, and what happens when a payment stops being a reimbursement and starts being ordinary pay.

## The common case

Most employees just receive their meal and own-car allowance automatically once HR has recorded eligibility, and the payroll system carries it forward month to month without anyone re-filing. A remote-work stipend usually follows the same pattern once the arrangement is declared.

## Where it goes wrong

**An allowance keeps paying out after the condition that justified it has ended** — a car sold, a remote-work arrangement quietly ending, a role changing — because nothing in the payroll system checks the condition, only the declaration. The allowance owner is responsible for reporting the change; the system will not catch it.

The rate tables and caps that decide how much of this is tax-free sit in the pages below; this page only maps where each allowance starts and where it turns into taxable pay.
