# Overseas trip cases

An overseas trip is read two ways at once: by the cost band it falls into and by the country or countries actually visited. Most trips agree on both; the case set exists for the ones that don't.

## The two views

Band drives the allowance and lodging figures. Country drives sign-off, insurance and any restricted-destination check. A trip can sit in one band for its allowance and still need country-level sign-off for a single leg.

## The common case

A multi-country trip where each leg carries its own band, or a single trip that changes band partway through because of an added city or an extended stay.

**A stopover is not automatically a destination** — it only earns its own band and its own night once it clears the length threshold; anything shorter is folded into the adjacent leg.

Where the trip's evidence turns out to be the harder problem — a foreign-language receipt from the added leg, say — that moves to the evidence case set rather than staying here.
