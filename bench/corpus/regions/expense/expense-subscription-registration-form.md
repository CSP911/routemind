# Subscription registration form

Before a recurring service goes on the corporate card, the requester files a registry entry — not just the first invoice. The form captures what a later audit needs and what a cancellation request will be checked against.

## Required fields
- Vendor name and billing cycle (monthly, annual, multi-year)
- Budget owner and cost centre
- Seat count at signup, if the licence is per-seat
- Renewal date and the notice period the vendor requires

## Approval by monthly cost

| Monthly cost | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 300,000 KRW | Team lead |
| 300,000–1,000,000 KRW | Division head |
| Above 1,000,000 KRW | Finance |

**A subscription started on a personal card "to get moving" before the form is filed is not backfilled into the registry** — it stays an ordinary reimbursement claim, evidenced like any other purchase, until a fresh registration goes through under the corporate card.

Once registered, the entry decides who owns the renewal and who has to act before it locks in.
