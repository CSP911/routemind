# Travel insurance coverage limits

These limits apply to the policy issued automatically on an approved overseas trip. They are per incident, not per trip — a trip with two separate incidents can draw against each limit independently.

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Medical treatment abroad | Up to 100,000,000 KRW per incident |
| Emergency evacuation | Actual cost, no cap |
| Baggage loss or damage | Up to 1,000,000 KRW per incident |
| Trip cancellation (covered causes only) | Up to 500,000 KRW |

## The gap people miss
**A laptop or phone lost with the bag is not settled under the baggage line at its replacement value.** The insurer pays out against the baggage cap for the bag and its ordinary contents; a company-issued device is tracked and replaced separately through IT asset management, not through this policy. Filing one combined claim for both usually just delays the baggage portion while IT's process runs on its own track.

Emergency evacuation has no cap because it is priced and billed directly between the insurer and the treating facility — you are not expected to pay and reclaim it yourself. What to do first, in order, is covered next.
