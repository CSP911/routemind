# Monthly window for SIM top-up requests

Recurring top-ups for company-owned SIMs are batched once a month, not charged individually as requests come in. Any change — a new line, a cancelled line, a different amount — has to clear before the cut-off or it carries over unchanged.

| Request | Deadline | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| New SIM added | 5th of the month | Added from next month's batch instead |
| Line cancelled | 5th of the month | One more month is still charged |
| Amount changed | 5th of the month | Old amount holds until the following cycle |

## The trap

**A cancellation submitted after the 5th does not stop that month's charge.** The SIM is already inside the batch that's about to run, and the saving only starts the month after — asking again on the 6th doesn't reach back into a batch that already closed.

Who signs off a new line before it ever enters the batch runs on a different track from this billing calendar, and the two shouldn't be confused.
