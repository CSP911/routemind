# Subscription renewal windows by billing cycle

Not every recurring charge gives you the same runway to stop it. The notice period is set by the vendor's billing cycle, not by company policy, so check which row you're in before you assume there's time.

## Cycles side by side

| Cycle | Notice needed to stop renewal | What hits the card |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Cancel any time before the next billing date | One month's fee, repeating |
| Annual | Cancel at least 30 days before the anniversary date | The full year, in one line |
| Multi-year | Requires division head sign-off to start; cancellation terms vary by vendor | Charged once per term |

## The window that isn't the billing date

**The 30-day annual notice period is measured from the renewal date, not from when you remember to act on it.** A cancellation filed on the anniversary itself is already too late for most vendors — the charge has already queued. If it lands anyway, it goes through the same renewal-review step as any unexpected charge, not a dispute.

Annual licences follow a similar clock but a different threshold — see the notice window for those separately.
