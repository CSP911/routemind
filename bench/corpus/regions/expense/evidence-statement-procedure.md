# Filing a statement of circumstances

## When this applies
Use this route whenever a receipt or invoice cannot be produced the normal way — a merchant that has closed, an e-receipt that can't be resent, a seller with no tax invoice function at all. It stands in for the missing document; it does not manufacture evidence, so it only works when you can genuinely describe what happened and why nothing better exists.

## Steps
1. Try the normal channel first — ask the merchant to resend or reissue.
2. If that fails, draft a statement of circumstances: date, merchant, amount, why the document can't be produced, and how you know the figure is right.
3. Attach it to the claim in place of the missing evidence and route it for approval as usual.

## While it's pending
A statement under review is not yet accepted evidence. **File it as soon as you know the receipt won't come, not the day the claim is due** — the underlying spend still crosses the 30,000 KRW line, and the standard 3–5 working day reissue window doesn't stretch just because you started late.

For amounts under roughly 100,000 KRW, check whether a disbursement form is the simpler route before drafting a statement at all.
