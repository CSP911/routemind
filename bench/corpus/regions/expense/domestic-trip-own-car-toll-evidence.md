# Evidence for highway tolls on an own-car trip

The 300 KRW per km rate covers fuel and wear, not the toll itself — tolls are claimed separately, and what counts as evidence depends on how the toll was paid.

| Payment method | What to attach |
|---|---|
| Hi-pass linked to a personal card | The card or Hi-pass statement showing each toll entry and amount |
| Cash at a manned tollgate | The paper receipt issued at the booth |
| Unmanned tollgate, no receipt printed | A disbursement form, since the amount is normally well under the 100,000 KRW ceiling |

**The mileage log by itself does not cover tolls, even though both sit on the same claim.** A start point, end point and distance figure proves the driving; it says nothing about what was paid at the gate, so a toll line without one of the three items above is rejected even when the mileage portion is accepted in full.

The own-car claim form is where both the mileage log and the toll evidence get entered together, one line for distance and a separate line for each toll charge.
