# Deadline to correct a day count

## The window
A missing or mismatched allowance day has to be flagged to your approver within **5 working days** of the trip's return date, before the claim is filed for that month's close. This mirrors the reporting window used elsewhere for refunds and changes that land after the fact — five working days is the standard grace period across expense corrections.

## What happens after the window closes
Once the claim has gone through that month's close, a day-count correction can no longer be made by simply editing the form. It has to go through the same statement-of-circumstances route used when evidence itself is missing, and correction through that route is not instant.

## The trap
**Waiting until the next trip to mention a missed day from a previous one does not work — each trip's correction window is tied to that trip's own return date, not to whenever you next happen to file a claim.** A missed day from three trips ago is, by then, effectively unrecoverable.

The same five-day habit applies to reporting an itinerary change that shifts which allowance band a trip falls into.
