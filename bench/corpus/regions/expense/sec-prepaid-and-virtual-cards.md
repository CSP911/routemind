# Prepaid and virtual cards — where to start

A prepaid or virtual card exists for situations a standard corporate card doesn't cover well: a vendor that won't accept a shared corporate number, a one-off online purchase where a reusable number is a liability, or an overseas merchant a physical card can't reach in time.

## When one is issued instead of a corporate card

| Situation | What's usually issued |
|---|---|
| A single online vendor, one purchase | A single-use virtual number |
| A recurring but low-volume need | A topped-up prepaid card |
| No corporate card holder available | A prepaid card issued to the requester |

## The common case

Someone needs to pay one vendor once — a conference site, a niche software trial, an overseas booking portal. A single-use number is generated, charged, and closed without ever becoming a standing card.

## The trap

**A balance left on a prepaid card after the purchase doesn't roll forward or refund itself — it sits idle until someone reports it, and an idle balance shows up as an open item at close.**
