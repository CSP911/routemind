# Who owns the budget number

The annual budget for a cost centre is set once a year by the division head, in consultation with finance, and it belongs to the division head from that point — not to finance and not to the team lead who spends against it.

## What each desk actually does

- **Division head** — sets and revises the number, signs off on reforecasts and on variances above the escalation threshold
- **Team lead** — spends against the number, files the monthly variance report, writes the explanation for any line that crosses threshold
- **Finance** — closes the ledger, consolidates every team's report, and checks that a required explanation is present, not whether it is convincing

**A team lead cannot move budget between lines or months on their own initiative, even temporarily "to be corrected later" — that only happens through a reforecast entry the division head has signed.** Spending ahead of a reforecast that hasn't been filed yet is treated as a plain overrun, not an anticipated adjustment.

This split mirrors the one between who approves a spend and who later has to account for it — the same distinction that governs a card dispute versus a lost card report.
