# Evidence in awkward cases

Most claims clear with an ordinary tax invoice. The cases that don't fall into two groups, and they are resolved differently.

## The two tracks

| Track | Covers |
|---|---|
| Receipt format | Foreign-language receipts, e-receipts that never arrived or need reissue |
| Merchant status | A merchant that cannot issue a tax invoice, VAT-exempt sellers |

A format problem means the document exists but isn't in the shape finance expects. A status problem means the merchant genuinely cannot produce what a normal claim would carry. They need different follow-up, so they're handled as separate case sets below.

## The common case

An overseas conference receipt printed in the local language, or an online e-receipt that bounced back to a dead inbox. Both look like missing evidence at first glance; neither is.

**A missing tax invoice is not automatically a merchant-status case** — check the merchant type before treating it as an exception, since a format fix is usually faster than a status workaround.

The same desk that clears these also owns the seven-day window on a disputed charge, where a wrong document and a wrong amount get confused just as often.
