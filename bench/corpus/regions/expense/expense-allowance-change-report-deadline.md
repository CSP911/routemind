# Reporting a change to a payroll allowance

## The window
A change that ends or alters eligibility — the car sold, the remote-work arrangement lapsed — has to reach HR within 5 working days of the change itself.

## Why the date on the form matters
The change also has to clear HR before the monthly payroll cutoff. A change reported within 5 working days but after cutoff still takes one more month to land in the payslip; the two deadlines run independently of each other, not against each other.

## The trap
**Reporting within 5 working days does not mean the allowance stops that same month.** If the change lands after cutoff, the run is already locked, and that month pays out as before — the overage then has to be recovered separately rather than simply not paid.

Whether that overage is repaid in cash or offset against a later claim is where the recovery procedure picks up.
