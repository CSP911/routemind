# Card use by situation — where to start

## What this covers

The everyday situations where the corporate card is the default payment method: subscriptions and their renewal, conference and seminar fees, taxi and ride-hailing, fuel and parking, mobile top-ups, and the handful of other recurring spends that follow their own rules. This section groups those situations; the section beneath it opens onto the detailed cases for each.

## The common case

For nearly all of these, the card is used once and the charge reconciles cleanly against a receipt with no further action from anyone.

## Where it goes wrong

**A subscription or a conference fee booked on the card can keep charging, or land outside its evidence window, long after the original approval — and neither event shows up automatically.** Someone has to be watching the renewal date; the detail pages below cover each situation's own version of that problem.

The purchasing pages next door cover the same spends when a PO is used instead of the card; this section only covers the card path.
