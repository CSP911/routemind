# Travel emissions reporting

Every trip claim carries an emissions entry alongside the fare and lodging lines: the mode used for each leg, the distance, and a factor applied per kilometre. This runs independently of whatever approved the booking — a mode-choice sign-off that lets a leg fly instead of ride the rail covers the cost, not the emissions line.

## What gets recorded
The claim form captures mode, distance and cabin class where relevant (see the per-trip emissions entry form). The trip claim system fills in distance from an approved itinerary automatically; a manually rebooked leg still needs the field re-entered by hand.

## Why it's separate from the mode-choice approval
**A mode-choice approval that lets a leg fly for cost reasons does not exempt the claim from the emissions comparison entry.** The two checks ask different questions — one protects the travel budget, the other feeds the year's reported figure — and a claim can pass the first while still needing the second filled in before it closes.

## Where it goes
Individual trip entries roll up into the company's annual emissions figure, reported for the calendar year the trip's claim closed in, not the year it was booked in. The rate table and the rail-preference rule that follow this page cover the numbers themselves.
