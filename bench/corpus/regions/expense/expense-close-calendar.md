# Close calendar

Expense close runs on a fixed monthly schedule, separate from the budget variance report deadline that follows it. Missing a cutoff does not lose a claim — it pushes it into the next month's close, which can distort a team's month-on-month comparison if it happens repeatedly.

## The schedule

| Stage | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Claim filing cutoff | Last working day of the month, 6pm |
| Card feed reconciliation | 1st working day of the following month |
| Sampling and review | 2nd–4th working day |
| Findings issued | 5th working day |
| Response window closes | 10th working day |
| Close locked | 11th working day |

**A claim filed on the cutoff day itself still has to clear card feed reconciliation the next morning — if the charge hasn't posted to the feed yet, the claim sits unmatched and falls into the following month regardless of when you filed it.** Filing early in the day, not merely before the cutoff, is what actually protects the month.

The budget variance report's own due date sits after close locks, not before it.
