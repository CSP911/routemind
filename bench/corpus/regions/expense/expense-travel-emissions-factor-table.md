# Emissions factors by mode

The factor multiplies booked distance to produce the number that lands on the emissions entry. It has nothing to do with the 300 KRW per km mileage rate — that pays the driver, this measures the trip.

## The rates

| Mode | Factor (g CO2e per km) |
|---|---|
| Rail | 14 |
| Domestic air | 133 |
| International air | 195 |
| Own car (mileage claim) | 192 |
| Taxi or ride-hailing | 175 |

**Do not read the 300 KRW per km mileage rate as an emissions figure — the two numbers happen to sit on the same claim but measure completely different things.** Mixing them up understates or inflates the entry by an order of magnitude.

## Applying it
Multiply the leg's distance by the mode's factor; a leg split across two modes gets two lines, one per mode, not a blended average.
