# Lodging cap by city

The lodging cap has exactly two tiers. Everything else on a domestic trip — the daily allowance, the fare rules — is the same nationwide.

## Caps

| Item | Seoul or Busan | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Lodging | 100,000 KRW a night, receipt required | 80,000 KRW a night, receipt required |
| Daily allowance | 20,000 KRW a day | 20,000 KRW a day |

**The city tier applies to lodging only.** The daily allowance of 20,000 KRW, covering meals and local transport without receipts, does not rise for Seoul or Busan even though lodging does — a common misreading is to assume the whole trip sits on the higher band once it touches either city.

A trip that passes through both a listed city and somewhere else on different nights is not on one rate for its whole length; each night is capped at the rate for the city it was actually spent in. See the worked case for a trip with two overnight stops for how that plays out in practice.
