# Corporate travel agency service fee

## Fee schedule
| Booking type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Domestic air or rail ticket | 5,000 KRW per ticket |
| International air ticket | 15,000 KRW per ticket |
| Hotel booking | 3,000 KRW per booking |
| Group itinerary, 5 or more travelers on one booking | 20,000 KRW flat, regardless of headcount |

## How it's charged
The fee is added to the agency's invoice against the trip's budget line, not to the traveler's corporate card. The group rate applies once, however many separate rooms or seats the itinerary covers, even across travelers on different return dates within the same trip request.

**It will not appear as a card charge, so there is nothing to reconcile against a receipt — do not file a disbursement form or expect a line on your statement for it.**
