# Reporting a deposit refund

A deposit paid on the card for a venue, equipment or accommodation is claimed like any other spend when it's charged. What happens next depends on whether it's returned.

## Steps

1. Keep the original claim reference for the deposit — you'll need it to match the refund against.
2. When the refund lands on the card or account, **report it within 5 working days.** Left unreported, it is treated as undisclosed income against you, not a clerical gap.
3. Attach the refund notice showing the amount and date credited.
4. Finance nets it against your next settlement, or requests direct repayment if the original claim was already reimbursed.

## The partial case

A deposit refunded minus a cancellation fee is reported as a partial refund, not a cancelled claim — the fee portion stays claimed, only the returned portion is reported.

A conference fee refunded under the same circumstances follows an identical clock, just with different paperwork behind it.
