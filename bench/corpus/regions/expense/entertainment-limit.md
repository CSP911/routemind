# Entertainment

## What counts
Meals, gifts and events for customers and partners. **A meal among our own staff is not entertainment**
— that is a staff welfare cost.

| Item | Cap |
|---|---|
| Meal | 50,000 KRW per head |
| Seasonal or occasion gift | 100,000 KRW each |
| Event or seminar | Approved in advance, no cap |

## Evidence
- **Above 30,000 KRW qualifying evidence is mandatory.** A simple receipt is not accepted →
  Qualifying evidence.
- Record **who attended and which company** on the receipt. Entertainment with no attendee list is
  disallowed in full.
- Condolence and congratulation payments up to 200,000 KRW may be evidenced by a copy of the invitation
  or notice.

The per-transaction card cap still applies — being entertainment does not lift it.
