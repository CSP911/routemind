# Overseas trip cases, at a glance

Overseas claims go wrong in a small, repeating set of ways once a trip stops being a single city on a single band for a fixed number of days. This page is a map, not a restatement.

## What's covered elsewhere

- **Band by country** — which region band a destination falls into, and the lodging and allowance figures attached to each.
- **Multi-country trips** — why nights are claimed separately per city rather than averaged.
- **A band change mid-trip** — why the switch only applies from the date of change forward.
- **Stopovers** — when a layover is just a connection and when it's a claimable night.
- **Exchange-rate moves** — which day's rate governs each charge when the won shifts mid-trip.
- **Prepaid lodging billed at home** — why a KRW charge made before departure doesn't get reconverted later.
- **Allowance with no attendance** — why personal days tacked onto a business trip get neither allowance nor lodging.

## The trap

**None of these cases waive the underlying band or evidence rules** — they only clarify how to apply the band table and the 30,000 KRW evidence line to an itinerary that isn't a clean single-city trip.

For the domestic equivalents — cancelled legs, two-stop trips, same-day overruns — see the domestic trip cases index.
