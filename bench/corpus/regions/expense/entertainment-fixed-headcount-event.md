# Booking an event with a fixed head count

Some venues price a dinner or reception by a headcount fixed at the time of booking, not by how many people actually show up.

## Steps

1. Confirm the head count with the venue before booking and calculate the cap as head count × 50,000 KRW.
2. If the venue's fixed price per person pushes the total above that cap, get the overage approved before booking, not after.
3. Book and pay on the card.
4. After the event, file the attendee list showing who actually attended, alongside the venue invoice showing the contracted head count.

## Why the two lists don't match

The venue bills the number it was told to expect. The attendee list records who was actually there. These can differ — a client cancels two guests at the last minute — and both documents still have to be filed together.

**The cap is checked against the contracted head count that was billed, not against the smaller number who actually attended.** A no-show does not shrink the cap; the company is still paying for the seat that was reserved and cannot be un-billed.

Where the shortfall is large enough that the fixed price looks like padding, flag it to the approver rather than filing it quietly — the same discretion that governs disbursement forms applies here.
