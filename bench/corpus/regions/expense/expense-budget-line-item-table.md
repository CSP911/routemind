# Budget line items and what rolls into each

## Why the split matters
A team's monthly report doesn't compare one lump sum against one lump budget — it breaks spend into line items, and a variance is checked against each line's own budget, not the team total.

| Line item | What sits under it |
|---|---|
| Travel | Domestic and overseas fares, lodging, daily allowance, mileage |
| Entertainment | Client meals, seasonal gifts, condolence and congratulation payments |
| Subscriptions and licences | Recurring software, annual memberships, mobile top-ups |
| Card miscellaneous | Anything charged to the corporate card that doesn't fit the lines above |

## The trap
**Spend that isn't tagged to a line item at the point of claim defaults to card miscellaneous, not to the line it actually belongs to.** A conference registration filed without its category flag shows up as a spike in miscellaneous and a phantom underspend in travel — both of which look like variances that need explaining, when the real story is a filing habit.

## Keeping it clean
Tagging happens at claim submission, before the monthly report is generated, so a mistagged claim can't be fixed by editing the report afterward — it has to be corrected at the claim.

The category a claim carries is set by the same evidence rules that decide what a claim needs attached in the first place.
