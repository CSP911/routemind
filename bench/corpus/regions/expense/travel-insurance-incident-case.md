# A worked case — theft during an approved overseas trip

A staff member on an approved Level 2 trip had a bag stolen from a taxi, containing a company laptop and personal items, and missed the connecting flight while filing a police report.

## What happened, in order
1. Called the insurer's assistance line within the hour; the insurer confirmed the police report requirement and flagged the claim reference.
2. Filed the police report before leaving the city, as required for any theft claim.
3. Notified the approver and travel desk the same day, well inside the 24-hour window.
4. Booked a replacement flight on the corporate card; the fare difference was later reimbursed as part of the disrupted-trip claim.

## How it settled
The stolen bag and personal contents were paid out against the 1,000,000 KRW baggage cap. **The laptop was not settled through this claim at all** — it went through IT asset management as a lost-device replacement, tracked separately from the insurance file. The missed-connection fare was accepted as a covered cancellation cost since the delay traced directly to the theft, not to a personal choice.

The claim was filed and closed within the 30-day window with no dispute. Who actually owns each half of a case like this — insurer intake versus internal asset replacement — is worth knowing before an incident, not during one.
