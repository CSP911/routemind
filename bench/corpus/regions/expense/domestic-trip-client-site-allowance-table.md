# What a no-hotel client-site day still pays

Some domestic trips involve no hotel night at all — a day trip to a client site, or a stay arranged and paid directly by the client. The lodging cap has nothing to attach to, but the rest of the claim still runs normally.

| Line | Still paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fare (rail/air) | Yes | Actual cost, receipt required as usual |
| Daily allowance | Yes, 20,000 KRW | Covers meals and local transport, no receipt needed |
| Lodging | No | Nothing to cap — omit the line, don't claim a substitute figure |
| Own-car mileage | Yes, if used | 300 KRW per km, logged separately |

**Do not invent a lodging line to "balance" the claim just because other trips that week had one — a day with no hotel simply has no lodging entry, and adding one gets the whole claim queried.** If the client covered a hotel night informally and no invoice exists, that's fine; it's not part of the company's claim either way.

Where the client site turns into an unplanned overnight stay, the trip moves into overrun handling instead.
