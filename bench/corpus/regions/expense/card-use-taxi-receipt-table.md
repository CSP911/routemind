# Taxi and ride-hailing evidence by fare

The evidence a taxi or ride-hailing receipt needs depends on the fare, the time, and whose account the ride was booked under — not just on whether a receipt exists at all.

| Situation | What's required |
|---|---|
| Up to 30,000 KRW | A simple receipt is fine |
| Over 30,000 KRW | Qualifying evidence required |
| Late night or early morning, any fare | A business reason noted on the claim, not just the fare |
| Booked under someone else's account | Rejected unless reassigned to the traveller it was actually for |

## The trap

**Two short rides on the same night, each under 30,000 KRW, are not automatically exempt if they were really one trip split by a stopover.** Combined fares from the same rider on the same evening are treated the same way split merchant charges are: added together for the evidence line, however many separate receipts they produced.

Whether the ride needed a business reason at all depends on the hour it was taken — that test is separate from the fare-based evidence table above.
