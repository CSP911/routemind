# Five ways a charge can look wrong

| Situation | Is it a dispute? | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Genuinely unrecognised charge | Yes | 7 days from statement |
| Merchant trading under a different name | No, once matched to receipt | n/a |
| Card lost or stolen | Not a dispute — a replacement case | Report immediately |
| Card blocked by fraud monitoring | Not a dispute — call to unblock | Immediately |
| Subscription charged despite on-time cancellation | No — billing failure, not a dispute | Claim as normal, chase refund separately |

## The trap in treating everything as a dispute
**Filing a dispute for a charge that turns out to be legitimate does not just get withdrawn quietly** — it goes through the same review queue as a real one, and a pattern of unfounded disputes from one card is what triggers the fraud monitoring that then blocks the card mid-trip.

Knowing which of these five you're actually looking at decides which desk you take it to next.
