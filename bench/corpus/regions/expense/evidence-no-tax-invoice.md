# A merchant that cannot issue a tax invoice

## Who this covers
Street stalls, small cash-only vendors and some overseas suppliers cannot generate a tax invoice, an invoice or a card slip carrying a business registration number — three of the four documents that depend on the seller being registered for them.

## Under 100,000 KRW
Use a disbursement form instead. **The disbursement form only substitutes for evidence up to roughly 100,000 KRW, at the division head's discretion — above that line there is no workaround.** A claim above it needs qualifying evidence from an invoiceable purchase, or it is rejected outright.

## Above the line
Where the spend is genuinely large and no qualifying evidence is possible, escalate to accounting before spending, not after. They can point to an alternative vendor or a payment route that does produce an invoice.

The same registration gap shows up with sellers that are exempt from VAT altogether, which is not quite the same problem.
