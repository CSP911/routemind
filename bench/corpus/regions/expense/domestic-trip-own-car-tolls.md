# Own-car trips and tolls

Using your own car for a domestic trip pays 300 KRW per km instead of fuel, with the start point, end point and distance recorded on the claim. That rate is a per-km cost, not a full accounting of everything the car generates on the trip.

## What is separate from the per-km rate

| Cost | Covered by the 300 KRW/km rate | Claimed separately |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel | Yes | — |
| Wear and general running cost | Yes | — |
| Tolls | No | Yes, with the toll receipt |
| Parking at the destination | No | Yes, with the receipt |

**Tolls do not fold into the per-km distance and cannot be backed out of the mileage total — they are a separate claim line with their own receipt.** A trip logged as distance only, with the toll cost silently added to inflate the per-km total, does not reconcile against the actual route and gets rejected on the distance record rather than approved on the total.

Fuel put directly on the corporate card and the per-km rate do not combine on the same trip; whichever method is used applies to the whole trip, not leg by leg.
