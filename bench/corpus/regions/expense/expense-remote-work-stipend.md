# The remote-work stipend

Staff approved for regular remote work draw a 100,000 KRW stipend through payroll each month, covering the general overhead of working outside the office — no receipt, no claim form.

## Why it is fully taxable

Unlike the meal allowance and the own-car allowance, the remote-work stipend has no statutory non-taxable ceiling attached to it. **The entire 100,000 KRW is taxed as ordinary income every month, not just the portion above some threshold — there is no threshold.** It appears on the payslip taxed the same way a salary increase would be.

This trips up anyone who has previously drawn the meal or car allowance and assumes a flat monthly payroll figure means the same tax treatment. The stipend's take-home value is lower than its headline figure for that reason, and no amount of paperwork changes it — it is a feature of the payment, not a filing gap.

Eligibility for the stipend, like the other two allowances, is set once and then runs automatically until it's changed — the next page covers the cutoff for making that change.
