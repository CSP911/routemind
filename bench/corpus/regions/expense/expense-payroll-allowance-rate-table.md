# Payroll allowance rate table

| Allowance | Monthly amount | Tax-free ceiling | Above the ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW | 200,000 KRW | Taxed as ordinary pay |
| Own-car allowance | 200,000 KRW | 200,000 KRW | Taxed as ordinary pay |
| Remote-work stipend | 100,000 KRW | None | **Taxable from the first won** |

## Why the remote-work stipend is different

The meal allowance and the own-car allowance both track a statutory non-taxable ceiling — as long as the monthly figure sits at or under 200,000 KRW, none of it counts as taxable income. The remote-work stipend has no such ceiling written into it. **The full 100,000 KRW shows up on the payslip as taxable income every month, not just the part that would exceed some limit** — there is no limit to exceed.

This is the detail that catches people moving between the three: assuming a flat monthly figure behaves the same way twice just because it arrived the same way once. It didn't, and payroll won't flag the difference for you.
