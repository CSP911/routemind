# Closing the month and auditing expense claims

Every claim submitted in a given month has to clear before that month locks, and a sample of cleared claims is checked afterward regardless.

## What it covers

The close calendar and its cutoffs, what the audit desk pulls for sampling, the findings that come up often enough to have a standard response, and the process for answering a finding once it's raised.

## The common case

A claim that cleared during the month without incident but gets flagged in the post-close sample for a receipt that turns out to be a duplicate or a merchant mismatch.

**A finding closed without a written response reopens automatically at the next cycle** — silence is not treated as resolution, so the response has to be filed even when the answer is simply that the claim was correct.

Disputed-charge cases are one of the recurring finding types, since a reversal that never posted looks identical to a claim that was never corrected.
