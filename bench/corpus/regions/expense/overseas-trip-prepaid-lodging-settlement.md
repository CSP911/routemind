# Settling a hotel prepaid before departure

## Why it needs a separate step
A hotel booked and charged to the card in KRW before the trip departs shows up on the statement weeks before the itinerary is finalised. It has to be matched back to specific nights and checked against that destination's band cap at settlement, not assumed to be within cap because it was pre-approved.

## The procedure
1. Attach the original KRW invoice to the claim, showing the nights it covers and the property.
2. At settlement, divide the total by the number of nights actually stayed.
3. Compare the per-night figure to the band cap for that country. Any excess over cap is charged to the traveller the same way a same-day-booked overage would be.
4. If the itinerary changes after prepayment — fewer nights used, or a different city — the invoice is corrected against the hotel, not silently absorbed into the claim.

## The trap
**Paying in KRW before departure does not exempt the booking from the band cap.** A traveller who prepays a higher-tier room on the assumption that a home-currency charge sits outside the usual overseas caps still has the per-night figure tested against the same table as a card swipe made at the front desk.

Where the trip itself changes band mid-stay, the comparison runs against whichever band applies to that specific night.
