# Card use across common situations

The eight situations below all use the corporate card, but each carries its own wrinkle. This page is the map; the detail sits in each situation's own page.

## Where each situation lands

| Situation | On the card? | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Subscriptions and renewals | Yes | Renews automatically; a renewal is a fresh charge, not the old invoice |
| Conference and seminar fees | Yes | Kept separate from any entertainment claim at the same event |
| Taxi and ride-hailing | Yes | Booking must be under your own name |
| Fuel and parking | Yes, with a company or rental car | Own car uses the 300 KRW/km mileage rate instead |
| Mobile top-ups | Yes, for a registered company number | A personal number is blocked |
| Deposits and prepayments | Yes | Refund reported within 5 working days of landing |
| Foreign currency charge | Yes | Converted at the transaction date's rate for the 30,000 KRW line |
| Annual licence | Yes | Each year's renewal needs its own evidence |

**A deposit or prepayment charged this month counts fully against that month's cap even though part or all of it will come back later** — it isn't netted off until the refund actually posts.

For the caps themselves, by rank, see the per-transaction and monthly limits.
