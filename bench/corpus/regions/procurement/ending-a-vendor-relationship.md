# Ending a vendor relationship

## Before the decision is final
Ending a relationship is a division head decision, escalated to the CEO if the vendor's annual spend sits in the CEO band. It follows either a disqualification trigger or a non-renewal outcome from a performance review — it is not a substitute for either.

## Steps
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Close or complete any purchase orders already placed |
| 2 | Notify the office & purchasing desk to mark the vendor record inactive |
| 3 | Confirm no further code of conduct re-sign is scheduled |
| 4 | Move the contract original into expired/terminated custody |

## What doesn't happen
**Ending the relationship does not remove the vendor record or its documents.** The registration form, certificate, and contract original stay in custody under the same retention rules as any ended contract — an inactive vendor can be reinstated later without re-registering from scratch, and the record has to be there if it is.

Whether a relationship reaches this point instead of a routine non-renewal usually comes down to how the renewal decision was made.
