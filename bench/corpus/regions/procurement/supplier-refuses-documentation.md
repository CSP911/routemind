# When a supplier will not send its papers

## What blocks the purchase order

No purchase order issues without a completed vendor form, a registration certificate, and bank details on file. A supplier that will not send these stops the process regardless of the purchase amount, the urgency, or how good the quote is.

## The code of conduct is one of the papers

For any vendor at or above 500,000 KRW, refusing to sign the supplier code of conduct is treated the same as refusing to send the certificate or bank details — the file is incomplete either way, and no purchase order issues on the strength of the other documents alone.

**A quote from a supplier who never completes registration does not count toward the quotes a band requires.** Three quotes gathered from three suppliers, none of whom send their papers, leave the purchase with nothing that can legally be ordered against.

Where a supplier's reluctance turns out to be tied to something found during screening rather than simple slowness, that moves the case to the disqualification triggers instead.
