# When one quote is enough

A band's quote count is not a suggestion. A 4,000,000 KRW purchase normally needs three quotes before it reaches the CEO. A sole quote is only acceptable when one of these applies, and the reason has to be written on the request, not just understood verbally:

- the supplier holds an exclusive right to the item (a licensed part, a sole distributor)
- the purchase continues an existing contract where re-quoting would break continuity
- a genuine emergency makes competitive quoting impractical before the need passes

## What a sole-quote reason does not change

**Waiving the extra quotes does not waive the approver.** A 4,000,000 KRW sole-quote purchase still goes to the CEO, exactly as three quotes would have required — the exception shortens the paperwork, not the approval chain. Treating a sole quote as a shortcut past the CEO as well is the mistake that gets a request bounced back.

The written reason is filed with the request, and it has to name which of the three grounds applies — "only supplier we could find quickly" is not one of them. Requests that lean on the sole-quote exception more than occasionally are usually a sign the vendor pool needs widening, not that the exception is broken.
