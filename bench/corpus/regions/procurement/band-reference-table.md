# Approval bands at a glance

| Band | Approver | Quotes |
|---|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead, delegated | 1 |
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | Division head, delegated | 2 |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | CEO | 3 |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | CEO, with a separate proposal | 3 plus a comparison |

## Which side of the line

The ranges are written as lower-bound-inclusive. A purchase of exactly 500,000 KRW sits in the division head band, not the team lead band, and a purchase of exactly 3,000,000 KRW needs the CEO, not the division head. **People read "500,000 – 3,000,000" as excluding the boundary figure on both ends, and it only excludes the top one.** If the request form shows a round number that matches a band edge, check which side it lands on before routing it.

## What the table does not show

It does not show whether the amount is net of VAT, how an annual agreement gets banded, or what happens when a small recurring order grows into a large one over the year. Those are separate rules, and getting the band right on paper is only half the job — the vendor still has to be registered and, above the lowest band, signed to the supplier code of conduct before the first order goes out.
