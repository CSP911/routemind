# Borrowing a signed original

1. Submit a request to the office & purchasing desk naming the contract ID and the reason for borrowing.
2. Sign the checkout log, stating an expected return date.
3. Return the original within 5 business days of checkout, or extend the entry before that date passes.
4. GA re-files the original and closes the log entry only once it has physically received it back.

## The trap

**Borrowers often treat the original as their own reference copy and photocopy it instead of returning it on schedule.** A photocopy does not close the log. The entry stays open — and the contract counts as out of custody — until the physical original (or archived e-signature record) is back in GA's hands, no matter how many copies were made in the meantime.

The fields the log actually captures are covered separately.
