# Receiving and inspection

Receiving and inspection form a critical part of asset management and quality assurance. This section focuses on verifying goods upon arrival, dealing with short or damaged deliveries, and the proper procedure for rejecting items. It is essential to document and handle delivery mismatches to safeguard company interests. **One notable pitfall is neglecting timely inspection, which can lead to acceptance of incorrect or damaged goods.** Detailed procedures for each scenario ensure compliant and efficient receiving processes.
