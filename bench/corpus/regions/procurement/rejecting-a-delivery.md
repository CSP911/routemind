# Rejecting a delivery

A delivery can be refused at the dock, before a goods receipt note is even opened, when it clearly fails one of a small set of checks: wrong item entirely, spec that doesn't match the order, damage severe enough that the goods are unusable, or no delivery note referencing a valid purchase order at all. Anything short of that — a minor shortfall, cosmetic damage on part of a shipment — is handled as a discrepancy against an accepted delivery, not a rejection.

A rejected delivery is noted as refused on the courier's paperwork, logged with the GA desk, and reported to the vendor as a rejection rather than a receipt. The purchase order is not cancelled by this. It stays open at its original value and band until the vendor either replaces the shipment or the order is formally cancelled through the same approval route it was raised on.

**Rejecting the goods does not undo the approval already given.** A 6,000,000 KRW order rejected at the dock is still a CEO-approved 6,000,000 KRW commitment until someone actively cancels it — refusing delivery doesn't quietly close it out.

Goods accepted despite reservations, rather than rejected outright, are recorded through the standard receiving note instead.
