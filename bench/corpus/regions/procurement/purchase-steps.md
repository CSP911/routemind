# Purchase request

1. Write down what is needed, how many, and the date it is wanted.
2. **Get quotes** — how many depends on the amount band → Approval thresholds by amount.
3. File the request as a draft. Attach the quotes and, for a new supplier, the vendor registration form.
4. Once approved, **the desk places the order.** Teams do not order directly.
5. After goods are received and checked, the tax invoice goes to accounting.

| What usually blocks it | Why |
|---|---|
| Only one quote | Fewer than the band requires — a sole quote needs a written reason |
| The supplier is not registered | Registration comes first; with none at the time of order, they cannot be paid |
| It was already bought | Buying first is not accepted as a rule, and can end up a personal cost |
