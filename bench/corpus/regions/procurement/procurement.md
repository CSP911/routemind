# Purchasing & Contracts

How to buy something the company does not already have, how far up the amount has to be approved, how
a new supplier is registered, and where the signed contract ends up. This area is about **things
bought from outside** — spending on a card that needs no supplier is Expenses, and what delegated
authority is in the first place is Documents & Approval.

## The order is fixed and it is the whole area
**Register the supplier → get the quotes → get the request approved → the desk places the order →
goods are checked in → the invoice goes to accounting.**

Almost every problem here is that order being run backwards. Agreeing a price with a supplier before
they are registered is the most common; buying the thing first and asking afterwards is the most
expensive, because it can end up a personal cost.

## Why teams do not order directly
Not bureaucracy — payment. An order placed by a team to an unregistered supplier produces an invoice
nobody can pay, and the supplier chases the person who called them. The desk places orders so that
there is always a registered counterparty and a purchase order behind every invoice that arrives.

## Amounts
Net of VAT, always. Orders to the same vendor for the same item **within three months are added
together** to find the band, so a purchase split across two requests is banded on the total. An annual
agreement is banded on the annual total, not the monthly rate.
