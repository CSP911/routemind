# Bands and quotes in practice

This section describes the application of procurement bands and how quotes are managed within each. Procurement bands determine the approval levels needed based on the total expenditure. The need for sole quotes is detailed, providing clarity on when one is sufficient and the reasons required for its acceptance. The importance of annual agreements is emphasized since these can streamline ordering processes and ensure consistency across the year. When splitting orders across bands or dealing with repeat purchases that cross a band, caution is vital. **One common pitfall is failing to document the rationale for sole-source procurement, which can lead to approval delays.** Detailed pages under this section provide step-by-step guidance for each scenario.

To deepen understanding, see the Process and components of purchase approval guidelines.
