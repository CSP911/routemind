# Vendor performance and renewal

A structured approach to vendor performance assessment ensures that suppliers meet the company's standards and contractual obligations. This section provides a framework for reviewing supplier achievements, highlights issues like missed delivery dates, and explains how to terminate unsatisfactory vendor relationships. The renewal decision process is addressed, underlining the importance of performance metrics. **Failing to document performance evaluations can lead to unchecked renewals of underperforming suppliers.** Each page offers detailed guidance on these aspects.

For comprehensive assessment, refer to Supplier due diligence materials.
