# Sanctions and ownership checks

## Steps

1. Run the legal entity name from the vendor form against the current sanctions and restricted-party lists.
2. Identify any individual or entity holding 25% or more of the supplier, directly or through a holding structure.
3. Run each of those beneficial owners through the same lists.
4. Record the result — clear or flagged — against the vendor file before registration completes.
5. Re-run the check annually for any vendor still active.

| Ownership stake | Check required |
|---|---|
| Under 25% | Not individually screened |
| 25% and over | Beneficial owner screened by name |
| Company itself | Always screened, regardless of ownership split |

## The trap

**The check is on the beneficial owner, not just the entity name.** A supplier can pass a name-only screen while being majority-owned by a sanctioned individual through an unrelated-looking holding company. Matching the legal name and stopping there is the single most common way this check gets waved through when it shouldn't be.

A flagged result here feeds straight into disqualification — what happens next is covered in the disqualification triggers table.
