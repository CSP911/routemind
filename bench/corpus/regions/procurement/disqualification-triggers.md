# What disqualifies a supplier

Any one of these is sufficient on its own. None of them are overridden by approval level — a CEO-approved purchase order does not survive a disqualification found afterward.

| Trigger | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Sanctions list match — entity or beneficial owner (25%+) | New and existing vendors |
| Refusal to sign the supplier code of conduct | New vendors only, blocks registration |
| Refusal of the annual code re-check | Existing vendors, triggers suspension |
| False information on the vendor registration form | New and existing vendors |
| Criminal conviction of the entity or an owner related to its business | New and existing vendors |

## The trap

**Disqualification is retroactive, not just a gate at registration.** A vendor cleared eighteen months ago and now mid-contract is not grandfathered in if a sanctions hit surfaces on a routine re-check. Open purchase orders are frozen and the contract custody file is flagged, even though the original registration was done correctly at the time.

Where a disqualified vendor's existing paperwork is filed pending closure is the same place a normal contract lives — see contract custody.
