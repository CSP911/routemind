# Splitting one purchase into several orders

The band is set by the size of the purchase, not the size of the purchase order. Ordering 2,000,000 KRW of the same equipment this week and another 2,000,000 KRW of it next week, to keep each order under the 3,000,000 KRW line, is one 4,000,000 KRW purchase split across two documents.

## The test is intent, not timing

**A genuinely staged delivery — agreed up front, on one contract, invoiced in instalments — is one purchase and bands on the full amount.** Two unrelated needs that happen to arrive the same month are two purchases and band separately. The difference is not how many order forms exist; it's whether the requester could reasonably have raised it as one request and chose not to.

Division heads approving a string of same-vendor, same-category orders that individually sit just under 3,000,000 KRW should ask whether they are looking at a split purchase before signing the second one. This is the same pattern that shows up in an annual agreement banded month by month instead of on its yearly total, and it gets caught the same way — by looking at the vendor's running total, not the single form in front of you.
