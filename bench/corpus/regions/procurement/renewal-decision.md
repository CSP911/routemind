# Making a renewal decision

## What triggers the decision
A renewal decision is due whenever an annual agreement approaches the end of its term, timed off the annual total that set its approval band in the first place — not off any single invoice inside the year.

## Outcome bands
| Performance review outcome | Renewal action |
|---|---|
| Continue | Renew at existing terms |
| Continue with conditions | Renew with a written corrective commitment attached |
| Flagged for non-renewal | Contract runs to term end, then closes |
| Disqualification trigger | Ends immediately, renewal does not apply |

## Approval
A renewal is approved at the same level the annual total would require if it were a new purchase — a 400,000 KRW/month service renews at the CEO band, because its annual total sits at 4,800,000 KRW, exactly as it did when first approved.

**Renewing on the same terms is still a new approval, not a continuation of the old one.** Letting a contract roll over without running it back through its band is the same gap that lets repeat-purchase creep happen at renewal instead of at the order level.

A vendor coming up for renewal also needs its code of conduct re-sign checked separately — a lapsed signature blocks the next purchase order even if the renewal itself is approved.
