# Registering an overseas supplier

## What replaces the local registration certificate

An overseas supplier has no Korean business registration number. File the vendor form with its certificate of incorporation or a tax residency certificate from its home jurisdiction instead, translated into English or Korean if the original is in neither.

## Screening runs exactly as it does for a domestic vendor

The 25% beneficial owner rule applies without adjustment: identify anyone holding 25% or more of the supplier, directly or through a holding structure, and screen them by name alongside the entity itself.

**Being outside Korea is not an exemption from sanctions screening.** The check runs against the same lists for an overseas entity as a domestic one, it applies to existing overseas vendors as much as new ones, and a supplier operating from a jurisdiction with no local sanctions regime of its own is not screened any less closely for it.

Settle how the contract states currency and payment terms before registration closes — that detail affects the account details filed, not the screening itself.
