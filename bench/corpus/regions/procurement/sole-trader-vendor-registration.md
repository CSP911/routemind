# Registering a sole trader as a vendor

## What replaces the corporate certificate

A sole trader has no corporate registration certificate to file. In its place, the vendor form is filed with the individual's business registration certificate — the one issued by the tax office to a sole proprietor — and the same bank details section as any other vendor.

## Whose name the bank account carries

The account offered for payment has to be in the sole trader's own registered name, exactly as it appears on that certificate. An account belonging to a spouse, an employee, or a separate trade name the person also operates under does not satisfy this.

**A sole trader operating under a shop or brand name still registers under their personal name.** The signage out front can say anything; the certificate and the bank account both have to carry the name the tax office has on file, and a mismatch between the two blocks registration the same way it would for a company.

Where the account name and the certificate name don't line up at all, treat it as an account holder mismatch rather than a sole trader question.
