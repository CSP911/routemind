# Renewal notice deadlines

## Notice periods by band
| Annual value band | Notice required before term end |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | No fixed notice, close or renew informally |
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | 30 days |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | 60 days |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | 90 days |

## Why the window matters
Most annual agreements carry an evergreen clause: if no notice is given, the contract renews automatically at the existing terms for another year. The notice period is how long before term end the renewal decision — or the decision not to renew — has to be recorded.

**Missing the notice window does not leave the contract open, it renews it.** A non-renewal flagged from a performance review after the deadline has passed still has to run through the full term it just auto-renewed into; the flag only takes effect at the next window.

The band a contract is measured against here is its annual total, the same figure that decided its approval level going in.
