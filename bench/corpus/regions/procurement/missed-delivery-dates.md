# A supplier who missed agreed dates

## The pattern
A packaging supplier's purchase orders specified delivery within 10 business days. Three consecutive orders arrived 4, 6, and 9 days late, each time with a different reason given after the fact rather than before the date passed.

## What should have happened at the first miss
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Log the miss against the purchase order, not just the goods receipt note |
| 2 | Notify the requesting team lead in writing |
| 3 | Ask the supplier for a corrective commitment before the next order is placed |

## Where this one went wrong
**Each late delivery was recorded as a receiving note, not as a missed-date event.** Nothing on the receiving side flags a delivery as late — it only records what arrived and its condition. By the third instance there was no single record showing a pattern, only three separate goods receipt notes that each looked, on their own, like a minor delay.

A pattern like this is exactly what a performance review is meant to catch before it reaches the point of ending the relationship outright.
