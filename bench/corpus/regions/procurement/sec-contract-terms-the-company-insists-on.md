# Contract terms the company insists on

The company enforces specific contract terms to protect its interests and standardize agreements. This section covers the mandatory clauses, such as payment terms and liability, while also highlighting areas open to negotiation. Properly balancing enforced and negotiable terms is crucial. **A common blunder is misunderstanding which terms are standard and non-negotiable, leading to disputes or disruptions in contract finalization.** The pages that follow detail each standard clause and provide insights into negotiating permissible aspects.
