# Standard contract clauses

## What every contract carries
Every vendor contract, regardless of value, includes six standing clauses: scope of work, payment terms, confidentiality, termination for cause, governing law (Korea), and a clause requiring the supplier's continued adherence to the code of conduct. These are drafted once by Legal and dropped into the template — no buyer or division head has authority to remove one, even at a supplier's objection.

## What varies by value
Liability caps and audit-access clauses scale with the contract's annual value, using the same bands that set approval:

| Annual value | Liability cap clause | Audit-access clause |
|---|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Not required | Not required |
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | Standard cap, no negotiation | Not required |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | Standard cap, no negotiation | Required |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | Negotiable within Legal's range | Required |

## The trap
**A supplier who insists on their own paper instead of the company template is not offering a shortcut — it is a sign the six standing clauses are about to go missing.** Any contract on supplier paper needs a full clause-by-clause Legal check before signature, not a skim.

Once the clauses are in place and the contract is signed, what happens to the paper itself follows the custody rule already covering originals.
