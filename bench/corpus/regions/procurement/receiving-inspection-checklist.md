# Checking goods in

1. Match the delivery note to the purchase order number. If there's no PO reference on the delivery, hold the goods at the dock and check with the GA desk before accepting.
2. Count units against the ordered quantity. Do this before signing, not from memory afterward.
3. Open enough packaging to check for visible damage — dents, crushing, moisture, broken seals. A sealed outer box tells you nothing about what's inside it.
4. Check the goods roughly match the spec ordered — model, size, grade — where that's checkable on sight.
5. Note anything wrong directly on the delivery note before signing it, and keep a copy.
6. Complete the goods receipt note the same day, recording what was actually received, not what was ordered.

**Partial inspection counts as full acceptance.** Checking the top layer of a pallet and signing for the whole shipment leaves no basis for a claim if the bottom layer turns out short or damaged — the signed note says the full quantity arrived in good order.

Anything found short or damaged at this stage moves to the relevant handling procedure, not back through this checklist.
