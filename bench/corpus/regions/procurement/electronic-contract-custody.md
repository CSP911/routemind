# Custody for e-signed contracts

An electronically signed contract does not need a paper original. The signed record held on the approved e-signature platform, together with its audit trail and certificate of completion, is the original for custody purposes.

## What GA still files

GA files the completion certificate and a PDF export of the signed document in the same contract register used for paper originals, cross-referenced to the vendor and contract ID. This satisfies the filing requirement even though nothing physical sits in the cabinet.

## The trap

**Printing an e-signed contract and treating the printout as the master copy is backwards.** The printout is a reference copy only. If the platform account lapses or the certificate is never exported and archived, custody is broken even though a paper copy exists on someone's desk — because the paper was never the original to begin with.

If the underlying agreement later expires or is terminated, the same retention rules apply to the archived certificate as to any paper original.
