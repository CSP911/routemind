# Liability and indemnity position

## The fixed position
Every contract at or above 500,000 KRW carries an indemnity clause covering IP infringement, breach of confidentiality, and gross negligence, with no cap on any of the three. This is a fixed company position — no supplier negotiates a cap on gross negligence or IP indemnity regardless of contract size.

## What can move
Ordinary liability for late delivery or defective goods carries a cap that scales with annual contract value:

| Annual value | Liability cap (ordinary breach) |
|---|---|
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | 100% of contract value |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | 150% of contract value |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | Negotiable, Legal sign-off required |

## The trap
**A supplier's standard terms almost always cap all liability at 100% of contract value, folding gross negligence and IP infringement into the same capped bucket as ordinary breach.** Signing that version silently waives the uncapped indemnity the company insists on. Any redline touching the liability section needs a side-by-side check against the standard clause, not a read-through for tone.

Liability terms sit inside the same document that custody rules protect once it's signed — a contract with a quietly capped indemnity is exactly what an audit hold is meant to catch.
