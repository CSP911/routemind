# Reviewing a supplier's performance

## When a review happens
Every vendor above the lowest approval band is reviewed at least once a year, timed to fall before their supplier code of conduct re-sign or contract renewal date, whichever comes first. High-value or single-source vendors can be reviewed more often at the division head's discretion.

## What the review covers
| Area | Checked against |
|---|---|
| Delivery | Dates and quantities on the goods receipt note |
| Quality | Rejection and rework record |
| Documentation | Registration certificate, bank details, code of conduct status |
| Pricing | Quoted vs invoiced amounts |

The division head signs off the outcome: continue, continue with conditions, or flag for non-renewal.

## What a review cannot do
**A clean performance score does not clear a sanctions or beneficial-owner match.** Disqualification triggers act independently of how well a vendor has performed — a supplier can pass every delivery and quality check and still be blocked or removed the moment a screening hit comes back.

A conditional continue outcome is the input a renewal decision starts from, not a substitute for one.
