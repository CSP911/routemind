# Contract custody

Where a signed contract lives, who holds it, and for how long. The rules are on the page below; this
is what they are protecting against.

## The problem it solves
A contract kept by the team that signed it is a contract that disappears when the team reorganises.
Every company discovers this the first time someone asks for an agreement signed four years ago and
the only person who knew where it was has left. So **one of the two signed originals goes to the
records room** and the team keeps neither.

## The same-day rule
The original is filed and the scan uploaded on the same day. Not because a day matters in itself, but
because "I will scan it tomorrow" is how originals end up in a drawer for a year. The scan is what
people actually use; the original is what is produced if there is ever a dispute.

## Electronic contracts
There is no original to file. Keep the PDF **together with the signing certificate** — the PDF alone
does not evidence who signed it, and separating the two at filing time is the equivalent of losing the
original.

## When the contract ends
Nothing happens. **The retention period governs, not the end date** — a contract that ended in 2025
with a ten-year retention is kept until 2035, and destroying it early is the error. Retention is
counted from the end of the business, so the clock starts when the contract ends rather than when it
was signed. → Document retention.

## Borrowing one
Logged with the records desk, and originals do not leave the building. If an outside party needs to
see it, send the scan.
