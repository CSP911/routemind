# Short deliveries

A shipment of 200 units arrives with 180 in the boxes. The receiver counts before signing, notes "180 of 200 received, shortfall noted" on the delivery slip, and records the actual count of 180 on the goods receipt note. The vendor is notified the same day, and the GA desk is copied so it can be tracked against the open purchase order.

The purchase order itself doesn't shrink to match. It stays open at the original quantity until the vendor either ships the remaining 20 units or issues a credit for them. Whichever happens, the goods receipt note is what invoicing checks against — an invoice billed for 200 units against a receipt showing 180 will be queried, not paid in full.

**A short delivery does not reduce the amount that was approved.** The purchase went through approval at its full value; a shortfall is a fulfilment problem to resolve with the vendor, not a reason to quietly re-scope the order down and let the difference disappear unrecorded.

Damage found alongside a shortfall is handled the same way as any other damaged-goods case — the two aren't mutually exclusive and both get noted on the same delivery slip.
