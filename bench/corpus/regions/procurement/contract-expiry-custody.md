# Custody after a contract ends

A contract ending does not close its custody file. The signed original — or its archived e-signature record — is retained for 5 years from the date the contract ended, not from the date it was signed.

| Status | Retention period runs from |
|---|---|
| Ended by expiry | End date, for 5 years |
| Terminated for cause | Termination date, for 5 years |
| Superseded by a renewal | End date of the superseded contract, for 5 years |

## The trap

**Teams assume the old original can be pulled once a renewal is signed and filed.** It can't. The expired contract's original stays in custody for its own 5-year window even after a replacement is in force, because tax and audit reviews can still reach back into the superseded term.

If the original is pulled into an audit before that window closes, the retention clock behaves differently again.
