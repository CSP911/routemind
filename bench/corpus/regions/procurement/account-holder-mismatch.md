# Vendor account holder mismatches

## What has to match

The bank account filed with a vendor's registration has to carry the exact name on their registration certificate — or, for a sole trader, the exact personal name on their individual business registration.

## Mismatches that come up

| Situation | Why it's blocked |
|---|---|
| Payment routed to an agency's personal account | Agency is not the registered vendor |
| Subsidiary's invoice, parent company's account | Names don't match the contracting entity |
| Company name on the account is abbreviated or an old trade name | Doesn't match the certificate on file |

**A mismatch is not resolved by a covering letter promising to fix it later.** Registration stays blocked until the account name and the certificate name are identical, because the account is what the whole check is protecting — a clean certificate paired with someone else's account defeats the purpose of having checked the certificate at all.

A sole trader hitting this is usually an account still held in a family member's name from before the business was registered.
