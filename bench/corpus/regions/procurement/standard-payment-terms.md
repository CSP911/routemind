# Standard payment terms

## Default schedule
Standard terms are 30 days from invoice, net of any advance already paid. Advances are the exception, not the default, and are capped by the same value band used for approval.

| Annual value | Advance allowed | Cap on advance |
|---|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | No | — |
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | No | — |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | Yes, with CEO approval | 20% of contract value |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | Yes, with CEO approval | 30% of contract value |

## Milestone payments
Contracts running longer than one quarter may split payment against delivery milestones instead of a single invoice, but each milestone still triggers the 30-day clock independently — there is no blanket extension for the final milestone.

## The trap
**An advance paid against a purchase order that was never re-approved for the advance clause is an unauthorised payment, not a timing issue.** The advance limit sits inside the contract text, not the purchase request, so raising the request at the right band doesn't itself clear the advance — Finance checks the contract before releasing payment.

Payment terms only bind while the contract exists; what happens to the signed original afterward is covered separately.
