# What can be negotiated

## Open to negotiation
A buyer may vary delivery schedule, payment currency (for overseas vendors), and the ordinary-breach liability cap above the 10,000,000 KRW band. Everything else — the six standing clauses, the indemnity position, the code of conduct clause, governing law — is fixed regardless of who is asking.

## Who signs off on a variation
| Variation | Sign-off required |
|---|---|
| Delivery schedule | Division head, same as purchase approval |
| Payment currency | Finance |
| Liability cap (10,000,000 KRW and over only) | Legal, then CEO |
| Anything else | Not available — use the standard clause |

## The trap
**"Everything is negotiable if the vendor is big enough" is the assumption that gets a buyer into trouble.** Vendor size and purchase value don't open up terms the value band doesn't already permit — a large contract with a major supplier still can't waive the code of conduct clause or cap gross-negligence indemnity, no matter how the negotiation is going.

Once a variation is agreed, it has to appear in the signed original itself, not in a side letter — a side letter isn't what custody or a later audit hold will go looking for.
