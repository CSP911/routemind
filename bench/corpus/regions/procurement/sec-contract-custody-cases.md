# Contract custody cases

Contract custody is critical for procurement integrity and compliance. This section outlines best practices for handling electronic contracts, monitoring contracts that ended, and processing requests to borrow original contracts. Guidelines for dealing with contracts under audit are provided, along with strategies to address and recover missing originals. **A frequent issue is the assumption that an electronic copy suffices when the original is required during audits.** Each page in this section provides specific instructions to mitigate such risks.

For additional information, refer to the Document retention techniques and challenges documentation.
