# Getting a sole-quote purchase approved

1. Confirm the purchase actually qualifies — exclusivity, contract continuity, or genuine emergency. If none applies, get a second quote instead of writing a waiver.
2. State the ground on the request form in the reason field, naming which of the three it is. "Could only find one supplier in time" is not itself a ground.
3. Route the request to the approver for the amount's band, exactly as if the full quote count had been met. A sole-quote purchase in the CEO band still goes to the CEO, still with the separate comparison proposal if it's 10,000,000 KRW or over — the proposal in that case compares the sole quote against the last known market rate, not against competing quotes.
4. File the reason with the request, not just in an email thread. It is what a later audit will look for.

## The step people skip

**Step 3 is the one that goes missing** — teams treat "sole quote approved" as informal permission to route the purchase to whoever is convenient, rather than to the approver the band actually requires. The quote count and the approver are set independently by the band; a waiver only ever changes the first one. Any vendor reached this way is still subject to the same code-of-conduct signature threshold as one selected through full competition.
