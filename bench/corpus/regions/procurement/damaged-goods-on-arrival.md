# Damaged goods

Damage is recorded before it's discussed. Photograph the item and its packaging as found, before unpacking any further — vendors and couriers routinely dispute damage claims where the packaging wasn't kept or photographed. Note the damage on the delivery slip in specific terms ("carton crushed on one corner, contents visibly dented") rather than a general "damaged" note, and complete the goods receipt note showing the item as received-damaged, not received-good.

Notify the vendor the same day. Most vendor agreements only allow a claim within a short window after delivery, so a damage note filed a week later may already be outside the vendor's own claims period even though it's well within any internal deadline.

**Discarding the packaging clears the scene the vendor needs to see.** A replacement or credit claim is far harder to support once the box, the pallet wrap, or the packing material has gone to recycling — keep it until the vendor confirms the claim is resolved.

Whether the item is repairable, replaceable, or a straight credit is a vendor negotiation the GA desk runs from here; receiving's job ends at documentation.
