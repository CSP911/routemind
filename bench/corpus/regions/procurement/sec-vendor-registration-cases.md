# Vendor registration cases

Effective vendor registration is foundational for procurement operations and compliance. This section covers registering sole traders, overseas suppliers, dealing with mismatched account holders, making one-off purchases, and handling suppliers reluctant to provide necessary documentation. **Failure to accurately capture details during registration can lead to significant processing delays and compliance issues.** Detailed processes in this section aid in overcoming common hurdles.

Consult Vendor registration for a one-off purchase for specific guidance.
