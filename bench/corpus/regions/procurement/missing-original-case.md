# A contract original can't be found

## What to check first

- The checkout log, for an open borrow entry that was never closed.
- The archive shelf under adjacent vendor codes — the most common cause is a misfile at the point of return.

## Escalation

If the original still isn't located, report it to the office & purchasing desk within 2 business days of discovery. GA then requests a certified copy from the counterparty, or, if that isn't available, arranges re-execution of the contract.

## The trap

**Teams sometimes treat a missing original as a filing inconvenience and let it sit unresolved rather than reporting it.** It isn't minor: an unreported missing original that surfaces during an audit is treated with the same severity as a fabricated record, because there is no way to distinguish "misfiled" from "never existed" without the report on file.

A hold placed on a contract for audit is a different situation — the original is accounted for, just temporarily unavailable.
