# Vendor registration for a one-off purchase

## Registration still happens

There is no informal path for a purchase that will only happen once. The vendor form, registration certificate, and bank details are filed exactly as they would be for a supplier expected to be used repeatedly — the approval band is set by the amount, not by whether the relationship is expected to continue.

## What a small one-off purchase skips

A one-off purchase under 500,000 KRW does not require the supplier code of conduct to be signed before the first purchase order, in line with the general rule that the code applies from the division-head band upward. The vendor form and registration certificate are still filed regardless of size.

**Treating a one-off purchase as too small or too temporary to register is how unregistered suppliers end up paid.** The amount decides who approves it and how many quotes it needs; it has no bearing on whether the supplier gets a vendor record.

Repeated "one-off" purchases from the same supplier are worth checking against the split-purchase trap before the second or third one goes through.
