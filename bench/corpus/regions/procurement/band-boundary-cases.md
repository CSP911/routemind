# Boundary amounts and which band they fall in

| Situation | Amount used for banding | Band |
|---|---|---|
| Item priced 480,000 KRW, plus VAT | 480,000 KRW (net of VAT) | Under 500,000 KRW |
| Item priced 500,000 KRW, plus VAT | 500,000 KRW (net of VAT) | 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW |
| Monthly service at 400,000 KRW, ongoing | 4,800,000 KRW (annual total) | 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW |
| Two orders of 2,000,000 KRW, same need, two weeks apart | 4,000,000 KRW (combined) | 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW |
| Single one-off purchase at exactly 3,000,000 KRW | 3,000,000 KRW | 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW |

## The two figures that trip people up

**VAT and calendar timing are the two things that quietly move an amount into a different band from the one it looks like it's in on the invoice.** A price tag under 500,000 KRW can still land in the band above once VAT is stripped out for banding purposes — no, the rule runs the other way: banding is done net of VAT, so a gross price that looks like it clears 500,000 KRW may in fact sit under it once VAT is removed. Check the net figure first, always.

These edge cases are exactly where a split purchase or a mis-banded annual agreement first becomes visible — the amount on the form looks fine, and only the total tells the truth.
