# Banding an annual agreement

An annual agreement bands on what it costs over the year, not on the size of any single invoice. A 400,000 KRW monthly service is a 4,800,000 KRW decision — CEO approval, three quotes — not a 400,000 KRW one that a team lead could sign off month by month.

## Where this goes wrong

**This is the band people most often get wrong, and it is usually because the request was written up as "monthly cost" rather than "total contract value."** A requester who quotes the monthly figure to a team lead, gets a quick approval, and only later totals the year has effectively bypassed CEO approval without meaning to.

| What's on the request | What it should band on |
|---|---|
| "400,000 KRW/month, ongoing" | 4,800,000 KRW annual total |
| "1,200,000 KRW/quarter, 4 quarters" | 4,800,000 KRW annual total |
| One-off purchase, no renewal clause | The invoice amount itself |

A renewal at a new rate re-bands on the new annual total from the renewal date, not the old one. If a service crosses into the CEO band on renewal, the comparison proposal that band requires has to exist before the renewal is signed, not after.
