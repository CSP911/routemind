# The comparison proposal for the top band

A purchase of 10,000,000 KRW and over needs three quotes, the same as the band below it, plus a separate written proposal comparing them before the CEO signs. The proposal is not a summary of the quotes — it's the case for choosing one of them.

## What it has to contain

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supplier names and quoted amounts | Shows the CEO the actual spread, not just the winner |
| Basis for elimination | Why the other two were not chosen |
| Non-price factors | Delivery time, warranty terms, prior relationship |
| Recommending manager | Who is putting their name behind the choice |

**The comparison proposal is required even when the choice is obvious — a sole existing supplier at a clearly fair price still needs the form filled out, because the requirement is triggered by the amount, not by how close the quotes are.** Skipping it on the reasoning that "there was really only one sensible option" is the most common reason a top-band request comes back unsigned.

The proposal is filed alongside the request and quotes, under the same custody rule that governs the signed contract itself once the order is placed.
