# Purchasing & Contracts — where to start

| What you are asking | Where it is |
|---|---|
| How to ask for something to be bought | Purchase request |
| How far this amount goes, how many quotes | Purchase request > Approval thresholds by amount |
| A supplier we have never used | Vendor registration → Vendor registration form |
| Where the signed contract is | Contract custody |
| I need a person | Office & purchasing desk |

**The order is fixed.** Register the vendor → get quotes → get the request approved → the desk places
the order → goods are checked in → the tax invoice goes to accounting. Agreeing a price with a
supplier *first* and registering them afterwards is the most common reason work is sent back.

**Amounts are net of VAT**, and orders to the same vendor for the same item within three months are
added together to find the band.

**What this area does not answer**: what delegated authority itself is (Documents & Approval >
Delegated authority), whether you could just put it on the card (Expenses).
