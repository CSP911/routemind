# Goods receipt note

Every delivery, whether it arrives complete or not, gets a goods receipt note the day it's received. It records: purchase order number, vendor name, quantity ordered, quantity actually received, condition of the goods, any discrepancy noted (short, damaged, wrong item), and the name of whoever received and checked it.

This note is what invoicing matches against the vendor's invoice before payment is released — not the purchase order on its own. An invoice billed for the ordered quantity against a receipt note showing a shortfall or damage will be held pending resolution with the vendor, rather than paid and corrected later.

**A missing or backdated goods receipt note stalls payment, not just the record.** If the note isn't filed the same day the goods arrive, invoicing has nothing current to check the invoice against and will hold the payment until someone reconstructs what actually happened at the dock — which is harder to do accurately a week on.

Short deliveries and damaged goods both get recorded here in the same way; the form doesn't change, only the entries in the discrepancy field do.
