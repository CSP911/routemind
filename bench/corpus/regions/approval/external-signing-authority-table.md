# Signing authority by transaction type

| Transaction | Signs | Evidence to counterparty |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase order, under 500,000 KRW | Team lead | Company seal, seal certificate if requested |
| Purchase order, under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head | Company seal, seal certificate if requested |
| Purchase order, 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO | Corporate seal, seal certificate |
| Contract, any value | CEO | Corporate seal, seal certificate |
| Signing via agent | Named agent | Power of attorney plus seal certificate |
| Overseas agreement | CEO | Corporate seal, plus destination legalisation |

## The trap in the table
**Amounts here follow the same net-of-VAT rule as internal approval — a 2,900,000 KRW order plus VAT is still a division head's to sign, but the invoice a counterparty sends often shows the VAT-inclusive total, and reading that figure against this table sends it to the wrong signer.**

Once the right signer is identified, whether the agreement needs to travel with a power of attorney or a plain seal certificate is a matter of scope, not value — covered under power of attorney scope.
