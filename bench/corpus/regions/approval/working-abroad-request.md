# Approving a request to work from abroad

## Authority

Working from abroad is not leave and it is not a business trip, but it is decided the same way an overseas trip is: by destination, not by duration or cost.

| Request | Authority |
|---|---|
| Working from abroad, any duration | CEO |

A request that costs the company nothing — no flights, no advance, no client meetings — still needs the CEO's approval once the destination is outside Korea.

## Consent beyond approval

**Assuming this is a leave request because no company money moves is the trap.** Leave bands run on working days; this runs on destination alone, and destination puts it at the CEO regardless of how the requester frames it. Beyond the CEO's approval, Legal has to consent on tax and immigration exposure before the draft can close — a consent a domestic leave request never triggers, and one that can take longer to clear than the approval itself.

## Filing

The draft carries the same fields as any other request — purpose, dates, and what happens to the person's duties while they're away — but the destination field is what routes it, not the reason given for the trip.

Whether that destination was added after a domestic itinerary was already approved is a separate problem, and it is not fixed by amending the existing draft.
