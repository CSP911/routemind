# How long a policy proposal sits in review

A policy proposal does not get the default that ordinary purchase drafts get. Consent silence turns into acceptance for most draft kinds once the response window closes — but a policy change is not among them, because it commits every future draft in that area, not just the one under discussion.

## What silence does, by kind

| Draft kind | Silence after the window |
|---|---|
| Purchase, hire | Counts as consent |
| Policy change | Does not count as consent — an explicit response is required |

## The trap

**Treating a policy proposal like a purchase draft and letting the review window lapse does not clear it.** A proposal that sits with Finance or Legal for weeks without an answer is still unreviewed, not quietly approved, and publishing it anyway leaves the policy without the consent the file is supposed to show if anyone checks later.

Once every listed team has responded, the proposal is ready for publication, where a different rule governs when it actually takes effect.
