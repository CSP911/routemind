# The band is set by the quote on file, not the final invoice

A purchase draft is routed to a band based on the quote attached when it is filed. That routing is a snapshot, not a running total — if the price moves afterward, the file has to catch up before payment, not after.

## Where this bites

| Scenario | What has to happen |
|---|---|
| Quote rises within the same band | No re-routing needed; note the change |
| Quote rises across a band line | Draft corrected and re-routed to the correct approver before payment |
| Quote falls across a band line | Draft may be amended to the lower approver, but the original approval stands until it is |

**A quote of 2,950,000 KRW that becomes an invoice of 3,050,000 KRW after a late-added delivery fee still crosses into CEO territory** — the division head's signature on the original draft does not stretch to cover it, even though the increase looks small next to the total.

Whether that correction is filed as a full amendment or a lighter fix depends on how far the number moved, which the procedure for correcting a purchase draft sets out next.
