# Minute filing deadline

A decision made in a meeting is only as good as the minute that records it. The minute must be filed within five business days of the meeting.

## The window

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Meeting produces a decision | Minute filed within 5 business days |
| Minute not filed within the window | Decision treated as undocumented until it is |

Filing late does not undo the decision, but it leaves the decision unsupported in the file for as long as the minute is missing — the same exposure as an unfiled draft.

## What the clock runs from

The clock starts on the date of the meeting, not the date the decision takes effect and not the date a related draft is signed. A decision made on the 2nd and only minuted on the 9th has already run past the window even if the draft it supports is filed on time.

**The trap is assuming the draft's timeline covers the minute.** A purchase draft filed promptly does not excuse a late minute for the meeting where the purchase was actually approved — they are tracked separately.
