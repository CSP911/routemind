# Chains and consent

This section elaborates on the approval pathways and consent mechanisms within the organization. It details which team gives consent for different actions, how to handle objections, and the implications of missing or incorrect consents. **An often-overlooked trap is assuming consent and approval are interchangeable**, which can lead to unauthorized actions and procedural reversals. Ensure that everyone involved understands their role within the chain to maintain a smooth approval process.

For insights on how delegation impacts day-to-day operations, see the "Delegation in practice" section.
