# Breach notification window

A breach is any loss, leak, or unauthorised access to personal data, whether it happens on a company system or in an email sent to the wrong address. The window starts at confirmation, not at suspicion — a report of something that might be a breach still has to be escalated immediately, so confirmation itself does not eat into the clock.

## Deadlines

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Escalate to the records desk | Within 24 hours of confirmation |
| Notify affected individuals | Within 5 business days of confirmation |
| File the incident record | Within 5 business days of confirmation |

**Escalating late does not reset the clock — both deadlines still run from confirmation, not from whenever the records desk was told.** A team that discovers a breach on a Friday and escalates the following Wednesday has already used four of its five days before the records desk has even opened a file.

A breach involving a document already past its scheduled destruction date is still reportable; a record that should not have existed is not one that is exempt from being lost.
