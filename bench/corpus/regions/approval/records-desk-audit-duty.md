# The records desk's role during an audit

When Legal or Finance opens an audit, the records desk is the function that keeps the affected documents out of that year's destruction list — it does not decide what counts as "under audit" in the first place.

## What the role covers
- Receiving the hold notice naming which documents, or which kind and date range, are affected
- Marking those documents so they are excluded automatically when the January list is built
- Releasing the hold once Legal or Finance confirms the audit has closed, and only then

## What it does not cover
The records desk cannot open a hold on its own judgment because a document looks sensitive, and it cannot decide an audit has closed without confirmation from the function that opened it. Its job is custody of the flag, not the decision behind it.

## The trap
**Silence from Legal is not the same as a closed audit.** If a formal release never arrives, the records desk keeps the document off the destruction list indefinitely rather than assuming enough time has passed.

That same custody role is what keeps the templates, seals, and the retention schedule itself in one place, outside of any single audit.
