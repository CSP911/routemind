# Seals

Seals authenticate documents, providing assurance of their origin and integrity. This section explores the distinctions between company and corporate seals, usage of seal certificates, and the alternatives such as electronic signatures. Each type of seal holds specific legal implications and is used according to the document's destination and type.

The typical mistake is **employing a corporate seal for local transactions instead of the appropriate company seal.** This mistake can nullify agreements and require re-processing of important documents, especially for international filings.

For a deeper dive into the practical application and secure storage of seals, review "Seal custody and checkout" and "Electronic signature in place of a seal".
