# The division head band in daily use

A division head's band sits between the team lead's and the CEO's, and covers more than spend.

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Spend | Under 3,000,000 KRW, net of VAT |
| Trips | Domestic only |
| Expenses | Over-cap claims (beyond a team lead's normal limits) |
| Seal | The company seal |

## The seal that isn't the other seal

**The company seal is not the corporate seal.** A division head can affix the company seal to routine paperwork — vendor forms, internal certificates — but the corporate seal, used for contracts and formal filings with outside parties, stays with the CEO regardless of the amount involved. Drafters who see "seal" in a checklist and reach for whichever one is in the drawer file the wrong seal into the record, and the document has to be reissued.

Domestic trips follow the same amount-independent logic on the other side of the border — a trip's destination, not its cost, decides who signs it.
