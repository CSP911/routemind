# Drafts that mix two kinds

A single draft sometimes touches two kinds at once — a new hire who also needs a service contract, or a purchase that quietly changes how the team buys from then on. The drafter still has to pick one lane on the form, and the rule is simple: **route by the most restrictive kind present, not by the largest amount**.

Hiring and dismissal go to the CEO regardless of pay. Contracts go to the CEO regardless of value. If either shows up anywhere in the draft — even attached to a purchase well under the division head's cap — the whole draft moves to CEO level.

| Draft contains | Routes as |
|---|---|
| Purchase only | By amount |
| Purchase + contract | Contract — CEO |
| Purchase + hire | Hire — CEO |
| Purchase that sets a standing rule | Policy change — by what it touches |

**Splitting the package into two drafts to keep the purchase piece at division-head level does not work if the purchase only exists because of the hire or the contract** — reviewers treat them as one draft for routing purposes even when they are filed separately.

A draft that was split this way and caught late does not get a pass because it was filed in good faith; see how a wrongly classified draft is corrected.
