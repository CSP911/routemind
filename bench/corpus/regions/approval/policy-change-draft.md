# Policy change drafts

A policy change draft covers anything from an update to the expense claim form to a change in leave entitlement. The approver is not chosen by amount — a policy change usually has no price tag — but by which authority area the policy sits under.

## Routing by subject

- Changes confined to one team's working method: team lead
- Changes to spend limits, seals, or domestic travel rules: division head
- Changes to pay, hiring, dismissal, or contracts: CEO

## What to attach

- The current wording and the proposed wording, side by side
- Who is affected and from what date

## The trap

A policy change that reads like an internal memo — "clarifying" how overtime is logged — can quietly touch pay. **If the change affects anything on the CEO's list, it needs the CEO's sign-off even when the draft looks routine and the division head would normally handle process updates.**

Some policy changes are urgent enough that they get actioned before the draft is even written — the next document covers what still applies when that happens.
