# The approval behind a purchase

In the company, purchase approvals are structured around a framework of bands, quotes, and a supporting draft document. Each purchase request is categorized into a specific approval band based on its monetary value, which subsequently dictates the number of necessary quotes. The draft document, an essential part of the process, consolidates all relevant quotes and justifications related to the purchase.

**Common scenario:** Typically, for an average purchase request, the employee submits a draft that includes all required quotes, which are then reviewed by the designated approvers based on the band's requirements. This streamlined process ensures accountability and transparency, promoting efficient purchase approvals. 

**Potential issue:** The one notable problem arises when quotes or draft documents are insufficient or incomplete at submission. This can lead to procurement delays, as either the draft does not meet the band specifications, or required quotes are missing or inadequate. Employees must ensure comprehensive and precise submissions to avoid such setbacks.
