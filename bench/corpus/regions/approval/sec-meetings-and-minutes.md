# Meetings and minutes

In our corporate environment, meeting minutes play a crucial role in documenting decisions and ensuring accountability. This section outlines which meetings require formal minutes, the essential elements each minute must capture, and the proper storage procedures for these records. Careful attention should be paid to identifying key meetings such as board meetings, internal review sessions, and any strategic discussions pivotal to company operations. 

A common error is **failure to capture all attendees**, especially those attending via teleconference or other remote means. Missing this crucial information can introduce significant inaccuracies and affect decisions tied to the meeting's outcome. Importantly, all minutes must be stored in the designated corporate repository to maintain accessibility and comply with audit trails.

For detailed procedures on how to effectively minute meetings and avoid common errors, refer to "Minute contents" and "Minute retention" under related topics.
