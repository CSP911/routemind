# Withdrawing and refiling a draft

A draft gets withdrawn when the facts change before approval finishes — the vendor's quote moves, the hire falls through, the contract terms get renegotiated. Withdrawal closes the original draft; it does not pause it.

## How to refile

1. Mark the original draft withdrawn, with a one-line reason
2. Write a new draft with the corrected figures or terms
3. Route the new draft through the chain from the start

## The trap

**A refiled draft is a new document, not an amendment to the old one.** The approval chain restarts from the drafter, and the retention clock restarts too — a withdrawn purchase draft does not inherit the sign-offs it already collected, even if only the amount changed by a small margin.

This matters most when the amount crosses an authority band on refiling: a draft withdrawn at 480,000 KRW and refiled at 520,000 KRW moves from the team lead to the division head, as if it had never been drafted before.
