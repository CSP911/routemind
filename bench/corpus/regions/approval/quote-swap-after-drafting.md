# A vendor swapped after sign-off

A team lead drafted an office furniture purchase at 2,600,000 KRW, naming Vendor A's quote as the winning one. The division head signed off inside their band. Before payment went out, procurement found Vendor B could deliver the same order for 2,550,000 KRW and switched, without touching the draft.

## What was actually approved

The division head's signature approved a purchase from Vendor A at 2,600,000 KRW, on the quotes filed at the time. It did not approve "whichever vendor turns out cheapest before payment." The band did not change here, which is why the switch went unnoticed for months.

**The approval attaches to the quote in the file, not to the eventual purchase.** A cheaper substitute after sign-off still needs the draft amended and, if the file's comparability breaks or the band shifts, re-routed — not just a changed invoice.

## Why it surfaced

An audit matched the payment record to Vendor B and found no draft mentioning Vendor B at all. The purchase was real and the price was fine; the file simply didn't describe what happened.

Whether the amendment needs a full re-approval or can be filed as a correction is a question of degree, covered next.
