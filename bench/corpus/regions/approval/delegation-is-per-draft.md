# Delegation does not carry forward between drafts

A division head has been the one signing off most of a team's purchases for months — order after order landing comfortably under 3,000,000 KRW, each one correctly approved at the right level. None of that history changes what happens on the next draft.

## What actually decides it

Each draft is judged on its own amount, filed on its own date, against the band in force at that moment. A track record of approving similar purchases builds trust, not authority.

**A division head who has been closing out most of a team's 2,800,000 KRW purchases has no special standing to sign one at 3,200,000 KRW.** That draft crosses into the CEO's band the same as it would for a division head who had never seen the vendor before — the prior approvals do not average out, accumulate, or transfer any weight to this one. The signature is invalid regardless of how routine the purchase looked next to the others.

This is the same failure as a split purchase read the other way: one looks at a single amount split across drafts, the other looks at a single approver's authority stretched across drafts it was never delegated to cover.
