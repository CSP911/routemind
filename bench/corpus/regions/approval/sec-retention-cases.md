# Retention cases

Effective document retention ensures compliance with legal obligations and aids efficiency. This guide covers retention periods for different document types and outlines special cases like expired contracts, audit holds, and destruction lists. Adhering to retention guidelines preserves data integrity and supports audits by keeping relevant records readily available.

**A common pitfall is premature document destruction,** especially in the case of documents flagged for audit. This negligence can severely hinder audit processes and lead to regulatory penalties. Ensure each document is checked against the January destruction list and flagged exceptions are addressed.

For retention-related queries, see documents covering "Retention period by document" and "Retention holds during an audit".
