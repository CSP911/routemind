# Minute contents

A minute that describes what was discussed but not who decided, and under what authority, is not usable if the decision is ever questioned.

## Required fields

| Field | What it captures |
|---|---|
| Date and attendees | Who was in the room, including anyone consenting on behalf of a team |
| Decision | What was approved, rejected, or deferred |
| Authority exercised | Who made the decision and under which band — team lead, division head, or CEO |
| Reference | The draft or subject the decision relates to, if one exists |
| Action items | What follows and who owns it |

## Expected effect, where it applies

If the decision involves spend of 3,000,000 KRW or more, the minute states the expected effect the same way the draft would — what the decision is meant to achieve, not just what it authorises.

**The trap is recording the discussion and skipping the authority line.** A minute that says "the team agreed to proceed" without naming who held the authority to approve is a summary, not a decision record, and will not stand up if the sign-off is checked later.
