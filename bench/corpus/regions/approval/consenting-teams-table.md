# Which team consents to what

Consent is separate from approval. The approver signs the draft; a consenting team confirms that the part of the business they own has no objection. Both have to be present before the draft is closed out.

## Consent by draft kind

| Draft kind | Consenting team | What they check |
|---|---|---|
| Hire / dismissal | HR | Headcount plan, contract terms, statutory notice |
| Purchase, division-head band and above | Finance | Budget line, cash flow timing |
| Contract | Legal | Liability clauses, termination terms |
| Policy change | The team it changes | Whether the change is workable as written |

## Consent is not a second approval

A consenting team cannot approve a draft in place of the person the amount or subject requires — a division head's consent on a hire does not replace the CEO's sign-off, because hiring is always CEO's regardless of pay.

**A draft with every consent gathered but the wrong approver signing is still incomplete — consent fills a different slot in the chain, not the approver's.**

Which team's consent is missing, and what happens when it surfaces after the draft is already closed, is its own problem.
