# Quotes required for a purchase draft

The number of quotes a drafter has to collect scales with the band the purchase is expected to land in, not with how confident the drafter already is about the vendor.

## By spend band

| Band | Quotes required |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | One quote |
| 500,000 KRW to under 3,000,000 KRW | Two comparable quotes |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | Three comparable quotes, or a written sole-source justification |

Amounts are read net of VAT, the same as the band table itself.

## What counts as a quote

A quote has to come from a separate vendor, in writing, dated, and priced against the same scope as the others in the file. A verbal price over the phone is not a quote until it is written down.

**Two prices from the same vendor's sales desk, one marked "standard" and one "discount," are one quote, not two** — the file needs a second vendor, not a second number from the first one.

The count is a floor, not a target. Whether the quotes actually compare to each other is a separate question, covered next.
