# The life of a policy

A policy moves through four stages before it governs anything, and a fifth if it is ever retired.

## The four stages

| Stage | Who acts | What moves it forward |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Drafter | Subject, purpose and effective date stated on the draft |
| Review | Consenting teams, division head | Consent recorded, or objection resolved |
| Publication | CEO or division head, by subject | Effective date fixed, retention clock starts |
| Withdrawal | Whoever held the authority to approve it | Withdrawal notice filed, ownership closed out |

## The trap

**A policy that has cleared review is not yet a policy.** Consent from the affected teams only clears the draft to reach the approver — it is still a proposal until someone with the matching authority publishes it with a stated effective date. A draft sitting in review, however uncontested, changes nothing on its own.

Who holds that authority once the policy is live is a separate question from who drafted it — covered next.
