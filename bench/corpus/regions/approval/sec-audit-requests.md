# Audit requests

Audit requests manage the documentation needed for internal and external audits, ensuring compliance with disclosure policies and regulatory standards. Critical elements include providing timely and complete files as requested. **The pitfall is releasing non-essential documents without approval**, possibly leading to informational breaches or procedural non-compliance. It is crucial to understand what documents are necessary and ensure that the disclosure process is validated by appropriate authorities.
