# How long a quote holds

Vendors price quotes to hold for a stated period, usually printed on the quote itself. Once that period lapses, the number on file no longer reflects what the vendor would actually charge.

## The window

Unless the quote states otherwise, a quote is treated as current for 30 days from its date. If a draft is still moving through the chain after 30 days — held at a consenting team, waiting on a reviewer, queued for the CEO — the drafter has to obtain a refreshed quote before the draft reaches the approver.

## Why this isn't automatic

**Nothing in the routing checks a quote's date.** A draft can sit in a queue for six weeks and still show a quote that expired a month ago, and the system will let the approver sign it anyway. It is the drafter's job to notice and refresh, not the reviewer's job to catch it after the fact.

A refreshed quote that comes back higher and crosses a band needs the draft corrected before signature, not settled by adjusting the invoice later — the rule for exactly that is next.
