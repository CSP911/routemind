# Personal data

Managing personal data requires compliance with privacy regulations and internal policies to protect individual privacy and maintain data integrity. This section provides an overview of permissible data collection practices, obtaining proper consent, and executing subject access requests. It's critical to take care during the consent process, ensuring all individuals are fully informed about what data is gathered and how it is used.

**A significant compliance risk arises when data is collected without explicit consent.** This oversight can lead to data breaches and legal actions against the company. In case of a data breach, promptly follow the breach notification protocols to mitigate damage.

Consult related documentation like "Breach notification window" and "Handling a subject access request" for detailed procedures and forms.
