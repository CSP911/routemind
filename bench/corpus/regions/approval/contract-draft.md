# Contract drafts

A contract draft covers any agreement the company signs with an outside party — a vendor, a landlord, a client. It is one of the few draft kinds where the approval table does not decide the approver: contracts belong to the CEO by kind.

## Signing authority

| Authority | Applies to |
|---|---|
| CEO | Signing contracts, corporate seal |
| Division head | Company seal on other documents |

A division head can put the company seal on internal paperwork but cannot sign a contract, even a low-value one, with the company seal alone.

## Retention

| Document | Kept for | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Contracts | 10 years | Commercial Act |

## The trap

A drafter sees a 1,200,000 KRW service contract, checks it against the spend table, and routes it to the division head because the amount is under 3,000,000 KRW. **The spend table governs purchases, not contracts** — any contract, at any value, needs the CEO's signature and the corporate seal.

A policy change that alters standard contract terms follows a different path again, set by what the change touches rather than what it costs.
