# Advance authority by amount

An advance is money released before the spend it covers is complete — a trip, a bulk purchase, a deposit. It is approved on the estimated amount, net of VAT, using the standard spend table, and it is not final until reconciled against receipts.

## Authority by requested amount

| Requested amount, net of VAT | Authority |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead |
| Under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO |

Above 3,000,000 KRW the draft must also state the expected effect, exactly as a purchase draft would.

## The trap

**Approval is set by the amount requested, not by the amount eventually spent.** A 450,000 KRW advance stays a team lead's signature even if the traveller only spends 300,000 KRW of it — but if the actual spend comes in over 500,000 KRW, the shortfall in the request has to be re-routed before the advance can close.

What happens to an advance that is never reconciled on time is its own problem, covered next.
