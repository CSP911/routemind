# Advance reconciliation deadline

An advance is not closed the day it is issued. The clock starts when the trip ends or the purchase is complete, not when the money was released, and it runs until receipts are filed against it.

## The deadline

| Event | Reconciliation window |
|---|---|
| Domestic trip or purchase | 5 business days after return or delivery |
| Overseas trip | 10 business days after return |

An advance still open after its window counts as unreconciled on record, whether or not the money was actually spent correctly.

## The trap

**The deadline runs from when the spend finished, not from when someone gets around to filing the receipts.** A traveller who returns on a Tuesday and files the following Wednesday has already used four of the five business days before opening a spreadsheet. Reconciliation logged late is treated the same as an advance never reconciled at all.

A shortfall found once reconciliation finally happens is a separate problem from the deadline itself.
