# A contract draft pasted into a free translation tool

A team lead pasted a draft contract into a free translation tool to check a clause before it went to the CEO for signature. The tool was the consumer tier of a product IT had already approved — at enterprise level, for a different purpose. The consumer tier retains input for training, so the contract's commercial terms, including price, were stored outside the company before the document had even been signed, let alone filed.

The gap surfaced only during IT's quarterly review of tool usage, weeks later. Legal then had to assess whether the exposure needed disclosure, and the contract itself still went on to CEO signature and its full ten-year retention as if nothing had happened to it in between.

**The exposure is not proportional to intent.** A quick check treated as a throwaway task is retained by the tool the same way a year of company documents would be — nothing about a five-minute use limits what the vendor keeps.

The tool was removed from personal use and only the enterprise tier remains on the approved list.
