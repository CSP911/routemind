# Approvals about people

Approvals about people cover leave of absence, business trips, and working-abroad requests. Each type has its own approval chain and evidential requirements, both of which are crucial for ensuring compliance with company policy. Common issues include misaligned leave dates and inadequate trip justifications, which can delay approval or lead to subsequent inquiries. **The key trap is not synchronizing between the documented approval and the actual event dates**, which may result in payroll errors or non-compliance notices. For further details, consult specific guidance on process checklists for each approval scenario.

To explore approval chains that may involve moving financials, refer to the "Approvals that move money" section.
