# Document retention

| Document | Kept | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Contracts | 10 years | Commercial Act |
| Tax invoices and books | 5 years | Framework Act on National Taxes |
| Pay and employment contracts | 3 years | Labor Standards Act |
| Social insurance filings | 3 years | |
| Drafts and proposals | 5 years | Company |
| General business records | 3 years | Company |
| Unsuccessful applications | 6 months | Personal Information Protection Act — keeping them longer is a breach |

## Destroying
1. Each January the records desk lists what has passed retention.
2. The owning team checks it — **anything under litigation or audit is not destroyed even when its
   period has run.**
3. It is entered in the destruction log and shredded.

The clock starts at **the end of the business (contract end, fiscal year end), not the date it was
written**. That is the part most often got wrong.
