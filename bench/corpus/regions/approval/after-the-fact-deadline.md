# Deadline for after-the-fact drafts

An after-the-fact draft has to be filed within 5 business days of the action it covers. The clock starts on the day the purchase, hire, or other action actually happened, not the day someone remembers to write it up.

## Why the window matters

| Missed by | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A few days | Draft still accepted, flagged late |
| Beyond 5 business days | Treated as unfiled — action stands undocumented |

An undocumented action does not get reversed, but it leaves no draft to retain, no approval trail, and no record for the retention schedule to apply to later.

## The trap

**"Urgent" and "late" are not the same thing.** An action taken urgently still has to be filed within the 5-day window like any other after-the-fact draft — urgency justifies acting before approval, it does not extend how long the write-up can wait.

A draft that misses the window entirely is different from one that gets withdrawn and refiled on purpose — the next document covers that case.
