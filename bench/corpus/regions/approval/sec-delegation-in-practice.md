# Delegation in practice

This section examines delegation in operational settings, including various roles and the delegation process across different organizational levels. Delegation can include appointing a deputy, allocating split purchases, and managing activities that transcend typical financial bands. **The primary challenge is failing to clarify the limits of delegated authority**, which can result in actions beyond approved limits or inadequate oversight. Always confirm delegated roles and boundaries to ensure compliance and smooth operations.
