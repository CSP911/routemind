# The team lead band in daily use

A team lead's authority is the smallest of the three bands, and the one drafters lean on most because it needs no one else's signature.

## What is inside the band

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Spend | Under 500,000 KRW, net of VAT |
| Leave | Under 3 days |
| Direction | Within the team only |

Anything crossing a limit — a fourth day of leave, a cross-team instruction — goes up regardless of how small the difference looks.

## Where it breaks

The spend limit is read per draft, and drafters sometimes treat "per draft" as license to keep drafts small. **Five orders of 100,000 KRW placed the same week for the same piece of equipment are one purchase with one true amount, not five separate ones** — a consenting team or an auditor reconstructs the total, and the team lead's signature does not hold once it does.

The leave and direction limits do not stack with the spend limit either; a team lead cannot trade an unused day of leave authority for extra spend.

Splitting a single purchase to stay inside this band is its own failure mode, covered next.
