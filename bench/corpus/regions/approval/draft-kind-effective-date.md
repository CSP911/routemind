# When each kind of draft takes effect

Approval and effect are not the same date. A draft can be signed off today and still not apply until later — or, for the urgent kind, apply to something that already happened.

| Kind | Takes effect |
|---|---|
| Purchase | On approval — spend is authorised from that date |
| Hire / dismissal | On the start or end date stated in the draft |
| Contract | On the date of signature, not the date of internal approval |
| Policy change | On the date stated in the draft |
| Urgent after-the-fact | Retroactively, from the date the action was actually taken |
| Withdrawn and refiled | On the new approval date — the old draft's timeline does not carry over |

**A policy change approved today does not take effect today unless the draft says so** — drafters who leave the effective-date field blank often find teams still working under the old rule for weeks, with the approval date mistaken for the effective date.

This is also why an urgent draft is checked against the action date rather than the filing date — see how it is filed after the fact.
