# Acting as a deputy

When a division head is on leave or travelling, a deputy can be named to keep drafts moving rather than stall the whole team until they return.

## What the deputy inherits

The deputy steps into the absent approver's band for the duration of the delegation — up to 3,000,000 KRW, domestic trips, the company seal — not their own. A team lead named as deputy is not still capped at 500,000 KRW while acting in that role.

**This only holds with a written delegation naming the deputy and the period it covers.** Without that memo on file, the stand-in has no more authority than their own position gives them, and any draft they sign above their normal limit is as invalid as if no one had signed it at all. The memo is checked alongside the draft, not assumed from the org chart.

A deputy's authority also ends the moment the delegation period ends, even if a draft they started is still open — it has to be picked up by the returning approver or handed to someone currently authorised.

Authority that looks continuous from one draft to the next is a separate trap, covered under how delegation applies per draft.
