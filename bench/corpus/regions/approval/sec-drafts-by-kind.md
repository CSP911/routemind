# Drafts by kind

Drafts by kind include various documentation formats like purchases, hires, contracts, and policy changes. Understanding the drafting requirements is imperative for ensuring compliance and correctness across different kinds. **The widespread trap involves misclassifying a draft, which can lead to delays in processing or additional scrutiny**. It is vital to correctly identify the nature of each draft to align with the corresponding procedural guidelines, ensuring efficient approval and execution.
