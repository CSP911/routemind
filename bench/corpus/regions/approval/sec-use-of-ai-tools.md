# Use of AI tools

The company has established protocols governing the use of artificial intelligence tools. These guidelines specify which AI tools are sanctioned for usage, delineating tasks for which they may be used. All employees must adhere to restrictions prohibiting the submission of confidential data into these tools without explicit approval.

**Common situation:** Employees seeking to implement a new AI tool or utilize existing ones must first receive authorization. The approved tools list is periodically reviewed and updated to incorporate trustworthy technologies, facilitating secure and proficient operations.

**Key challenge:** A common risk arises when employees erroneously paste sensitive or proprietary information into AI tools without realizing such data breaches! Secure handling of information and adherence to data privacy norms is crucial for ensuring compliance and safeguarding company interests.
