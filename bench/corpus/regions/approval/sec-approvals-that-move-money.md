# Approvals that move money

This section focuses on approvals required for various financial transactions such as over-cap spending, advances, and emergency expenditures. Approvals in this category often intersect with budget regulations and audit checks. **A common pitfall is submitting a request without adequate justification or neglecting to update budget allocations**, leading to delays and potential denial. Ensure that all financial implications are captured accurately within the request to prevent reimbursement issues. For subsequent details on completing these processes efficiently, visit the guidelines under "Audit requests".
