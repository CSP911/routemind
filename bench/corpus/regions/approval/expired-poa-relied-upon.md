# A power of attorney relied on after its expiry

A division head issued a power of attorney to a team lead in March, covering a single 1,800,000 KRW equipment order with a named supplier, expiring at the end of April. The order was delayed and finally placed in June. The team lead signed using the same POA, and the supplier — who had kept the March copy on file — accepted it without asking for a current one.

## What went wrong
**The power of attorney had expired in April; nobody reissued it before the June signing.** Doc admin's file showed no active POA for that transaction past April 30. The June signature was not backed by any valid delegation, which meant the order was not properly authorised on the company side, regardless of what the supplier held on file.

## What should have happened
The team lead should have gone back to the division head for a fresh POA once the order slipped past April, naming the same transaction with a new expiry. A supplier holding an old copy is not a substitute for checking the current one — the date on the document is the only thing that matters, not how recently it was produced.

Reissuing correctly is no different from issuing the first one — same fields, same seal, covered under power of attorney issuance.
