# Annual agreements are judged on the year, not the invoice

A cleaning contract billed monthly at 260,000 KRW looks, invoice by invoice, like it sits comfortably inside a team lead's or division head's band. It doesn't — the agreement is one commitment, and its value is the total across the year it runs.

| What's billed | What's compared to the band |
|---|---|
| 260,000 KRW / month | 3,120,000 KRW / year, net of VAT |

At 3,120,000 KRW a year, this agreement is over the 3,000,000 KRW line and belongs to the CEO, even though no single invoice ever crosses it.

## Where it breaks

**A division head who signs the first month's invoice at 260,000 KRW is not the approver for the year.** The draft has to be filed once, against the full annual value, before the first payment goes out — not built up invoice by invoice until someone notices the running total. A drafter pricing a recurring agreement totals it across its full term before checking the spend table, the same way they would for a one-off purchase of the same size.

Renewals reset this calculation — a renegotiated rate is checked against the band again, not carried over from the prior year's approval.
