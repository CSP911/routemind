# Leave of absence authority

## Bands

| Duration | Authority |
|---|---|
| Under 3 days | Team lead |
| 3 days or more | Division head |

The count is in working days, not calendar days.

## The working-day trap

**A leave of absence measured against a long weekend can look like less time off than it actually costs in authority, or more.** Thursday and Friday off, either side of a Saturday and Sunday, is two working days — it stays with the team lead. The same four consecutive days starting on a Tuesday cross into three working days and need the division head, even though the calendar block looks identical on the leave form. Drafters who count by calendar span instead of working days route requests to the wrong desk in both directions.

## What doesn't change the band

Whether the leave is paid, unpaid, sick, or personal does not move the authority — only the working-day count does. A division head who wants to shorten someone's notice period or waive the count entirely is not exercising delegated authority; that request has to be drafted and approved on its own terms.

The same question — what actually decides authority, not cost or category, but a single deciding fact — comes up again with trips that change destination after approval.
