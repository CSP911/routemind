# Where minutes are kept

Once signed off, a minute is filed with the records desk, referenced against the decision or draft it records, and held for whatever retention that decision carries.

## What is kept where

| Location | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Records desk | Signed minutes, cross-referenced to the draft or decision |
| Team shared drive | Working notes only — not the filed record |
| Doc admin | Retention schedule that governs how long the filed minute stays |

A minute stored only on a team's own drive, however complete, is not the filed record. It has to reach the records desk to count.

## Access

Compliance and audit can pull a filed minute the same way they pull a draft, by its reference. A minute that never left the team's drive is invisible to that process, which is exactly the gap an audit finds months later.

**The trap is treating "we wrote it down somewhere" as equivalent to filing it.** A minute that exists only in someone's notes has the same standing as a decision no one recorded at all.
