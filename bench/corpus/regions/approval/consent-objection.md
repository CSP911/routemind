# When a consenting team objects

A team lead drafts a 2,600,000 KRW equipment purchase and routes it to Finance for consent before it goes to the division head. Finance objects: the budget line for the quarter is already committed elsewhere.

## What the objection blocks

The draft stops at Finance. It does not go to the division head with the objection attached and a note asking them to overrule it — the division head's authority is over the spend amount, not over Finance's objection. The drafter has to either revise the draft to answer the objection or withdraw it.

## What resolves it

Finance withdrawing the objection in writing, or the drafter changing the draft enough that the original objection no longer applies. Either way the resolution is filed with the draft, not just noted verbally between the drafter and Finance.

**An approver who signs a draft with an unresolved objection sitting in the file has not closed it out — the objection still has to be answered on the record.**

An objection at least leaves a trail. A consent that was never sought in the first place is harder to catch.
