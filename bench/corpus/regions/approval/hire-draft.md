# Hiring drafts

A hiring draft covers a new hire, a contract renewal that changes terms, or a dismissal. Unlike a purchase, the amount involved — starting salary, severance — does not set the approver. Hiring and dismissal sit with the CEO by kind, not by size.

## What has to be attached

- The role and reporting line
- Proposed salary and start date
- For dismissal: the basis and any severance calculation

## Expected effect

If the position affects annual payroll by 3,000,000 KRW or more, the draft states the expected effect the same way a large purchase would — what the role is meant to deliver.

## The trap

A team lead who normally closes out spend under 500,000 KRW cannot approve a part-time hire at 400,000 KRW a month on the strength of that authority. **Hiring and dismissal are CEO matters regardless of the pay figure attached to them** — the spend table in the general approval rule does not apply here.

Once a hire clears, its terms usually get written into a contract draft, which carries its own signing rule independent of amount.
