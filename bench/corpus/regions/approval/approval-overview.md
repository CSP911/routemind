# Documents & Approval — where to start

| What you are asking | Where it is |
|---|---|
| How to write a draft | Draft documents > Draft document template |
| Whom to put in the chain | Approval chain |
| Can a team lead close this out | Approval chain > Delegated authority |
| How long to keep it, may I destroy it | Document retention |
| The company seal | Records desk |

**Two things go wrong repeatedly.**
- **Leaving a consenting team out.** It can be approved and still not happen. Money needs accounting,
  people need HR, an outside contract needs the office desk.
- **Sending something upward that is already delegated.** Delegated authority is an authority, not a
  preference; routing around it out of habit breaks the rule in the other direction.

Retention is counted from **the end of the business, not the date the document was written**.

**What this area does not answer**: how many quotes an amount band needs (Purchasing & Contracts >
Approval thresholds). The authority lives here; the table that applies it to purchasing lives there.
