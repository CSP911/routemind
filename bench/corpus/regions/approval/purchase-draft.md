# Purchase drafts

A purchase draft covers any spend on goods or services — office equipment, software licences, vendor contracts under the spend threshold. It states the authority level, the amount net of VAT, and what happens if the purchase is never made.

## Authority by amount

| Amount (net of VAT) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead |
| Under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO |

## Expected effect

Above 3,000,000 KRW, the draft must state what the purchase is expected to achieve, not just what it costs. A CEO-level purchase with no stated effect gets sent back before it is read properly.

## The trap

**Amounts are net of VAT.** A drafter working from a quote of 480,000 KRW plus VAT sees a total near 528,000 KRW and routes it to the division head. It should stay with the team lead — the threshold is calculated on the net figure, and VAT is not part of it.

Once the amount and approver are settled, the same draft has to carry the right people in the chain — see how hiring drafts differ in who can never be skipped.
