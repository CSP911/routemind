# How long a consenting team has to respond

Consent has to be sought before a draft reaches its approver, and a consenting team has a limited window to raise an objection before the drafter can move on without an explicit reply.

## The default window

| Situation | What applies |
|---|---|
| Consenting team gives no response within 3 business days | Draft may proceed, logged as "no objection raised" |
| Consenting team objects within the window | Draft stops until resolved |
| Hire, dismissal, or contract drafts | No default lapse — explicit consent is required regardless of elapsed time |

## Why hires and contracts are excluded

These are the kinds where the consenting team's sign-off is checking something a missed deadline shouldn't paper over — statutory notice on a dismissal, or liability terms on a contract. Silence on these is not treated as consent no matter how long it runs.

**A drafter who lets a hire draft proceed on the 3-day default has not gathered consent — for these kinds, "no response" is a stalled draft, not a cleared one.**

Once consent is in hand, how it gets recorded in the draft file is what determines whether it survives a later audit.
