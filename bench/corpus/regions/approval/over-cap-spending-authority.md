# Over-cap spending authority

An over-cap expense is not a fresh purchase — it is spend that exceeds a line a team lead or division head already had approved earlier in the year. The overage itself still has to clear a chain, and the band it lands in is read from the overage amount alone.

## Authority by overage amount

| Overage amount, net of VAT | Authority |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead |
| Under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO |

## The trap

**A team lead cannot sign off a 700,000 KRW overage just because they approved the original 2,000,000 KRW budget line it belongs to.** The original approval covered the line as drafted; the overage is judged on its own size, the same way a fresh purchase would be. If the overage crosses into division-head or CEO territory, it needs that signature regardless of who owns the underlying budget.

Where the money for an overage is drawn before the paperwork catches up, the same logic applies to an advance issued against it.
