# Who clears an audit release, by document

The clearance needed to hand a document to an audit request depends on the document's own kind, not on how the request was phrased. The table below sets the floor; a request spanning several kinds is cleared at the highest one it touches.

| Document kind | Internal audit | External auditor |
|---|---|---|
| General business records, drafts under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head | Division head |
| Purchase drafts at 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO | CEO |
| Contracts | CEO | CEO |
| Pay and employment records | Division head | CEO |
| Documents under seal (company or corporate) | CEO | CEO |

**Division head clearance for internal audit does not carry over to an external auditor asking for the same file.** Internal audit sits inside the company's own chain, so a division head can answer for their own team's records — but an external auditor is outside that chain entirely, and pay records in particular move up to the CEO once the audience changes.

Nothing on this table overrides a hold already placed on a document — a flagged file stays out of any release until the hold clears, whichever kind of audit is asking.
