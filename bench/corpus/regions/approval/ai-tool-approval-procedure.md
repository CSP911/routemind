# Approving a new AI tool

1. The drafter proposes the tool, stating its intended use and whether it will process personal data.
2. IT confirms whether the vendor offers a non-training enterprise tier and reviews the security terms.
3. Legal consents if the tool will handle personal data, draft contract terms, or unpublished figures.
4. Approval authority follows the subscription cost against the ordinary spend bands: under 500,000 KRW net of VAT, team lead; under 3,000,000 KRW, division head; 3,000,000 KRW and over, CEO.
5. Once approved, IT adds the tool to the list and records the tier that was cleared.

| Subscription cost, net of VAT | Approves |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead |
| Under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO |

**A low cost does not shorten the consent step.** A 150,000 KRW tool that ingests customer contact details still needs Legal's sign-off, even though a team lead can approve the spend alone.

The tools this produces, and what still may not be entered into them, are covered on their own pages.
