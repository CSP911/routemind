# Emergency spend ceiling

An emergency is spend needed before there is time to draft and route anything — a burst pipe, a failed component, a client deadline that cannot wait. It does not remove the need for authority; it only changes when the paperwork catches up.

## Ceiling for acting alone

| Spend, net of VAT | Who may act before drafting |
|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead, alone |
| Under 3,000,000 KRW | Division head, or a team lead with the division head's verbal sign-off |
| 3,000,000 KRW and over | CEO's verbal sign-off, even in the emergency |

## The trap

**Filing promptly afterward does not cover a team lead who spent 1,200,000 KRW alone without getting the division head's verbal sign-off first.** The urgency explains why the draft came after the money moved; it does not explain why the wrong person authorised it. The draft still has to be filed inside the standard after-the-fact deadline, and it still has to carry the right signature.

Whether that money went out as a direct spend or as an advance changes what has to be reconciled once the dust settles.
