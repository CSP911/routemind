# When the retention clock starts

Retention periods are years measured from a fixed point, and that point is not always the day someone happens to file the document.

## Default: date of creation or filing
For most kinds — drafts and proposals, general business records, tax invoices — the clock starts on the date the document is created or filed. A general business record filed in March 2024 clears its three-year period in March 2027.

## Contracts: the clock starts at signing
A contract's ten-year period runs from the date the CEO signs it, not from the date it takes effect, not from renewal, and not from the date it ends. A contract signed in January 2020 that only started delivering services in June 2020 still clears retention in January 2030.

## The trap
**Early termination does not move the start date.** A contract cancelled after eight months is held for the full ten years from signing, same as one that ran its full term — termination shortens the relationship, not the retention period.

What early termination does change is worked through in the file it leaves behind.
