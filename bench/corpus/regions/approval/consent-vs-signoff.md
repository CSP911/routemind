# Consent is not sign-off

The chain has one approver, decided by amount or subject. It can have several consenting teams, decided by what the draft touches. Confusing the two roles is the most common way a chain gets built wrong.

## What consent can do

A consenting team can object, and an objection has to be resolved before the draft goes to the approver. What consent cannot do is stand in for the approver's authority. Finance consenting to a purchase does not mean Finance approved it — the division head or CEO still has to sign, according to the spend band.

## What silence means

If a consenting team says nothing within the response window, the draft may proceed, but that is recorded as no objection raised, not as agreement given. The distinction matters if the draft is challenged later.

**A drafter who reads silence as consent and files the draft as fully consented has recorded something that never happened — the file should show "no response," not "consented."**
