# Which meetings are minuted

The trigger is the outcome, not the calendar entry. A weekly team sync that ends without a decision needs nothing. The same sync that ends with a division head signing off an over-cap expense needs a minute, filed the same as if it had happened in a scheduled review.

## The rule

| Meeting produces | Minuted? |
|---|---|
| No decision, discussion only | No |
| A spend, hire, contract, or policy decision inside any authority band | Yes |
| A consenting team's objection or sign-off given verbally in the meeting | Yes |
| A status update with no approval attached | No |

## What counts as a decision

If someone with authority says "approved" or "go ahead" in the room, that is a decision, whether or not a draft was already circulating. The draft may follow, but the minute records the moment the authority was exercised.

**The trap is assuming an informal setting exempts the decision from minuting.** A division head approving a purchase over coffee is still a division head exercising a band, and it still needs the same paper trail as if it had happened at a desk.
