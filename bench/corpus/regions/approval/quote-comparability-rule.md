# Comparable quotes

Meeting the quote count does not, by itself, satisfy a reviewer. The quotes on file have to price the same thing.

## What comparable means

Each quote has to cover the same specification, quantity, delivery terms, and warranty period as the others. A quote for a bare unit and a quote for the unit with installation and a year of support are not two competing prices for one purchase — they are prices for two different purchases.

## Why this matters at the band line

A drafter choosing between quotes of 2,850,000 KRW and 3,150,000 KRW is choosing an authority band as well as a vendor, if the two quotes cover different scope. The reviewer has to be able to see that the cheaper one is cheaper because it does less, not because it is a better deal.

**A quote assembled by stripping line items until it lands under 3,000,000 KRW is not a competing quote — it is the same purchase with the scope quietly narrowed to fit a band.** If the narrower scope is what's actually wanted, that has to be the stated scope from the start, not a discount discovered after the other quotes came in.

Whether the quotes named in the draft are still the ones being paid against is where this stops being a paperwork question, which the next case covers.
