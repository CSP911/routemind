# Correcting a purchase draft after the quote changes

Prices move between drafting and payment more often than the paperwork accounts for. This is the sequence for fixing the file when they do.

## Steps

1. Attach the new quote or invoice alongside the original — never replace it, so the file shows both numbers.
2. Check whether the new amount stays inside the band the draft was already routed to.
3. If it stays inside the band: note the change on the draft and let the existing approval stand.
4. If it crosses a band line, upward or downward: withdraw the draft and refile it fresh, addressed to the correct approver.
5. If any consenting team's sign-off was conditioned on the original amount, get their consent again on the corrected figure before it goes forward.

## The trap

**A drafter who only updates step 1 — attaching the new quote — and skips checking the band has fixed the record without fixing the authority.** The file will look consistent to anyone reading it later, right up until someone checks whether the person who signed actually had the standing to sign that amount.

A withdrawn-and-refiled draft starts its own chain and its own retention clock from the refiling date, not from whenever the original quote was first attached.
