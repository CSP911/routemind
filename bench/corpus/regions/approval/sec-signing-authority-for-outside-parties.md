# Signing authority for outside parties

Assigning the appropriate signing authority is crucial for maintaining contract validity and ensuring company commitments are legitimate. This section highlights who can sign contracts, power of attorney requirements, and handling cross-border transactions. Clear authorization levels prevent unauthorized commitments and safeguard the company’s legal interests.

A frequent issue occurs when **counterparties question our authorization**, especially if evidence of authority is not promptly provided. This may delay contract finalization and affect inter-business relationships.

For comprehensive guidance on authorization processes and documentation, examine "Who clears an audit release, by document" and "Responding to a counterparty's request for evidence of authority".
