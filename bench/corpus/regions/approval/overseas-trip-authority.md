# A short trip abroad still needs the CEO

A team lead books a two-day trip to Fukuoka for a supplier visit. The total cost, including flights, comes to 380,000 KRW — well under the team lead's own 500,000 KRW spend limit, and less than plenty of domestic trips that get signed off without a second thought.

## Why the amount doesn't help here

**Domestic and overseas are the line that matters, not the cost.** A division head can sign off a domestic trip regardless of how expensive it gets within their band, but any trip that crosses a border is the CEO's regardless of how cheap it is. The team lead's own spend authority, which would easily cover 380,000 KRW for a domestic errand, does nothing for this draft — it was never a spend question.

A drafter checking only the amount against the spend table files this as routine and self-approves it; the draft is invalid the moment someone notices the destination field, and the trip has to be re-filed and re-approved, sometimes after the traveller has already left.

The same subject-over-amount logic covers hiring, dismissal, contracts, and the corporate seal — none of them are decided by what they cost.
