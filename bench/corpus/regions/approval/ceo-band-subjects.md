# What stays with the CEO no matter the amount

Most of the CEO's authority is the top of the spend table — 3,000,000 KRW and over. But part of it is not about spend at all.

| Subject | Amount that matters |
|---|---|
| Hiring / dismissal | None — always CEO |
| Contracts | None — always CEO |
| Overseas trips | None — always CEO |
| Corporate seal | None — always CEO |
| Spend | 3,000,000 KRW and over |

## The trap in the small ones

**A 200,000 KRW single-page consulting contract still needs the CEO's signature.** It is a contract, and contracts are decided by what they are, not by what they cost. A drafter who checks the spend table, sees an amount well inside a division head's band, and routes it there has filed it correctly by amount and incorrectly by subject — and the chain has to be redone from the top.

The clearest version of subject beating amount is a trip across a border costing less than the office coffee budget, covered next.
