# Power of attorney issuance

## Fields required
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Grantor | Name and position, must hold the authority being delegated |
| Agent | The named individual signing on the grantor's behalf |
| Counterparty | The specific outside party the agent may sign with |
| Transaction | What is being signed — amount, subject, or contract reference |
| Expiry | A fixed date, not "until revoked" |

## Where it's sealed
Doc admin holds the seals and will not stamp a power of attorney missing any of the above fields. A POA delegating a contract or the corporate seal is refused outright — that authority is not the grantor's to give, whatever position they hold.

**An agent who receives a POA does not gain the grantor's whole band — only the one transaction it names.** A power of attorney covering a 2,000,000 KRW order does not let the agent sign a second, unrelated order at any amount.

Once issued, the counterparty may still ask to see it before proceeding — what to hand over is the next document.
