# A contract that ended early

A two-year supply contract, signed by the CEO in March 2022 at a value well above the CEO band, is terminated by mutual agreement in November 2022 after the vendor misses three consecutive deliveries. The vendor relationship ends after eight months, and the drafter asks whether the file can now be treated as a shorter-lived record.

## What retention actually tracks
Retention is set by document kind, not by how long the arrangement ran. This is a contract, so it carries the ten-year period under the Commercial Act regardless of the eight months it was active. The clock started at signing in March 2022 and runs to March 2032.

## What early termination does change
The termination notice, the reason, and any settlement become part of the same contract file rather than a separate short-lived record, and are held for as long as the contract is.

## The trap
**A contract that ended early is not a smaller record.** Treating it as a general business record because the relationship was brief drops it to a three-year period it was never entitled to, and destroys evidence years before the statutory ten are up.

Whether a file like this can be pulled forward for destruction anyway is a question an audit hold answers, not the calendar.
