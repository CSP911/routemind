# Policy and its own lifecycle

Policies guide our operations and ensure compliance with both internal and external standards. This section maps out the lifecycle of a policy, from proposal through reviews, until its publication or withdrawal. Understanding the ownership chains and review stages is critical for seamless policy change management and ensuring accountability.

Mistakes often occur when **the policy owner changes during revision**, leading to lapses in oversight and confusion over document status. This can delay necessary updates and affect compliance with current standards. Clearly define responsibilities and establish a consistent review calendar.

For specific details on submitting and amending policies, refer to "How a policy is withdrawn" and "Authority at each stage of a policy's life".
