# Booking cases

This part of Attendance & leave covers what happens when a booking doesn't run in a straight line — a half-day against a fractional balance, a day cancelled after it was approved, leave that falls in the last week of December, a call made the same morning, or leave that overlaps a public holiday or a weekend. None of these are exotic; between them they cover most of the tickets that land on the HR desk in a normal month.

## The two groups below

The cases split into two sets. One covers requests filed ahead of time — the ordinary shape of booking leave, just with a wrinkle attached. The other covers same-day and after-the-fact situations, where the request and the event sit close enough together that the system's usual sequence — request, approval, then absence — doesn't quite hold.

## What goes wrong

**The trap is treating a booking case as a special form of leave entry rather than a special sequence of the same one.** The balance rules, the approval chain and the cancellation deadlines are unchanged in every case below — what changes is the order events happen in, or what happens once one of them is missing. Read a case for the sequence, not for a different rate.

The entitlement side of Attendance & leave has its own case set, built the same way — read that next if the question is about days available rather than days booked.
