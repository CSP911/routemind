# Flexible and remote working

Flexible working covers three separate things that get recorded differently: a movable start and finish time around a fixed core, a monthly flexitime balance that can run slightly over or under, and remote days that substitute for an office day without being leave.

## Core hours
Everyone, remote or in-office, is expected to be reachable during a fixed midday band. Outside that band, start and finish times move within a wider window.

## The flexitime month
Hours are totalled over a calendar month, not a rolling week. A small surplus carries forward; a shortfall has to be cleared or it converts to a leave deduction.

## Working from home
A remote day is logged in its own system menu, separate from leave. It only counts as a full attendance day if core hours were actually met from home.

## The hybrid week
Most teams run a fixed split of office and remote days per week. The split resets every week — it does not bank.

**Working from home does not suspend core hours** — a remote day logged with no presence during the core band is a shortfall, not a normal day, regardless of total hours worked. The next pages cover each of these in detail, starting with the core hours band itself.
