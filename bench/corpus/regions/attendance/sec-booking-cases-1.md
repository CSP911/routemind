# Booking cases: requests you file ahead of time

These eleven cases cover leave booked in advance where the plain request form doesn't quite fit — a half-day taken against a balance that's already fractional, quarter-days combined in the same week, leave that falls in the last week of December, leave that spans a public holiday or a weekend, and the handful of variations on each. The common thread is that the employee knows the date before they file, so the questions are about balance and priority, not about timing.

## The common case

Most of these resolve the same way: check the balance in the unit it's actually held in, book the days that fall on working days, and let the system exclude the holiday or weekend on its own. The exceptions are the ones where December crowds several requests onto the same handful of working days, and priority has to be decided by something other than submission order.

## What goes wrong

**The trap is assuming a half-day and a quarter-day draw against the same rounding rule.** They don't always, and a request that looks fine against a whole-day balance can still fail once the balance is fractional. Check the case for the exact combination before telling someone their request is fine.

The other half of this set — bookings made the same morning or undone after the fact — sits in the next section.
