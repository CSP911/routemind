# A claim filed before attendance confirmation

An employee returns from a two-day domestic trip on a Friday and files the daily allowance claim that same afternoon, keen to get it into the next payslip. The line manager, however, doesn't confirm the attendance record until the following Monday, once the trip report is reviewed.

## What the system does

The claim form checks the attendance record at the moment it is submitted, not at some later point. On Friday the record still reads "requested," so the claim is rejected outright rather than held in a queue waiting for Monday's confirmation.

## What has to happen next

The employee has to refile the claim after Monday's confirmation goes through. **The Friday submission does not count as the claim being "in" — there is no partial credit for filing early, and the clock for the claim's own deadline only starts once the refiled claim is accepted.**

The same reasoning applies when the two systems disagree on the dates themselves, not just on status.
