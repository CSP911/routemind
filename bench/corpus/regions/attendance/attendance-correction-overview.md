# Attendance corrections and disputes

An attendance record is wrong in one of three ways: nobody entered it, someone entered it twice, or someone entered it correctly but you disagree with what it says. Each of these needs a different fix, not the same complaint routed to the same person.

## Missing records
A day worked with no clock-in or clock-out is not treated as absence by default — see the missing-record case for how it is reconstructed and confirmed.

## Duplicate records
Two entries for the same day (commonly a mobile check-in and a PC login both firing) do not average out or cancel — the system keeps both until someone resolves which one stands.

## Disputes
Disagreeing with how a team lead logged your day — as unauthorized absence rather than leave, for instance — goes through the escalation procedure, not a second correction form.

**A correction request filed without naming which of these three it is gets sent back before anyone looks at the evidence.** The form asks for the type first because each type moves through a different approval step.

The deadline for all three tightens once the month closes — that window is covered next.
