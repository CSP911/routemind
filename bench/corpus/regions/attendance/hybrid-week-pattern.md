# The hybrid week pattern

A hybrid arrangement fixes how many days a week are spent in the office versus remote. The split is set per calendar week and does not roll over.

| Pattern | Office days | Remote days |
|---|---|---|
| Standard hybrid | 3 minimum | 2 maximum |
| Full office week | 5 | 0 |
| Team-agreed exception | Set by manager, on file with HR desk | — |

Both counts apply to the same Monday–Sunday week; a day used or unused does not carry into the next one.

**Working extra office days one week does not earn extra remote days the next** — the 3-and-2 split resets every week regardless of what happened before it. Any pattern other than the standard split needs a team agreement on file with the HR desk, not just a verbal arrangement with the manager. Who actually signs off that agreement is covered next.
