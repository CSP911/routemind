# When a trip allowance claim reaches your payslip

The flat daily allowance for a business trip is not paid automatically once the trip closes. It has to be claimed on the trip allowance claim form, and the date that form is filed decides which payslip it lands on.

## The claim window

| Claim filed | Payslip |
|---|---|
| Within 5 business days of the return date | Same month's payslip |
| After 5 business days but before month close | Next month's payslip |
| After month close | Next month's payslip, no exception |

## Why the window matters

Payroll runs the allowance line once a month, alongside ordinary overtime and trip-day hours. A claim that arrives after the cutoff cannot be inserted into a payslip that has already been finalised, regardless of how early the trip itself took place.

**Filing the claim "eventually" because the allowance is a fixed amount, not a variable one, is the mistake that pushes it a full month late — the amount doesn't change, but the payslip it hits does.**

The hours behind the same trip follow their own cutoff, covered separately where a trip crosses a payroll close.
