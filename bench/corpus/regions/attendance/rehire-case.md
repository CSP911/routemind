# Case: a rehire after a gap in service

An employee resigned in January 2020 with 4 years of service and was rehired in July 2021 — an 18-month gap. Under the rehire continuity rule, a break of under 90 days preserves the original service date; anything longer restarts it. Eighteen months is well past that line, so the service-start-date is reset to the July 2021 rehire date.

## What that changes

| Service years | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| 1 year and over | 15 days |

The 4 years from the first stint do not carry into the accrual ladder — the person is a first-year joiner again, entitled to 15 days flat, not the 15+1 they had earned before leaving.

## The trap

**The groupware record often keeps the original employee ID and shows the 2016 hire date on the profile screen, because that field is separate from the one the entitlement calculation actually reads.** Seeing an old hire date on the profile does not mean the accrual engine is using it. Check the service-start-date field itself, not the display.

That field, and who is allowed to edit it, is documented on its own page.
