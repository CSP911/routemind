# Splitting parental leave into two blocks

## Why a split is allowed

An employee does not have to take parental leave as one continuous period. It can be split into two blocks against the same child, provided both blocks fall within the eligibility window and both are agreed with the HR desk in advance.

## Notice for the second block

| Block | Notice required |
|---|---|
| First block | At least 30 days before it starts |
| Second block | At least 30 days before it starts |

**The second block is not automatically covered by the notice given for the first — it is a new case and needs its own 30-day lead time**, even if both blocks were mentioned in the original conversation with HR.

## Gap between blocks

There is no minimum or maximum gap enforced between the two blocks; the only fixed constraint is that both must sit inside the child's eligible age window at the time each block starts.

The lead time here matches the one used for a December booking that touches the priority window — see december-leave-booking-deadline.
