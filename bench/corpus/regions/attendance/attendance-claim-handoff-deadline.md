# The window to confirm attendance before the claim clock starts

A trip's attendance record must move from "requested" to "confirmed" within 5 working days of the traveller's return. The claim's own filing deadline does not start counting until that confirmation happens.

| Stage | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Attendance record confirmed | Within 5 working days of return |
| Claim filed | Counted from confirmation, not from return |
| Confirmation still pending after 5 days | Escalated to the HR desk |

## Why the gap matters

An employee who returns from a trip and waits for the manager to confirm the record is not losing days off their claim window — the claim window hasn't opened yet. **Counting the claim deadline from the return date instead of the confirmation date is the mistake that makes people think they've missed a deadline they haven't reached yet.**

If confirmation itself stalls past 5 working days, the case moves to the mismatch escalation path rather than sitting indefinitely.
