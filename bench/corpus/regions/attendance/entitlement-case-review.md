# Requesting a review of an entitlement case

Any of the cases above can be checked by the HR desk if the number on record looks wrong.

## Steps

1. Identify which case applies — transfer, rehire, leave of absence, milestone, or a leaver's final balance.
2. Gather the supporting document: the transfer memo, the rehire contract, the leave of absence approval, or the resignation date.
3. Submit both to the HR desk with the employee's current recorded service-start-date.
4. HR desk recalculates the entitlement against the correct table and updates the record if it was wrong.

## The trap

**This has to happen before the leave year closes — once it closes, the correction window is gone and the balance for that year is locked, even if the underlying case was always correct.** Being right about the case does not matter if the request arrives after the deadline.

That window, and exactly how long it stays open, is covered on its own page.
