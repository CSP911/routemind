# Attendance corrections and disputes

Attendance corrections and disputes cover the processes involved when attendance records are inaccurate or need to be challenged. Issues often arise after the month's closing, such as when a day worked is missing from the records, or conflicting reports show two records for the same day. When disputes occur, especially regarding approval decisions or entered data with a team lead, it is crucial to follow the escalation procedures to resolve them efficiently.

**A common pitfall is failing to address discrepancies before the month closes**, leaving unresolved issues or corrections that might affect pay or entitlements. Ensure all disputed records are verified and any necessary corrections are submitted promptly according to established guidelines.

For those managing these scenarios, understanding the various forms, deadlines, and escalation paths is essential to maintain accurate attendance records and resolve disputes effectively.
