# Flexible and remote working

Flexible and remote working arrangements allow employees to work outside of the traditional office environment, promoting adaptability in work hours and locations. Core hours must be maintained, with proper recording of time spent working from home and adherence to the hybrid work week schedule. This section details the structures supporting a flexitime month and the management of home and office workdays.

**The most common issue arises when employees underreport or misrecord their flexible hours**, which can lead to disputes or adjustments in recorded time. Ensure that both remote and core hours are consistently documented in company systems.

Refer to company policy for guidance on maintaining compliance within flexible working structures and to address any issues that may arise regarding attendance or output.
