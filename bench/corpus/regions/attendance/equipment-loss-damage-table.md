# Equipment loss and damage charges

These charges apply once the equipment return deadline on leaving has passed without a return, or when returned equipment shows damage beyond normal wear.

| Situation | Charge |
|---|---|
| Laptop not returned | Full replacement cost, capped at KRW 1,500,000 |
| Phone not returned | Full replacement cost, capped at KRW 800,000 |
| Damaged beyond normal wear | Lower of repair cost or 50% of replacement cost |
| Returned late but undamaged, within the 5-day window | No charge |

**The cap is fixed regardless of the actual model or its market price that year — a top-spec laptop lost the week it was issued is still charged at the KRW 1,500,000 cap, not its purchase price.** Employees sometimes assume a cheaper device attracts a proportionally smaller charge; it does not, unless repair cost is lower than the cap.

Charges are deducted from the final payslip in the same run confirmed under equipment lending approval role.
