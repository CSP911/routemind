# Overtime and hours — where to start

This section covers requesting overtime in advance, how night and holiday hours are treated differently from ordinary overtime, the weekly cap that all of it counts against, and the recurring problem of hours that were worked but never went through a request.

## The common case
Overtime requested before the work happens, approved, then logged against the weekly cap — that's the path the rest of the system assumes. Night and holiday hours run through the same request, just counted under different terms once they're logged.

## Where it breaks down
The cap is weekly, not per-request, so a second small request in the same week can fail even though each one looks fine on its own. And hours that were worked without a request filed at all are not simply back-dated into the cap — they go through a separate correction path with its own evidence requirements.

**The trap**: treating "worked but never requested" as a paperwork gap to fix after the fact. By the time it's noticed, the correction is often harder to get approved than the overtime itself would have been.

Hours and caps sit upstream of the payroll allowances that ride on overtime pay — get the hours right first.
