# How a leave day in the leaving month is paid

A day of annual leave taken in the month someone resigns is paid exactly like any other leave day — at the normal daily wage, deducted from the balance as usual. It is not treated as part of the final leave payout.

## The two outcomes

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Leave day taken before the confirmed leaving date | Paid at the normal daily wage, deducted from balance |
| Leave day booked but the leaving date moves earlier | Forfeited — not paid, not converted to payout |
| Balance still unused as of the final day | Converted to the unused leave payout |

## Where this trips people up

**A leave day already booked past a moved-up leaving date is not automatically added to the unused balance and paid out — it simply disappears from the record, because it was never taken and the day it was booked for no longer falls inside employment.** Only whatever balance remains genuinely unused on the actual final day converts to a payout.

The wage basis used for that payout, and which payslip it reaches, are both fixed separately from the days themselves.
