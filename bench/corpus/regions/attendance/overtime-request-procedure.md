# Requesting overtime in advance

## The normal path
1. Submit the request in the groupware Overtime menu, stating the expected start and end time.
2. Get line manager approval **before the overtime starts** — not at the end of the shift, and not the next morning.
3. Once approved, the hours are locked in against that day's attendance record.

## The late-request exception
If the need for overtime only becomes clear once the shift is already running, a request can still be filed within 3 working days afterward, with the manager's approval noting why it was late. Anything filed later than that is not converted to paid overtime.

**A verbal "go ahead and stay" from a manager is not a request. Until it is entered and approved in the system, the hours sit as ordinary attendance, not overtime.**

Rates and how night or holiday hours change the calculation are covered separately.
