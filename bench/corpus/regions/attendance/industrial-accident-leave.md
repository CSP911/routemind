# Industrial accident and injury leave

## How it's different from ordinary sick leave

An injury sustained in the course of work is covered by industrial accident compensation insurance, not by annual leave or the ordinary sick leave track. The absence is fully paid through the insurance scheme, is never deducted from the annual leave balance, and counts as continuous service the same way ordinary working time does.

## What triggers this track

The employee, or their manager on their behalf, reports the incident to the HR desk as an industrial accident rather than an illness. Once HR confirms the report and the hospital's initial certification, the absence is coded on this track from its first day — not from whenever the paperwork clears.

## The trap

**An industrial accident is never quietly recoded as ordinary sick leave just because the insurer's payment is slow.** The coding is fixed the moment HR confirms the report; a delay in reimbursement doesn't push the absence back onto the annual leave balance in the meantime.

The report itself has its own deadline — see reporting an industrial accident.
