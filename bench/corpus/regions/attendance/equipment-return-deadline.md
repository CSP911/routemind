# Equipment return deadline on leaving

Any laptop or phone still signed out must be returned to IT within 5 working days of the last working day.

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Last working day | Trigger date |
| Equipment returned to IT | Within 5 working days |
| Return confirmed by IT and logged | Feeds into final pay processing |

If equipment is not returned within the 5 working days, the charge set out in the equipment loss and damage charges table is deducted from the final payslip. This is separate from, and does not delay, the leave balance payout deadline covered under the final leave settlement.

**Returning equipment after the final payslip has already been issued does not reverse the charge automatically — recovery of the deducted amount is a separate reimbursement request, not a payroll correction.** Employees who expect to be delayed past the 5 working days should tell IT before the deadline, not after.

The same 5-day window applies regardless of whether the departure is a resignation or a transfer within the group.
