# Who approves overtime

## Line manager
Approves every overtime request before the hours are worked, checking that it fits within the 12-hour weekly cap and that the stated start and end time are realistic for the task described.

## HR desk
Does not approve individual requests, but reviews the overtime record monthly for two things: requests that repeatedly sit near the weekly cap, and hours logged as ordinary attendance that look like unrequested overtime. Reviews any exceptional request to exceed the weekly cap before a manager can sign off on it.

**A manager's approval is necessary but not sufficient for anything above the standard cap — without the HR desk's sign-off first, an over-cap request is rejected by the system regardless of what the manager intended.**

Payroll reads directly from approved requests; neither role adjusts a payslip after the fact without a filed correction.
