# Sick leave and injury

Sick leave and workplace injuries require careful management to ensure employees are supported during health-related absences. Considerations include how sick days interact with annual leave, the evidence required for validation, handling of industrial accidents, and long-term illness procedures. Proper filing and approval processes help mitigate operational disruptions and ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.

**A frequent pitfall is not adequately documenting or submitting required evidence**, which can lead to delays in leave approval or complications with compensation. Ensure all relevant documentation and timelines are followed for both employee and company protection.

Consult specific guidelines on sick leave entitlements, injury reporting, and procedural requirements to manage these situations effectively and ensure prompt recovery and return to work.
