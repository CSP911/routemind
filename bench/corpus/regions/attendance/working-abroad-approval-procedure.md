# Requesting to work from another country

## Before you file
Confirm the destination country, the start and end dates, and how the arrangement affects the cumulative day count in that country over the trailing 12 months — the HR desk holds this figure, not the employee's own trip history. A stint of 90 days or less needs line manager approval only. Anything longer, or anything that would push cumulative days in that country past 150, needs the HR desk as a second approver before it is confirmed.

## Notice

| Stint length | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Up to 90 days | At least 14 days before the start date |
| Over 90 days, or pushing cumulative days past 150 | At least 30 days before the start date |

**The 30-day notice is measured from the start date you are requesting, not from the day you submit the form** — filing on day 29 does not count as meeting it, and the request is returned rather than queued.

Once approved, the arrangement is entered as a remote-work record, not a trip. The day count itself, and what happens as it nears the tax threshold, is covered separately.
