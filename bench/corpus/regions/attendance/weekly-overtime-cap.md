# The weekly overtime cap

Overtime is capped at 12 hours per week on top of the standard 40-hour week. The cap is enforced by the system at the point a request is submitted, not after the hours are worked.

## How the week is counted
The cap runs on the calendar week, not the payroll cycle. A request that would be fine against a payroll-month total can still be rejected if it pushes a single calendar week over 12 hours.

## Going over the cap
There is no standard route to exceed it. An exceptional request needs the HR desk's sign-off before the manager approval step, and is expected to be rare — recurring requests near or over the cap are flagged for review regardless of whether any single one was approved.

**Approving overtime informally at team level does not override the cap — the system blocks the submission itself, so a manager who tells someone to "just stay" past the limit is creating hours with nowhere to be recorded.**
