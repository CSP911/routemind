# Equipment not returned at resignation

An employee's last working day is the 20th. Under the equipment return deadline on leaving, the laptop is due back to IT by the 27th (5 working days later).

By the 27th, IT has not received the device and the employee has not raised a delay. IT logs the item as not returned and notifies the HR desk.

## What happens next

- The HR desk applies the laptop charge from the equipment loss and damage charges table: full replacement cost, capped at KRW 1,500,000.
- The charge is deducted from the final payslip in the same processing run, alongside the leave balance settled under final leave settlement.
- The employee returns the laptop three weeks later, after the final payslip has already gone out.

**Because the return came after the final payslip, the charge is not simply reversed — the employee has to file a separate reimbursement request, and it is paid on the following month's run, not corrected on the original one.** Filing the reimbursement request does not require a new loan agreement, since the loan has already closed.

This is the same handoff point where a leaver's equipment file and their leave balance file both close out.
