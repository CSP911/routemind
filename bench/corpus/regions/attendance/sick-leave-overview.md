# Sick leave and injury

## What this covers

Sick leave isn't a separate pot of days sitting next to annual leave — it's a set of rules for how an illness or injury absence gets coded once it happens, and what evidence changes that coding.

## Where each piece lives

- Short absences without a certificate are usually just annual leave with a different reason attached — see sick days and annual leave.
- The certificate threshold and what it changes is in the evidence table and the certificate form.
- An injury sustained on the job runs on a separate, fully-paid track — see industrial accident and injury leave, and the report procedure.
- An illness that runs past the certificate stage has its own conversion point — see long-term illness.

## The one thing to remember

**None of these tracks sit outside annual leave by default — they replace it only once the right evidence is filed.** An employee who assumes a sick day is automatically separate from their annual balance is the case HR corrects most often.
