# What a late approval does to the trip allowance

The daily trip allowance is only released once an approved trip exists in the attendance system — it does not matter how the approval got there, only that it did.

## Approval timing sets the claim start

| Trip status at departure | Allowance claim window starts |
|---|---|
| Approved before departure | Date the trip closes |
| Approved after departure, via the exception process | Date the exception approval is recorded |

A trip cleared through the post-departure exception process is not backdated for allowance purposes. The claim window counts from the day the exception actually clears, not from the day the traveller left.

## The trap

**Filing the allowance claim before the exception approval is on record gets it rejected as unsupported — payroll has nothing to check it against — and the whole claim has to be resubmitted once the approval finally lands.** Employees who submit the claim from memory on the day they return, before HR has processed the exception, lose that first attempt for nothing.

Once the approval is on file, the claim still runs against the same filing deadline as any other trip allowance.
