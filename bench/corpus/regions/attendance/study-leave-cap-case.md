# Study leave reaching its one-year cap

## The situation
An employee has been on approved study leave for 10 months to complete a certificate programme. The programme runs long, and the employee requests a 4-month extension to finish it — a total of 14 months.

## What actually happens
The HR desk can only approve 2 more months, bringing the total to the 12-month cap. The remaining 2 months the employee still needs cannot be granted as study leave regardless of the justification — the cap applies to the category, not to the reason.

## What the employee does next
To cover the extra 2 months, the employee has to submit a separate unpaid personal leave request, approved on its own form and subject to its own 6-month cap. Service continuity is unaffected either way — both categories count time away toward service in full — but the leave is now recorded under two different categories on the attendance system, not one continuous block.

**Assuming an unchanged business reason justifies extending the same category past its cap is the mistake — the ceiling is fixed per category, and the only way past it is to move to a different one.**

The form for that second request is the one used for personal reasons generally.
