# The deadline to get a trip approved

A trip request has to be fully approved — not merely submitted — by a set number of working days before departure.

| Trip type | Approval deadline |
|---|---|
| Domestic | At least 2 working days before departure |
| Overseas | At least 5 working days before departure |

## What the count is measured against
The deadline is measured against the departure date, and in working days, not calendar days. **A weekend or public holiday between submission and departure does not shrink the count** — a request submitted on a Thursday for an overseas trip departing the following Wednesday has cleared only 3 working days, not 5, and is late even though four calendar days sit between submission and travel.

## Missing the deadline
A request that clears approval after these deadlines but before departure is still an ordinary approved trip — the deadline governs how much notice the approver gets, not whether the trip is valid. A request that is still pending, or not yet submitted, when departure actually arrives is a different situation, covered by the exception path for approval given after the fact.
