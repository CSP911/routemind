# Working abroad

Understanding the groundwork for temporary assignments abroad is crucial for employees and management. This section outlines what constitutes "working from another country temporarily" and explores scenarios that could initiate tax implications, such as the number of days spent working overseas. Employees must secure approval before commencing remote work from another country to ensure compliance with tax and visa regulations.

**A critical concern is the threshold of days worked abroad that can trigger tax questions**, potentially leading to unexpected liabilities. Employees and approvers need to be aware of these thresholds to plan accordingly.
