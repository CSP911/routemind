# Swapping a hybrid office day

The hybrid week pattern fixes which days are spent in the office and which at home, but an occasional swap is allowed when a single day needs to move — a client meeting lands on a home day, or a home day needs to shift for a personal appointment.

## Notice required

| Swap type | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Swap within the same week | At least 1 working day before the swap day |
| Swap into the following week | At least 3 working days before |
| Swap on the same day | Not permitted — request the next available slot |

## Approval and recording

The line manager approves a one-off swap the same way they approve the standing hybrid pattern. Approval alone does not update the attendance record.

**A swap agreed by chat message does not appear in the attendance system on its own — the day still has to be logged through the ordinary WFH recording procedure before it starts, or it reads as an unscheduled absence from the office.** A manager's "sure, go ahead" is not a substitute for that entry.

Swaps cover single days only; a standing change to the weekly pattern itself goes through the flexwork approval role instead of this procedure.
