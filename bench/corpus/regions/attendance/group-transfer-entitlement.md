# Entitlement when you transfer from a group company

## What counts as continuous service

Service at another company in the group carries into your leave entitlement here **only if the move is logged as an internal transfer, not a resignation and rehire**. If your contract with the previous entity was formally terminated and a new one issued here, the system sees two separate hires and your service clock restarts at zero.

## What HR needs from you

Bring the transfer memo or group mobility letter to the HR desk before your first pay cycle closes here. HR uses it to backdate your service-start field to the original hire date at the group company.

## Service and entitlement

| Total service (across group) | Annual leave |
|---|---|
| 1 year and over | 15 days |
| 3 years and over | 15 days, +1 every 2 years (capped at 25) |

Without the memo, you accrue as a first-year joiner regardless of how long you actually worked in the group — the recalculation cannot be applied retroactively once the current leave year is closed.

A move without a group transfer memo instead follows the ordinary rehire rule, covered next.
