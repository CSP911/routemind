# Attendance correction deadline

| When the discrepancy is found | Deadline to file |
|---|---|
| Before the month closes | Any time before close, via the normal correction form |
| After the month closes | Within 10 calendar days of the close date |
| After the 10-day window | Requires HR desk override — not guaranteed |

Filing within the 10-day window does not mean the correction lands on the month just closed automatically. It only means HR will still accept and process it without extra sign-off.

**Missing the 10-day window does not make the record permanent — it just means the fix now needs a manager's written override on top of the usual evidence, and it will carry to the following month's payslip instead of the one it belongs to.** Waiting past the window because "it's only a few days" is the single most common reason a correction takes an extra month to land.

Which payslip a late correction actually reaches is set out in the post-close correction table.
