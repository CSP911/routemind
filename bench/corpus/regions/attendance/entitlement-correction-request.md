# Requesting an entitlement correction

## Deadline

Raise a suspected entitlement error with the HR desk **within the same leave year it occurred — corrections are not backdated across a year boundary once the balance has closed.**

## What to bring

- Your service start date, or group transfer memo if relevant
- The leave year in question
- What you believe the correct day count should be

## Why the deadline is firm

Once a leave year closes, unused days are either carried over under the standard rule or forfeited, and payroll has already reconciled any payout tied to it. Reopening a closed year means unwinding both.

| Timing | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Raised within the same leave year | Corrected, balance adjusted |
| Raised after year-end close | Generally declined |

This is the same desk that decides transfer and rehire bridging cases, so bring supporting documentation the first time you raise it.
