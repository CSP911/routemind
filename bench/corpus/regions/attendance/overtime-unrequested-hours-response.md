# Handling hours logged with no overtime request

The attendance system records door and login times regardless of whether an overtime request exists. Every week, these logs are checked against approved requests.

## The steps

1. At week close, the manager reviews any day where logged hours extend past the ordinary end time.
2. Each extended block is matched against an approved overtime request for that date.
3. Where no match exists, the hours are recorded as plain attendance only — no premium, no banked time off.
4. The HR desk audits this list monthly as part of its standard overtime audit.

## The trap

**Logging the hours in the attendance system does not itself create an overtime request, and a manager cannot approve one after the fact.** Only a request approved before the hours were worked — or filed inside the narrow late window described in the overtime request procedure — turns logged time into paid overtime. Everything else is simply attendance.

This is the mechanism behind the standard unrequested-overtime outcome: the hours are real, but nothing converts them into a paid claim after the week has closed.
