# The handover from attendance to a claim

A business trip generates two records: the attendance entry that says the trip happened, and the claim that asks for the daily allowance against it. The claim system does not calculate anything on its own — it reads the attendance record. If that record isn't there, or isn't confirmed, the claim has nothing to attach to.

## The order that has to hold

Attendance confirmation always comes first. A claim filed against a trip whose dates still show as "requested" rather than "confirmed" is not queued to wait — it is returned to the employee to refile once the attendance side catches up.

## Where each failure is handled

- Filing a claim before the dates are confirmed: see the case on an early claim.
- The two systems showing different dates for the same trip: see the mismatch procedure.
- Who has the final word when they disagree: see the resolution role.

**A claim referencing an unconfirmed attendance record does not fail quietly — it is rejected outright and has to be resubmitted from scratch, which is why the order matters more than the paperwork.**

This handover sits right before the daily allowance itself takes over on the payslip.
