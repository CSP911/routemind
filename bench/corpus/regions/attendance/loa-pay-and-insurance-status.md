# Pay and insurance during leave of absence

## What continues and what stops

| Leave type | Salary from the company | Four major insurances |
|---|---|---|
| Study leave | Unpaid | Continue; employee self-pays both shares |
| Family-care leave | Unpaid | Continue; employee self-pays both shares |
| Unpaid leave | Unpaid | Suspended unless the employee opts in to self-pay both shares |
| Military leave | Not paid by the company; statutory pay comes from the relevant authority | Suspended for the leave, reinstated on return with no gap in service credit |

## The default is lapse, not continuation

Study and family-care leave keep the four insurances active only because the employee is billed directly for the full premium, employer and employee share together. Unpaid leave carries no such default — coverage simply stops unless the employee asks the HR desk to set up self-payment before the leave starts.

**Unless the employee actively opts in, letting insurance lapse during unpaid leave is the expected outcome, not an error for HR to fix — there is no automatic backfill for the missing months once the leave ends.**

The service credit itself is unaffected by any of this; for which categories keep the service clock running while pay and insurance stop, see the category breakdown.
