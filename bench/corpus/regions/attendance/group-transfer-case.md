# Case: a transfer from a group affiliate

An employee joined Affiliate X on 1 March 2019 and moved to us on 1 July 2022 under an internal group transfer memo. Because the move is documented as a transfer rather than a resignation and a new hire, the service-start-date field keeps the original 2019-03-01 date.

## What the entitlement looks like at transfer

| Service years at transfer | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| 3 years and over | 15 days, +1 every 2 years (capped at 25) |

By 1 July 2022 the employee has passed the 3-year mark, so entitlement is already 15 days and running on the same 2-year ladder as if they had always worked here — the years at Affiliate X count.

## The trap

**If the transfer is filed as a resignation followed by a new hire — even for a same-day, same-group move — the service-start-date field is overwritten with the new join date, and the years at the affiliate are lost for good.** The accrual table then reads the person as a first-year joiner. This is a paperwork problem, not a policy one: the fix is getting the transfer memo into the file before the record is created, not after.
