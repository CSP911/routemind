# The trip allowance claim

The daily allowance is not paid automatically once a trip request is approved. It has to be claimed separately, after the trip itself has been closed out in the attendance system.

## What the form needs

- The approved trip request reference number
- The confirmed start and end dates as recorded in the attendance system, not the dates originally requested
- Domestic or overseas designation, which sets the rate

## Filing window

The claim must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the trip's return date. Claims filed later are not automatically rejected, but they miss the next payroll run and carry to the one after.

**Filing the claim before the trip is closed out in the attendance system is the mistake that gets it bounced back.** The form pulls its dates from the closed attendance record, so a trip still showing as "in progress" has nothing for the claim to attach to — the trip has to be settled first, then claimed.

Once the allowance is claimed, the rest of the trip becomes an expense matter, handled where the trip and the expense system meet.
