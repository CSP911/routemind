# Entitlement for a rehire

## Default: service resets

A rehire is treated as a new joiner. Prior service does not automatically restore your old leave tier — you start again on the first-year proration table, and your 3/5/7-year milestones recount from the new hire date.

## When service is bridged

The HR desk can bridge service so the old hire date is reused, but this is a decision they make on request, not a default. **Ask for it explicitly when you sign the new contract — bridging is not applied retroactively once your first leave year as a rehire has closed.**

## What bridging changes

| Without bridging | With bridging |
|---|---|
| First-year days by join month | Full year-one entitlement, no proration |
| 3/5/7-year clock restarts | Clock continues from original hire date |

If your gap between contracts was actually a move to another group company rather than open unemployment, check the group-transfer entitlement case first — it may already cover you without a separate bridging request.
