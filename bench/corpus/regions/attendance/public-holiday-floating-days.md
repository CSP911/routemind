# Statutory holidays vs floating holidays inside a leave block

A public holiday that falls inside a booked leave block is not always free of charge to the balance — it depends on what kind of holiday it is.

## Treatment

| Holiday type | Inside a booked leave block | Deducted from balance |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory public holiday | Skipped automatically | No |
| Company floating holiday | Must be booked as the floating day itself | Yes, unless booked as such |

## Why this trips people up

Statutory holidays are excluded from every leave block without any action from the employee, as set out in the public holiday leave case. Floating holidays are a company benefit rather than a statutory one, and the system has no way to know an employee intended a given day as the floating holiday unless it is booked that way.

**A floating company holiday landing inside a leave block is deducted as an ordinary leave day unless it is separately booked as the floating holiday — it is not skipped the way a statutory holiday is.**

Which days count as statutory in a given year is published by the HR desk each January.
