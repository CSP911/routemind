# What counts as night and holiday hours

## Night hours
Any hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00 count as night hours, whether or not they are also overtime. A shift that starts before 22:00 and runs past it only has the portion after 22:00 counted as night hours.

## Holiday hours
Holiday hours are any hours worked on a public holiday or a company-designated rest day, regardless of what time of day they fall in.

**A shift that crosses midnight is split at 00:00 for recording purposes — logged as two separate attendance entries, one per calendar day, even though it is one continuous shift.** This matters most when the shift crosses into or out of a holiday: only the hours actually falling on the holiday date carry the holiday premium.

Both categories are recorded automatically once hours are entered against the correct time window; no separate request is needed beyond the overtime request itself.
