# Case: pinpointing the day the extra day starts

An employee joined on 14 September 2019. Their 3-year mark falls on 14 September 2022, not on 1 January 2022 or 1 January 2023. The extra day is added at 3 years, again at 5, again at 7, and so on until the total reaches the cap of 25 — and each of those additions lands on the anniversary date itself.

## Before and after the anniversary

| Period | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Before 14 Sep 2022 | 15 days |
| From 14 Sep 2022 | 16 days |

Anyone checking their balance on 1 January 2022 will still see 15 days — the bump has not happened yet — and should not report it as an error.

## The trap

**Checking the balance on 1 January of the milestone year and assuming the higher figure should already be there is the single most common cause of a false correction request.** The date that matters is the anniversary, not the calendar year boundary.

If the anniversary date on record looks wrong, the field it comes from is the one to check first.
