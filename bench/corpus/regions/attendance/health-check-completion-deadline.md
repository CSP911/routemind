# The window to complete the annual check

The annual health check must be attended, and its result category entered into the attendance system, by 30 November of that calendar year. This gives HR time to chase any outstanding follow-up before the year closes.

| Stage | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Appointment attended | By 30 November |
| Result category entered in attendance system | Within 5 working days of attendance |
| Any required follow-up scheduled | Before year-end |

An employee who joins after 1 October is not required to complete that year's check; their first check falls due the following calendar year, on the same 30 November cycle as everyone else.

**Attending the check in December does not fail the deadline by itself, but it removes any margin for a delayed result or a required follow-up before the year closes.** A late attendance that surfaces a "follow-up required" result with no time left to schedule it before 31 December carries the follow-up into the new year as an open item, not a missed one.

What happens when the deadline passes with nothing booked at all is covered as a separate case.
