# Overtime and hours

## What this covers
Overtime is any time worked beyond the standard 40-hour week, plus two categories that carry a premium regardless of the weekly total: night hours and holiday hours. All three are tracked in the same groupware menu, but each is governed by a different rule.

## The pieces
- **Requesting in advance** — overtime is submitted and approved before it starts, not logged afterwards.
- **Night and holiday hours** — carry a premium on top of the ordinary overtime rate, and the two can stack.
- **The weekly cap** — the system will not let a request push a week past 12 hours of overtime on top of the standard 40.
- **Hours worked and never requested** — the most common attendance dispute in this area, and the one HR audits first.

**Working late does not create paid overtime by itself — the request is what does, and it has to exist before the clock does.**

Approval routing and the exact rates are covered in the pages that follow; this one is the map.
