# Who approves working abroad

## Line manager
The line manager approves the arrangement itself — that the work can be done remotely from the proposed country without disrupting the team, and that the dates fit around any planned leave. This approval alone is enough for a stint of 90 days or less in a country where the employee has no other working-abroad days on record this year.

## HR desk
The HR desk approves anything longer, and anything that adds to a country where the employee already has working-abroad days logged, because the desk is the only place the cumulative count is kept. It also owns the tax-day threshold: once a request would put someone within 30 days of the 183-day mark, the HR desk reviews the case before signing off, regardless of who the line manager is.

**Line manager approval on its own is not sufficient once cumulative days in a country pass 90, even if each individual stint looked short enough to approve alone** — the desk has to be brought in to check the running total, not just the trip being requested.

Confirmation of the arrangement lands in the attendance system the same way an overseas trip's approval does, though the two records are never merged.
