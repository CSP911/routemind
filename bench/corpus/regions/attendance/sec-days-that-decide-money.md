# Days that decide money

The financial impact of attendance is determined by various days that affect allowances and payouts. These include decisions around allowances for trip days, handling leave days in a leaving month, and payouts for unused leave. The proration of salary in joining months also plays a pivotal role in determining financial outcomes.

**One common error is miscalculating or overlooking a prorated month**, leading to discrepancies in payroll or missing allowances. Make sure to identify and confirm critical days in any financial calculation or adjustment. Properly documented and accounted for, these days ensure accurate financial treatment and prevent disputes later.

Refer to detailed guidelines on each specific scenario to ensure all financial aspects are accurately calculated and processed.
