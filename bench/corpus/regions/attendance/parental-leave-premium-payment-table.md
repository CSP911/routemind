# How the insurance premium is billed during parental leave

Coverage continuing is one question; who actually pays the premium each month while there is no salary to deduct it from is another.

## Billing by insurance type

| Insurance type | During the leave |
|---|---|
| National pension | Employee portion may be deferred on request, resumes on return |
| Health insurance | Employee portion billed monthly unless deferral is requested |
| Employment insurance | Suspended — no premium due while on leave |

## The trap

**An unpaid, non-deferred health insurance premium does not simply disappear — it accumulates as a debt collected from the first payslip after return.** An employee who assumes silence means the company is absorbing the cost can come back to a first month's pay noticeably smaller than expected, purely from several months of premiums being recovered at once.

Which coverage this premium actually keeps active — and what lapses instead — is the separate question the insurance coverage rule answers.
