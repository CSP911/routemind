# Recording an accident at work

When an accident happens during working hours, the attendance record has to reflect it immediately — separately from, and before, the industrial accident report itself is filed.

1. The line manager logs the incident time and the point at which the employee stopped working in the attendance system, coded as "workplace accident," on the day it happens.
2. This entry does not require the injury to be confirmed as an industrial accident yet — it only marks that work stopped and why.
3. The employee or manager then files the industrial accident report within the deadline set out in the reporting procedure.
4. Once the report is confirmed, the HR desk updates the attendance entry to reflect the paid industrial accident leave track rather than an ordinary absence.

**Leaving the attendance entry as an unexplained early departure until the report clears is the mistake that causes payroll to treat the hours as unpaid.** The "workplace accident" code has to go in on the day itself, even if the report takes longer to confirm.

The deadline for filing the report, and how the resulting leave is paid, are covered in the industrial accident procedure and leave rules.
