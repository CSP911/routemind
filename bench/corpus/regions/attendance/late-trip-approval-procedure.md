# Requesting approval for a trip that already departed

A trip that departs while its request is still a draft, still pending, or was never submitted does not become invalid — but it stops following the ordinary approval deadline and moves onto this path instead.

## Steps
1. Submit or resubmit the trip request immediately — do not wait until return.
2. File the trip approval exception form alongside it, stating the departure date already passed.
3. The HR desk reviews and signs off regardless of trip type, even for a domestic trip that would normally need only the line manager.
4. Once approved, the attendance record is entered as an exception-approved trip, not an ordinary one.

## The window
The exception form must be filed **within 3 calendar days of the departure date, not the return date**. A two-week overseas trip filed on day 10 of travel is outside the window even though the traveller hasn't returned yet.

## What it doesn't fix
Exception approval clears the attendance record. It does not extend the separate deadline for claiming the trip's daily allowance, which still runs on its own schedule.
