# Equipment lending approval role

Three roles touch a piece of lent equipment, and each does one part only.

| Stage | Role | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Issue | IT team | Issues the device, holds the signed loan agreement form |
| Return | Line manager | Confirms the employee has physically handed the equipment back |
| Deduction | HR desk | Applies any charge from the equipment loss and damage charges table to final pay |

The line manager's confirmation covers the physical handover only — it does not close the loan by itself. IT still has to log the device as received, and that log is what the HR desk checks before processing the final payslip.

**A line manager confirming "equipment returned" in an email or a chat message is not the same as IT logging the return — if IT has no entry, the deduction goes through on schedule regardless of what the manager said.** Employees relying on an informal confirmation from their manager are the ones most often caught by this.

The same HR desk that applies an equipment charge also confirms the leave balance under entitlement-case-payroll-cutoff before either reaches the final payslip.
