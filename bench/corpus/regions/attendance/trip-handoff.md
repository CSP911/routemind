# Handover from trip to settlement

This is where attendance and expenses meet. Out of order, the claim comes back.

1. **Within three days of returning**, confirm the trip dates in the attendance system (HR desk checks).
2. Calculate the daily allowance from the confirmed days — a day that is not in attendance pays nothing.
3. File the expense claim. Attach flight and lodging receipts and local card slips.
4. For an overseas trip, state the date whose exchange rate you used.
5. The allowance rides on pay as a tax-free item → anything above the cap is taxed.

| What usually blocks this step | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Trip days do not match attendance | Attendance on a business trip |
| There is no receipt | Qualifying evidence · Case: receipts lost on an overseas trip |
| The allowance came through as taxable | Tax-free allowances |
