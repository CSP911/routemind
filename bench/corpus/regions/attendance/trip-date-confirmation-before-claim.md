# Confirming dates before a claim can be filed

The attendance system carries a trip through three states: requested, confirmed, and closed. A daily allowance claim can only be filed once the record reaches confirmed — the state the line manager sets once the traveller is actually back and the dates match what was approved.

## Why "requested" isn't enough

A requested trip can still change: dates slip, a leg gets cancelled, an overseas trip gets rerouted. The claim form pulls its dates directly from the attendance record, so filing against a requested entry means filing against numbers that might not survive the trip.

| Attendance status | Claim allowed? |
|---|---|
| Requested | No |
| Confirmed | Yes |
| Closed (already claimed) | No — duplicate |

**Filing the claim the same day the traveller returns, before the line manager has confirmed the record, is the single most common reason a claim bounces back.** The fix isn't to resubmit faster — it's to wait for the confirmation.

Once confirmed, the claim itself runs on its own filing deadline, counted separately from this step.
