# Priority order for last-week-of-December requests

The last week of December fills up early every year. When more requests land for that week than the team can release at once, they are not approved in the order they were filed.

## How priority is set

| Basis | Applies |
|---|---|
| Team head's queue order | Set once per year, published before November |
| Length of service | Used only to break a tie within the same queue position |
| Date filed | Not used as the primary basis |

## Practical effect

A request filed on 1 November can still lose out to one filed on 20 November if the team head's queue places the later filer first for coverage reasons.

**Submitting early does not guarantee a slot for the last week of December — priority follows the queue the team head sets, not the order requests arrived in.**

Whether a granted block can actually span into 1 January without being rejected is a separate question, covered by the year-end leave window.
