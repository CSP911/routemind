# Who signs off a change to parental leave

An extension, an early return, or the start of a second block all touch two different concerns, and each concern sits with a different approver.

## The two approvers

- **HR desk** — confirms the request meets the notice window, checks it against the child's eligibility age, and updates the recorded leave dates.
- **Line manager** — confirms the team can accommodate the change, particularly a return earlier or later than planned, and flags anything relevant to what role the employee comes back to.

## The trap

**A manager's agreement is not enough on its own.** A manager can happily accept an early return with two weeks' notice, but if the HR desk has not logged the new date, the employee's attendance record still shows them on leave, and the return is not valid for payroll or benefits purposes until that update goes through.

Where the return lands on a different team than the one the employee left, this same manager sign-off is only the first step, not the whole answer.
