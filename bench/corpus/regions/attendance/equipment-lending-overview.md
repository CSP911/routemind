# Equipment lending overview

Any laptop, phone or peripheral issued for work use is company property on loan, not a personal purchase. It stays lent until it is formally returned, whether the employee is on site, on a trip, on leave of absence, or leaving the company altogether.

## What is covered

A laptop or phone signed out through IT counts. Personal devices used for work under a BYOD arrangement do not — there is nothing to return, so nothing here applies to them.

## Where each rule lives

- The paperwork at handover: see the equipment loan agreement form.
- What happens during a leave of absence: see equipment during leave of absence.
- The window to return equipment on leaving, and what happens if it slips: see equipment return deadline on leaving and equipment loss and damage charges.
- Who signs off at each stage: see equipment lending approval role.

**Equipment handed over without the loan agreement form signed is not officially lent — if it goes missing, there is no record to charge the cost against, and the loss is absorbed by the company instead of the employee.**

The same HR desk that clears a leaver's final balance also clears their equipment file before the last payslip runs.
