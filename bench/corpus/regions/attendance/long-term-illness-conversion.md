# When a long illness becomes leave of absence

## The threshold

An illness absence that reaches 90 consecutive days automatically converts to unpaid leave of absence from day 91. This applies whether the first 90 days were covered by a certificate under the ordinary sick leave track or not.

## What changes and what doesn't

Pay stops from day 91, matching the pay status of other unpaid leave of absence categories. Service continuity is unaffected — the same principle that keeps 12 months of leave of absence from resetting the service clock applies here, so the anniversary date and milestone-year timing keep moving as normal.

## The trap

**The conversion at day 90 is automatic — it does not wait for the employee, the manager, or HR to file anything, and pay stops on day 91 regardless of whether a fresh certificate is on file.** An employee expecting sick pay to continue until someone processes paperwork will find the gap already open.

A worked example of this crossing is in the 90-day case.
