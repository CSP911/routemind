# A trip that spans a weekend

An employee travels Friday through Monday for a client site visit, with the actual work happening Friday and Monday and Saturday/Sunday spent purely in transit and at the hotel with no work performed.

## What gets recorded

| Day | Status |
|---|---|
| Friday | Worked (trip day) |
| Saturday | Not worked — travel only, no duty performed |
| Sunday | Not worked — travel only, no duty performed |
| Monday | Worked (trip day) |

Saturday and Sunday are not entered as trip days simply because the employee was away from home. **The trap is assuming that being on a trip over a weekend automatically converts those two days into paid working time or automatic compensatory leave — it does not, unless actual duty was performed on that specific day.** If the client meeting itself had run on Saturday, that day would be entered as worked and would follow the weekend-duty rule instead.

The attendance system does not distinguish "traveling" from "at home" — only "duty performed" or not, so the requester must mark each day correctly at the time of entry.
