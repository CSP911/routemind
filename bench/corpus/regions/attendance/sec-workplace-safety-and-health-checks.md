# Workplace safety and health checks

Annual workplace health and safety checks are essential components of employee welfare, requiring careful planning and execution. This section details how these assessments are scheduled, **recorded, and the entitlement to time off for participation**. Each health check must be meticulously documented to comply with legal standards and internal policies.

A particularly pressing issue occurs when there's an accident at work, which must be reported promptly to avoid regulatory repercussions. Failing to report or document these incidents appropriately can result in non-compliance.

For those navigating these processes, subsequent sections will elaborate on specific documentation requirements and accident reporting procedures.
