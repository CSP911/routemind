# Reporting a leave of absence inside a first-year balance

A first-year joiner's balance is still being prorated month by month. If leave of absence starts before that first year closes, HR needs to know quickly enough to freeze the accrual at the right point.

## The deadline

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Leave of absence starts during a first, prorated year | Report to the HR desk within 5 working days of the start date |

Reporting late does not restore anything — it only delays the correction to the record that the year-end lapse calculation will later read.

## Why this is tighter than it looks

The confirmation deadline for a new joiner's day count runs on its own separate clock; this deadline exists specifically for the LOA start, because it is the event that stops further accrual mid-year.
