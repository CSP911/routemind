# Attendance dispute escalation procedure

Not every wrong-looking record is a data entry problem. Sometimes the team lead logged the day exactly as intended — coding it as unauthorized absence rather than leave, for example — and the employee disagrees with the judgment call, not the timestamp.

1. The employee files the correction form checked "disputed entry," stating what the record currently says and what it should say instead.
2. The team lead responds in writing within 5 working days, either agreeing or setting out why the record stands.
3. If they don't agree, the HR desk reviews both statements and any evidence — leave requests, prior approvals, messages — and makes the final call. The team lead's disagreement does not block the HR desk from resolving it.

**A dispute does not pause the correction deadline — if the 10-day window from the month's close passes while the two sides are still arguing, the fix still needs the late-correction override even though the delay wasn't the employee's fault.** File on time and let the disagreement play out afterward.

Once a disputed record is resolved, it follows the same payroll timing as any other late correction.
