# Case: the final balance for someone who leaves mid-year

An employee at 6 years of service (entitlement 16 days, having passed the 5-year milestone) resigns effective 30 September, having worked 9 of the 12 months in the leave year. Entitlement for the year is prorated to the months actually worked before payout is calculated.

## The arithmetic

| Step | Figure |
|---|---|
| Annual entitlement | 16 days |
| Prorated for 9/12 months | 12 days |
| Days already used | 5 days |
| Final balance paid out | 7 days |

## The trap

**If the resignation date falls just after an anniversary that raised the entitlement, the higher figure applies to the months worked in that anniversary year — not the lower figure from before the bump.** Settling the payout against last year's entitlement number understates what is owed.

The payout formula itself, and why the milestone date matters to it, is covered where the final settlement is calculated.
