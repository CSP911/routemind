# Requesting payout of unused leave

Unused leave does not convert to cash automatically. Whether it lapses at year-end or is paid out at resignation, the payout has to be requested on this form and confirmed by HR before it is added to a payslip.

## What the form needs

- Current leave balance as shown in the attendance system
- Reason for payout: resignation, or year-end lapse where payout applies
- Line manager's confirmation that the balance shown is accurate
- Employee signature and date

## Submission

The form must reach the HR desk before the relevant payroll cutoff — the last regular monthly cutoff for a year-end lapse, or the leaver cutoff for a resignation. Missing either cutoff pushes the payout to the following month's run.

**Submitting the form without the line manager's balance confirmation attached is the most common reason it bounces back — HR will not process a payout against a balance nobody but the employee has signed off on**, and resubmitting with the same gap only repeats the delay.

Once the payout is approved, the wage basis and the payslip it lands on both follow fixed rules that don't depend on how the request itself was filed.
