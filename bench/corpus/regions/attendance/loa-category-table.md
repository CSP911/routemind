# Leave of absence categories at a glance

## Basis, duration and pay

| Category | Basis | Maximum duration | Pay during leave | Service continuity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Study leave | Company approval | Up to 1 year | Unpaid | Continues |
| Military service leave | Statutory | Length of service obligation | Unpaid | Continues, reinstatement guaranteed |
| Family care leave | Statutory | Up to 90 days per instance | Unpaid | Continues |
| Unpaid personal leave | Company approval | Up to 6 months | Unpaid | Continues |

## Why the basis column matters
Study leave and unpaid personal leave are discretionary — the HR desk can decline them if the team cannot absorb the absence. Military leave and family care leave are not: once the statutory conditions are met, the leave has to be granted regardless of staffing.

**None of these four carry the premium-sharing arrangement that applies during parental leave — that split between what the company pays monthly and what is deferred is specific to parental leave and does not extend to this list.** Health insurance continues, but the employee is billed for the full premium unless a separate arrangement is agreed.

The parental leave premium table covers what does apply there.
