# Recording a same-morning leave call after the fact

## What the form is for
A same-morning leave request is approved by phone or message before the day starts, but the attendance system has no field for a verbal approval. The same-morning leave notification form is what turns that approval into a recorded leave day rather than an unexplained absence.

## When to file it
File it the same day, before the end of business — not the next morning. A same-morning approval that is never logged on the form leaves no record behind it, and both payroll and attendance reports will show the day as unaccounted rather than as leave.

## What must be on it
| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Date and unit | Full day, half-day or quarter-day |
| Approver | Name of the person who gave verbal approval |
| Time of contact | Before the employee's normal start time |

**The form records the leave — it does not grant it.** Approval still has to have happened before the start time named on the form; filing the form afterwards with a later contact time does not retroactively make a late call valid.
