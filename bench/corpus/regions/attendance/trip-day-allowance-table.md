# Trip day allowance rates

The trip allowance is a flat daily payment on top of salary, separate from whatever the trip's hours are coded as for attendance purposes.

## Rates by destination

| Trip type | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Domestic | 30,000 KRW |
| Overseas | 70,000 KRW |

A trip day is any calendar day on which the employee is recorded as travelling on an approved trip request, including the departure and return days.

## What the rate does not depend on

**The allowance is paid per calendar day of the trip, not per hour worked that day.** A flight that departs at 11pm still draws the full day's rate for that calendar date, even though almost none of the actual working hours fall within it. Splitting a trip day into a partial allowance based on hours worked is not how this is calculated, and requesting it that way will be rejected.

A trip spanning a weekend or public holiday still draws the daily allowance for those days even though the hours are coded differently for attendance.
