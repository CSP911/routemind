# Unpaid personal leave request form

## When to use it
This form covers personal leave of absence that does not fall under study, military, or family care leave — reasons the employee does not have to disclose to the line manager in detail, only confirm with the HR desk.

## What it records
- Requested start and end date
- Total requested duration, up to the 6-month cap
- Line manager acknowledgement
- HR desk approval and the attendance system entry it triggers

## Routing
The form goes to the line manager first, then to the HR desk for final approval. Approval has to be recorded before the leave starts — a request submitted after the absence has already begun is not backdated.

**Using this form for a leave that actually qualifies as study, military, or family care leave is the mistake to avoid — the wrong category miscodes the leave in the attendance system, and the service-continuity and evidence rules that apply to the correct category do not attach retroactively.** File it under the right category from the start.

Return notice for this category, and the other three, is covered separately.
