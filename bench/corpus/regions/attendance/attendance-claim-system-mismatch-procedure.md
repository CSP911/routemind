# When attendance and claim dates don't match

Occasionally a claim is filed with dates that don't line up with the confirmed attendance record — a day added, a day dropped, or an overseas dateline recorded differently in each system.

## Steps

1. The claim is held, not rejected outright — a date mismatch is treated as needing correction rather than being void.
2. The employee or the line manager checks the confirmed attendance record first; it is the reference copy.
3. If the attendance record itself is wrong, it goes through the standard attendance correction request before anything else moves.
4. Once the attendance record is right, the claim is either corrected to match it or refiled.
5. The HR desk clears the claim only after the two show identical dates.

**A mismatch is never resolved by editing the claim to whatever the employee remembers — the attendance record is corrected first, and the claim is made to match it, never the other way round.**
