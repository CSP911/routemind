# Payroll cutoff for a final-balance payout

## The cutoff
| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Final balance confirmed with HR desk | At least 5 business days before the final payroll run |
| Payout included in last payslip | Same cycle, if confirmed in time |
| Payout processed separately | Following cycle, if confirmed late |

For a leaver at 6 years of service resigning effective 30 September, with 16 days' annual entitlement prorated to 12 for the 9 months worked and 5 already used, the resulting 7-day final balance only reaches the September payslip if HR has signed it off within the window above.

## The trap
**Missing the cutoff does not forfeit the balance — it just delays it to the next payroll cycle**, which for someone leaving the company can mean weeks after their last working day, paid separately rather than folded into the final salary. Employees who expect a single clean final payment should raise the final-balance case the moment resignation is confirmed, not on their last day.

The 7-day figure itself follows the calculation set out for a leaver's final balance — this page only governs when it's paid, not how it's counted.
