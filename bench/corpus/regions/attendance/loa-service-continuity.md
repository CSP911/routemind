# Service continuity across a leave of absence

## The service clock keeps running

Time on an approved leave of absence — childcare leave, unpaid personal leave, medical leave — counts toward your total years of service for entitlement purposes. Your 3/5/7-year milestone dates do not shift because you took the leave.

## But it does not generate new days

**The months spent on leave of absence are excluded when that year's leave day count is calculated, even though they still count toward your milestone year.** A full year of childcare leave, for instance, moves you from year 2 to year 3 of service, but you return with a balance prorated for the time actually worked.

## Worked example

| Event | Effect on service years | Effect on that year's days |
|---|---|---|
| 12 months on leave of absence | +1 year (unaffected) | Prorated down for time worked |

Because the milestone clock keeps running through a leave of absence, someone can return from a year off and find their entitlement has jumped a tier — exactly when that jump takes effect is covered next.
