# Requesting study leave

## Before submitting
Study leave needs a study plan attached to the request — the institution, the programme, and its expected end date. Without this, the HR desk will return the request rather than process it.

## The approval chain
1. Submit the request and study plan to the line manager at least 30 days before the intended start date.
2. Once the manager confirms the team can cover the absence, the request goes to the HR desk for final approval and entry into the attendance system.
3. The HR desk confirms the maximum duration allowed — up to 1 year — against the study plan's end date.

## Renewal
Study leave is not renewed automatically at the end of the approved period. A programme running longer than planned needs a fresh request, and the total time on study leave still cannot exceed the one-year cap regardless of how many separate requests make it up.

**Treating study leave as extendable the same way a parental leave block is extended is the mistake to avoid — there is no "second block" mechanism here, only a hard ceiling.** Past that ceiling, any further absence has to be requested as unpaid personal leave instead.

What happens when a request actually reaches that ceiling is worked through separately.
