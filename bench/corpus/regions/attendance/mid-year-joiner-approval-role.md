# Who confirms a mid-year joiner's proration

Confirming a new joiner's prorated leave is a routine calculation, not one of the escalated cases that goes to the HR desk for a ruling — but it still needs a defined owner or the day count sits unconfirmed until someone tries to book leave.

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Confirm the join date entered is correct | Line manager |
| Calculate the prorated day count from the joining-month table | HR desk |
| Enter the confirmed figure into the attendance system | HR desk |
| Flag any transfer-in or LOA complication for case review | Line manager, on discovery |

The line manager's role is limited to confirming the join date is right and raising anything unusual — a transfer, a known upcoming leave of absence — early enough that HR calculates the right number the first time.

**A join date corrected after the prorated figure has already been entered does not automatically recalculate the balance** — someone has to redo it by hand, which is why catching the date early matters more than catching the balance later. Escalated complications from that point follow the same approval authority as any other entitlement case.
