# Settling leave balance for someone who left

## Steps

1. HR calculates entitlement earned up to the last working day, prorated for the partial year, at the day rate in force on that date — not the rate the person would have reached at their next milestone.
2. Unused accrued days are compared against days actually taken.
3. Any unused balance is paid out with final salary; days taken beyond entitlement are deducted.

## The trap

**Someone who resigns one day before a 3/5/7-year anniversary is settled at the lower rate — the extra day is not prorated in for time served since the last milestone.** This catches people who time their notice period around the wrong date.

| Situation | Settlement |
|---|---|
| Left before milestone | Old rate, prorated |
| Left after milestone | New rate, prorated |
| Unused days | Paid out |
| Days taken beyond entitlement | Deducted from final pay |

Final settlement runs alongside the rest of the leaver checklist handled at the HR desk.
