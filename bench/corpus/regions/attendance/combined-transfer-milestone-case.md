# Case: a transfer and a milestone landing in the same year

An employee transferred from a group affiliate in March 2022 with their original 2019 service date preserved. Their 5-year anniversary, measured from 2019, falls in March 2024 — two years after the transfer, at the new employer, but on the old clock.

## Why the milestone still fires on the old date

| Event | Effect on service-start-date | Effect on 3/5/7 ladder |
|---|---|---|
| Documented group transfer | preserved | ladder continues unbroken |
| 3 years and over | — | 15 days, +1 every 2 years (capped at 25) |

Because the service-start-date was never reset, the accrual engine has no idea a transfer happened — it just sees continuous service and applies the bump on schedule.

## The trap

**A manager who only remembers the transfer date, not the original hire date, will expect the milestone two years later than it actually lands, because they are counting from the wrong anchor.** The anchor is always the field, not memory.

That field's ownership rules apply here exactly as they do to a plain transfer case.
