# Leave of absence other than parental

This section addresses non-parental leave categories, including study, military service, family care, and unpaid leave. Each type of leave has unique implications for service continuity and eligibility for benefits. Understanding these nuances is essential to managing the leaves effectively, particularly how they affect entitlements and service milestones.

**A common error is failing to account for service continuity**, particularly with unpaid leave, which may inadvertently interrupt service and affect entitlements. Always cross-check company policy to determine how various leaves impact an employee's service record and benefits.

Ensure proper communication and documentation for each leave type as per company policy to prevent future disputes or misinterpretations related to service continuity and entitlement impacts.
