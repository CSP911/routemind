# The annual health check requirement

Every employee, regardless of role or contract type, attends a general health check once every calendar year. The company arranges the appointment through its designated provider; an employee who has already had an equivalent check elsewhere in the same year may submit that result instead, provided it falls within the same calendar year and covers the standard panel.

| Employee category | Frequency | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Office-based, non-hazardous duty | Once per calendar year | Company-designated provider, or equivalent self-arranged |
| Hazardous-duty roles | Once per calendar year, plus any provider-required follow-up | Company-designated provider only |

Completion means the appointment took place and the result category has been entered into the attendance system — not merely that an appointment was booked.

**Booking the appointment is not the same as completing the check.** An employee who books a slot for January but the year closes before attending it is recorded as not completed for that calendar year, and the requirement restarts rather than carrying over.

What gets recorded from the result, and how much paid time is given to attend, are each covered separately.
