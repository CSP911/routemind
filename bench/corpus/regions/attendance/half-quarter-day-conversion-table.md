# Combining half and quarter-day bookings

## The conversion table
| Unit | Fraction of a day | Quarter-days |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter-day | 0.25 | 1 |
| Half-day | 0.5 | 2 |
| Full day | 1.0 | 4 |

## Combining units on the same day
A quarter-day booked in the morning and a quarter-day booked in the afternoon of the same calendar day add up to a half-day of leave used — the attendance system records one leave line per day, so the second entry adds to the running total rather than sitting beside it as a separate quarter-day.

**Adding a third quarter-day later in the same day does not create a strange 0.75-day entry — it silently rounds the day up to a full day used**, and the balance deduction reflects that rounded total, not the sum of what was individually requested.

## What this means for approval
Because the combined total is what routes for sign-off, a small quarter-day request that pushes an existing booking into half-day territory needs half-day approval, even though each request looked minor on its own.

A day already booked this way can only be corrected through the leave request amendment form, not by adding another quarter-day on top of it.
