# Who approves a trip

A submitted trip request needs a different combination of sign-offs depending on where it goes.

## Domestic
The line manager is the sole approver. Once they approve, the request becomes an attendance entry and the trip is confirmed.

## Overseas
The line manager approves first, then the HR desk. The HR desk checks the trip against insurance coverage and allowance eligibility for travel outside Korea before clearing it. **Both approvals are required — the manager's is not sufficient on its own**, and a request sitting with manager approval only is still pending, not confirmed, no matter how close departure is.

## If an approver is unavailable
A line manager or HR desk reviewer who cannot act before departure does not remove the requirement; the request escalates instead, following a fixed backup chain rather than waiting indefinitely.

Which of these two approvers actually gets consulted, and how fast, depends on the trip's lead time — covered next.
