# Notifying an employee of an entitlement case outcome

## Steps
1. HR desk confirms the case (transfer, rehire, LOA, milestone, or final balance).
2. The service-start date or accrual figure is updated in the system.
3. The employee is notified in writing of the new entitlement and its effective date.
4. The employee has the standard correction window to flag a discrepancy.

## The trap
**Notification confirms that a decision was made — it does not itself change the effective date.** A milestone bump, for instance, takes effect on the anniversary regardless of when HR gets around to sending the notice; an employee who reads a notification dated three weeks after their 5-year anniversary should not assume the extra day only started from the notification date.

Treat the written notice as a record of what already happened, not as the trigger — and if the figures in it look wrong, raise it within the correction window rather than waiting for the next one to come around.
