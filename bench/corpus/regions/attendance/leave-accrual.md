# Leave entitlement

How many days are granted, by length of service. The table is on the page below; this is how the
count is arrived at and why two people who joined the same year can have different numbers.

## What counts as service
Continuous employment from the joining date, including periods of leave of absence. It is not
adjusted for part-time or for time on secondment — service is service. What *is* adjusted is the
entitlement for the year in which you were absent, which is prorated to days actually worked.

## The three regimes
- **Under a year.** One day for each completed month, up to eleven. A month is completed on the day
  before the same date next month — join on the 15th and the first day arrives on the 14th.
- **One year and over.** Fifteen days, granted whole on 1 January.
- **Three years and over.** Fifteen plus one for every two further years, capped at twenty-five. The
  increase lands on the 1 January after the anniversary, not on the anniversary.

## Where the first year goes wrong
Someone who joins in July accrues monthly through their first calendar year *and* receives a prorated
grant on the following 1 January. **These are not added.** The monthly accrual stops when the grant
arrives. The proration table below gives the figure for each joining month; use it rather than
calculating, because the rounding is not obvious.

## Lapse and payout
Days lapse on 31 December. The lapsed balance is paid at the ordinary daily rate in the January pay
run. Nothing carries over, including days that were booked and cancelled in December.
