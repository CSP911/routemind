# Leave booked in a final week

An employee's last day of employment is confirmed as 31 August. With 9 days of annual leave remaining, they book leave for 25–29 August, the final working week before the date on file.

## What happens to the week

The 5 booked days are deducted from the balance in the normal way, coded exactly as any other leave block. The last day of employment stays 31 August — the same date agreed with HR before the leave was booked, not a date extended by the days taken.

## What happens to the rest

After the 5 days are deducted, 4 days remain unused. Since there is no further working time before 31 August to take them, those 4 days are paid out rather than lapsing.

**Booking every remaining day as leave to avoid a payout, rather than leaving some unused, is a valid choice — but it has to be booked and approved before 31 August, not assumed automatically.** Days not booked in time are paid out; they are not carried past the last day of employment.
