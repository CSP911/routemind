# When entitlement is recalculated

## Triggers

HR recalculates your leave entitlement automatically on:

- Your service anniversary (see the 3/5/7-year milestone table)
- Return from a leave of absence
- A logged internal transfer from a group company
- A rehire where service has been bridged

## What does not trigger a recalculation

**Changing job title, team, or manager does not touch your leave entitlement — only service length and the events above do.** Requests to recalculate for any other reason are declined at the HR desk.

| Trigger | Recalculated? |
|---|---|
| Anniversary | Yes |
| Return from leave of absence | Yes |
| Promotion | No |
| Team transfer, same company | No |

If you believe a recalculation was missed, raise it before your current leave year closes — that process is covered next.
