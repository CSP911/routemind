# The year your leave increases at 3, 5 and 7 years

## The band

| Service | Annual leave |
|---|---|
| 1 year and over | 15 days |
| 3 years and over | 15 days, +1 every 2 years (capped at 25) |

The extra day is added at 3 years, again at 5, again at 7, and so on until the total reaches the cap of 25.

## When the increase takes effect

The increase applies from your service anniversary date, not from the start of the calendar leave year. **If your anniversary falls mid-year, you're on the old day count up to that date and the new one afterward — the leave year is split, never rounded up or back.**

| Anniversary reached | Days before | Days after |
|---|---|---|
| 3rd year | 15 | 16 |
| 5th year | 16 | 17 |
| 7th year | 17 | 18 |

HR applies this automatically on the anniversary; nothing needs to be filed. If someone leaves before an upcoming milestone, their final balance is settled at the pre-milestone rate — covered next.
