# Core hours and the flexible band

Every working day splits into a fixed core and a flexible band either side of it. The core applies identically whether the day is worked in the office or from home.

| Segment | Window | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Flexible start | 07:00–10:00 | Choose any start time in this window |
| Core hours | 10:00–16:00 | Mandatory presence or reachability |
| Flexible end | 16:00–20:00 | Finish once the daily total is met |
| Daily total | — | 8 hours, excluding a 1-hour lunch |

Lunch is not counted inside the core band — a lunch taken between 12:00 and 13:00 does not need cover, but the core hours either side of it still do.

**Arriving after 10:00 or leaving before 16:00 is a core-hours breach even if the full 8 hours are worked that day** — the system flags it separately from a simple shortfall, and repeated flags are what a manager actually reviews, not the monthly total. This is the band the flexitime month is measured against, covered next.
