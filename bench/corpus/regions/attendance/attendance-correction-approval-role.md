# Attendance correction approval role

The line manager confirms which version of the day is correct — that a missing clock-in matches a day actually worked, that one of two duplicate entries is the real one, or that a disputed record should read differently. The HR desk does not re-investigate this; it takes the manager's confirmation and makes the system edit.

| Step | Who |
|---|---|
| Confirm what the record should say | Line manager |
| Amend the attendance system entry | HR desk |
| Approve an override past the 10-day deadline | HR desk, with manager's written confirmation attached |

**The HR desk will not edit a record on a manager's verbal say-so, even for an obvious case like a forgotten clock-in on a day everyone remembers the employee being in the office.** The confirmation has to be in writing on the correction form itself, not a message referenced from it.

When the manager and the employee cannot agree on what the record should say, the case moves to escalation instead.
