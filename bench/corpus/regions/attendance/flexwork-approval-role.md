# Who approves a flexible or remote arrangement

Two different approvals apply to flexible and remote working, and they cover different things.

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Approving a specific WFH day or a hybrid split | Line manager |
| Entering the standing arrangement into the system | HR desk |
| Auditing monthly flexitime balances | HR desk |

The line manager's approval covers whether the arrangement fits the team's workload. It does not, by itself, make the arrangement official in attendance records.

**A pattern agreed verbally with a manager but never entered by the HR desk does not exist for audit purposes** — if a shortfall or a core-hours breach is later reviewed, the system only shows what HR entered, not what was discussed. Any hybrid split or recurring WFH day needs to reach the HR desk before it can be relied on. Overtime approval follows the same manager-then-HR split, covered separately.
