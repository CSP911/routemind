# Two attendance records for one day

An employee clocked in from the office PC at 9:02 and, separately, the mobile app registered a check-in at 9:14 after a delayed sync. The attendance system now shows two entries for the same morning — neither one obviously wrong, both technically real.

The employee files the correction form checked "duplicate record," lists both timestamps as shown in the system, and states which should be kept — normally the earlier, since it reflects actual arrival. The line manager confirms and the HR desk deletes the later entry rather than merging or averaging the two.

**Leaving both entries in place is not neutral — the system reads the pair as two separate presence windows and can flag the day as an anomaly on its own, which is worse than either single entry would have been.** Duplicate records should be resolved even when neither timestamp is disputed and the difference looks trivial.

Not every disagreement is this clean — when the employee and the team lead disagree on what actually happened, it goes to escalation.
