# A month closing with a flexitime shortfall

An employee finishes March 6 hours short of the standard monthly total, having started late on several days without making up the time.

Under the flexitime month rule, the shortfall is not deducted immediately. It carries into April with a requirement to clear it before the April close.

By the end of April, the employee has only made up 4 of the 6 hours — 2 hours remain open.

| Point | Status |
|---|---|
| End of March | 6 hours short, carried to April |
| End of April | 2 hours still short |
| Outcome | Deducted as a quarter-day of leave |

**A shortfall that survives two consecutive month closes converts to a leave deduction without a separate approval step** — the employee does not get a warning at the point of conversion, only a lower leave balance on the next check. Clearing a shortfall inside the same month it opens is the only way to avoid this. The half/quarter-day conversion used here follows the same table as any other partial-day leave.
