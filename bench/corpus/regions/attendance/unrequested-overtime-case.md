# Overtime worked but never requested

An employee stays 3 hours past the standard shift to finish a deliverable, without submitting an overtime request beforehand or within the 3-working-day late-filing window.

## What happens to the hours

| Step | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Hours worked | 3 hours past standard shift |
| Request filed | None, at any point |
| Overtime pay | Not applied |
| Attendance record | Logged as ordinary hours worked, not overtime |

**The hours are real and show up in the attendance system, but without a request they are never converted to paid overtime — being able to prove the work happened is not the same as having requested it.** HR does not backfill a request on an employee's behalf, and a manager's after-the-fact acknowledgement does not substitute for one filed in time.

The only way to correct this after the window has closed is to raise it as an exception with the HR desk, and approval is not routine.
