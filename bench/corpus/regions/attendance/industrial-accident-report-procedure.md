# Reporting an industrial accident

## Steps

1. Notify the line manager as soon as the injury happens, or as soon as the employee is able to.
2. File the accident report with the HR desk within 3 days of the injury.
3. Submit the hospital's initial certification along with the report.
4. HR forwards the report and certification to the insurer and confirms the industrial accident coding back to the employee and manager.

## Why the deadline matters

Insurance coverage and the fully-paid coding described in industrial accident and injury leave both depend on this report being on file. An injury that isn't reported inside the window risks being treated as ordinary illness by default, which draws on annual leave instead.

## The trap

**The 3-day clock starts from the date of the injury, not from the date the employee is well enough to come in and file it.** A manager who waits for the employee to recover before reporting routinely misses the window; the report can and should be filed by the manager on the employee's behalf if needed.

Once the accident track is confirmed, no further certificate under the ordinary sick leave evidence table is required for the same absence.
