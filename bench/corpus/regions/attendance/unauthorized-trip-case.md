# A trip taken without a request

An employee travels to a supplier's site for two days on their own initiative, without filing a trip request beforehand, and only reports it to their manager on return.

## How it's recorded

Because no approved request exists for the dates in question, the two days cannot be entered as trip days after the fact. The manager has two options: retroactively approve the trip through the trip request form, dated and noted as a late filing, or treat the two days as unauthorized absence.

**The trap is assuming that a trip clearly done for company business is automatically credited once it's explained — it isn't, until someone with approval authority files the retroactive request.** A verbal "that's fine, thanks for going" from a manager is not itself an attendance record.

Even where retroactive approval is granted, the late filing is noted as such — it is not backdated to look like an ordinary pre-approved trip.
