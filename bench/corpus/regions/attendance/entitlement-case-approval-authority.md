# Who approves an entitlement case

## Approval by case type
| Case type | Who confirms the facts | Who signs off |
|---|---|---|
| Group transfer | Originating affiliate's HR + receiving HR desk | HR desk |
| Rehire | Previous employment record | HR desk |
| Leave of absence | Approved LOA record | HR desk |
| 3/5/7-year milestone | System, from the service-start date | No approval — automatic |
| Final balance (leaver) | Resignation date and usage log | HR desk + payroll |

## What actually happens
For four of the five case types, someone at the HR desk has to look at a document and make a call. The milestone case is the exception: **it is not a decision anyone signs off on, it is a calculation that fires on the anniversary date recorded in the service-start date field.** A manager "approving" a milestone bump early or late has no effect on when it actually applies — the date field decides it, not the approval.

Where the milestone case can go wrong is elsewhere: if the service-start date itself is wrong, the automatic bump lands on the wrong day, and there is no approval step meant to catch that.

If you're unsure whether a case even has an approver, the service-start-date record says who can touch it and who can't.
