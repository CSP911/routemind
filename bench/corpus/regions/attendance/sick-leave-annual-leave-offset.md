# Sick days and annual leave

## The default

Any absence due to illness or injury is deducted from the annual leave balance from the first day, exactly like a booked holiday, unless a doctor's certificate is filed. This applies whether the absence is a single day or several in a row.

## Where the offset stops

Once a certificate is filed for an absence of 3 or more consecutive days, the days covered by that certificate — from day 3 onward — are coded as unpaid sick leave instead, and stop drawing down the annual balance. The first two days of any absence are never returned to the balance, certificate or not.

## The trap

**Employees routinely assume that filing a certificate reimburses the whole absence back into their annual leave balance — it doesn't. Only the days from the certificate's coverage forward are protected; the opening days are already spent.**
