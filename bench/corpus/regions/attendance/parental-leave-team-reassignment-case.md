# Returning to a different team after parental leave

## The case

An employee goes on a six-month parental leave block. While they are away, their original team is restructured and their role no longer exists in its previous form. On return, they are placed in a different team at the same job level and pay.

## What is guaranteed and what is not

**Parental leave guarantees the same level and pay on return — it does not guarantee the same desk, team or manager.** An employee who assumes their exact prior role is protected by rule will find that only the level and pay are the protected terms; the team assignment itself is a normal management decision.

## Recording the change

The HR desk logs the new team assignment as part of closing out the leave case, alongside the confirmed return date, so the attendance record reflects the employee's actual reporting line from the day they come back.

For how a transfer changes — or does not change — entitlement itself, see group-transfer-entitlement.
