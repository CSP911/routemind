# Returning from parental leave early

## The case

An employee's parental leave was agreed from 1 March to 31 August — six months. The employee asks to return on 1 May instead, four months early.

## What happens to the remaining period

The HR desk confirms the earlier return date and updates the record; the employee resumes normal duties and pay from 1 May. **The four unused months are not banked, added to the annual leave balance, or paid out — parental leave that is not taken simply lapses.** It is leave of absence for a specific purpose, not a leave-day entitlement.

## What still applies

The employee's service clock, which kept running throughout the original six-month block, is unaffected by cutting the block short — the return date only changes when normal accrual and duties resume.

For how the service clock behaves across a full, unshortened block, see loa-service-continuity.
