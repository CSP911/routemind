# Who acts on a follow-up health check result

A "follow-up required" or "restricted duty" result splits responsibility between two roles, and neither one substitutes for the other.

| Step | Role responsible |
|---|---|
| Receiving the result category from the provider | HR desk |
| Scheduling and confirming the follow-up appointment | HR desk |
| Releasing the employee's time to attend | Line manager |
| Adjusting duties if "restricted duty" applies | Line manager, on HR desk's instruction |

The HR desk holds the result and drives the follow-up; the line manager's role is limited to giving the employee the time and adjusting the work itself once told to.

**A line manager who tries to schedule the follow-up directly with the provider is acting outside the process.** The HR desk is the only point of contact with the provider, precisely because the manager is never shown the result that made the follow-up necessary.

Where the underlying result data is stored, and who can see it, is set out in the health check record table.
