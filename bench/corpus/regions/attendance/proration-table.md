# Mid-year joiner proration

Days granted on the first 1 January after joining: `15 × (months from joining to 31 December ÷ 12)`,
rounded up.

| Joined in | Granted on the first 1 January |
|---|---|
| January | 15 |
| February | 14 |
| March | 13 |
| April | 12 |
| May | 10 |
| June | 9 |
| July | 8 |
| August | 7 |
| September | 5 |
| October | 4 |
| November | 3 |
| December | 2 |

This is **separate from the one-day-per-month accrual in the first year**. The two are not added
together: at the one-year mark the monthly accrual is replaced by the prorated grant. Confusing cases
come up every year — check them with the HR desk.
