# A stint that crosses the tax threshold

An employee already had 140 days logged working from the same country earlier in the year, approved in two separate stints of 70 days each. In August they requested a third stint of 50 days from that same country, which would bring the rolling 12-month total to 190 days.

## What happened
The request landed with the line manager first, who approved it on the basis that 50 days alone was well under the 90-day single-stint cap. The HR desk caught the total at intake, because the cumulative count is theirs to hold, not the manager's — the 190-day total crossed the 183-day threshold before the stint had even started.

**Line manager sign-off on a single stint does not clear the tax threshold check — the HR desk reviews the running total regardless of how short the newest request looks on its own.** The stint itself was not blocked, but the case was referred for a local tax residency assessment before the start date was confirmed, and the approval was held pending that outcome.

The same cumulative logic applies below 183 days too, once a country's running total passes 90 — see the approval role for when the HR desk has to be brought in.
