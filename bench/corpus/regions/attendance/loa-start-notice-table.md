# Notice required before starting leave of absence

## The four notice periods

| Leave type | Notice before start |
|---|---|
| Study leave | At least 30 days before the start date, agreed with the HR desk |
| Family-care leave | At least 14 days before the start date |
| Unpaid leave | At least 30 days before the start date, agreed with the HR desk |
| Military leave | As soon as the call-up or enlistment notice is received |

## Why military leave doesn't fit the table

Study, family-care and unpaid leave are all requests the employee chooses to file, so a standard window gives the HR desk and the line manager time to plan around the absence. Military leave isn't a request in that sense — it's triggered by a call-up date the employee doesn't control.

**A military leave notification filed with less than 30 days before the enlistment date must still be granted; the shortfall is not treated as a late request.** Line managers sometimes push back on two weeks' notice the way they would on a late annual leave booking — that comparison doesn't apply here.

Once any of these four is under way, the notice clock that matters shifts to the other end of the leave — see how much warning each type needs before the employee actually returns.
