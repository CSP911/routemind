# A trip cancelled after approval

A trip is approved for a Wednesday–Friday client visit. On Tuesday afternoon the client postpones and the trip is called off entirely.

## What has to happen

1. The employee or the approving manager submits a cancellation against the original trip request — the same day, if possible.
2. The three days revert to ordinary attendance: the employee is expected to work as normal, not treated as on leave or on trip.
3. If the cancellation reaches HR after Wednesday has already started, that day is handled as a late cancellation rather than a clean withdrawal.

**The trap is assuming that because the trip never happened, the record clears itself — it does not.** An approved trip request sits in the system as a trip until someone actively cancels it, and an unresolved trip record left in place past month end is the more common cause of a wrong payslip than the trip itself ever was.

The notice needed to cancel cleanly follows the same table used for cancelling a booked leave day.
