# Booking leave across the year-end

## The split
Leave taken up to and including 31 December draws from the current year's balance. Leave taken from 1 January draws from the new year's grant, which is only available once that year's entitlement has posted in the system — not simply on the calendar date itself.

## The trap
**A single leave request spanning, say, 30 December to 2 January is silently split into two balances by the system, and the January portion can be rejected for insufficient balance even though the employee's new-year entitlement is perfectly adequate**, because the new grant has not posted yet at the moment the request is submitted.

## What to do
Book the December days and the January days as two separate requests, and submit the January one only once the new year's balance shows in the system. A request submitted too early against a balance that is still zero fails the same way as any other short-balance request.

Anyone planning a break over the turn of the year should check the new balance has posted before booking the January half.
