# Overtime, night and holiday rates

| Category | Premium |
|---|---|
| Ordinary overtime (beyond the 40-hour week) | +50% |
| Night hours (22:00–06:00) | +50% |
| Holiday work | +50% |
| Night hours worked during holiday work | +100% (both premiums stack) |

## Reading the table
Night and holiday premiums are paid on top of ordinary overtime whenever the hours also count as overtime. A night shift that is part of someone's normal rostered hours still earns the night premium even if it is not overtime at all.

**The stacked row is the one payroll gets asked about most — an employee working overnight on a public holiday is not paid two separate overtime premiums, but the night and holiday premiums do stack on top of each other.**

Which hours count as "night" or "holiday" for this purpose is defined on the next page.
