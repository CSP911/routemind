# Equipment loan agreement form

This form is completed and signed before any laptop or phone is handed to an employee. It is the only record that makes an item officially lent rather than simply issued.

## When it's used

At initial issue, at reissue after a repair or replacement, and at reissue following a return from leave of absence where the item was handed back in the meantime.

## What it records

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Device type and serial number | Recorded by IT at handover |
| Condition at issue | Noted by IT, checked again at return |
| Date issued | Start of the loan period |
| Employee signature | Confirms receipt and condition |
| Department authorisation | Line manager sign-off |

**A verbal handover, even one the line manager is aware of, does not count — if the form was never signed, the equipment loss and damage charges table cannot be applied against that employee.** IT will not issue a second device to replace an unrecorded one without a separate incident note.

The signed original stays with IT until the matching return entry is logged.
