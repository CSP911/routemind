# Workplace safety and health checks

Every employee attends a company-arranged health check once a year under the Occupational Safety and Health Act. This area covers four things: the check itself, what ends up on the attendance and HR record, the paid time off it carries, and what happens when an accident occurs at work rather than at a routine appointment.

## What this covers
- The annual check: who is required to attend and by when
- What is recorded: the result category that goes on file, not the medical detail
- Time off: the paid half-day, or full day for an off-site provider, granted to attend
- An accident at work: the attendance entry made immediately, separate from the industrial accident leave and reporting rules

**The health check record and an industrial accident report are not the same file.** A missed check is a compliance gap; an accident is a claim. Filing one in place of the other delays both, since each is reviewed by a different step in the process.

For the paid leave track that follows a confirmed workplace injury, see the industrial accident leave and reporting procedure. For how a routine sick day is coded instead, see the sick leave overview.
