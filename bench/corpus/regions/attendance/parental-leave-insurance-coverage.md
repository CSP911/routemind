# Insurance during parental leave

## Coverage while on leave

| Insurance | Status during parental leave |
|---|---|
| National health insurance | Continues; employer share still paid, employee share billed separately |
| National pension | Continues; employee may apply to defer their contribution |
| Employment insurance | Continues at no cost while on approved parental leave |
| Industrial accident insurance | Suspended — it only covers time actually worked |

## The trap

**Continued health insurance coverage does not mean continued salary.** Parental leave is unpaid unless a separate paid arrangement has been agreed, and the employee's insurance premium share is still due even though no salary is being paid to deduct it from — HR bills it directly.

## Reconciling on return

Any deferred pension contribution or billed premium is reconciled against the first payslip after return, alongside the confirmed return date and team assignment.

For how the return date itself gets fixed before payroll needs it, see mid-year-joiner-confirmation-deadline for the equivalent timing logic on new joiners.
