# Trip approval before the trip

This section covers how employees should navigate trip approvals well ahead of their travel dates. Drafting a comprehensive trip request is a crucial starting point, allowing the required time for review by the appropriate approvers. Notably, overseas trips require a different approver who handles international travel specifics, ensuring compliance with additional corporate guidelines.

A widespread error emerges when **trips are approved after the employee has already departed**, jeopardizing reimbursement and compliance with policy standards. Failure to obtain approval pre-departure can lead to compliance issues and missed reimbursements.

Each page delves into the personnel involved and exceptions that may require special handling. For further insights, look at the guidelines on temporary work assignments abroad.
