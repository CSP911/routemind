# Who resolves an attendance–claim mismatch

Two roles touch a mismatched trip record, and they don't share the same authority.

## Line manager

Confirms and, if needed, corrects the attendance record itself. Only the line manager can move a trip's status from requested to confirmed, and only the line manager can approve a correction to dates already logged.

## HR desk

Does not touch the attendance record. Its job is to check that the claim form matches the confirmed attendance record before releasing the allowance for payment. If the two still disagree, the HR desk sends the claim back rather than adjusting either side itself.

**The HR desk cannot approve a claim by amending the attendance dates to fit it — that authority sits with the line manager alone, even when the mismatch is small and the correction looks obvious.**

Where each system actually stores its version of the trip is worth knowing before either role gets involved.
