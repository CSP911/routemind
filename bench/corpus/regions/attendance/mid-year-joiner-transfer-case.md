# A mid-year joiner who transferred in

An employee transferred from a group affiliate on 1 September, bringing five years of documented, unbroken service with them. On paper this looks like a joiner who started partway through the year — the attendance system's join date field shows 1 September.

## Why the proration table does not apply here

The joining-month proration table is built for someone starting their service from zero. This employee is not: their preserved service date already places them past both the 1-year and 3-year marks, so their entitlement is already 15 days (plus any milestone bump already earned) for the whole current year, not a fraction of it based on four remaining months.

**Entering the transfer date as a plain join date lets the system auto-prorate the employee as a new hire, cutting their entitlement to a few months' worth of days they have already earned years ago.** HR must flag the record as a group transfer with a preserved service date before the proration wizard runs, not correct it afterward.

The evidence required to make that flag stick is the same checklist used for any other transfer case — see the entitlement-case evidence checklist.
