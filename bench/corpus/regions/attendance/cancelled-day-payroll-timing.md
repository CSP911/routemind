# Cancelling a booked day before the payroll cutoff

## The two clocks running at once
Cancelling a day of leave restores the balance in the attendance system immediately, but that restoration only reaches payroll if it happens before the monthly cutoff payroll uses to lock hours. A cancellation entered after the cutoff still frees the balance for future bookings, but the current month's payslip is processed as though the day were taken.

## Steps
1. Cancel the booking in the attendance system as soon as the plan changes.
2. Confirm the balance shows as restored on the same screen.
3. If the cancellation is entered on or after the payroll cutoff date, notify the HR desk directly — the correction carries to the following month, not the current one.

**A day cancelled after the cutoff is not an error in the attendance system — it is only out of step with that month's payslip, and it self-corrects the next month.** Cancelling it a second time to "fix" the mismatch will double the restored balance instead.

## Where this differs from a rejection
This procedure only applies to a day that was approved and later cancelled. A request that never had enough balance to be approved is rejected outright, not cancelled — see the short-balance cases for that outcome.
