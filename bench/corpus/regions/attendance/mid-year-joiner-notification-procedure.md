# Notifying a mid-year joiner of their leave entitlement

Once a prorated balance is confirmed, the joiner still needs to be told what it is and when it disappears — this is a separate step from the confirmation itself, and skipping it is how a joiner ends up discovering their days lapsed only after 1 January.

## The steps

1. HR confirms the prorated day count using the joining-month table.
2. HR sends the joiner written confirmation of the figure and the 31 December lapse date, within the same 14-day window used for entering the balance.
3. If the joiner's record involves a transfer-in or an upcoming leave of absence, HR notes the complication separately and routes it through the relevant case procedure instead of the standard notice.
4. The joiner acknowledges receipt; the acknowledgement is filed with the joining record.

**A confirmed balance that was never actually communicated to the joiner is still enforced on the 31 December lapse date** — the notice step is a courtesy for planning, not a condition of the rule taking effect. This is why the notice and the confirmation are tracked as two separate line items rather than assumed to happen together.

Where this handoff goes next — into the joiner's first actual leave booking — follows the same request and approval flow as any other employee's.
