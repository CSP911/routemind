# Equipment during leave of absence

What happens to a signed-out laptop or phone during study, military, family-care, parental or unpaid leave depends on how long the leave runs, not which category it falls under.

| Leave length | Handling |
|---|---|
| Under 30 days | Equipment stays with the employee, no action required |
| 30 to 90 days | Line manager decides whether it is returned to IT or retained |
| Over 90 days | Returned to IT for the duration, reissued against a fresh loan agreement form on return |

## Steps

1. The line manager flags the leave of absence to IT once it is agreed with the HR desk.
2. For leave over 90 days, IT arranges collection before the start date.
3. On return, IT reissues the same or a replacement device and a new agreement form is signed.

**Equipment retained during unpaid leave is still a company asset, not a personal item on hold — if it is lost or damaged while the employee is off payroll, the equipment loss and damage charges table still applies in full.**

This sits alongside, not instead of, the notice periods in the LOA start notice table.
