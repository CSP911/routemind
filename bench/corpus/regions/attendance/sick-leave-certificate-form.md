# The sick leave certificate form

## What it captures

The form records the diagnosis category (not the diagnosis itself), the first and last day of the absence it covers, the issuing doctor or hospital, and the employee's signature confirming the dates. It is the single document HR needs to move an absence out of the annual leave balance.

## Filing window

The form must reach the HR desk within 5 days of the employee returning to work, matching the window in the evidence table. HR does not accept a certificate submitted while the employee is still away — it has to be filed after return.

## What it doesn't do

**Submitting the form does not backdate the coding of days already recorded as annual leave.** Only the days the certificate is dated to cover, from day 3 of the absence onward, are moved to unpaid sick leave; the first two days stay deducted from annual leave regardless of when the form arrives.

An absence that starts on the job rather than off it doesn't use this form at all — see industrial accident and injury leave for the separate track.
