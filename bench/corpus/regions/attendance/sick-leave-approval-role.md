# Who approves a sick leave request

Sick leave has two separate approvals, and they happen at different times.

## Same-day approval by the line manager

A verbal or messaged notice to the line manager before the working day starts is enough to cover the absence itself — this is the same standard used for any same-morning leave request. The manager's approval means the day is recorded as taken. It does not decide whether the day comes out of the annual leave balance or is coded separately.

## Coding change by the HR desk

That decision waits on the evidence. Once a certificate is submitted, the HR desk reclassifies the day and, where the certificate qualifies, restores it against the annual leave balance instead of deducting it.

**A manager's approval only confirms the absence happened — it does not by itself keep the day out of the annual leave balance.** Until a certificate reaches the HR desk, the system defaults to deducting the day as ordinary leave. An employee who assumes verbal approval settled the coding, and never files the certificate, will find the day still sitting against their annual leave balance at year-end with no easy way to reverse it.

This is why the certificate form exists as a separate step from the initial notice, not a formality that can be skipped once the manager has said yes.
