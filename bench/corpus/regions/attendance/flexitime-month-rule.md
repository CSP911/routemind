# The flexitime settlement month

Flexitime is settled once per calendar month, not per week. The system totals every day's hours against the 8-hour standard and carries the difference to a running balance.

| Balance at month close | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Surplus, up to 4 hours | Carried forward to next month |
| Surplus beyond 4 hours | Lost — not banked, not paid |
| Shortfall | Must be cleared within the following month |
| Shortfall still open after that | Deducted as leave, per the half/quarter-day rule |

**The carry-forward cap is 4 hours, not the full surplus.** An employee who finishes a month 9 hours ahead only keeps 4 of them into the next month — the other 5 do not appear anywhere and cannot be claimed later. A shortfall is treated more strictly: it does not lapse quietly, it eventually becomes a real deduction from leave balance if it is never worked off.
