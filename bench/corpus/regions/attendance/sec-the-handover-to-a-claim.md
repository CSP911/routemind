# The handover to a claim

Transferring verified attendance records to initiate a claim is the key focus of this section. The process relies on accurate confirmation of attendance dates before any claim can be officially filed. In practice, this involves synchronizing information from attendance systems with the claims system. A common workflow is first ensuring attendance is validated, then moving to claim filing.

**One frequent challenge: a claim is filed before attendance confirmation**, leading to discrepancies between the two systems. This can result in delays and the need for manual correction. The primary hazard here is when inconsistencies arise between attendance and claims data, as the two systems must reconcile perfectly to avoid complications.
