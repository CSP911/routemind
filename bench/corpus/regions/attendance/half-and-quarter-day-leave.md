# Half-day and quarter-day leave

## Booking units
Leave can be booked in units smaller than a full day: a half day (0.5) covering either the morning or the afternoon, and a quarter day (0.25) covering a two-hour block within one of those halves. Both are entered through the same leave menu as a full day, with the unit selected at booking time.

## Deduction from balance
| Unit | Deducted from balance |
|---|---|
| Full day | 1.0 day |
| Half day (AM or PM) | 0.5 day |
| Quarter day | 0.25 day |

**Booking a morning half day and an afternoon half day on the same calendar date is recorded as one full day taken, not as two separate 0.5-day entries** — the system merges them once both halves of the same date are booked, and the balance moves accordingly.

Quarter-day leave still needs the same manager sign-off as a full day; nothing about the smaller unit shortens the approval step.

Notice periods for these smaller bookings follow the same expectations as any other leave request.
