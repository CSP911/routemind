# Where flexible and remote work is recorded

Two menus hold flexible and remote work data, and they are not interchangeable with the leave or overtime menus.

| Menu | Holds |
|---|---|
| Flexitime | Daily clock-in/out times and the running monthly balance |
| Remote work | Individual WFH day entries and their approval status |
| Leave | Booked leave days — not used for remote days |
| Overtime | Hours beyond the standard day — not used for flex balance |

The Flexitime menu is where the monthly surplus or shortfall actually lives; the Remote work menu only marks where a day was worked, not how many hours.

**Logging a remote day under the Leave menu instead of Remote work is the mistake that miscounts attendance** — it silently consumes a leave day for what should have been an ordinary working day with no balance impact at all. The menus for overtime and trip hours sit alongside these and are covered in their own pages.
