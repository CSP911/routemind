# Accrual and lapse at the first 1 January

| Joining date | Accrued by 31 Dec | On 1 January |
|---|---|---|
| Before 1 July | Prorated days per the joining-month table | Unused days lapse; new entitlement begins |
| 1 July or later | Prorated days per the joining-month table | Same — no carryover |
| Any joining date, days used before 31 Dec | Deducted from the accrued total as taken | Only the remainder is subject to lapse |

The mechanism is the same use-it-or-lose-it rule that applies to any other year-end balance: nothing about being a first-year joiner exempts the days from lapsing.

**Days accrued in November or December still lapse on 31 December if unused, even though the employee had almost no time to take them.** This is the complaint HR hears most often from joiners who started in the second half of the year — the fix is to flag it during onboarding, not to argue for an exception afterward, because there isn't one.

From 1 January, entitlement is recalculated based on actual service completed, which is where a leave of absence spanning the new year changes the outcome — see the worked case.
