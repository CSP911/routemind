# When the payout reaches a payslip

Confirming the payout amount and getting it onto a payslip are two different steps, and the payroll close date sits between them.

## Timing bands

| When the balance is confirmed | Payslip it reaches |
|---|---|
| Before that month's payroll close | The final payslip, same month |
| After that month's payroll close | The following month, as an off-cycle payment |

## The trap

**A balance confirmed the day before someone's last working day can still miss the payroll close if that close falls earlier in the month than the last working day itself.** Payroll close dates do not move to match a resignation date — an employee leaving on the 28th of a month whose payroll closes on the 20th will see the payout the following month regardless of how early the balance was confirmed relative to their own departure.

Getting the confirmation itself filed in time is a separate deadline, covered elsewhere in this section.
