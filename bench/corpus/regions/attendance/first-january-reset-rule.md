# How entitlement resets on the first 1 January

A mid-year joiner's leave year does not start on their join date — it runs on the same calendar year as everyone else's. The join date only determines how many days they accrue before the first 31 December closes that partial year out.

## What resets and what doesn't

On 1 January following the join date, the prorated first-year balance is replaced by a fresh calculation:

| Service completed by 1 January | Entitlement from 1 January |
|---|---|
| Under 1 year | Continues on the monthly proration basis until the anniversary |
| 1 year and over | 15 days |

Nothing from the first partial year rolls forward as a head start — the new balance is calculated on its own terms from the service length reached, not added to whatever was left unused.

**Treating the reset as "last year's leftover plus this year's grant" produces a balance that is too high and gets flagged in the next audit.** The reset is a replacement, not an addition.

Where this gets more complicated is a joiner whose first year included a leave of absence — the months on leave do not count toward the service length that this table reads from, which is the subject of the next case.
