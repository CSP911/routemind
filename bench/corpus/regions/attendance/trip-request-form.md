# Trip request form

The trip request form is filed by the employee before departure and routed to the line manager for approval. It captures the destination, purpose, departure and return dates in Korean calendar dates, and whether any portion of the trip falls on a weekend or holiday.

## When it has to be filed

| Trip type | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| Domestic trip | At least 3 business days before departure |
| Overseas trip | At least 5 business days before departure |

Approval through this form is what makes the trip's days appear as trip days in the attendance system rather than as ordinary working days or, if nothing is filed at all, as an unrecorded absence.

**The trap is treating an email or a verbal "go ahead" from a manager as sufficient — only an approved entry through this form reaches the attendance system.** A trip discussed and agreed informally, with the form filed only after return, is a late filing and is recorded as one.

A trip filed and completed exactly as requested moves next to the expense claim, not back through this form.
