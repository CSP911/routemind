# Missing the annual check deadline

An employee has not booked or attended the annual health check by 30 November. No appointment is on file, and no result category exists in the attendance system.

What happens:

1. The HR desk flags the record as incomplete on 1 December.
2. The line manager is notified and asked to release the employee for an appointment within 10 working days.
3. If the check is still not attended by 31 December, the gap is logged as a compliance exception on the employee's file — it is not treated as if the check happened, and it does not carry forward as extra entitlement the following year.

| Outcome | Applies |
|---|---|
| Attended within 10 working days of the flag | Recorded against the year it was due, no exception logged |
| Not attended by 31 December | Compliance exception logged, requirement restarts for the new year |

**A missed check is not quietly absorbed into next year's cycle.** The following year's check is still due by its own 30 November deadline; it does not extend to cover the gap, and the exception stays on file regardless.

The time off used for the eventual appointment follows the same half or full day rule as any other year.
