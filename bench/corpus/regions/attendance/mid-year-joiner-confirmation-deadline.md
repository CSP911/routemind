# When a mid-year joiner's prorated balance must be confirmed

A new joiner's prorated leave balance must be confirmed and entered into the attendance system **within 14 days of the join date**. This is not a grace period for taking leave — it is the point by which the figure has to exist in the system at all, since a request made against an unconfirmed balance has nothing to check against.

## Two deadlines, not one

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Prorated balance confirmed and entered | Within 14 days of join date |
| Year-end lapse review for unused first-year days | By 1 December |

The second deadline exists because unused days accrued in the first partial year lapse on 31 December regardless of how late in the year they were earned. HR runs a reminder pass by 1 December so a joiner who started in October is not surprised on 1 January.

**Missing the 14-day confirmation window does not extend the joiner's deadline for using the days it produces** — the lapse date stays 31 December either way, so a late confirmation just shortens the usable window further. This is the same lapse boundary that any December leave request runs into under the year-end booking rules.
