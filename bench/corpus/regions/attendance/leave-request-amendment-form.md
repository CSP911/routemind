# Leave amendment request

Once a leave day is booked, cancelling it, shortening it, or converting it to a half-day is done through the amendment form, not a fresh leave-apply request.

## Fields required

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Original leave date(s) | Identifies the booking being changed |
| Amendment type | Cancel, shorten, or convert to half/quarter-day |
| Reason | Free text, required for HR desk review |
| Approver | Same line manager who approved the original booking |

## Why it exists as a separate form

The amendment form updates the existing attendance system entry in place. A fresh leave-apply request for the same dates does not know about the original booking.

**Submitting a new leave-apply request instead of the amendment form creates a second, duplicate entry — the original booking is not automatically cancelled or overwritten, and both may show as deducted until the HR desk manually clears one.**

Which deadline applies to the amendment depends on the situation being amended — see the cancellation deadline table for the specifics.
