# Military service leave and reinstatement

## Why it cannot be declined
Military service leave is granted under statute, not company discretion. Once an employee presents a call-up or enlistment order, the leave has to be approved — there is no business-need test and no headcount exception. The leave lasts as long as the service obligation itself, unpaid, and the service date keeps running exactly as with any other leave of absence: months away are added to service in full.

## Reinstatement on discharge
An employee returning from military service leave must be reinstated to the same position or one of equivalent status and pay. This is a duty on the company, not a request the employee has to justify. The employee should notify the HR desk of the discharge date as soon as it is confirmed, and confirm the return date within 7 days of discharge.

**Expecting the standard 30-day advance notice used for other leave of absence categories is the mistake here — a call-up notice is often measured in days, not weeks, and the leave must still be granted on that timeline.** Reinstatement notice runs the other way: short, and counted from discharge, not from a planned end date.

Family care leave, covered next, runs on a very different notice pattern.
