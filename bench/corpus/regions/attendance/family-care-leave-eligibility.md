# Family care leave eligibility

## Who it covers
Family care leave is available to care for a spouse, parent, parent-in-law, or child who needs care due to illness, accident, or old age. It is not available for routine caregiving that does not involve a specific care need — a school closure or a minor illness does not qualify on its own.

## Evidence and duration
The HR desk requires a medical certificate or equivalent documentation confirming the need for care before the leave is granted. Leave runs up to 90 days per instance, and can be taken as a single block or split into shorter blocks within that cap.

## Repeat requests
A new instance of family care leave for the same family member and the same underlying condition does not reset the 90-day cap. Reaching it closes that instance; a further absence for the same reason has to be requested as unpaid personal leave instead.

**Assuming the 90-day cap resets whenever the leave is interrupted and resumed is the mistake — the cap tracks the instance, not the block, and HR checks prior usage against the same family member and condition before approving another request.**

Unpaid personal leave, covered on its own form, picks up where this cap leaves off.
