# Trip approval exception form

This is the only form that can bring a departed trip into the attendance system without a prior approval. It is separate from the ordinary trip request form, which only works before departure.

## When to use it
Use it whenever a trip's request was still a draft, still pending, or missing entirely at the moment of departure — regardless of whether the delay was the traveller's fault or an approver's.

## What it asks for
- The original or reconstructed trip request (dates, destination, purpose)
- The reason approval did not clear before departure
- Line manager sign-off for a domestic trip; line manager and HR desk sign-off for an overseas one

## The trap
**The 3-calendar-day filing window is counted from departure, not from the day the traveller notices the request never went through.** Someone who realises on day 5 of a trip that nothing was ever approved has already missed the window — the form is still submitted, but it now goes through the escalation review reserved for late filings rather than the standard exception route.

A trip approved this way still needs its own allowance claim filed on the ordinary schedule.
