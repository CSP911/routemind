# What has to match between the two records

Before a claim clears, four fields are checked against the confirmed attendance record. Where they disagree, the attendance system wins.

| Field | Authoritative source |
|---|---|
| Trip dates | Attendance record |
| Domestic or overseas | Attendance record |
| Traveller's confirmed status | Attendance record |
| Number of allowance days claimed | Claim form, capped by the attendance date range |

## Why the last row is different

The claim form is allowed to claim fewer allowance days than the attendance record shows — a traveller might skip claiming a day they spent on personal leave attached to the trip. It is never allowed to claim more.

**A claim that lists more allowance days than the confirmed attendance record spans is rejected on that field alone, even if every individual date on the claim matches an actual day of the trip.**
