# HR desk

| | |
|---|---|
| Owns | Leave entitlement, approving leave of absence, attendance exceptions, employment certificates |
| Reach | Groupware message, or ext. 2140 (People team) |
| Will decide for you | Attendance situations the rules do not cover — a late leave request, leaving early during a half day, entitlement during a leave of absence |
| Will not decide | Pay amounts and insurance settlement (Payroll desk), travel amounts (Office & purchasing desk) |

Settle an attendance exception **with the HR desk before putting it in the system**. Filing it first
and having it rejected leaves that period recorded as absence.
