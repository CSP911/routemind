# Recording a day working from home

1. Submit a "Remote work" entry in the attendance system by 09:00 on the day itself, or the evening before for a planned day.
2. The line manager approves the entry before it is treated as confirmed.
3. Work the day meeting core hours (10:00–16:00) exactly as if in the office.
4. The system logs the day's hours against the same flexitime total as any office day.

A remote day is not a leave type and does not touch the leave balance. It is a location flag on an otherwise ordinary working day.

**An unlogged remote day is not automatically recognised, even if the hours were genuinely worked** — with no entry, the system shows an unexplained absence for that day, and the burden is on the employee to backfill the entry and get it approved after the fact, which is not guaranteed. Where the entry actually sits in the system, alongside the flexitime menu, is covered next.
