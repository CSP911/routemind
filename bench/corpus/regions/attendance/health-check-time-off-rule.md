# Paid time off for the annual check

Attending the annual health check is paid time, not a deduction from annual leave. A same-day appointment with the company-designated provider carries a half-day of paid time off; an appointment requiring travel to an off-site provider, or same-day follow-up testing, carries a full day.

| Appointment type | Paid time granted |
|---|---|
| On-site or nearby, same-day result | Half day |
| Off-site provider, or same-day follow-up required | Full day |

The half or full day is entered in the attendance system as "health check," a category separate from annual leave, sick leave and overtime. It does not draw down the annual leave balance and does not use the half/quarter-day conversion table applied to leave bookings.

**Booking the check for the afternoon and then requesting the morning off separately as annual leave is not how this works.** The health check entry already covers the appointment window; a leave request submitted alongside it for the same half-day is redundant and will be queried by the HR desk.

Employees who miss their scheduled window without rebooking are covered in the no-show case that follows.
