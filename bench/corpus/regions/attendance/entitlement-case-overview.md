# Why these five events get their own case

## The five
- A transfer from a group company
- A rehire after a gap in service
- Service that continues across a leave of absence
- The year the 3/5/7-year increase lands
- A departure and its final balance

## Why they're handled separately
Ordinary annual leave runs on one clock: join date, then the accrual table. These five events are the ones where that clock either pauses, resets, jumps, or has to be closed out — so each one is checked individually rather than left to the standard formula.

**None of the five works the same way as the others.** Milestone and leave-of-absence continuity run off the service-start date without anyone filing anything, while transfer, rehire and final balance all depend on a document reaching the HR desk before the leave year closes around them. Treating all five as "the system will sort it out" is the single most common reason a case gets missed.

For what each one actually does to the service date or accrual, one table lays them out side by side rather than leaving it to memory.
