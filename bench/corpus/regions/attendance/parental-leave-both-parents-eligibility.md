# Parental leave for both parents

## Eligibility test applies per parent

Parental leave is granted per employee, not per household. Each parent who works here is assessed independently against the standard test — a child aged 8 or under (or in the second year of primary school or below), and at least 6 months of service.

## One parent's leave does not reduce the other's

**Both parents can each take a full parental leave period for the same child — one parent's leave does not use up or shorten the other's entitlement.** There is no rule requiring the household to choose only one parent, and no requirement that the two leaves be staggered rather than run at the same time.

## Requesting it

Each parent files a separate case. Dates for each are agreed with the HR desk at least 30 days before that parent's leave starts, exactly as for a single-parent request.

For the order in which a household chooses to apply when only one parent intends to take leave, see childcare-apply.
