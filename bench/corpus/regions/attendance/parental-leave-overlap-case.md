# Both parents on leave at the same time

## The case

Both parents of a five-year-old work at the company. Each applies separately for a four-month parental leave block, and both request overlapping dates — 1 April to 31 July — for the same child.

## The outcome

Both requests are approved independently. **There is no rule requiring the two parents to stagger their leave, and no requirement that a "primary caregiver" be designated** — each parent's eligibility is assessed only against their own service record and the child's age, not against what the other parent is doing.

## What HR still checks

Each case is logged, evidenced and approved on its own file, exactly as if the two employees had no connection to each other; the only shared fact between the two cases is the child's date of birth, used to confirm the eligibility window for both.

For the underlying eligibility test both cases are checked against, see parental-leave-both-parents-eligibility.
