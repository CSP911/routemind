# Attendance & Leave

How much leave you get, how to take it, what a business trip does to your attendance, and what happens
when you go on parental leave. Everything here ends at **how many days count** — what those days are
worth in money is Payroll, and what a trip pays is Expenses.

## The rule the whole area rests on
**A record that is not in the system does not exist.** Not for attendance, not for the pay run, not
for an audit a year later. A team lead saying "take Friday off" is an agreement; it becomes leave when
it is in the groupware and approved. The same is true in reverse — a trip taken without an approved
trip request leaves that day blank, and blank records as absence.

## Two deadlines worth memorising
- **The 3rd of the following month.** The attendance month closes then. After that a correction is no
  longer yours to make — it goes through the HR desk.
- **31 December.** Unused leave lapses. It does not carry into the new year; the lapsed balance is
  paid out instead, which is worth less to most people than the day off would have been.

If your situation is not in these pages, it is probably an exception the HR desk decides — and it is
much cheaper to ask before you enter it than to have it rejected afterwards.
