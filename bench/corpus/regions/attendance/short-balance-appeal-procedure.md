# What happens after a short-balance rejection

## The outcome is final for that request
A request rejected for insufficient balance is not held, queued or partially approved. **There is no appeal that restores the missing balance — the only ways forward are to shorten the request to fit what is available, or wait until the next accrual date adds days.**

## Steps to resubmit
1. Check the current balance in the attendance system before resubmitting anything.
2. Submit a new request for fewer days, or a smaller unit, that the balance actually covers.
3. If the shortfall is only a quarter or half-day, confirm the unit matches what remains — a half-day balance cannot cover a full day even when the dates themselves are free.

## What does not help
Resubmitting the same multi-day request unchanged will fail again for the same reason: the system checks the balance fresh each time, it does not remember or negotiate around a prior attempt.

## Where the check actually happens
The balance is checked at approval, not at submission — see leave balance check timing for the point in the flow where a short balance actually blocks a request.
