# Equipment lending

Equipment lending pertains to the process and rules for borrowing company-owned assets, such as laptops and phones, which are necessary for employees to perform their duties. This area also covers responsibilities and procedures for equipment return, particularly at the end of employment or during a leave of absence.

**A frequent oversight is failing to properly document lending agreements**, which can lead to misunderstandings or loss of equipment. Always ensure that proper records are kept when equipment is issued, and clear instructions are communicated about return obligations and deadlines.

Follow company protocols for lending, usage, and return to safeguard company assets and ensure smooth operations, especially when transitions occur.
