# Carrying flex hours into the next month

Flexitime lets you work outside the core hours band and bank the difference, but the bank does not carry forward without limit. Each month closes on the same date the flexitime-month-rule uses to total hours, and only part of any surplus survives past that point.

## What carries and what doesn't

| Surplus at month end | Carried forward | Forfeited |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 5 hours | Full amount | None |
| 5.01–10 hours | 5 hours | Remainder |
| Over 10 hours | 5 hours | Remainder |

A shortfall is handled separately — see the flexitime-month-rule and the shortfall case for what happens when banked hours don't cover the month.

## Applying the cap

The cap resets every calendar month regardless of how the surplus built up, whether from early starts, late finishes, or one long day. There is no year-end reconciliation that restores what was forfeited earlier in the year.

**Only 5 hours of surplus carry into the next month — the rest is forfeited at the close, not banked for a quieter week later.** Employees who put in a heavy week expecting to draw it down afterward often find the balance already capped by the time they check it.

Core hours still apply on every working day regardless of how much flex is banked or forfeited.
