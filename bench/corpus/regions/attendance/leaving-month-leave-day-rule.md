# A leave day in your leaving month

Leave booked in the calendar month someone leaves is deducted from the balance exactly as it would be at any other time. What changes is what that day does — or does not do — to the employment record.

## What stays fixed

The official last day of employment is whatever date HR has on file for the resignation or transfer, agreed before the leave is booked. Booking annual leave for the final days of employment does not push that date back, and it does not need to — the leave is simply taken and deducted before the employment ends.

## The trap

**Employees sometimes assume that using up remaining leave extends their employment by that many days. It does not.** The last working day and the last day of employment are the same date on file regardless of how many leave days fall inside the notice period. Any balance left over once that date arrives is paid out, not carried forward as extra employment time.
