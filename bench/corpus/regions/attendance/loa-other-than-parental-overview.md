# Leave of absence outside parental leave

## The four categories
Leave of absence outside the parental leave rules falls into four kinds: study leave, military service leave, family care leave, and unpaid personal leave. Each has its own approval track, its own maximum duration, and its own evidence requirement, but all four are judged by the same standard when it comes to service and entitlement.

## The shared mechanic
None of the four change the service date. Twelve months away adds twelve months to service exactly as if the employee had stayed at their desk, so a milestone anniversary landing during any of these leaves still arrives on schedule. What does change is the entitlement side: the leave days earned for the year the employee was away are prorated down to actual time worked, not the full twelve months.

## Where each is detailed
Military leave carries a statutory reinstatement duty; family care leave has its own eligibility test and per-instance cap; study leave runs on a fixed one-year ceiling; unpaid personal leave is requested on its own form. Return notice differs by category too.

**Confusing "service is unaffected" with "entitlement is unaffected" is the mistake that causes disputes — the milestone bump still lands on time, but that year's leave grant will be smaller than a full year's.**

How that plays out when a milestone actually falls mid-leave is worked through separately.
