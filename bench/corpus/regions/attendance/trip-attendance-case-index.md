# Trip attendance cases

Most business trips are unremarkable: dates match the request, the trip finishes inside one payroll month, and the days are entered exactly as approved. The cases below are the ones where that assumption breaks.

## The five situations

| Situation | What makes it different |
|---|---|
| Weekend trip | Travel or duty falls on a non-working day |
| Overseas trip crossing dates | The calendar date abroad and the Korean attendance date disagree |
| Cancelled after approval | The trip is called off but the record isn't automatically cleared |
| Taken without a request | No approved booking exists before departure |
| Spanning month close | The trip starts in one payroll month and ends in the next |

**None of these are edge cases you can leave to the attendance system to sort out on its own — each one needs someone to actually touch the record.** The system reflects what is entered; it does not infer intent from an itinerary.

For what a trip day's hours actually count as once the record is correct, see the general trip attendance rule.
