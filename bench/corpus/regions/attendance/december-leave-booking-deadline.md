# Deadline for booking leave in the last week of December

## The date
Requests for any day between 25 and 31 December must be submitted at least 30 days in advance — by 25 November — the same lead time as a parental leave request, and for a similar reason: HR needs to see the whole team's December requests together before approving any of them individually.

## Why the date is fixed rather than "as early as possible"
| Submitted by | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 25 November or earlier | Considered in the priority round |
| After 25 November | Only approved if capacity remains once the priority round is settled |

**A request filed on 26 November is not rejected by the attendance system — it will still accept it — but it has missed the round where competing requests are actually compared**, and a colleague who filed earlier can still take the day even if their request arrived after this one.

## After the deadline
Once the priority round closes, any remaining December capacity is allocated in submission order. See the December booking priority rule for how competing requests for the same week are resolved once that round is underway.
