# Bonuses and incentives

Bonuses and incentives are crucial motivational tools within the payroll structure but come with their own sets of rules and timelines. They may be regular, discretionary, or performance-based payments. Understanding the taxation of these earnings is essential, as they could push an individual into a higher tax bracket. Timing also varies, requiring precise coordination with an employee's leave or exit month for correct handling of pro-rata payments. **The common misconception is that bonuses are automatically tax-free before the year-end settlement.** On exit, signing bonuses with clawback conditions need careful consideration about repayment obligations if conditions are not met.
