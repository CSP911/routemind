# What builds the annual pay figure

The number discussed at hire or promotion — "annual salary" — is not a single payslip line. It is built from three layers.

## Components

| Component | Guaranteed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | Yes | Fixed monthly amount, paid every month regardless of performance |
| Fixed allowances | Yes | Meal, own-car and similar capped lines, where the employee qualifies |
| Target bonus | No | Included in the quoted figure at target, paid only if the relevant bonus's conditions are met |

## Why it matters

**The annual figure divided by 12 is not monthly gross pay.** A large share of it can be target bonus, which may pay at less than target, more than target, or not at all depending on the bonus type. Basic pay and fixed allowances are the only pieces certain to show up on every payslip.

This is also why two employees quoted the same annual figure can see very different monthly totals — one carries a larger guaranteed share than the other.

See bonus-types-overview for how each bonus component is actually calculated and paid.
