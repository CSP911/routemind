# When an over-claimed deduction surfaces late

## How it's found
Sometimes a dependant later turns out to have exceeded the income test for the year, or a receipt used for a deduction turns out to be duplicated elsewhere. It can come up in a later year's filing, an NTS audit, or the employee's own review.

## What happens
The refund already paid on that deduction is reclaimed. If the employee reports it before the NTS does, it's usually collected as a straightforward adjustment on a coming payslip. If the NTS finds it first, penalty interest is added on top of the repayment.

## The trap
**Waiting to be caught costs more than reporting it.** Self-correcting an over-claim recovers only the amount overpaid; letting the NTS find it during a review adds interest on top, and can take longer to resolve since payroll is then acting on the NTS's figures instead of its own.

How any of these repayments actually reach the payslip is worth setting out on its own.
