# Filing after both settlement windows have closed

Payroll's internal cutoff and the NTS's post-settlement amendment window each give a chance to fix a missed claim. Once both have passed, neither payroll nor an amendment form can help.

## When this applies

The employee missed the internal cutoff for that year's run, and the window for filing an amended claim with the NTS afterward has also closed.

## Steps

1. Gather the same evidence that would have gone into the original claim — receipts, dependant documents, deduction records.
2. File a comprehensive income tax return (종합소득세 확정신고) directly with the NTS between 1 and 31 May of the following year.
3. Pay any additional tax owed, or receive any refund, directly from the NTS.

**Payroll cannot process anything at this stage — it does not touch the payslip, and any refund bypasses payroll entirely.** People often bring late paperwork to the payroll desk expecting a fix; past this point, the desk can only point them to the May filing.

The internal cutoff that starts this clock is set out separately.
