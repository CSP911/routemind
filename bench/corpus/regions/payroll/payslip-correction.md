# Correcting a payslip error

## The rule: no reissue

A payslip already paid is not reversed or reissued once it's out. Any correction — an overpayment to claw back, or an underpayment to top up — is carried forward as an adjustment line on the following month's payslip.

## What the adjustment line looks like

| Direction | Line label | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Underpaid last month | Prior-month adjustment (addition) | Added to next month's gross pay |
| Overpaid last month | Prior-month adjustment (deduction) | Subtracted from next month's gross pay |

## The trap

**An underpayment correction sitting on next month's payslip looks identical to a bonus if the label is missed**, and gets asked about as one. Check the line label before assuming a jump in gross pay is extra pay rather than a fix for last month's shortfall — the payroll desk logs these separately from bonus payments precisely because they're mistaken for each other.

The same lag between earned and paid shows up with retroactive rises, covered separately.
