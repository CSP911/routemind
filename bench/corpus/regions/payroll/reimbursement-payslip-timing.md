# When a reimbursement claim reaches the payslip

| Submitted | Lands on |
|---|---|
| Before the monthly cut-off | That month's payslip, as a nontaxable payment line |
| After the monthly cut-off | The following month's payslip |

## No claim, no line

A standing allowance appears on the payslip automatically every month once it's set up. **A reimbursement does not — if nothing is submitted, nothing is paid, and a late claim is not backdated onto the payslip of the month the cost was actually incurred.** It simply appears the month it clears the cut-off, evidence and sign-off complete.

This is the same cut-off logic that governs a retroactive pay correction landing on the next payslip rather than reopening the original one.
