# A garnishment order and a resignation

An employee with an active garnishment line resigns before the underlying debt is cleared. The order does not close with the employment — it follows the money.

## What carries over

The same protected-portion logic applies to severance pay, but it is recalculated against the severance amount itself, since severance is paid as a single lump within 14 days of the last day, not across monthly payslips. The final regular payslip and the severance payment are treated as two separate wage events, each tested against the bands on its own terms.

**An active garnishment order does not lapse when the underlying employment does.** Payroll carries it into final pay and into the separate severance settlement, recalculating the deductible amount for each lump rather than applying one running total.

The order stays open until the court or creditor confirms the debt is satisfied — resignation alone never closes it.
