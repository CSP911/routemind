# Choosing between an allowance and a reimbursement

## One mechanism per standing category

Meals, own-car use, childcare and research are set up once as standing allowances and stay that way. An employee cannot ask payroll to reimburse a month's actual fuel receipts on top of the own-car allowance, or instead of it, without first stopping the standing line — the two do not run in parallel for the same category.

## Travel is decided trip by trip

Travel works differently. For a single trip, the traveller chooses either the flat daily allowance (20,000 KRW domestic, USD 50–80 overseas, treated as a reasonable stand-in for actual cost) or reimbursement of the real, receipted spend for that trip — not both.

**Taking the flat rate for a trip and then also submitting receipts for the same trip's meals or transport pays the trip twice.** One of the two claims has to be withdrawn before payroll processes either.
