# How a bonus is taxed

## The annualized method
A bonus is not taxed at a flat rate. Payroll annualizes it: the bonus is added to regular pay already paid this year, the tax due on that combined figure is read off the same simplified tax table used for monthly pay, and tax already withheld on regular pay is subtracted. What's left is withheld from the bonus itself.

## Steps payroll follows
1. Take year-to-date regular pay and tax already withheld.
2. Add the bonus and annualize the result.
3. Read the tax due on the annualized figure from the simplified table.
4. Subtract tax already withheld year-to-date.
5. Withhold the remainder from the bonus.

## Insurance
Employment, pension and health deductions apply to a bonus at the same rates as regular pay — 4.5% pension, 3.545% health, 0.9% employment — but see the separate note on which base actually moves.

**The withholding rate on a bonus is not a fixed percentage set by policy.** It depends on how much regular pay has already been taxed this year, so an identical bonus can withhold different tax for two employees on different basic salaries.

This is a withholding estimate, not the final liability — how it settles is covered next.
