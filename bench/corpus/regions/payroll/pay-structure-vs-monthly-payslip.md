# From the annual figure to the monthly payslip

The annual figure agreed at hire or promotion is a planning number. The payslip each month only ever shows a slice of it.

## What actually lands monthly

- Basic pay — the annual basic pay figure divided evenly across 12 months.
- Fixed nontaxable allowances — paid at their flat monthly amount, where the qualifying condition is met that month.
- Bonus — paid only on its own calendar, in whatever amount its type and performance result produce.

## The trap

**Gross pay on any one payslip is not the annual figure divided by 12.** A month with no bonus payment looks far smaller than a month that includes one, even though both belong to the same annual figure. Comparing a single payslip's gross pay against the annual number quoted at hire will almost always look like a shortfall — it isn't one, it's just a month without the bonus component.

For what actually makes up basic pay itself, see payslip-basic-pay.
