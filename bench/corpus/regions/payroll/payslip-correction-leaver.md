# Correcting a payslip after the person has left

The standard fix for an over- or under-payment is to carry the adjustment into the following month's payslip rather than reissue the original. That relies on there being a following month.

**The standard fix does not apply once someone has left — payroll cannot carry an adjustment onto a payslip that will never be issued.** The final payslip closed the employment; there is no next line for a correction to sit on.

## What happens instead

- Payroll recalculates the correct figures for the period in question.
- An underpayment is settled as a direct transfer for the difference, not a new payslip.
- An overpayment is recovered by request, usually a repayment into the same account the salary was paid from.
- Neither of these touches severance pay, which is already handled outside the payslip on its own schedule, separately from ordinary pay.

## Why this comes up

Errors on a leaver's figures tend to surface late — a missed allowance, an overtime hour logged after the leave date, an insurance base recalculated during annual settlement. By the time it's found, the payslip cycle that would normally absorb it is closed, so the fix has to move outside the payslip.

Who signs off on a leaver's revised figures before payroll transfers the difference is set out under payslip-change-signoff.
