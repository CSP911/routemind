# Overtime, night and holiday premiums

## The premium rates

| Type | Premium | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Overtime (beyond the standard week) | +50% | Hourly basic pay rate |
| Night work (10pm–6am) | +50% | Hourly basic pay rate |
| Holiday work, first 8 hours | +50% | Hourly basic pay rate |
| Holiday work, beyond 8 hours | +100% | Hourly basic pay rate |

## Premiums stack

**These premiums add on top of each other rather than replacing one another.** An hour of overtime worked at night on a holiday carries all three premiums at once, not whichever single premium is highest. The payslip line for that hour will show a combined multiplier, which looks wrong against any of the individual rates above read in isolation but is correct when they're read together.

The basic pay these premiums are calculated against is the same figure shown on the payment lines.
