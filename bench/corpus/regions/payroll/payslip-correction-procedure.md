# Carrying a correction forward

## When this applies
Used when an over- or under-payment is found after a payslip has already gone out, and reissuing it isn't practical.

## Steps
1. Payroll confirms the amount and the month it belongs to.
2. The difference is entered as its own line on the next payslip — a prior-month adjustment — not folded into the current month's basic pay or allowance lines.
3. An underpayment is added as extra pay; an overpayment is deducted; both before tax.
4. Insurance and tax lines recalculate on the corrected total for that next month, not for the month the error happened in.

**The adjustment line changes the month it appears in, not the month it belongs to.** A March underpayment fixed in April's payslip counts as April income for tax purposes, even though the pay itself relates to March's work.

## What payroll needs from you
Flag the discrepancy before the next payroll run closes. Once that month's insurance and tax have been filed, the fix moves to the month after.

The reasoning behind why this doesn't reissue the original payslip is covered separately.
