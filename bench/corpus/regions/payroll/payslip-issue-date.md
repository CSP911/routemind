# Payslip issue date and error window

## When it's issued
Payslips are issued on the same pay date each month, alongside the pay itself.

## Reporting window
| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Flag an error for same-month correction | Before that month's payroll run closes |
| Miss the window | Fix carries to next month's payslip as an adjustment line |

**A missed window doesn't mean the error goes uncorrected — it means the fix arrives a month later than expected.** Employees who assume a missed deadline forfeits the correction end up not raising it at all, which is the actual mistake.

## What to send when flagging
The month affected, the line affected, and the amount you believe is wrong — payroll cannot open a prior month's figures on a general request alone.

How that carried-forward fix is calculated is covered separately.
