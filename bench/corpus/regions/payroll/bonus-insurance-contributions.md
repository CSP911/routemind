# Bonus and the insurance contribution base

## Pension
National pension is calculated on the standard monthly income fixed each July, not on pay actually received that month. A bonus does not raise the pension deduction in the month it's paid, and it does not feed into the July reset either — it sits outside the standard monthly income figure entirely.

## Health insurance
Health insurance is also charged monthly on standard income, but the April settlement reconciles the whole prior year's actual income against what was charged — and a bonus is part of that actual income.

| Line | Affected in bonus month? | Affected at annual settlement? |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | No | No |
| Health insurance | No | Yes — included in April settlement |
| Long-term care | No | Follows health line |
| Employment insurance | Yes — charged on the bonus itself | N/A |

**A bonus paid in November does not show up as a bigger pension or health deduction that month.** It surfaces four months later, in the following April's health settlement, as part of the reconciliation against actual income — easy to miss if the bonus and the settlement are handled by different people.

The mechanics of that April reconciliation are covered in the health settlement note.
