# Equity income and the year-end settlement

## Where it sits
Value taxed at vesting or exercise is added to that month's income and withheld the same way a bonus is — as an estimate, using the annualized method. It is not a separate, final tax; it is folded into the employee's total earned income for the year and settled properly the following February alongside every other payslip line.

## What that means at settlement
An employee with a large vesting event mid-year often sees a bigger swing at year-end settlement than employees without equity income, because the monthly withholding estimate was calculated on a projection that assumed that month's income repeated all year.

**A one-off vesting month does not get its own separate settlement.** The extra tax withheld in that month is reconciled together with every other month's income in the single February run — there is no mechanism to settle the equity event on its own timeline, even though the underlying tax event happened on a fixed, one-time date.

Dependant claims and manual deductions filed for that year apply against this same combined total, exactly as they do for a bonus year.
