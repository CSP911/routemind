# Payment lines on a payslip

## The standard lines

| Line | What it covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | Contracted monthly salary | The base for overtime and severance calculations |
| Fixed allowances | Meal, own-car, position, childcare, research | Paid monthly regardless of hours worked |
| Overtime / night / holiday premium | Extra hours beyond the standard week | See the separate premium table |
| Performance bonus | Discretionary or contract-linked | Taxed in full, no allowance treatment |

## Gross pay is not take-home pay

Gross pay at the top of the payslip adds every line above together, before any deduction. **It is also not the same as the taxable base** — meal, own-car, childcare and research allowances up to their 200,000 KRW caps sit inside gross pay but are stripped out again before income tax is calculated. Comparing gross pay directly to the bank transfer, or to the tax withheld, will not reconcile; the deduction lines explain the gap, not an error in the payment lines.
