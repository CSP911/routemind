# The annual pay review cycle

Pay reviews run once a year, on a fixed calendar regardless of an employee's join date.

## Timeline

| Stage | Timing |
|---|---|
| Manager proposals submitted | Early January |
| HR calibration | Mid January |
| Outcome notified to employee | Late January |
| Effective date | 1 January |

## The trap

**The effective date is always 1 January, even though the outcome is not confirmed until late January.** January's payslip is run at the old rate because the new figure isn't signed off yet. The difference for that month is then paid as a retroactive lump on the February payslip, taxed as a single addition rather than spread back over January.

Anyone reviewing a payslip in January or February should expect this mismatch rather than treat it as an error. See payslip-retroactive-rise for how that lump is calculated and withheld.
