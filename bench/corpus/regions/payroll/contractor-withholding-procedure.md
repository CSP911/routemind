# Paying a freelancer's invoice

## Steps

1. Contractor submits an invoice carrying their business registration number (or resident registration number if unregistered) and a description of the work.
2. Payroll checks the invoice against the signed service agreement — no agreement on file, no payment.
3. Payroll calculates the 3.3% withholding and pays the net amount to the contractor's account.
4. Payroll remits the withheld tax to the tax office by the 10th of the following month.
5. Payroll issues a withholding receipt to the contractor, showing the gross invoice, the tax withheld, and the net paid.

## The trap

**An invoice with no registration number can't be processed at all** — payroll has no basis to withhold correctly and no way to file the remittance, so the invoice is returned rather than paid on a best guess. This is the single most common reason a freelancer's first payment is late: the invoice format from their own business software doesn't always carry the number payroll needs.
