# Severance and leaving pay

This section offers comprehensive coverage on the calculation of severance pay and paying out leave balances when an employee departs. It addresses different retirement pension types and the related tax implications. The timing of these payments and their tax treatments are critical components carefully outlined in the following pages.

One common error is not correctly classifying the retirement pension type, leading to inaccurate tax deductions. **Pay close attention to the final classification as it has significant tax consequences.**

To navigate detailed calculations and specific scenarios, consult the specific pages within this section dedicated to severance and leaving payments.
