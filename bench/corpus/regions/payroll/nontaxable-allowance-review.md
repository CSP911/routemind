# Reviewing a nontaxable allowance's condition

Once a childcare, research or own-car allowance is set up, nothing forces the employee to report when the qualifying condition stops holding — a child ageing past six, a move out of a qualifying role, a car sold. Payroll's review catches what isn't self-reported.

## The check

1. HR flags role changes and leaver dates to payroll each cycle.
2. Payroll cross-checks childcare allowances against the ages on file annually, not monthly.
3. Own-car allowances are checked against expense claims for mileage each time a claim is submitted.

**The annual childcare check means a condition failure can run for up to eleven months before it's caught.** A child turning seven in January isn't picked up until the annual check, typically at year-end — by which point eleven months of allowance have been paid tax free against a condition that stopped holding in January. Those months are corrected at year-end settlement, not clawed back as they occur.

## Employee's part

Reporting the change yourself as soon as it happens avoids a lump correction landing on one settlement — the same trap as an uncorrected back-dated rise landing as a single figure.
