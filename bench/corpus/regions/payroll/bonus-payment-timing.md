# When bonuses are paid

## Fixed-date bonuses
A regular bonus follows the date set in the employment contract, most often split as a mid-year and year-end payment. Missing that date because of a payslip cut-off pushes the payment to the next run — it does not roll into the following month's amount.

## Discretionary-date bonuses
A performance bonus has no contractual date. It is paid once results are confirmed, typically in the first quarter after year-end, on whatever date HR notifies payroll.

## Typical bonus calendar

| Bonus | Usual timing | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| Regular (mid-year) | June payslip | Contract |
| Regular (year-end) | December payslip | Contract |
| Performance | February–March payslip | HR, after results |
| Signing | First or second payslip after joining | Offer letter |

**Being on the headcount when the bonus is calculated does not guarantee payment.** Most regular and performance bonuses also require the employee to still be employed on the payment date itself — a leaver whose last day falls between calculation and payout can be excluded under the contract wording, and payroll pays only what HR confirms.
