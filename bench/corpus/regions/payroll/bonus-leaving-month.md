# A bonus falling in a leaver's final month

## The setup
An employee's regular bonus is scheduled for June. Their last working day is 20 June, before the bonus payment date on the 25th.

## What decides the outcome
Whether the bonus is paid at all depends on the eligibility wording in the contract or bonus scheme, not on payroll's discretion. Most schemes require the employee to be employed on the payment date itself, which a resignation before that date fails.

## If it is paid
Where the contract allows a bonus regardless of the exact leave date, payroll includes it on the final payslip, taxed under the same annualized method as any other bonus, and subject to standard pension, health and employment deductions as if the employee were staying.

## If it is not paid
Where eligibility isn't met, the bonus simply does not appear on the final payslip — no partial payment, and no separate settlement for it later.

**A contract promising an annual bonus does not by itself guarantee a leaver receives it.** The employment-on-payment-date condition, when present, overrides the general entitlement, and payroll pays exactly what HR confirms.

What else is deliberately absent from a leaver's final payslip is covered in the leaving payslip checklist.
