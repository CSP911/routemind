# Payroll desk

Runs the pay calculation, social insurance enrolment and settlement, the year-end settlement and
leaving pay. Contacts and the monthly cut-offs are on the page below.

## What it decides
- **What is paid, from what attendance shows.** The desk does not judge whether a record is right; it
  reads what attendance closed with.
- **Whether something is taxable.** Tax-free caps, the trip allowance, an allowance paid for a day with
  no attendance behind it.
- **Insurance during a leave of absence** — deferral, suspension, and the settlement afterwards.
- **Leaving pay**, including unused leave paid out and the final month's proration.

## What it will not do
Change an attendance record. If the days are wrong, that is the HR desk, and the correction has to
land there before payroll can do anything with it. Asking payroll to "just add the day" produces a
payment with no record behind it, which is the thing an audit finds.

## Before you ask
Open the payslip beside the attendance record for the same month. The great majority of queries
resolve there — a leave day that was never approved, a trip day that was not confirmed, overtime that
was not requested in advance. If they disagree, say which two figures you are comparing; it is much
faster than "the amount looks wrong".

## Timing
Only items received **by the 15th** reach that month's pay. Something raised on the 20th is not
refused, it simply settles in the following month, and being told that in advance is usually the
answer people actually wanted.
