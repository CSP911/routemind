# Childcare allowance when the child turns seven

The 200,000 KRW childcare allowance is tax free only while the child is aged six or under.

## The month it changes

An employee's child turns seven in September. The allowance keeps appearing on the payslip at 200,000 KRW unless payroll is told to stop it — but from September the condition is no longer met.

| Month | Child's age | Allowance paid | Exempt |
|---|---|---|---|
| August | 6 | 200,000 KRW | Yes |
| September | 7 | 200,000 KRW | No |

**Failing the condition taxes the whole line, not just an excess over a cap.** This is the opposite of a month over the cap: there, only the overage above 200,000 KRW is taxed and the rest stays exempt. Here, the cap was never breached — the condition was — so the full 200,000 KRW becomes ordinary taxable pay for that month and every month after, until the allowance is stopped.

## What payroll needs

Notify payroll of the child's birthdate at setup so the stop is scheduled automatically; a late notice means back-taxing months that should already have stopped.
