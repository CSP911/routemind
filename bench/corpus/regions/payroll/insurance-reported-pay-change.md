# What a reported pay change does to insurance

A promotion, a contract change, or any pay move large enough to shift someone's income bracket can be reported to the pension and health authorities outside the normal April/July cycle. Once filed, it updates the standard monthly income used for contributions from the month after the report.

## What responds to a report

| Line | Needs a report to change mid-year |
|---|---|
| National pension | Yes |
| Health insurance | Yes |
| Employment insurance | No — recalculated automatically each month from actual pay |
| Industrial accident | No — same as employment insurance |

## The trap

**Filing a reported pay change updates contributions going forward only — it does not correct the months already billed at the old base.** Those earlier months are picked up later, at the April health settlement or the July pension reset, not by the report itself. A manager who expects an immediate lump-sum correction on the next payslip will not see one; they will see it months later, folded into the next scheduled reset.
