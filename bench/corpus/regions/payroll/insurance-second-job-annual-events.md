# Second job and the annual settlement events

A second job doesn't get folded into one combined insurance record. Each employer runs its own line, and the two never compare notes.

## Which lines run per employer

| Line | Runs per employer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | Yes | Each employer contributes 4.5% on what it pays |
| Health insurance | Yes | Each employer contributes 3.545% on what it pays |
| Employment insurance | Main job only | Tied to the primary employment record |
| Industrial accident | Yes | Workplace coverage, always per site |

## The April and July events don't combine income

The April health settlement reconciles each employer's own health line against the income that employer actually reported — not the sum of both jobs. The July pension reset does the same: each employer refixes its own pension base off its own reported pay, on its own July date.

**Neither event aggregates the two incomes.** An employee expecting one combined true-up figure across both jobs will get two separate ones instead, on two separate payslips, each correct only for that employer's share.

Enrolment and deregistration at either job still follow the ordinary triggers — see how a second job is set up in the first place before assuming this settlement behaviour applies to a job that hasn't started the four insurances yet.
