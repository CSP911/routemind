# Allowance lines and their tax split

## Why one line can be two numbers
Several allowances appear as a single figure on the payment side but are split between taxable and nontaxable when withholding is calculated. The payslip does not print the split — it only surfaces in the year-end settlement documents.

## Common allowance lines
| Line | Monthly cap treated as nontaxable | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW | Not paid alongside meals in kind |
| Own-car allowance | 200,000 KRW | Own vehicle, used for work; not with a mileage claim |
| Childcare allowance | 200,000 KRW | Child aged 6 or under |
| Research allowance | 200,000 KRW | Qualifying roles only |

**A line paid above its cap is not fully taxed — only the excess is.** A childcare allowance of 250,000 KRW against the 200,000 KRW cap taxes the 50,000 KRW difference; the rest stays exempt.

## Where the split shows up
The taxable portion folds into the income tax and local income tax lines. The nontaxable portion never touches the pension, health, long-term care or employment insurance lines either, since those are also calculated on pay excluding nontaxable allowances.

The caps themselves, and the conditions attached to each, are listed in full separately.
