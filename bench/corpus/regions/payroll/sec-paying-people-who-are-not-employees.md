# Paying people who are not employees

This section covers the intricacies of processing payments to individuals who are not classified as employees. It includes guidelines for handling contractors, freelancers, and overseas contractors, each contributing unique considerations to the payroll process. The common case involves ensuring compliance with all legal and tax obligations, such as withholding taxes appropriately. 

A frequent pitfall is incorrectly treating a contractor as an employee, which can lead to misclassification issues and potential penalties. **This happens most often when the distinction between the working conditions of a contractor and an employee are not clear.**

For more precise rates, applicable tax withholdings, and procedural steps, refer to the pages dedicated to each specific scenario beneath this section.
