# Social insurance movement events at a glance

Movements happen for different reasons on different clocks. This table lines them up.

## Movements and timing

| Event | Trigger | Lands on |
|---|---|---|
| Enrolment | First day worked | Day-one payslip (pension delayed if start is after the 15th) |
| Leaving | Last day worked | Final payslip; pension charged in full if the date is after the 15th |
| April health settlement | Prior year's confirmed income | April payslip only |
| July pension reset | Prior year's average income | July payslip through the following June |
| Reported pay change | Employer files an interim change | Month after the report is filed |
| Second job enrolment | New qualifying employment starts | Per employer, from that job's day one |

## The trap

**The April and July resets run on separate clocks and never combine into one adjustment.** A raise reported in June can still miss the July pension reset if the paperwork lands after the cut-off, in which case it waits for the next July — not the next available payslip.
