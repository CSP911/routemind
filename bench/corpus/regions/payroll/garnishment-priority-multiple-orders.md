# When more than one garnishment order arrives

More than one creditor can file against the same employee at the same time. Payroll cannot split the deductible portion evenly between them — it has to apply a priority order.

## Priority

| Priority | Type of claim |
|---|---|
| 1 | Tax and social insurance arrears (National Tax Service, NHIS) |
| 2 | Child or spousal support arrears |
| 3 | Civil judgment debts, in the order they were served |

Only after a higher-priority claim is satisfied does the deductible portion become available to the next one in line.

**A later order does not wait behind an earlier one if it carries higher priority.** A support order served after an existing civil judgment can still move ahead of it and take the deductible wage first, leaving the earlier creditor to wait.

Where an order doesn't clearly state which category it falls into, payroll treats it as a standard civil judgment until told otherwise by the issuing court.
