# Unpaid leave deduction calculation

| Line | Proration basis |
|---|---|
| Basic pay | Days worked ÷ calendar days in the month |
| Nontaxable allowances (meal, own-car) | Same ratio as basic pay |
| Insurance deduction lines | Fall automatically, since they run on pay excluding nontaxable allowances |

The ratio uses calendar days in the month, not working days — the same basis used for a mid-month join or leave.

**Unpaid leave is prorated on calendar days in the month, not on working days.** A five-day unpaid stretch inside a 31-day month removes 5/31 of pay and allowances, regardless of how many of those five days would have been working days. Counting working days instead produces a different, wrong figure — and one that doesn't match how the rest of the payslip is prorated.
