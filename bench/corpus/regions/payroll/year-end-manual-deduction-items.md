# Manual deduction categories and their caps

The pre-fill service pulls card spending and insurance premiums automatically, but it does not know which of those payments qualify as a deduction, or apply the caps below. That still has to be entered by hand.

## The categories

| Category | Cap or rate | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Medical expenses | No upper cap, but only the amount over 3% of gross pay counts | Receipts from a licensed medical institution or pharmacy |
| Education expenses | 3,000,000 KRW per child (school), 9,000,000 KRW per child (university) | Tuition and school fees only, not private tutoring |
| Donations | 15% of the donated amount up to 10,000,000 KRW, 30% above that | Registered charity or religious body |
| Housing loan interest | Up to 18,000,000 KRW a year | One home only, loan used to buy or build it |

**The 3% medical floor is calculated against your own gross pay, not against the total on the card statement pulled into the pre-fill.** A household with 4,000,000 KRW of card-paid medical spend and 6,000,000 KRW gross pay only deducts the amount above 180,000 KRW — most of the figure people expect to claim never clears the floor.

Evidence requirements for each category are covered separately in the manual-deductions submission guide.
