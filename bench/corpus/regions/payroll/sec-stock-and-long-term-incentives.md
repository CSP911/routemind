# Stock and long-term incentives

This section explores the process of managing employee stock options and long-term incentives. Topics include stock grants, vesting schedules, tax events, and the treatment of these incentives upon an employee's departure from the company.

An often overlooked issue is misunderstanding the tax implications at the time of vesting, which can create significant discrepancies. **Specifically, failures often arise when employees do not adequately plan for tax events.**

In-depth explanations and precise requirements can be found in the specific pages addressing each aspect under this section.
