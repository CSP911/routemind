# Who qualifies for the research allowance

The 200,000 KRW research allowance is tax free only for qualifying roles — broadly, staff whose job is defined as research or development duties, not anyone who occasionally contributes to a project.

## The condition

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Research allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Qualifying roles only |

**A role change out of a qualifying position doesn't grandfather the allowance.** An employee moved from an R&D team into a management role keeps drawing the same payslip line unless someone flags the move — but the exemption stopped the day the role changed, not the day payroll is told about it. Once flagged, the allowance is stopped or reclassified as ordinary taxable pay from the change date, and any month paid tax free after that date is corrected at the next settlement.

## Where the line sits

Managers signing off a role change should tell payroll in the same cycle; this allowance has no proration for a partial qualifying period — it's exempt for a full month or not at all, the same all-or-nothing test as the childcare allowance's age condition.

Where a receipt overlaps with a flat allowance instead of a role changing, the trap is different — see the travel allowance page.
