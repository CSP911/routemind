# Who signs off a payslip change

## Retroactive rises
A backdated pay change only reaches payroll after HR confirms the effective date and the new figure in writing. Payroll does not act on a verbal instruction, however senior it comes from.

## Corrections
An over- or under-payment correction can be initiated by payroll itself once it's caught, or flagged by the employee, but the amount and month must be confirmed before it's entered as an adjustment line.

## Leaving payslips
The final payslip's last working day and unused leave figure come from HR, not from the employee's own record. **Payroll uses HR's confirmed date even if it differs from the employee's own count of days worked.**

## Escalation
Disputes over a figure go back to HR first. Payroll only adjusts once HR has re-confirmed a number — it does not referee a disagreement between an employee's count and HR's record.

Where queries on any of this go, and by when, is covered separately.
