# Tax-free allowances, case by case

This section outlines the variety of tax-free allowances available, providing details on the caps and conditions for each. It discusses exceptions and what happens when a condition fails or a cap is exceeded, with implications for payroll and taxation.

A prevalent issue involves failing to apply the cap correctly, which can lead to unintentional taxable income. **Ensure all conditions are met and documented, especially when multiple allowances are involved, to avoid issues.**

For specifics on caps, conditions, and calculation examples, refer to the relevant pages under this section.
