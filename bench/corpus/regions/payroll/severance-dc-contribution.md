# DC contribution rate

Under a DC plan, the company must pay in at least 1/12 of the employee's annual wage — one month's worth — for each year of service, credited to the employee's own retirement pension account.

## The minimum

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum annual contribution | 1/12 of total annual wage (≈ one month's average wage per year of service) |
| Contribution frequency | At least once a year; many run monthly or quarterly |
| Who manages the account | The employee, through the pension provider the company has set up the plan with |

## What this replaces

Once an employee is on DC, the company has no further severance obligation beyond making that contribution correctly and on time — there is no separate lump sum calculated at leaving under the DB formula.

**Missing or underpaying a DC contribution is a recoverable shortfall, not something written off at year end.** The company remains liable for the gap, and it doesn't quietly disappear because a reconciliation window has closed.

DB, by contrast, defers the whole calculation to the leaving date — covered in the calculation and comparison pages.
