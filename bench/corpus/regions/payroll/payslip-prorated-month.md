# A prorated month's payslip

## When it applies

Anyone joining or leaving partway through a calendar month has that month's basic pay, fixed allowances and insurance base calculated on a daily rate: pay for the month × days actually employed ÷ days in that month.

## What gets prorated

| Line | Prorated? |
|---|---|
| Basic pay | Yes |
| Meal, own-car, childcare, research allowances | Yes |
| Insurance and tax | Recalculated on the prorated base |
| Performance bonus | Only if the bonus is itself contract-defined as prorated |

## The trap

**A nontaxable allowance does not survive proration at full value.** Someone joining on the 15th of a 30-day month does not receive the full 200,000 KRW meal allowance that month — they receive half of it, and the exemption cap for that month shrinks with it. Expecting the flat monthly figure quoted elsewhere to appear unprorated is the most common mismatch reported to the payroll desk.

The same daily-rate logic applies, in reverse, on the final payslip when someone leaves.
