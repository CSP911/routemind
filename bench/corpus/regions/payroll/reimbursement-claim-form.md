# Submitting a reimbursement claim

## What the form captures

- claimant name and cost centre
- date the expense was incurred
- amount, in the currency actually paid
- expense category (travel, client cost, supplies, other)
- stated business purpose
- receipt or invoice attached
- approving manager's sign-off

## What gets it rejected

**A claim missing its receipt is returned unprocessed, not queued for later payment as taxable income.** This is stricter than a claim that simply lacks a manager's sign-off, which payroll holds and chases — a missing receipt means there is nothing to certify, so the form goes back to the claimant rather than sitting in the queue.

Once corrected and resubmitted, the claim is treated as newly received for cut-off purposes, not backdated to the original submission date.

A claim that does reach payroll complete, but genuinely has no receipt to give, is handled differently — that case is covered next.
