# Enrolling a new joiner in social insurance

Coverage starts from the first day of actual work, not from the day the contract is signed or HR paperwork clears. Payroll files each enrolment with the relevant authority within 14 days of the start date.

## Enrolment by insurance

| Insurance | Covered from | Filed by |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | First day worked, with one exception below | Within 14 days |
| Health insurance | First day worked | Within 14 days |
| Employment insurance | First day worked | Within 14 days |
| Industrial accident | First day worked | Automatic, employer-funded, no employee filing |

## The trap

**National pension does not always start in the month someone joins.** If the start date falls on or after the 16th of the month, pension enrolment — and the first contribution — begins the following month instead. Health, employment and industrial accident insurance are unaffected by this and still run from day one, which is why a first payslip can show four insurance lines with three different start logics behind them.

The reverse question — what happens to these same lines on someone's last day — is covered next.
