# Allowance or reimbursement

Payroll pays for the same kind of expense — meals, travel, use of a personal car — through two different mechanisms, and confusing one for the other is the most common cause of a wrongly taxed line.

## Standing allowance

A fixed monthly figure paid regardless of what was actually spent that month. It sits on the payslip every pay period, is capped at 200,000 KRW for meals, own-car use, childcare and research, and needs no receipt — only the underlying condition (a child aged 6 or under, a vehicle registered to the employee) has to be confirmed once and reviewed periodically.

## Reimbursement

A repayment of an actual, evidenced cost: a taxi receipt, a client dinner, a trip's expense above the flat daily rate. It carries no monthly cap, but every claim needs its own receipt and stated business purpose — payroll cannot pay it tax free on trust alone.

**The two cannot run against the same expense in the same period.** An employee who draws the standing meal allowance and then expenses a lunch receipt has had the same meal paid twice; one of the two has to be reversed.
