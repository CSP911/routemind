# April and July events for a partial year

Both annual events assume a full year of data behind them. Someone who wasn't employed for the whole period gets a shortened version, not a skipped one — except in one case.

## Joiners

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Joined before July | Gets the standard July reset like everyone else |
| Joined after July | Pension base is fixed at enrolment, held until the *next* July reset |
| Any joining date | First meets the April health settlement the following spring, prorated to months actually worked |

## Leavers

A leaver's pension and health records close out at deregistration, not at the next April. **The April settlement does not run for someone who has already left** — their partial-year health true-up is settled at the leaving date itself, using whatever income exists up to that point, not carried forward to the following April's batch.

Deregistration also has its own pension cut-off point, separate from this settlement timing — worth checking before assuming a leaver's last insurance figures match their final payslip.
