# Severance pay worked example

An employee resigns after exactly 3 years of continuous service (1,095 days). Basic pay was 3,000,000 KRW a month for the final 3 months, with no bonus or irregular allowance paid in that window.

## The average wage

| Step | Figure |
|---|---|
| Total wages, last 3 months (91 days) | 9,000,000 KRW |
| Average daily wage | 9,000,000 ÷ 91 = 98,901.10 KRW |

## The severance amount

| Step | Figure |
|---|---|
| Formula | 98,901.10 × 30 × (1,095 ÷ 365) |
| Result | 98,901.10 × 30 × 3 = 8,901,099 KRW |

**The three years here happen to make the final multiplier a clean 3 — most leavers land on a fraction of a year, and the days-based ratio does the rounding, not a whole-number years figure.** Service is counted in days throughout, never rounded up to the next full year.

This figure is what DB pays out directly, or what a DC account should have accumulated to by the same date — the difference between the two types is covered separately.
