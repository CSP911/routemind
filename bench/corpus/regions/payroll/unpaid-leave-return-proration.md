# Worked example: returning mid-month from unpaid leave

An employee on 3,000,000 KRW basic pay and the standard 200,000 KRW meal allowance takes unpaid leave from the 1st to the 10th of a 30-day month, returning to work on the 11th. Days worked: 20 of 30.

| Line | Full amount | Ratio | Prorated amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | 3,000,000 KRW | 20/30 | 2,000,000 KRW |
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW | 20/30 | 133,333 KRW |

Insurance deduction lines are then calculated on the reduced pay figure, not the full monthly amount.

**The nontaxable allowance is cut down by the same ratio as basic pay** — a fixed monthly figure does not survive at full value just because the cap is expressed as a flat number. Paying the full 200,000 KRW meal allowance for a month with ten unpaid days overstates the tax-free portion and understates the taxable base.

The same "days into money" logic runs the other way when leave is banked rather than spent.
