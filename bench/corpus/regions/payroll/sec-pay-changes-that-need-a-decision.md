# Pay changes that need a decision

Certain payroll alterations require formal decisions, such as mid-cycle raises, off-cycle bonus payments, or salary advances. These require advanced planning and approval due to potential impact on budget allocation, taxation, and salary structure. **Employees often mistakenly believe these changes take immediate effect without necessary approvals.** Understanding the process helps manage expectations and ensures proper documentation and compliance with company policies and legal frameworks. Each scenario — from retroactive pay increases to emergency salary advances — has distinct paths and criteria outlined in the sub-pages.
