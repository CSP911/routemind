# Grant types and how they differ

## The three award types
| Award type | What vests | Tax event | Value taxed |
|---|---|---|---|
| RSU | The unit itself, converting to a share | Vesting date | Market value of the share on the vesting date |
| Stock option | The right to buy at a fixed exercise price | Exercise date | Market value at exercise, minus the exercise price |
| Performance share unit | The unit, only once the performance condition is confirmed met | Vesting date, after confirmation | Market value of the share on the vesting date |

## Why the distinction matters to payroll
RSUs and performance shares generate a tax event automatically the moment the vesting condition is satisfied — payroll doesn't wait for the employee to do anything. Stock options sit dormant until the employee actively chooses to exercise; a fully vested option that is never exercised never creates a payslip line at all.

**Vesting and exercising are not interchangeable for options** — an option can vest years before it's exercised, and the tax event only ever tracks the later date.
