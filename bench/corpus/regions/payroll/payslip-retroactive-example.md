# A retroactive rise, worked through

## The scenario
A raise from 3,000,000 KRW to 3,300,000 KRW basic pay is approved in June, backdated to April 1. Two months — April and May — were already paid at the old rate.

## The catch-up
| Month | Old basic pay | New basic pay | Difference owed |
|---|---|---|---|
| April | 3,000,000 KRW | 3,300,000 KRW | 300,000 KRW |
| May | 3,000,000 KRW | 3,300,000 KRW | 300,000 KRW |

The 600,000 KRW difference is paid in June, on top of June's own basic pay at the new rate.

**The back-pay is taxed as June income, not spread back over April and May.** It lands in one lump on the June payslip and can push that month into a higher withholding band, even though the extra money was earned over three separate months.

## Insurance follows the same lump
Pension, health and employment insurance deductions for June are also calculated on the lump-inclusive total, not on April and May's original figures re-opened.

How the monthly withholding band is picked in the first place is covered separately.
