# Payslip

Every payment and every deduction, line by line. The full list is in the table below, and the tax-free
lines have their own page under that.

## How to read it
The top block is what you were paid, the bottom is what was taken off, and the difference is what
arrives on the 25th. Base pay is the annual figure divided by twelve and does not move. Everything
else on the payments side is either an allowance with a fixed monthly amount or something calculated
from attendance — overtime, the trip allowance, a prorated joining or leaving month.

## The lines people ask about
- **Meal allowance and own-car allowance.** Fixed monthly amounts, tax free up to their caps, and both
  have a condition attached. The own-car allowance cannot be drawn in the same month as a per-kilometre
  mileage claim for the same vehicle — that is double counting and the amount moves back into taxable
  pay.
- **Overtime.** Only hours requested in advance. Hours worked and not requested do not appear, and that
  is not an omission the desk can fix afterwards.
- **Trip allowance.** For confirmed attendance days only.
- **"Other" on the deductions side.** Unexplained card spending, advances, union dues. If it appears
  unexpectedly, it is nearly always the first of those.

## Joining and leaving months
Prorated by days: base pay ÷ days in the month × days employed. Allowances are prorated the same way.
Unused leave is paid out in the final pay, at the ordinary daily rate.

## If a figure is wrong
Compare against attendance for the same month before asking. Payroll calculated from that and nothing
else, so a disagreement is almost always visible there.
