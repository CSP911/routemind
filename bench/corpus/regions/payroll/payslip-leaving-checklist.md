# Final payslip checklist

## What must be on it
- Basic pay and allowances prorated to the last working day
- Unused annual leave paid out as a separate line
- Any outstanding correction from a prior month, settled here rather than carried forward again

## What must not be on it
**Severance pay is not one of the lines.** It is calculated and paid separately, usually within 14 days of the last working day, under its own schedule. Looking for it on the payslip itself is the mistake people make.

## Before it's issued
| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Last working day confirmed with HR | Sets the proration ratio for every line |
| Unused leave days confirmed | Determines the leave payout figure |
| Any open correction identified | Settled now, not deferred to a month that no longer exists |

The proration math behind the shortened lines is the same one used for a mid-month join.
