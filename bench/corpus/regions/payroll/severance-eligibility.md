# Severance eligibility

Severance pay is owed only once continuous service reaches one full year. Below that threshold, no severance is calculated or paid — not a reduced amount, none at all.

## What counts toward the year

Continuous service runs from the first day of employment to the last, including approved leave of absence such as parental leave, unless the contract was broken by a genuine gap in employment. A fixed-term contract renewed back-to-back with no break counts as continuous.

## What affects the count

| Situation | Effect on the one-year count |
|---|---|
| Unpaid leave of absence, approved | Counts, does not pause the clock |
| Resignation followed by rehire after a gap | Restarts the count from the rehire date |
| Transfer to a related company, by agreement | Carries over only if the agreement says so in writing |

**Someone who resigns at 11 months and 25 days gets nothing, not a prorated 11/12 of the formula.** The one-year line is a hard cutoff, not a ramp.

Whether someone clears the line is checked before the calculation is run, and the calculation itself works from average wage and days served — covered next.
