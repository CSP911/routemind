# The deductions the pre-filled service never carries

| Deduction | Evidence needed | Why the pre-fill misses it |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly rent (wolse) | Lease contract, resident registration matching the address | No third party reports private rent |
| Cash donations | Receipt from the receiving organisation | Only reported if the organisation is registered for it |
| Education fees paid direct | Receipt from the institute or tutor | Not reported unless affiliated |
| Overseas medical expenses | Invoice and proof of payment | Foreign providers don't report to the NTS |
| A dependant added mid-year | Family register or equivalent proof | Registration hasn't caught up with NTS records |

## The trap
**Having the deduction is not the same as having it accepted.** The rent deduction in particular is rejected if the lease address doesn't match the resident registration on file — a mismatch that's common when someone moves but doesn't update their registration right away.

Once these are gathered, the dependants sitting on the same claim need their own check before submission.
