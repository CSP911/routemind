# The leaving payslip

## What's different from a normal month

- Basic pay and fixed allowances are prorated to the last working day, on the same daily-rate logic as a mid-month join.
- Unused annual leave is paid out as a separate line, at the daily basic pay rate.
- Insurance deductions are calculated on the final prorated pay, then stop from the following month.
- Any outstanding adjustment lines from a prior correction are settled here rather than carried forward again.

## What is not on it

**Severance pay is not a line on the leaving payslip.** It is calculated and paid separately, usually within 14 days of the last day, under its own settlement process — looking for it among the payslip lines is the most common question raised on the last day of employment.

Insurance treatment during any prior leave of absence is covered separately.
