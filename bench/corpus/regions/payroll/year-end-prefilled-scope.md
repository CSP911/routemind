# What the pre-filled service actually covers

## What comes in automatically
The pre-filled data draws on what other institutions already reported: national pension and health insurance premiums, card spending registered under the employee's resident registration number, insurance premiums paid to registered insurers, and prior withholding already recorded on the payslip.

## What it never sees
Anything not reported to the NTS by a third party stays blank. Monthly rent paid to a private landlord, cash donations to organisations not registered for the deduction, education fees paid directly to a tutor or an unaffiliated institute, and medical expenses paid overseas all have to be entered and evidenced by the employee.

## The trap
**A pre-filled total is not a completed return.** It's a starting figure. If nothing is added or confirmed, payroll settles on the pre-filled figure alone, and any deduction the employee was entitled to but didn't add is simply missing — there's no automatic retry, only a later correction.

Which manual deductions come up most often is worth knowing before the pre-fill opens each year.
