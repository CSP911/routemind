# A pay change reported in settlement month

Reporting a pay change doesn't always land where the calendar suggests. The April and July events each look at different data, and a same-month report can miss one of them entirely.

## April: no interaction

The April health settlement reconciles the completed prior year's actual income. A pay change reported in April affects this year's pay going forward — it has no effect on last year's figure and is not folded into that April run.

## July: interaction depends on timing

| Reported | Result |
|---|---|
| Before the reset is processed | Captured in this July's reset |
| After the reset is processed | Waits for next July's reset |

The reset date is a cut, not a window. A change filed on the day of the reset can land on either side of it depending on when processing actually runs that morning.

**A pay change reported "in July" is not automatically "in this July's reset."** Missing the processing cut by a day means the new base doesn't take effect for another twelve months, not one.

Confirm which side of the cut a change fell on before assuming it's reflected — the reported pay change record itself will show which reset picked it up.
