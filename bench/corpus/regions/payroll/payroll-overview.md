# Payroll & Insurance — where to start

| What you are asking | Where it is |
|---|---|
| What this line on my payslip is | Payslip |
| Whether it is taxed | Payslip > Tax-free allowances |
| Why the insurance deduction changed | Social insurance |
| What to submit for year-end, and when | Year-end tax settlement → deadlines |
| The amount looks wrong | Payroll desk |

**Three dates drive everything.** Attendance closes (3rd of the following month) → pay queries close
(the 15th) → pay day (the 25th). Nothing is calculated before attendance closes, so if pay looks
wrong, **compare the payslip against attendance first**.

**A tax-free allowance over its cap is taxed only on the excess** — the exemption is not cancelled
wholesale. A trip's daily allowance is the exception worth knowing: it is only tax free for days that
attendance confirms, and an allowance for a day with no attendance is simply taxable pay.

**What this area does not answer**: how much the daily allowance is (Expenses > Travel expenses), how
many days of leave you may take (Attendance & Leave).
