# The protected portion of wage

Korean civil execution rules protect part of every wage from seizure, regardless of how large the underlying debt is. Payroll applies these bands every month the order is active.

## The bands

| Monthly wage (net) | Protected portion |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,850,000 KRW | Entire wage protected — not garnishable |
| 1,850,000–3,700,000 KRW | 1,850,000 KRW protected, flat |
| 3,700,000–6,000,000 KRW | Protected = wage − (wage − 1,850,000) ÷ 2 |
| Over 6,000,000 KRW | Protected = 3,000,000 KRW + (wage − 6,000,000) ÷ 3 |

## What "wage" means here

The bands apply to net wage — after pension, health, long-term care, employment insurance, and income tax have already come out — not to gross pay, and not to the taxable base used in the year-end settlement.

**The protected portion is recalculated every month against that month's actual wage, not fixed once when the order first lands.** A month with overtime or a bonus can push the deductible amount up even under the same order.

The deducted balance still has to land somewhere specific on the payslip.
