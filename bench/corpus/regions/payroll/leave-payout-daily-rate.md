# Leave payout daily rate

Converting a day of unused leave into money needs a daily rate, and payroll does not use a plain fraction of basic pay to get it.

| Item | Basis |
|---|---|
| Unused leave day rate | Average wage — the same wage figure severance is calculated on |
| What it includes | Basic pay, recurring allowances, and average overtime over the reference period |
| What it excludes | One-off or irregular payments not expected to recur |

Because average wage folds in overtime and allowances that a plain basic-pay figure wouldn't, the leave payout rate is usually higher than basic pay divided by the days in a month.

**The daily rate is not basic pay divided by 30.** Treating it that way understates the payout for anyone who regularly works overtime or carries a fixed allowance, since neither would be reflected in a basic-pay-only fraction.

Once the rate is fixed, the remaining question is when the resulting figure actually lands on a payslip.
