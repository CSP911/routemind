# Confirming a pay review outcome before payroll acts

A review outcome does not reach payroll automatically. It has to be confirmed in writing first.

## Steps

1. HR finalises the calibrated outcome for each employee.
2. The new basic pay figure and effective date are recorded on the compensation change form.
3. The employee's manager and HR both sign the form.
4. The signed form is sent to payroll.
5. Payroll applies the change from the effective date, running a retroactive catch-up if the effective date has already passed.

## The trap

**A manager telling an employee their new number in the review meeting is not the same as payroll receiving it.** Nothing changes on a payslip until the signed form arrives — an employee quoting a verbal figure from their manager should not expect it on the very next payslip if the paperwork hasn't caught up.

The same signoff requirement applies to corrections and leaver figures — see payslip-change-signoff for the full list of what needs a signature before payroll moves.
