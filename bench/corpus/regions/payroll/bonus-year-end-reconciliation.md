# Bonus tax and the year-end settlement

## How it fits in
Tax withheld on a bonus during the year is only an estimate produced by the annualized method. It is not the final figure. Like every other line of earned income, the bonus is folded into gross annual salary for the year-end settlement in February, and the actual tax due is calculated against the full year's income and deductions.

## What that means in practice
If the annualized method over-withheld across the year, the surplus comes back as part of the year-end refund. If it under-withheld — for example because a large bonus landed early in the year before enough regular pay history existed to annualize accurately — the shortfall is collected the same way as any other year-end shortfall, through the February payslip.

**A bonus does not get its own separate tax settlement.** It is not reconciled on its own; it disappears into the same annual figure as basic pay, overtime and every other taxable line, and the employee sees only the combined result.

The steps for the year-end settlement itself, including what has to be submitted and by when, are covered in the year-end submission notes.
