# What a contractor invoice needs

Before payroll can withhold and pay a contractor's invoice, the invoice — or the claim form attached to it — has to carry the following.

## Required fields

- Contractor's full name and, for a Korean freelancer, their business registration number (or resident registration number if unregistered); for an overseas contractor, their foreign tax identification number
- Description of the service and the period it covers
- Gross invoice amount, and currency
- Bank account details for payment
- Reference to the signed service agreement covering the work
- For an overseas contractor claiming a treaty rate, the certificate of tax residence

## The trap

**An invoice missing the registration or tax ID field is returned unprocessed, not queued for later** — payroll has no legal basis to withhold at any rate without it, and a payment made without withholding creates a liability payroll then has to chase back from the contractor. The same rule applies whether the contractor is domestic or overseas; only which number is required changes.

The rate that number determines — 3.3% domestic, up to 22% overseas — is set out earlier in this set.
