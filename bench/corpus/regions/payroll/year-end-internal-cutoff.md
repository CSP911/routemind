# Missing the settlement submission cutoff

## What the cutoff protects
Payroll needs every dependant, deduction and receipt in hand before the settlement run closes, so the refund or shortfall lands on the payslip in the same cycle as everyone else's.

## What happens after it closes
An item submitted after the cutoff isn't lost, but it doesn't get processed with the rest of the settlement. Payroll runs the settlement on whatever was submitted on time, and the missed item has to be claimed directly with the NTS during the comprehensive filing window in May.

## The trap
**Missing the cutoff does not mean missing the deduction — it means claiming it yourself, later, without payroll's help.** People treat a missed cutoff as a lost cause and drop the claim entirely, when the May filing exists precisely to catch this.

A claim that made the cutoff but turns out to need a correction is a different process again.
