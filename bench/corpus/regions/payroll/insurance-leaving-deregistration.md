# Deregistering someone who leaves

Payroll files deregistration with each authority within 14 days of the last working day. Health and employment insurance coverage ends on that last day; industrial accident cover ends the same way, automatically.

## What ends when

| Insurance | Coverage ends | Filed by |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | Depends on the day of the month — see below | Within 14 days |
| Health insurance | Last working day | Within 14 days |
| Employment insurance | Last working day | Within 14 days |
| Industrial accident | Last working day | Automatic |

## The trap

**A leaver whose last day falls on or after the 16th of the month is still charged a full month's national pension contribution for that month**, the mirror image of the enrolment rule. Someone who resigns on the 20th pays pension for the whole month even though their basic pay is prorated for only the days actually worked. This is a pension-specific rule — it does not apply to health or employment insurance, which simply stop on the last day.

The health side of a leaver's contributions can still move again the following April, at the annual settlement described next.
