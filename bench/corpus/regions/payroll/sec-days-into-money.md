# Days into money

The conversion of leave days into payroll assets involves understanding how unused leave is compensated, prorated pay months, and deductions for unpaid leave. For example, an employee leaving mid-month might receive prorated earnings, calculated based on actual days worked. Additionally, an outstanding leave balance can lead to a payout, often at a predetermined rate. **Employees often incorrectly assume all leave types warrant a payout or correct pro-rata calculations apply without specific conditions.** Each situation, whether it's cashing out unused leave or handling unpaid leave, has distinct rules detailed on dedicated pages. Understanding these payouts helps in planning finances effectively.
