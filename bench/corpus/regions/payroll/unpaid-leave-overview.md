# Unpaid leave of absence

Unpaid leave — personal leave without pay, or the unpaid portion of certain statutory leave — stops pay for the days taken. Insurance enrolment generally continues through the absence, with its own suspension rules covered separately; this is about what happens to the payslip.

## What it affects

- Basic pay for the month is cut down to the days actually worked.
- Nontaxable allowances tied to that month (meal, own-car) are cut down the same way.
- Deduction lines calculated on pay (pension, health, employment insurance) fall with it, since they run on pay excluding nontaxable allowances.

**Unpaid leave does not pause the monthly pay cycle.** Payroll still runs that employee's payslip on the normal schedule — it just prorates the month down to days actually worked, the same mechanical proration a mid-month joiner or leaver goes through.

The proration itself follows a fixed rule, covered next.
