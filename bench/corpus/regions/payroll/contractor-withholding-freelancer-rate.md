# Withholding on a Korean freelancer's invoice

Payroll withholds tax before paying any invoice from a Korean-resident freelancer providing services as an individual, not as an employee. The rate is fixed regardless of the invoice size.

## The rate

| Line | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax withheld | 3% | Invoice amount, excluding VAT |
| Local income tax | 10% of income tax withheld (0.3%) | Same base |
| Total withheld | 3.3% | Invoice amount, excluding VAT |

Payroll pays the freelancer the invoice amount net of 3.3%, and remits the withheld amount to the tax office by the 10th of the following month.

## What the freelancer still has to do

**The 3.3% withheld here is not a final tax — it's an advance.** The freelancer reports the full income on their own comprehensive income tax return the following May and settles the actual liability then, crediting whatever payroll already withheld. Payroll's only ongoing duty is to issue a withholding receipt each time it pays, so the freelancer has the record to file with.
