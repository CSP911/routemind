# Allowance or reimbursement

In payroll, employees receive funds either as allowances or reimbursements, which have different purposes and tax implications. An allowance is typically a pre-calculated, regular payment for specific expenses like travel or meals. A reimbursement, however, requires submission of actual expense evidence, such as receipts, to justify the exact amount spent. **The trap here is submitting a reimbursement claim for an item covered by an existing allowance**, as it will likely be rejected, and could lead to delayed payment or tax adjustments. Precise categorization is crucial to ensure compliance with tax regulations and to minimize processing errors. In-depth details for each type, including tax handling and acceptable evidence, are found in the associated sub-pages.
