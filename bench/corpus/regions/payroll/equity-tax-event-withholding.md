# Tax withholding at vesting and exercise

## What gets withheld and when
The taxable event is valued in KRW on the day it occurs — the vesting date for RSUs and performance shares, the exercise date for options — using that day's closing market price, converted at the same day's exchange rate for foreign-listed stock.

## Method
Payroll runs the resulting amount through the same annualized withholding method used for a performance bonus: the value is treated as an addition to that month's income and withheld accordingly, not taxed at a flat separate rate.

| Event | Taxable value |
|---|---|
| RSU / performance share vesting | Market value of shares on vesting date |
| Option exercise | (Market value at exercise − exercise price) × shares exercised |

**The withholding happens whether or not the employee sells the shares.** Payroll withholds tax the month the shares vest or the option is exercised; what the employee does with the shares afterward — hold them, sell at a gain or a loss — is a personal capital gains matter outside payroll's withholding, not something that adjusts the amount already withheld.

The estimate withheld this way is settled properly the following February, in the same reconciliation cycle as a bonus.
