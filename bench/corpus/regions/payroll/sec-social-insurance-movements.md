# Social insurance movements

This section focuses on managing changes related to social insurance, including health settlements in April and pension resets in July. It addresses what to do when there are reported pay changes or when enrolling and deregistering employees under various circumstances, such as holding second jobs.

A typical mistake is failing to update the employee's insurance details promptly, which can lead to discrepancies and penalties. **Ensure updates are timely and verified with the current regulations to avoid issues.**

For detailed actions required for each event, see the dedicated pages under this section.
