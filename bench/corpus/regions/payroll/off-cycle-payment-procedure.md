# Requesting an off-cycle payment

An off-cycle payment is a one-time amount paid outside the monthly run — a relocation payment, a correction too large or too urgent to wait, an ad hoc award. It does not follow the standard payslip cut-off and needs its own approval before payroll will process it.

## What qualifies

Off-cycle payments are for amounts that can't reasonably wait for the next regular payslip. Anything that can wait goes through the normal monthly run instead — an off-cycle run costs payroll extra processing and is not a convenience.

## Approval and lead time

| Step | Who | Lead time before payment |
|---|---|---|
| Request with amount and reason | Requesting manager | — |
| Approval | Department head and payroll | At least 5 working days |
| Payment | Payroll, separate run | Same week approval clears |

## Tax treatment

**An off-cycle payment is withheld through the same monthly simplified tax table as ordinary wage, not the bonus method.** It is added to that month's basic pay for the purpose of reading the table, then reconciled at year-end like any other monthly figure — arriving outside the normal cycle doesn't make it a bonus.

Where the payment is instead a draw against pay not yet earned, it's an advance, not an off-cycle payment — the two are approved and recovered differently.
