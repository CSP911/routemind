# Pay bands and what they mean

Every role sits in a band. The band sets the range within which basic pay can move; it does not set the figure itself.

## Band ranges

| Band | Level | Annual basic pay range (KRW) |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Associate | 28,000,000 – 38,000,000 |
| B2 | Senior associate | 38,000,000 – 50,000,000 |
| B3 | Manager | 50,000,000 – 68,000,000 |
| B4 | Senior manager | 68,000,000 – 90,000,000 |

## What the band controls

The band caps how far a merit increase can go without a promotion. Two people in the same band can be paid very differently within that range depending on tenure and past reviews.

**Sitting at the top of a band is not itself a promotion trigger.** An employee who reaches the ceiling of B2 stays at that ceiling until a promotion moves them to B3 — the band does not expand to keep raising them.

Movement within a band and movement across a band follow different rules, covered next.
