# Tax on severance pay

Severance pay is taxed as retirement income, a separate tax category from the earned income taxed on a monthly payslip. It is never added into the year-end settlement of salary income.

## How it's withheld

The company calculates retirement income tax using its own deduction schedule and rate table at the point of payment, withholds it from the lump sum, and pays it over — the employee never sees this figure pass through monthly withholding.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Retirement income deduction | Applied to the gross severance amount first |
| Tax rate | Progressive, applied to the deducted base, not the gross figure |
| Local income tax | 10% of the retirement income tax, same as on earned income |

## What the employee gets

A retirement income withholding receipt, separate from the regular income tax withholding receipt, showing the gross severance, the deduction, and the tax withheld.

**This tax is settled at payment — there is no later year-end adjustment for it the way there is for salary income.** Treating it as something the following February's settlement will true up is the mistake; retirement income is closed out the day it's paid.

The 14-day payment deadline this tax rides on is covered on the payment timing page.
