# When the own-car allowance's ownership condition fails

## The condition
The own-car allowance is tax free only when the vehicle is the employee's own and is used for work. Payroll relies on the claim being accurate when it's set up, not on checking it every month.

## Where it breaks
- The car is leased or owned by the company, not the employee
- The car is registered to a family member and never transferred
- The employee stops driving for work but the allowance keeps running unchanged

## What happens once it's caught

| Situation | Condition met? | Tax treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Own vehicle, used for work | Yes | 200,000 KRW exempt |
| Company-leased vehicle | No | Full 200,000 KRW taxable |
| Family member's car, no transfer | No | Full 200,000 KRW taxable |

**A condition failure taxes the whole allowance for the month, not just an amount over a cap — there is no ceiling to measure against once the underlying requirement isn't met.** That's a different failure from running over the 200,000 KRW cap, where only the excess is added back.

Payroll corrects this from the month the condition is confirmed to have failed, not retroactively, unless the change was never reported at all.
