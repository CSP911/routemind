# Claiming a conditional nontaxable allowance

Childcare, research and own-car allowances only start paying tax free once payroll has evidence the condition is met — the meal allowance is the exception, since it has no supporting document to file.

## What to submit

| Allowance | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Childcare | Child's birth certificate or family register showing age six or under |
| Research | Confirmation from HR that the role is a qualifying position |
| Own-car | Vehicle registration in the employee's name, plus confirmation no mileage claim runs alongside |

**Submitting the form doesn't backdate the exemption to when the condition first became true** — it starts from the payslip cycle the form is processed in, not from the qualifying event. An employee whose child was born in March but who files the childcare form in June only gets the allowance exempt from June's payslip onward; March to May isn't corrected retroactively unless flagged before those payslips issued.

## Renewals

Research and own-car conditions aren't re-checked automatically at this stage — see the review procedure for how payroll catches a lapsed condition after the form has already been approved once.
