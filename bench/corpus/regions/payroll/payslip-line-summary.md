# Payment and deduction lines at a glance

## Payment side

| Line | Note |
|---|---|
| Basic pay | Set by contract or band; not the same as gross |
| Overtime / night / holiday premiums | Multiplier on the hourly basic pay rate |
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW, nontaxable |
| Own-car allowance | 200,000 KRW, nontaxable |
| Childcare allowance | 200,000 KRW, nontaxable |
| Research allowance | 200,000 KRW, nontaxable, qualifying roles only |

## Deduction side

| Line | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | 4.5% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Health insurance | 3.545% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Long-term care | 12.95% of the health premium | The health insurance line, not pay |
| Employment insurance | 0.9% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Income tax | Simplified tax table | Pay band and declared dependents |
| Local income tax | 10% of income tax withheld | Follows the income tax line |

## Where people get caught

**The four major insurance lines are never calculated on gross pay.** Gross includes the nontaxable allowances; the insurance base does not. Two employees with identical gross pay can carry different deduction totals if their allowance mix differs — a heavier meal or research allowance shrinks the base those five lines are drawn from, even though the top-line figure looks the same.

How the income tax line itself is set, and why a lower monthly rate doesn't lower what's owed for the year, is covered under payslip-withholding-tax.
