# Meal allowance when meals in kind are also given

The 200,000 KRW meal allowance is only tax free where meals in kind — a cafeteria, catering, delivered lunches — are not also provided. That's a condition, not a cap.

## Cap and condition

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Not paid alongside meals in kind |

## What breaks it

A team moves into an office with a subsidised cafeteria, and payroll keeps paying the standing 180,000 KRW meal allowance on top of it. The line is under cap, so it looks fine on a monthly report.

**It isn't a cap problem, so the partial-taxation rule doesn't apply — once meals in kind are also being provided, the entire meal allowance becomes taxable, not just the amount over 200,000 KRW.** Being under cap doesn't rescue a condition that's already failed.

Payroll picks this up the same way it checks any standing allowance's condition: on review, not by watching the monthly figure.
