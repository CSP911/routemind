# A promotion that lands mid-year

An employee is promoted from band B2 to band B3, effective 1 June, with basic pay rising from 4,200,000 KRW to 5,300,000 KRW a month.

## What changes and when

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Band | B2 | B3 |
| Monthly basic pay | 4,200,000 KRW | 5,300,000 KRW |
| Effective payslip | May | June |

The new figure appears in full on the June payslip. There is no partial June figure and no backdating into May.

## The trap

**A mid-year promotion is not retroactive the way the annual review is.** The annual review's effective date is fixed to 1 January and often lands as a lump-sum catch-up; a promotion's effective date is whatever the signed promotion form states, and pay simply starts from the next payslip that follows signoff. Waiting for a delayed signoff means a delayed pay change, not a backdated one.

Payroll only acts once the promotion form has been signed off — see payslip-change-signoff for who has to confirm it first.
