# Deduction lines on a payslip

## The standard lines

| Line | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | 4.5% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Health insurance | 3.545% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Long-term care | 12.95% of the health premium | Calculated on the health insurance line, not on pay directly |
| Employment insurance | 0.9% | Pay excluding nontaxable allowances |
| Income tax | Simplified tax table | By pay band and number of dependents |
| Local income tax | 10% of income tax withheld | Follows the income tax line automatically |

## Each line has its own base

**No single deduction is a flat percentage of gross pay** — four of the six lines exclude nontaxable allowances from their base, and long-term care is a percentage of another deduction rather than of pay at all. Reconciling a deduction by multiplying gross pay by its stated rate will not match the line on the payslip.
