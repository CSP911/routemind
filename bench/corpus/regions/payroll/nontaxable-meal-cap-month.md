# A month over the meal cap

An employee normally draws the standard 200,000 KRW meal allowance each month, tax free, on the condition that meals in kind aren't also provided. In March, a one-off catering arrangement pushed the line to 260,000 KRW.

## What gets taxed

| Meal allowance paid | Cap | Amount taxed |
|---|---|---|
| 260,000 KRW | 200,000 KRW | 60,000 KRW |

**The whole line does not become taxable just because it crossed the cap** — only the 60,000 KRW above it joins the taxable base for that month. The 200,000 KRW under the cap keeps its exempt status. This is the same treatment as any other capped allowance running over: the exemption survives up to the cap, and only the excess joins ordinary pay for withholding and social insurance purposes.

## What resets the next month

Once the line returns to 200,000 KRW, the allowance is exempt in full again — March's overage doesn't carry forward or get clawed back later at year-end settlement.
