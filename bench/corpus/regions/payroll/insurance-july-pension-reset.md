# The July pension contribution reset

National pension contributions are calculated on a "standard monthly income" figure rather than raw monthly pay. That figure is refixed every July, using the average monthly income reported for the prior year, and stays fixed for twelve months — July through the following June.

## The cycle

| Period | Base used |
|---|---|
| July – following June | Prior year's average reported monthly income |
| Reset date | July payslip |
| Next reset | The following July |

## The trap

**The new base takes effect on the July payslip itself — it is not applied retroactively to earlier months in the year**, even though the figure it's built from is a full prior-year average. An employee who got a large raise in March will not see their pension contribution reflect it until July, unless the raise triggered an interim reported pay change.

That interim path — reporting a pay change outside the July cycle — is covered next.
