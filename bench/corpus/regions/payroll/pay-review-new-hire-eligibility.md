# When a new hire enters the review cycle

New hires are not reviewed on a schedule tied to their own start date. They enter the same fixed annual cycle as everyone else.

## The cutoff

| Join date | First eligible review |
|---|---|
| On or before 1 October | Following January |
| After 1 October | January of the year after that |

## The trap

**Joining just after the cutoff means waiting almost a full extra year, not a prorated few months.** An employee who joins on 15 October is treated the same as one who joins in December — both wait for the January that falls more than a year after their start date, with no partial or early review to bridge the gap.

This cutoff is separate from probation or performance timelines; it only governs eligibility for the annual pay review described in pay-review-cycle.
