# The year-end snapshot for dependant status

Once a dependant clears the relationship and income tests, when exactly they qualify still needs a fixed date — and it is not the date something changed.

## The test date

Age and income are both assessed as of 31 December of the tax year. A child who turns 20 in March is treated as 20 for the entire year, not as a qualifying dependant for the first two months and a non-dependant after. There is no partial-year credit.

## What this means in practice

| Event during the year | Result at year-end |
|---|---|
| Child turns 20 before 31 Dec | Not a dependant for any month of that year |
| Child turns 19 before 31 Dec | Dependant for the full year |
| Dependant's income rises above the threshold mid-year | Not a dependant for any month of that year |

**This is the opposite of how the childcare allowance works.** The nontaxable childcare allowance drops out the month a child turns 7 — a mid-year cutoff. The year-end dependant test has no mid-year cutoff at all; it looks only at the single snapshot on 31 December.

Two family members claiming the same dependant despite passing this test separately is a different problem, covered under the double-claim rule.
