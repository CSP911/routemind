# How a settlement correction reaches the payslip

| Situation | When it's resolved | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| Missed the submission cutoff | Comprehensive filing in May | Direct with the NTS, not through payroll |
| Amended before the settlement run | Same settlement cycle | Reprocessed into that month's refund figure |
| Amended after the settlement run | Next payslip | Adjustment to withholding, as with any correction |
| Over-claim self-reported | Next available payslip | Repayment deducted from net pay |
| Over-claim found by the NTS | After the NTS review closes | Repayment plus penalty interest, deducted from net pay |

## The trap
**Every repayment comes off net pay, not out of a separate refund pool.** A large enough over-claim can take more than one payslip to recover in full, the same way a large under-withholding does — payroll doesn't hold a pot to draw from, it just adjusts what's paid out each month until the balance is clear.

The same next-payslip mechanism handles ordinary payslip errors, not just settlement corrections.
