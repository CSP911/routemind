# Income tax withholding on a payslip

## How the monthly figure is set

Monthly income tax withheld is read off the simplified tax table by pay band and number of declared dependents. Employees may ask payroll to apply the table at 80%, 100%, or 120% of the standard figure.

| Option | Effect on the monthly line |
|---|---|
| 80% | Lower monthly withholding |
| 100% | Standard withholding |
| 120% | Higher monthly withholding |

## The trap

**Choosing 80% does not lower the tax owed for the year — it only shifts when it's collected.** Less is withheld each month, and the shortfall is settled, with the rest owed, at the year-end settlement instead. Choosing 120% works the other way: more withheld monthly, a smaller balance or a refund at settlement. Neither option changes the total; both change only the size of the year-end bill.

How that year-end bill is actually calculated is covered in the settlement steps.
