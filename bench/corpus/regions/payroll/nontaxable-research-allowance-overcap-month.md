# Research allowance over cap in a month

The research allowance is capped the same way as the other three: 200,000 KRW a month, tax free, and only for staff in a qualifying role.

## The cap

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Research allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Qualifying roles only |

## What a breach actually does

A lab technician's research allowance line comes to 260,000 KRW one month — a project bonus folded into the same line by mistake. **Only the 60,000 KRW above the cap is taxed; the 200,000 KRW underneath it stays exempt.** The line is not thrown out wholesale just because it's over.

| Line | Paid | Taxable portion |
|---|---|---|
| Research allowance | 260,000 KRW | 60,000 KRW |

This is a cap breach, not a condition failure — the role still qualifies, so the mechanism is the same partial taxation used for meals and own-car use, not the full write-off that follows a role that stops qualifying at all.

Whether the role itself still qualifies is a separate check, run on its own schedule and covered next.
