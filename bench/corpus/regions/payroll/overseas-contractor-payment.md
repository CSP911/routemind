# Paying an overseas contractor

A contractor based outside Korea providing services to the company is paid under a different default rate than the 3.3% domestic freelancer rate, because Korean tax law treats the payment as Korean-source income earned by a non-resident.

## The default rate

| Line | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax withheld | 20% | Invoice amount |
| Local income tax | 10% of income tax withheld (2%) | Same base |
| Total withheld | 22% | Invoice amount |

## The treaty exception

If the contractor's country of residence has a tax treaty with Korea, a reduced rate — sometimes 0% — can apply instead. **The reduced rate is never automatic.** Payroll can only apply it if the contractor supplies a certificate of tax residence from their own tax authority before the payment is made. Without it on file, payroll withholds the full 22% and the contractor has to claim any refund themselves, directly with the Korean tax office — payroll cannot process a refund after the fact.

Whichever rate applies, the invoice still has to carry the details set out in the next document before payroll can act on it.
