# The cost of an over-claim depends on who finds it

When a deduction that shouldn't have been claimed is discovered, what it costs depends entirely on who caught it and when.

## What each path costs

| Who finds it | Additional charge |
|---|---|
| Employee self-corrects within the amendment window | Tax shortfall only, no penalty |
| Employee reports it after settlement has already paid out | Tax shortfall plus 10% |
| Found by an NTS review or audit | Tax shortfall plus up to 40%, plus daily interest |

**The percentage is applied to the tax difference the over-claim caused, not to the deduction amount itself.** A wrongly claimed 500,000 KRW childcare allowance does not cost 10% of 500,000 KRW — it costs 10% of the tax that allowance should never have reduced, which is usually a much smaller number.

Where each of these amounts is actually collected — payslip deduction, direct payment, or offset against a later refund — is covered in the recovery routing guide.
