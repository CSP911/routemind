# A mid-month join, worked through

## The scenario
An employee joins on the 15th of a 30-day month, on a basic pay of 3,000,000 KRW and the standard 200,000 KRW meal allowance.

## What gets prorated
| Line | Full month | Days worked | Prorated amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | 3,000,000 KRW | 16/30 | 1,600,000 KRW |
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW | 16/30 | 106,667 KRW |

**The nontaxable allowance is prorated the same way basic pay is — it does not survive at full value just because it's a fixed monthly figure.** Someone expecting the full 200,000 KRW meal allowance in a partial first month will be short by the days not worked.

## What isn't prorated
Insurance rates — 4.5% pension, 3.545% health, 0.9% employment — apply to whatever the prorated pay comes to. They are percentages of pay, not fixed lines, so there is nothing separate to prorate on the deduction side.

The same math runs in reverse on a leaver's final payslip.
