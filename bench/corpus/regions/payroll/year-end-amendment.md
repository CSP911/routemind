# Amending a claim after it's submitted

## Before the settlement run
1. Contact the payroll desk with the correction and the supporting document.
2. Payroll replaces the submitted figure with the corrected one.
3. The settlement runs on the corrected claim — no separate cycle is needed.

## After the settlement run
Once the settlement has already been processed, the fix follows the same route as any other payslip correction: it lands as an adjustment on the next month's withholding rather than reopening the settlement itself.

## The trap
**An amendment after the run doesn't produce a fresh refund line.** It's folded into the next payslip's tax figures the same way an ordinary over- or under-payment is fixed — the employee sees the correction, not a second settlement.

Not every late change is a missed deduction — some are over-claims that only surface after the refund has already been paid.
