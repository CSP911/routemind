# Payslip lines

## Payments
| Line | What it is |
|---|---|
| Base pay | Annual salary ÷ 12 |
| Meal allowance | 200,000 KRW a month, tax free |
| Own-car allowance | 200,000 KRW a month, tax free (own vehicle, used for work) |
| Overtime | Only hours approved in advance |
| Trip allowance | For the days attendance confirms |

## Deductions
| Line | What it is |
|---|---|
| Pension, health, long-term care, employment insurance | See the social insurance rates |
| Income tax and local income tax | On the simplified table, settled at year end |
| Other | Unexplained card spending, advances, union dues |

- **A tax-free line over its cap is taxed on the excess only** → Tax-free allowances.
- The month someone joins or leaves is prorated by days: base pay ÷ days in the month × days employed.
