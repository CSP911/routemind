# Moving within a band versus moving to a new band

Basic pay can change for two different reasons, and only one of them is a promotion.

## Within-band movement

An annual review can raise basic pay anywhere inside the current band. This is a merit increase — the job grade, band, and title are unchanged.

## Across-band movement

A promotion moves the employee into a new band, with a new range. It carries its own effective date and its own signoff, separate from the review cycle.

## The trap

**Reaching the ceiling of a band does not automatically push someone into the next one.** An employee already at the top of their band who receives another strong review result does not get moved to the next band by default — the review can only hold them at the ceiling or leave them unchanged until a promotion is separately approved.

This is why a flat outcome at review time is not always a sign of a weak result — it can mean the band itself has run out of room. See pay-band-structure for the ranges each band covers.
