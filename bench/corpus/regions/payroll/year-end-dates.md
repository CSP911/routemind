# Year-end settlement deadlines

| | When |
|---|---|
| Pre-filled data opens | 15 January |
| Company cut-off | **31 January** |
| Additions and corrections | Until 10 February (nothing after) |
| Reflected in | February's pay (the 25th) |

## If you miss it
- Miss the company cut-off and you file it yourself in **May, with your income tax return**. The company
  cannot do it for you.
- If you find a deduction you left out after filing, **an amended claim** recovers it for up to five years.
- If you find you over-claimed, correct it yourself — a clawback carries a penalty.

The cut-off is tied to the withholding filing calendar, not to company convenience. It is not extended.
