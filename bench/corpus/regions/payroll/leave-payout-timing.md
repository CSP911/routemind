# Leave payout timing

Leave payout and severance are often calculated for the same leaver in the same week, but they don't run on the same clock.

## At leaving

Leave payout is added to the final payslip and follows the payslip's own issue schedule, not a fixed statutory countdown.

## While still employed

Where the company operates a use-it-or-lose-it leave year, unused days are costed and paid on the first payslip after the leave year closes, alongside the balance reset.

**The 14-day settlement window belongs to severance, not to leave payout.** If final payslip processing slips past severance's own deadline for any reason, severance still has to clear within its 14 days on its own separate track — leave payout has no matching statutory deadline forcing it to keep pace.

Leave payout is one way days become money for someone still employed. Unpaid leave works the same conversion in reverse.
