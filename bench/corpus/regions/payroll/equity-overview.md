# Stock and long-term incentives

## What a grant is
A grant is a promise, not a payment — it does not touch payroll until part of it vests. Restricted stock units (RSUs), stock options and performance share units are the three award types the company uses.

## The two payslip-relevant events
Payroll only acts twice in an award's life: when it vests (RSU, performance shares) or when it's exercised (options). Everything before that — the grant date, the vesting schedule ticking forward — is HR and the plan administrator's record, not payroll's.

## Leaving before the end of the schedule
Unvested units are forfeited the day active service ends. **A grant vesting is not the same as a grant paying out** — an employee can see units marked "vested" in the plan portal and still see no payslip line until the underlying shares are actually issued or the option exercised.

The grant types, the withholding on the vesting event itself, and what a resignation does to the unvested balance are each covered separately below.
