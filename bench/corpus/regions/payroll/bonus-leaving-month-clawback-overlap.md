# A signing bonus clawback in the leaving month

An employee resigns before the minimum-service date attached to their signing bonus. The clawback, scaled down for time already served, falls due in the same month their final payslip is issued.

## Order of deduction

| Line | Comes off | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Final basic pay and remaining allowances | Final payslip | Prorated by the usual mid-month rules |
| Signing bonus clawback | Final payslip, up to net pay | Deducted before the balance is released |
| Any shortfall | Separate recovery | Invoiced to the employee directly |

**The clawback cannot pull the final payslip into negative pay.** If the amount owed is larger than what's left after normal deductions, payroll takes only what the net pay covers. The remainder does not roll forward, and it does not get taken from severance — it is collected as a separate invoice outside the payslip entirely.

Severance itself is untouched by any of this: it is calculated and paid under its own schedule, not as a line on the leaving payslip.
