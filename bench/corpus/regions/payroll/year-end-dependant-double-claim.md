# When two people claim the same dependant

## What happens
The NTS cross-checks dependant claims by resident registration number across all filings. A parent listed by two siblings, or a child listed by both parents where only one household should claim them, gets flagged automatically.

## The outcome
Both claims are rejected until the family agrees who keeps the deduction and resubmits. Nothing is awarded by default to whoever filed first — the system doesn't decide this, the family does, and payroll can't resolve it on the employee's behalf.

## The trap
**Assuming the earlier filing wins is the trap.** Filing first doesn't secure the deduction; it just means that employee's settlement is held up alongside the other's until the duplicate is withdrawn from one side. Sorting this out ahead of submission avoids both settlements stalling at once.

A separate kind of timing problem is missing the submission cutoff altogether.
