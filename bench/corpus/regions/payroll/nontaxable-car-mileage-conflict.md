# Own-car allowance and a mileage claim

The 200,000 KRW own-car allowance is tax free only when the vehicle is the employee's own and it's used for work — and not alongside a separate mileage claim for the same use.

## The condition, not the cap

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Own-car allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Own vehicle, used for work; not paid alongside a mileage claim |

**Running both isn't a cap problem — it fails the condition outright.** An employee who receives the standing 200,000 KRW allowance and also submits mileage claims for the same trips doesn't get taxed on an excess; the exemption itself stops applying, because the condition that let it through — no parallel mileage claim — no longer holds. Payroll treats the whole 200,000 KRW as ordinary taxable pay for any month a mileage claim lands.

## What to do instead

Choose one route per vehicle use: the standing allowance, or itemised mileage. Mixing them inside the same month is the trap; running them in different months for genuinely different purposes is not.

The childcare allowance carries a similar all-or-nothing condition, tied to the child's age rather than to a parallel claim.
