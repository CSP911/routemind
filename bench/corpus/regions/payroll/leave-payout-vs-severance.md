# Leave payout against severance, side by side

A leaver can receive both an unused-leave payout and severance in the same week, calculated on similar wage figures but taxed and timed on entirely separate tracks.

| | Leave payout | Severance |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Unused leave days at leaving or year-end settlement | One year or more of service, ending employment |
| Wage basis | Average wage | Average wage |
| Where it appears | On the leaving payslip, as ordinary income | Never on the leaving payslip — paid separately |
| Tax treatment | Ordinary salary withholding table | Retirement income schedule, its own settlement |
| Timing | Follows payslip issue schedule | Within 14 days of the last day, unless extended by agreement |

**Leave payout and severance never merge even though both convert unused days into a final figure.** Adding them together on one line, or assuming severance's 14-day deadline governs the leave payout too, produces a leaving payslip that doesn't match either figure payroll actually owes.
