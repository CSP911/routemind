# Who counts as a dependant for the settlement

## The tests
A dependant has to pass both a relationship test and an income test.

| Relationship | Age condition |
|---|---|
| Spouse | No age condition |
| Children | 20 or under |
| Parents (including spouse's) | 60 or over |

Each one also has to clear the income test: total annual income of 1,000,000 KRW or less, or earned income alone of 5,000,000 KRW or less if they have no other income.

## The trap
**A dependant who earns even slightly over the threshold does not qualify at a reduced rate — the exemption is lost entirely.** This is the opposite of how the capped allowances behave, where going over the cap only taxes the excess. Here there's no partial credit: one won over the line and the whole deduction for that dependant disappears.

Where two family members both try to claim the same dependant is a separate problem, covered next.
