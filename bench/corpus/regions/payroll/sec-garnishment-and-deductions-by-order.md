# Garnishment and deductions by order

Court-ordered deductions are mandates requiring a portion of an employee's wages to be withheld for debt repayment. Payroll processes these orders considering legal obligations while protecting a part of the employee’s income, defined as the protected portion, from garnishment. Employees must understand how these deductions appear on the payslip and that the protected amount can differ based on legislation. **A mistake here is assuming all deductions are negotiable, leading to potential non-compliance with court rules.** Managing such financial deductions is crucial to ensure both compliance and take-home pay resilience, with detailed procedures available on related pages.
