# The basic pay line

## What it is
The basic pay line is the fixed monthly salary agreed in the contract, before any allowance, overtime premium or deduction is applied. It is the reference figure every multiplier line — overtime, night work, holiday work — is calculated from.

## How it's set
Basic pay is set at hiring and only changes with a contract amendment: a promotion, a raise, or a role change. It does not move month to month on its own. If the figure on the payslip changes without paperwork behind it, that's a correction, not a raise.

## What it is not
**Basic pay is not gross pay.** Gross pay adds every payment line — basic pay, allowances, overtime — together. Basic pay is only the base of that stack, and it's the base the hourly overtime rate is derived from, not the allowances sitting next to it.

## Deriving the hourly rate
| Component | Formula |
|---|---|
| Hourly basic rate | Monthly basic pay ÷ standard monthly hours |
| Overtime pay | Hourly basic rate × 1.5 |

The multipliers themselves, and when each applies, are covered separately.
