# Unvested awards on leaving

## The rule
Active service must continue through a vesting date for that tranche to vest. Resignation, termination, or non-renewal all stop the schedule on the last active day; every tranche still scheduled after that day is forfeited outright.

## What is not affected
Shares that already vested before the last day are the employee's regardless of how the employment ends — forfeiture only ever applies to what hasn't vested yet.

**A notice period does not extend the schedule.** An employee serving out a 30-day notice does not pick up a vesting date that falls on day 35 of that notice just because they were still technically employed when the grant was issued — the forfeiture is fixed to the actual last active day on payroll's record, not the day the resignation was submitted.

Retirement and redundancy are not treated as exceptions to this by default; any accelerated vesting on those exits is a plan-document term, confirmed by HR before payroll processes it, never assumed.

Vested options don't disappear on leaving, but they don't stay open indefinitely either — the exercise window is fixed separately.
