# When the monthly rent deduction fails its condition

The monthly rent deduction (월세 세액공제) is one of the manual claims payroll sees most often, and one of the most commonly rejected.

## The condition

The employee must not own a home — not the one they're renting, and not any other property anywhere in the country. Renting a place near the office while a home is owned elsewhere, even if unoccupied, fails the test.

## What trips it

- A home inherited but not yet sold
- A spouse's property, since the household is assessed together
- A jeonse deposit on a separate property held as an investment

**Owning a home anywhere in the country disqualifies the claim, regardless of why the employee is renting somewhere else.** This is the single most common reason a rent deduction claim comes back rejected after the fact rather than caught up front — the pre-fill has no way to see property ownership.

Once a rejected rent claim has already gone through, it's corrected the same way as any other amendment.
