# Childcare allowance against a daycare receipt

The childcare allowance is a standing payment, up to 200,000 KRW a month, for an employee with a child aged 6 or under. It's paid regardless of what the daycare actually charges that month.

## Cap and condition

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Childcare allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Child aged 6 or under |

## Where the double count happens

An employee also submits the daycare's monthly invoice as an expense claim, on top of drawing the standing allowance. **The allowance already covers the cost up to 200,000 KRW; expensing the same invoice claims it a second time, and payroll can't tell it's the same cost unless the receipt is checked against the standing line.**

This runs the same way the daily travel allowance conflicts with expensing the same trip's receipt — a standing tax-free payment and a reimbursement claim can't both stand on the same underlying cost, whichever allowance is involved.
