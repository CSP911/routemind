# Standard vesting schedule

## The default schedule
Grants vest over four years: a one-year cliff, then the remaining balance in equal quarterly instalments over the following three years. Nothing vests before the cliff, regardless of how long the grant has been held.

| Point in schedule | What vests |
|---|---|
| Before month 12 | Nothing |
| Month 12 (cliff) | 25% of the grant |
| Each quarter after, months 15–48 | 6.25% of the grant |

## What counts as a vesting date
A vesting date is fixed at grant and does not move for weekends, holidays, or leave of absence taken during the year — unpaid leave delays insurance and pay, but it does not delay a vesting date already set in the award agreement.

**A partial year of service does not prorate a vesting tranche.** An employee who leaves one week before the month-12 cliff receives none of that 25% — there is no pro-rata credit for the eleven months already served, unlike leave payout or severance which do prorate by days worked.

What that forfeiture actually removes from the employee's holding is covered under leaving mid-cycle.
