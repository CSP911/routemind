# What a reimbursement claim needs before payroll pays it tax free

## The minimum record

Every reimbursement claim needs, attached to the request:

- the original receipt or invoice, not a card statement summary
- the date of the expense
- the amount actually paid
- a stated business purpose
- the requester's manager sign-off

A claim missing any one of these is not a valid reimbursement — payroll has no basis to certify it as a repaid business cost rather than income.

## What happens without it

**The claim is not rejected — it is paid as taxable income instead.** Payroll still processes the amount, but without evidence it cannot be certified as a genuine cost repayment, so it is added to gross pay and taxed like any other payment line, with the usual insurance deductions following it.

This is different from a standing allowance, where the condition is checked once and the monthly figure then runs untouched — a reimbursement is re-evidenced every single time.
