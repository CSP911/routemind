# The April health insurance settlement

Health insurance is billed monthly through the year on an estimate. Each April, that estimate is reconciled against the employee's actual confirmed income for the prior year, once the tax filing data is available. The difference — a top-up or a refund — is settled through the April payroll run.

## What moves

| Line | Affected by the April settlement |
|---|---|
| Health insurance (3.545%) | Yes — recalculated against confirmed prior-year income |
| Long-term care | Yes — it is 12.95% of the health premium, so it moves with it |
| National pension (4.5%) | No |
| Employment insurance | No |

## The trap

**The April settlement adjusts health insurance only — it does not touch national pension.** Pension runs on its own annual cycle, reset every July rather than April, off a different income base. An employee who sees a health top-up in April and expects a matching pension adjustment the same month will not see one; that comes three months later.
