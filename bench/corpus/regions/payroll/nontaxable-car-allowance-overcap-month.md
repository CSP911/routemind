# A month the own-car allowance runs over cap

## The scenario
An employee who drives their own vehicle for work claims 240,000 KRW of own-car allowance in a month with heavier client travel. The standing cap is 200,000 KRW a month.

## What gets taxed

| Allowance paid | Cap | Taxable portion |
|---|---|---|
| 240,000 KRW | 200,000 KRW | 40,000 KRW |

**Only the 40,000 KRW above the cap is taxed — the whole 240,000 KRW does not lose its tax-free status just because one month ran over.** The exemption survives up to the cap; only the excess is added to that month's taxable pay.

## Why the cap doesn't move with need
A heavier travel month doesn't raise the ceiling. If work mileage genuinely justifies more than 200,000 KRW, that calls for a mileage claim on top of actual trips — and the own-car allowance and a mileage claim for the same vehicle can't run together in the same month.

Running over the cap is the easy failure to spot on a payslip. Losing the condition behind the allowance entirely is harder to catch, and it taxes the whole line, not just the overrun.
