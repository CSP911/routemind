# Severance payment timing

Severance pay is due within 14 days of the employee's last working day, regardless of which pension type applies.

## The window

| Situation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Standard leaving | 14 days from last working day |
| Extension | Only by written agreement between employer and employee, for a specific new date |

There is no automatic extension for payroll processing delays, missing bank details, or an unfinished handover — none of these push the deadline on their own.

## What actually gets paid

Under DB, the company pays the calculated lump sum directly. Under DC, the "payment" is the final contribution being credited and the account becoming available to the employee to draw down or roll over — the company's obligation ends at that credit, not at withdrawal.

**The 14-day clock runs from the last working day, not from the date the final payslip is issued or the date the resignation was accepted.** Those two dates are often earlier, and using them instead understates how much time has actually passed.

Missing this window without a written extension is a compliance breach, independent of whatever the tax treatment on the payout turns out to be — covered next.
