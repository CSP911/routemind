# Overtime premiums moved to attendance

From **2026-01-01**, overtime premiums are set by the table in **attendance**
(`sec-hard-overtime`), not by anything in payroll.

| still here | id | status |
|---|---|---|
| Overtime, night and holiday premiums | `payslip-overtime` | the **oldest** rule, correct only before 2024-07-01 |

The pages above were not withdrawn and nothing in them says they were replaced. **They are the
oldest version and they stopped being correct at 2024-07-01** — eighteen months before this move. There
are three versions in all:

| in force | where |
|---|---|
| until 2024-07-01 | the pages above, here in payroll |
| 2024-07-01 to 2025-12-31 | `hard-overtime-v2`, in attendance |
| from 2026-01-01 | `sec-hard-overtime`, in attendance |

So a question dated anywhere after 2024-07-01 is answered in attendance, not here — including dates before
2026-01-01, which is the case this note used to get wrong. `hard-overtime-legend-revision` in
attendance is the full version table.
