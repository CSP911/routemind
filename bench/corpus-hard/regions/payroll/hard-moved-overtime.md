# Overtime premiums moved to attendance

From **2026-01-01**, overtime premiums are set by the table in **attendance**
(`sec-hard-overtime`), not by anything in payroll.

| still here | id | status |
|---|---|---|
| Overtime, night and holiday premiums | `payslip-overtime` | the old rule, correct only before 2026-01-01 |

The pages above were not withdrawn and nothing in them says they were replaced. They remain correct
for anything dated before 2026-01-01 and for nothing after it. If the question is about a date on or
after 2026-01-01, leave payroll and open `sec-hard-overtime` in attendance.
