# Expenses

What the corporate card may be used for, how much a trip pays, and what has to be attached for any of
it to be accepted. This area is about **what the company pays for and on what evidence** — how many
days a trip counted is Attendance, and whether an allowance is taxed is Payroll.

## Three numbers decide most questions
- **30,000 KRW.** Above it, qualifying evidence is required. Below it a simple receipt is fine.
- **Your per-transaction card cap.** It differs by grade, and splitting a payment to get under it is
  treated as exceeding it.
- **The daily allowance.** Paid without receipts, but only for days that attendance confirms.

## Card or cash — the distinction that matters
**A card slip is qualifying evidence.** Losing the paper receipt for something paid by card costs you
nothing. Almost every rejected claim traces back to cash: a cash receipt taken against a mobile number
instead of the company registration number, or a cash spend over 30,000 KRW with only a simple
receipt. If there is a choice, pay by card.

## Approval after the fact
There is almost none. Spending over a cap is accepted where it was approved **in advance**; the same
spend explained afterwards is not. The one place this bends is a genuine emergency, and that goes
through the office & purchasing desk on ext. 2210 the same day, not at month end.
