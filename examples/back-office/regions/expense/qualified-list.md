# Qualifying evidence

## What qualifies
- A tax invoice or an invoice
- A credit card slip (corporate or personal)
- A cash receipt — **issued for business expense, not for personal deduction**

## What does not
A simple receipt, a handwritten receipt, a statement of account, a deposit slip, a bank transfer record.

## By amount
| Amount | What is needed |
|---|---|
| Up to 30,000 KRW | A simple receipt is fine |
| Over 30,000 KRW | **Qualifying evidence required.** Without it, a 2% penalty applies |
| Entertainment over 30,000 KRW | Qualifying evidence plus the attendee list |
| Condolence or congratulation up to 200,000 KRW | The invitation or notice will do |

When taking a cash receipt, **it has to be issued against the company registration number**. One taken
against a mobile number lands as a personal deduction and cannot be used as a company cost — this is
the single most common reason a claim is rejected.
