# Expenses — where to start

| What you are asking | Where it is |
|---|---|
| How much I may put on the card | Corporate card > Card limits |
| A meal or a gift for a client | Corporate card > Card limits > Entertainment |
| The card is lost | Corporate card > Lost card and misuse |
| How much a trip pays | Travel expenses > Domestic / Overseas |
| I have no receipt | Evidence > Qualifying evidence, and the lost-receipt case |

**Three boundaries decide almost everything here.**
- **30,000 KRW** — above it you need qualifying evidence.
- **The per-transaction cap** — it differs by grade, and splitting a payment does not get around it.
- **Card or cash** — a card slip *is* qualifying evidence, so losing the paper receipt does not matter.
  What causes trouble is always cash.

**What this area does not answer**: how many days count as a trip (Attendance & Leave), whether the
daily allowance is taxed (Payroll & Insurance), how to buy something (Purchasing & Contracts).
