# Corporate card

The card is the ordinary way to spend company money. It carries its own caps, its own blocked
categories, and its own procedure when it goes wrong — the limit table and the loss procedure are on
the pages below.

## What it is for
Work spending that is small enough not to need a purchase request: travel, supplies, client meals,
subscriptions already approved. Anything that needs a quote or a contract goes through Purchasing
instead, whatever the amount.

## What it is not for
- **Personal spending, even repaid later.** It is not a cash-flow convenience; a repaid personal charge
  is still an unexplained charge on the statement.
- **Anything in a blocked category.** Entertainment venues, duty free, gift vouchers, jewellery, online
  games. The authorisation is declined at the terminal, so this is not a rule you can accidentally
  break — but people do try to work around it, and that is what gets noticed.
- **Splitting a payment** to stay under the per-transaction cap. Two charges to the same merchant on
  the same day for one purchase are read as one.

## What the statement asks of you
Every charge needs a reason recorded against it. Weekend charges and anything between 22:00 and 06:00
need one explicitly — not because they are suspect, but because they are the ones that cannot be
guessed at later. **An unexplained charge is recovered from pay at month end.** That is not a penalty;
there is simply no other way to close the statement.

## If something has gone wrong
Stop the card first and ask questions second. The order matters, and it is on the loss page.
