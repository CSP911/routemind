# Vendor registration form

## Fields
| Field | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Business registration number | Must match the certificate exactly |
| Trading name / representative | The registered name, not the name on the shopfront |
| Business type and category | As written on the certificate |
| Payment terms | Month-end close, paid end of the following month, unless a reason is given |
| Bank account | **The account holder must match the registered name.** Otherwise a letter of authority is needed |
| Contact | Whoever issues the tax invoice |

## Filed with it
- Copy of the business registration certificate
- Copy of the bank book
- Corporate registry extract (companies only)
- Consent for national ID collection (sole traders and freelancers)

Submit to the office & purchasing desk. A mismatched account holder is the top reason for rejection.
