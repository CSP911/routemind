# Approval thresholds by amount

| Amount (net of VAT) | Approval | Quotes |
|---|---|---|
| Under 500,000 KRW | Team lead, delegated | 1 |
| 500,000 – 3,000,000 KRW | Division head, delegated | 2 |
| 3,000,000 – 10,000,000 KRW | CEO | 3 |
| 10,000,000 KRW and over | CEO, with a separate proposal | 3 plus a comparison |

- **Do not split to land in a lower band.** Orders to the same vendor for the same item within three
  months are added together.
- A sole quote with a written reason (sole supplier, continuity with an existing system) may drop one
  approval step.
- An annual agreement is banded on **the annual total**, not the monthly rate.

The authority itself is set by the delegation rules — this table is that authority applied to purchasing.
