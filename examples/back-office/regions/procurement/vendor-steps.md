# Vendor registration

1. Collect their papers — business registration certificate, bank details, and for a company, the
   corporate registry extract.
2. Fill in the vendor registration form → see the form.
3. Submit it to the office & purchasing desk. Registration takes **two working days**.
4. Only after registration can an order be placed or an invoice paid.

- Sole traders and freelancers are **subject to withholding**, so a consent form for their national ID
  is also needed.
- For an overseas supplier, collect the invoicing address and the SWIFT code. Registration takes five
  working days.
- A one-off purchase still needs registration. "It is only this once" is the most common cause of delay.
