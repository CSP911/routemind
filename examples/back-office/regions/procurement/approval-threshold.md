# Approval thresholds

How far up each amount band has to go, and how many quotes it needs. The table is on the page below.

## Two things at once
The band decides **who approves** and **how many quotes** together, which is why they are one table
rather than two. More money means both more authority and more comparison; the two move up together
and neither substitutes for the other. Three quotes do not let a team lead approve something in the
CEO's band.

## The band is on the total
- **Net of VAT.** A 480,000 KRW item plus VAT is in the lowest band.
- **Same vendor, same item, three months — added together.** This is what "do not split" means in
  practice, and it is checked against past orders rather than taken on trust.
- **An annual agreement bands on the annual total.** A 400,000 KRW monthly service is a 4,800,000 KRW
  decision, not a 400,000 KRW one. This is the band people most often get wrong, and it is usually
  honest rather than deliberate.

## Where the authority comes from
Not from here. The authority itself is set by the delegation rules in Documents & Approval; this table
is those rules applied to purchasing. That matters when the two seem to disagree — the delegation page
governs, and this table is the one that gets updated.

## The sole-quote route
A written reason, and it may drop one approval step. "Only they make it" and "it has to work with what
we already run" both qualify. "They were quickest to reply" does not. The reason is filed with the
request and is read later, so it is worth one honest sentence.
