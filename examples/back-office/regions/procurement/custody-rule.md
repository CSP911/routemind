# Contract custody

- Of the two signed originals, **one goes to the records room** held by the office desk. The team that
  signed it does not keep it.
- The scan goes into the groupware document store under `contracts/<year>/<vendor>`. The original is
  filed and the scan is uploaded **on the same day**.
- An electronic contract has no original — keep the PDF together with the signing certificate.
- How long it is kept follows the retention schedule → Document retention.
- Do not destroy it when the contract ends if retention still has time to run. **The retention period,
  not the end date, is what governs.**

Borrowing an original is logged with the records desk. Originals do not leave the building.
