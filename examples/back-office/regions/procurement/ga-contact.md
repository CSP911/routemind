# Office & purchasing desk

| | |
|---|---|
| Owns | Purchase requests, vendor registration, corporate cards, supplies, the office itself |
| Reach | Ext. 2210 / the shared "Office" groupware account |
| Cut-offs | Purchase requests close **Tuesday and Thursday morning**; same-day orders by 11:00 |

- The desk registers vendors and places orders. **Teams do not agree a price with a supplier directly**
  — coming to register one after the price is settled means starting again.
- For a lost card, stopping it with the issuer comes first and telling the desk comes second.
