# Approval chain

| Role | Who | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Drafter | You | Answerable for the content |
| Reviewer | Team lead | That the content is right |
| Approver | The delegated authority | The decision to go ahead |
| Consent | The team it affects | If they object, it does not proceed |
| Reader | People who should know | Cannot raise an objection |

## When consent is required
- Money going out → Corporate support (accounting)
- Anything about people → HR desk
- An outside contract → Office & purchasing desk
- Anything touching personal data → the data protection lead

- **A draft filed without a required consent does not get carried out, even once approved.** It is the
  most common reason something is refiled.
- The approving step is set by the delegation rules. Do not add a step out of habit — once the
  delegated authority has approved, it is done.
