# Draft document template

## Fields
| Field | How to write it |
|---|---|
| Title | `[Category] Subject — purpose`. E.g. `[Purchase] 10 monitors — new joiners` |
| Purpose | Two or three lines on why it is needed. Write the outcome, not the backstory |
| Authority | The clause or the number of the decision above it. **If there is none, write "new" rather than leaving it blank** |
| Amount | Always say whether it is net of or including VAT |
| Expected effect | Required above 3,000,000 KRW |

## Attachments
- Anything with an amount: the quotes (as many as the threshold table requires)
- A contract: the draft contract and the counterparty's registration certificate
- A spend: the receipt or qualifying evidence

A draft with no category tag in the title is sent back by the records desk — it cannot be found later.
