# Delegated authority

Not everything goes to the CEO. Most spending, most leave and most day-to-day direction is closed out
by a team lead or a division head, and the exact bands are in the scope table below this page.

## What delegation means here
An authority, not a preference. If a team lead may close it out, a team lead **closes it out** — the
draft does not pass through the division head on the way, and adding that step is as much a breach as
approving beyond your band would be. The reason is practical: every extra step is a day, and a chain
that nobody believes in is one people work around.

## Reading the bands
- **Amounts are net of VAT.** A 480,000 KRW order plus VAT is a team lead's, not a division head's.
- **A purchase may not be split** to land in a lower band. Orders to the same vendor for the same
  item inside three months are added together.
- **Subject beats amount.** A contract is signed by the CEO however small it is, because what is
  being committed is not only money.
- **An annual agreement is banded on the annual total**, not the monthly instalment.

## When the holder is away
Their standing deputy approves, at the same level. Leave and business trips are registered in the
groupware, so the deputy is already known — you do not need to ask.

If you are unsure which band something falls in, the records desk will tell you before you file, which
is much cheaper than finding out after it has been approved by the wrong person.
