# Records desk

The desk that owns the paperwork of the company itself: the draft templates, both seals, the
retention schedule and the records room. Its contact details and the seal procedure are on the page
below this one.

## What it decides for you
- **Whether a draft is filed correctly.** A draft with no category tag in its title comes back — not
  to be difficult, but because nobody will find it in two years.
- **Whether a document may be destroyed.** Retention is counted from the end of the business, and the
  desk runs the destruction list every January.
- **Who may take out a seal, and for what.** The corporate seal needs the CEO; the company seal is
  delegated to the division head.

## What it does not decide
The content of your draft, or whether the thing you are asking for is a good idea. That is the
approval chain's job. The desk checks that a document is findable, correctly authorised and kept for
as long as it has to be.

## When to come here first
- You are signing something with an outside party and need a seal.
- You have been asked for a document from years ago and cannot find it.
- A team is moving or closing and its records have to go somewhere.
- Something is under audit or litigation — tell the desk, because those documents must **not** be
  destroyed even when their retention has run out.
