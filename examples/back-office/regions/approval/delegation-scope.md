# Delegated authority

| Authority | Scope |
|---|---|
| Team lead | Spend under 500,000 KRW, leave under 3 days, direction within the team |
| Division head | Spend under 3,000,000 KRW, domestic trips, over-cap expenses, the company seal |
| CEO | Spend of 3,000,000 KRW and over, signing contracts, hiring and dismissal, the corporate seal, overseas trips |

## The principles
- Delegation is **an authority, not a preference.** Sending something upward that is delegated breaks
  the rule as much as approving beyond your authority does.
- When the holder is away, **their standing deputy** approves. It does not move up a level.
- Amounts are **net of VAT**, and a purchase cannot be split to land in a lower band.
- Something urgent may be done on a verbal approval and filed **as a draft within three days**. With no
  draft afterwards, it becomes a personal cost.
