# Records desk

| | |
|---|---|
| Owns | Draft templates, the company and corporate seals, retention, the records room |
| Reach | Ext. 2105 (Corporate support) |

## Getting a seal
1. File a draft — what it is for, who the counterparty is, how many copies.
2. The corporate seal needs **CEO approval**; the company seal is delegated to the division head.
3. A seal certificate is valid for three months from issue. Do not take them out in advance.

Destroying a document always goes through the records desk. A document destroyed inside its retention
period cannot be recovered.
