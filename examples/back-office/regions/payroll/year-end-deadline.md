# Year-end deadlines

The company cut-off, when the money moves, and what happens if you miss it. The dates are in the table
below.

## Why the cut-off is where it is
It is tied to the withholding filing calendar the company has to meet, not to internal convenience.
That is why it does not move, and why asking for a few extra days does not work — the filing behind it
has a statutory date.

## Missing it is not a disaster
It is an inconvenience with a known route. **File it yourself in May with your income tax return.** You
get exactly the same deductions; what you lose is the company doing the arithmetic and the refund
arriving in February rather than in the summer. Nobody is penalised for missing the company cut-off.

## What cannot be fixed later, and what can
- **Can**: a deduction you forgot. An amended claim recovers it for up to five years.
- **Can**: an over-claim you noticed yourself. Correct it and there is no penalty.
- **Cannot**: an over-claim found by the tax office first. That carries one.
- **Cannot**: getting the company to reopen the settlement after the correction window. The filing has
  gone.

## The rhythm
Pre-filled data opens mid-January, the company cut-off is the end of January, corrections run into
the first days of February, and the result lands in February's pay on the 25th. If you are going to be
away in January, upload what you have before you go — a partial submission that is corrected in
February is much better than nothing submitted at all.
