# Tax-free allowances

| Allowance | Cap | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Meals | 200,000 KRW a month | Where meals in kind are not also provided |
| Own-car use | 200,000 KRW a month | Own vehicle, used for work. Not alongside a mileage claim |
| Trip daily allowance | **Reasonable actual cost** | Within the rates set in policy; the excess is taxed |
| Childcare allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Child aged 6 or under |
| Research allowance | 200,000 KRW a month | Qualifying roles only |

## Why the trip allowance keeps coming up
- The policy rates (20,000 KRW domestic, USD 50–80 overseas) are treated as **reasonable actual cost
  and so tax free**.
- But **an allowance for a day attendance has not confirmed is not tax free.** With no attendance
  behind it, it is simply pay, and taxed.
- Claiming a meal receipt as an expense on a day you also drew the allowance is **double counting**, and
  that amount is moved back into taxable pay.

Going over a cap does not cancel the exemption — **only the excess** is taxed.
