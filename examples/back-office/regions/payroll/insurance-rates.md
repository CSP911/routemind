# Social insurance

| Insurance | Employee | Employer |
|---|---|---|
| National pension | 4.5% | 4.5% |
| Health insurance | 3.545% | 3.545% |
| Long-term care | 12.95% of the health premium | Same |
| Employment insurance | 0.9% | 1.15%+ |
| Industrial accident | — | By industry rate |

## Why it suddenly changes
- **The April health insurance settlement**: recalculated on last year's actual pay, then collected or
  refunded through April's pay.
- The pension's standard monthly income is reset **every July**.
- A pay rise takes effect when it is reported, not from the month it was agreed.

## During a leave of absence
- Cover continues. **Health insurance may be deferred**, and the deferred amount is paid in instalments
  after returning.
- Pension contributions may be suspended (that period does not count toward the contribution record).
- The payroll desk files both — tell them as soon as the leave is confirmed.
