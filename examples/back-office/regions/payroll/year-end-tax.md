# Year-end tax settlement

Income tax is withheld each month on a simplified table, which is deliberately approximate. The
settlement in January reconciles a year of approximations against what you actually owed, and the
difference is refunded or collected in February's pay. The steps are on the page below and the dates
under that.

## What you actually have to do
Most of it is already there. The tax office's pre-filled service carries insurance, medical, card
spending, pension and most donations. **Your job is the things it cannot see**: rent, glasses, school
uniforms, some medical costs, overseas tuition, donations to smaller bodies.

## Dependants are where money is lost and clawed back
- A relative over the income or age limit cannot be claimed, and claiming one is recovered later with
  a penalty rather than simply rejected now.
- **Only one person may claim any given dependant.** Two parents claiming the same child, or siblings
  each claiming a parent, is the most common cause — and both claimants are clawed back, not just the
  second.
- Decide within the family before anyone files. Afterwards it is two corrections instead of one
  conversation.

## What is not income
Tax-free allowance lines were never taxable, so they are not part of the income the settlement works
from. Including them is a common self-inflicted error that makes the numbers look wrong.

## If it goes wrong afterwards
A deduction you missed can be recovered by an amended claim for up to five years. Something
over-claimed should be corrected by you — found by the tax office instead, it carries a penalty.
