# Payroll & Insurance

What each line on a payslip is, what is paid free of tax, how the social insurance deductions are
worked out, and what you have to do at year end. This area answers **what you are paid and what is
taken off** — how many days you worked is Attendance, and how much a trip's allowance is set at is
Expenses.

## Three dates, in order
1. **The 3rd of the following month** — attendance closes. Nothing is calculated before this.
2. **The 15th** — pay queries close. An item raised after it lands in the following month.
3. **The 25th** — pay day, or the preceding working day when that is a holiday.

The order is the useful part. If pay looks wrong, **compare the payslip against attendance first**,
because payroll read attendance and nothing else. Most "the amount is wrong" questions are an
attendance record that was never entered.

## The one rule that prevents most surprises
**A tax-free allowance over its cap is taxed on the excess only.** The exemption is not cancelled
wholesale — going 30,000 KRW over a 200,000 KRW cap taxes 30,000 KRW, not 230,000 KRW. The single
exception worth knowing is the trip allowance, which is tax free only for days attendance confirmed;
with no attendance behind it, it is ordinary pay and taxed in full.
