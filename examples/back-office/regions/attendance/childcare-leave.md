# Parental leave

Who may take it, how far ahead to arrange it, and what it does to pay, insurance and next year's
leave. The eligibility and the step-by-step are on the page below.

## What it is
Up to a year of unpaid leave to care for a child, in as many as two blocks, available to both parents
for the same child. It is an entitlement rather than a request, but the dates are agreed — the
thirty days' notice exists so a team can be covered, not so the leave can be refused.

## What it costs and what it does not
- **No pay from the company.** The benefit comes from employment insurance and **you claim it
  yourself** — the company confirms your employment, it does not file for you.
- **Social insurance continues.** Health insurance payment can usually be deferred for the period and
  settled on return; the payroll desk arranges this and it is worth asking before you go, not after.
- **Service continues to count.** Your length of service is unaffected.
- **Next year's leave is reduced** in proportion to days actually worked. This is the part that
  surprises people on their return, and it is not an error.

## Before you go
Hand over properly and tell the HR desk who is covering what. Most of the difficulty on return is not
about entitlement — it is that nobody recorded what was left mid-flight.

## Coming back
Confirm your return two weeks before. Not confirming is not treated as resigning, but placement takes
longer when nobody knew the date. If you want to return early or extend, that is a fresh agreement
with the HR desk, and the same thirty days applies to an extension.
