# Leave entitlement

Granted on a fiscal-year basis (1 January – 31 December).

| Service | Days granted |
|---|---|
| Under 1 year | 1 day per completed month (up to 11) |
| 1 year and over | 15 days |
| 3 years and over | 15 days, +1 every 2 years (capped at 25) |

- Days are granted **on 1 January**; in the first year they accrue monthly from the joining date.
- Unused days **do not carry over.** They lapse on 31 December and the lapsed balance is paid out.
- Someone who joined partway through the year gets a prorated first year → see the proration table.
