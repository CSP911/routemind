# Booking leave

Requesting, approving and cancelling. The exact deductions and time blocks are in the procedure page
below; this is when to do it and what happens if you do not.

## The ordinary case
File it in the attendance system, your team lead approves, and it appears in attendance. Three or more
consecutive days also need the division head. **Until it is approved it is not leave** — an absence
with a pending request in the system records as absence.

## Timing
- **Book ahead where you can.** There is no minimum notice for a single day, but cover is the team
  lead's problem and early notice is the difference between a routine approval and a conversation.
- **Same-morning leave** needs the team lead's verbal agreement first, then the system entry. The
  entry alone does not undo the absence record — somebody has to have known.
- **After the fact** is only for illness, accident or bereavement, with evidence, within three days of
  returning. Anything else filed late is not leave.

## Changing and cancelling
Cancel it yourself in the system at any time up to the start of the leave. Once it has started, only
the HR desk can, and they will ask what actually happened on the day. Half days cancelled after they
have begun are not restored.

## Things that quietly go wrong
- **Booking over a public holiday.** The system does not deduct it, but people plan around the wrong
  balance.
- **A quarter day taken twice in one day.** Only one is allowed; the second is rejected after the fact.
- **Leave in the last week of December.** It is deducted from the year that is ending, not the new
  one, and if the balance is short the request fails silently at the approval step.
