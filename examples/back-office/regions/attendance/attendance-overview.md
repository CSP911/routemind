# Attendance & Leave — where to start

| What you are asking | Where it is |
|---|---|
| How many days I have, how to take them | Annual leave → entitlement / booking |
| I joined mid-year, so how many | Annual leave > Entitlement > Mid-year joiner proration |
| Parental leave | Parental leave |
| Attendance for a business trip | Attendance on a business trip → Handover to settlement |
| Where do I enter it | Attendance system |
| The rules do not cover my situation | HR desk |

**What this area does not answer**: how much a trip pays (Expenses), what happens to insurance during
leave of absence (Payroll & Insurance). Attendance goes as far as *how many days count* — *how much is
paid* is somewhere else.

One thing to remember: **a record that is not in the system does not exist.** A verbal approval is not
attendance, and it does not reach the pay run either.
