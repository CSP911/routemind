# Attendance on a business trip

- A trip reaches attendance **only after the trip request is approved**. A trip taken without one
  leaves attendance blank, which records as absence.
- A trip day counts as **8 hours worked**. Travelling time is not added to it.
- A trip over a weekend or public holiday is not holiday working — it settles as **one day off in
  lieu**, and it has to be approved in advance.
- For an overseas trip the departure day and the arrival day each count as a day. Where time zones
  make the dates overlap, record them on local time.

Once attendance is confirmed the work moves to expenses → Handover from trip to settlement.
