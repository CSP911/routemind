# Booking and cancelling leave

1. Attendance system > Leave request — pick the type (full, half, quarter day) and the dates.
2. The chain is **the team lead alone**. Three or more consecutive days also goes to the division head.
3. Once approved it appears in attendance. Until then, the leave has not been taken.

| Type | Deducted | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Full day | 1 day | All day |
| Half day | 0.5 day | 09:00–14:00 or 14:00–18:00 |
| Quarter day | 0.25 day | In 2-hour blocks, once a day |

- **Cancel in the system yourself until the leave starts.** After it starts, the HR desk does it.
- A late request is accepted **only for illness, accident or bereavement**, filed with evidence within
  three days of returning.
- Leave booked the same morning needs the team lead's verbal agreement first — an approval in the
  system alone does not undo an absence record.
