# Parental leave

## Eligibility
- A child aged 8 or under (or in the second year of primary school or below), with 6 months of service.
- Up to a year, in up to two blocks. Both parents may take it.

## Applying
1. Agree the dates with the HR desk **at least 30 days before it starts**.
2. File a draft document — attach the family relation certificate and the parental leave form.
3. The chain is team lead → division head → HR desk.
4. Once confirmed, the HR desk explains the employment-insurance benefit claim (the company does not
   file it for you).

## While on leave
- No pay is paid. **Social insurance continues**, and health insurance payment may be deferred — check
  with the Payroll desk.
- The period counts as service for entitlement, but **that year's leave is reduced in proportion to
  days actually worked.**

## Coming back
- Confirm your return **two weeks before**. Not confirming is not read as resigning, but it delays
  where you are placed.
