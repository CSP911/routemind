# Attendance system

Groupware > Attendance. Everything in this area is entered, approved and stored here, and the menu map
is on the page below.

## What it is for
It is the record, not a convenience. Pay, leave balances, trip allowances and any later audit are all
read from this system and from nothing else. That is why so many of the rules in this area end with
"in the system" — a decision that lives only in a chat message has no effect on any of them.

## The rhythm of a month
- Entries are made as things happen — leave before it starts, trips before they start, overtime
  requested in advance.
- **The month closes on the 3rd of the following month.** Until then you can correct your own records.
- After the 3rd, corrections go through the HR desk, and they need a reason. The pay run has already
  read the month by then, so a late correction usually settles in the following month's pay.

## What it will not do for you
- It does not chase approvals. A request sitting unapproved is not leave, and nobody is told.
- It does not warn you that your balance is short until the approval step fails.
- It does not know about anything agreed verbally, including with your own team lead.

## If something is wrong in it
Fix it yourself before the 3rd. After that, message the HR desk with the dates and what should have
been recorded. Do **not** file a corrected entry on top of a wrong one — two records for one day is
harder to unpick than one wrong record, and it is the state that most often reaches payroll wrong.
