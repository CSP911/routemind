# HR desk

The desk that decides the attendance and leave questions the rules do not cover. Its contacts and the
boundary with the other desks are on the page below.

## What it owns
Leave entitlement and its calculation, approving leave of absence, attendance exceptions and
corrections after the month has closed, employment certificates, and the return arrangements after a
long absence.

## What it decides that nothing else can
- **An attendance situation the rules do not reach.** A late leave request, leaving early during a
  half day, what counts as service for someone who transferred in, entitlement during a leave of
  absence. These are decided case by case and recorded.
- **Whether a correction may be made after the 3rd.** The month is closed by then and the pay run has
  read it, so the desk decides whether to correct it in place or settle it in the next month.

## The order that saves time
**Settle the exception with the desk before you put it in the system.** An entry that is rejected
afterwards leaves the period recorded as absence in the meantime, and unpicking that is more work
than the original question. A message with the dates and what actually happened is enough to start.

## What it will send you elsewhere for
| You are asking about | Desk |
|---|---|
| How much a trip pays | Office & purchasing |
| A payslip line, or insurance during leave | Payroll |
| Whether a spend is allowed | Office & purchasing |

The desk is on ext. 2140, and groupware message is usually faster.
