# Attendance system (Groupware > Attendance)

| What you are doing | Menu |
|---|---|
| Booking or cancelling leave | Attendance > Leave request |
| Checking days remaining | Attendance > My attendance |
| Registering a trip | Attendance > Trip request (reflected in attendance once approved) |
| Entering overtime | Attendance > Overtime (only hours requested in advance count) |

- The month closes on **the 3rd of the following month**. After that, corrections go through the HR desk.
- **A record that is not in the system does not reach the pay run.** A verbal approval is not a record.
